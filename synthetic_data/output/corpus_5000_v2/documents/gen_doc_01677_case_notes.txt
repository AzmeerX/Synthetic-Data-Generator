Date: 28 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01677.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Barrowfield Ventures Limited against Cresswell Industries Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Barrowfield Ventures Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-129.
