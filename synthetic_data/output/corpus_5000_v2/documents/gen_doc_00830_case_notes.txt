Date: 7 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00830.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Westerby Ventures Limited against North Yarmouth Holdings Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Westerby Ventures Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-526.
