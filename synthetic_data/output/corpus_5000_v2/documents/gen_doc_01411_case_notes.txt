Case notes for North Southwold Partners Limited against North Southwold Energy Limited

Date: 4 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01411.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Southwold Partners Limited against North Southwold Energy Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Southwold Partners Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-443.

Quin Wyndham signed the Debenture LEG-443.Quin Zouch is the chair of North Southwold Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent North Southwold Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 4 January 2025.

North Southwold Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Southwold Partners Limited.

North Southwold Partners Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 9 January 2025.

Rhea Ellery is a shareholder of North Southwold Partners Limited.

Quin Whitlock is a family member of Quin Wyndham.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Southwold Partners Limited owns North Southwold Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Southwold Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Southwold Energy Limited on 4 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Quin Pell noted that a former employee alleged that North Southwold Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
