Case notes for North Quarrymere Energy Limited against North Quarrymere Systems Limited

Date: 3 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01068.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quarrymere Energy Limited against North Quarrymere Systems Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quarrymere Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-134.

Kellan Everard signed the Loan Note LEG-134.Kellan Grenville is the chair of North Quarrymere Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember International Bank Limited lent North Quarrymere Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 5 January 2025.

North Quarrymere Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quarrymere Energy Limited.

North Quarrymere Energy Limited owes Pember International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 10 January 2025.

Kellan Langford is a shareholder of North Quarrymere Energy Limited.

Kellan Delacourt is a family member of Kellan Everard.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quarrymere Energy Limited owns Juniper Cross Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quarrymere Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quarrymere Systems Limited on 3 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that North Quarrymere Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
