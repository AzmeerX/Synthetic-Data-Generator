Board meeting minutes of North Pinewood Capital Limited

Date: 5 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01372.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Langford LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Nyle, Quin Quade and Alden & Langford LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Pinewood Capital Limited owns North Pinewood Marine Limited.Quin Nyle is the chair of North Pinewood Capital Limited.

Quin Thorne is a shareholder of North Pinewood Capital Limited.

Quin Kerr is a family member of Quin Lorne.

Red Commercial Bank Limited lent North Pinewood Capital Limited GBP 22 million on 16 December 2023.

North Pinewood Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Pinewood Capital Limited.

North Pinewood Capital Limited owes Red Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 21 December 2023.

Alden & Kerr LLP represents North Pinewood Capital Limited in the matter.

North Pinewood Capital Limited owns Jarrow Cross Silo.

Jarrow Cross Silo is located in Ashcombe Bay.

North Pinewood Capital Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-408.Quin Lorne signed the Lease LEG-408.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Jarvis, chair of North Pinewood Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Pinewood Ventures Limited on 5 February 2024.

Bram Jarvis confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Pinewood Ventures Limited by North Pinewood Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Pinewood Ventures Limited by North Pinewood Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
