Board meeting minutes of North Dunmere Marine Limited

Date: 25 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00574.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Sable LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Delacourt, Bastian Fairfax and Alden & Sable LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Dunmere Marine Limited owns North Dunmere Energy Limited.Bastian Delacourt is the chair of North Dunmere Marine Limited.

Bastian Ingram is a shareholder of North Dunmere Marine Limited.

Bastian Ashby is a family member of Bastian Balfour.

Harrow Private Bank Limited lent North Dunmere Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 29 July 2025.

North Dunmere Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunmere Marine Limited.

North Dunmere Marine Limited owes Harrow Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 3 August 2025.

Briar & Ward LLP represents North Dunmere Marine Limited in the matter.

North Dunmere Marine Limited owns Denmoor Marina.

Denmoor Marina is located in Ashcombe Cross.

North Dunmere Marine Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-295.Bastian Balfour signed the Services Agreement LEG-295.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Whitlock, chair of North Dunmere Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Dunmere Systems Limited on 25 March 2025.

Kellan Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Dunmere Systems Limited by North Dunmere Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Dunmere Systems Limited by North Dunmere Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
