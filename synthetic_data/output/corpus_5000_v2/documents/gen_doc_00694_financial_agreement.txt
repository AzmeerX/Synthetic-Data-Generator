Facility agreement between Stan International Bank Limited and North Merrivale Marine Limited

Date: 30 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00694.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Stan International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Merrivale Marine Limited.

North Merrivale Marine Limited owns North Merrivale Energy Limited.Denmoor Private Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 4 March 2024.

North Merrivale Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Marine Limited.

North Merrivale Marine Limited owes Denmoor Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 9 March 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Merrivale Marine Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-403.

Daria Kingsley signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-403.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Merrivale Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Merrivale Ventures Limited on 30 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
