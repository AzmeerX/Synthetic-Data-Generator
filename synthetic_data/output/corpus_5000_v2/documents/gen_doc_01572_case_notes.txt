Case notes for North Grafton Marine Limited against Stanbrook Industries Limited

Date: 23 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01572.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Grafton Marine Limited against Stanbrook Industries Limited in Glenport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Grafton Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-589.

Tamsin Whitlock signed the Supply Agreement LEG-589.Tamsin Yardley is the chair of North Grafton Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Private Bank Limited lent North Grafton Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 2 October 2024.

Oakhaven Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grafton Marine Limited.

North Grafton Marine Limited owes Denmoor Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 7 October 2024.

Ulric Dunmore is a shareholder of North Grafton Marine Limited.

Tamsin Villiers is a family member of Tamsin Whitlock.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Grafton Marine Limited owns North Grafton Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Grafton Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Stanbrook Industries Limited on 23 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that North Grafton Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
