Board meeting minutes of North Eversley Group Limited

Date: 26 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01540.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ashby LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Thorne, Tamsin Vale and Briar & Ashby LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Eversley Group Limited owns North Eversley Logistics Limited.Tamsin Thorne is the chair of North Eversley Group Limited.

Tamsin Zell is a shareholder of North Eversley Group Limited.

Tamsin Quade is a family member of Tamsin Rook.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent North Eversley Group Limited GBP 46 million on 18 November 2023.

North Eversley Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eversley Group Limited.

North Eversley Group Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 23 November 2023.

Alden & Hastings LLP represents North Eversley Group Limited in the matter.

North Eversley Group Limited owns Kestrel Wharf.

Kestrel Wharf is located in Ivybridge.

North Eversley Group Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-560.Tamsin Rook signed the Side Letter LEG-560.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Mercer, chair of North Eversley Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Eversley Capital Limited on 26 April 2027.

Calder Mercer confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Eversley Capital Limited by North Eversley Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Eversley Capital Limited by North Eversley Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
