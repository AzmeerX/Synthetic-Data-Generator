Case notes for North Fingal Capital Limited against North Fingal Property Limited

Date: 17 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01558.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Fingal Capital Limited against North Fingal Property Limited in Elmford High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Fingal Capital Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-576.

Tamsin Ingram signed the Side Letter LEG-576.Tamsin Kingsley is the chair of North Fingal Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent North Fingal Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 12 May 2024.

North Fingal Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fingal Capital Limited.

North Fingal Capital Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 17 May 2024.

Tamsin Pemberton is a shareholder of North Fingal Capital Limited.

Tamsin Hastings is a family member of Tamsin Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Fingal Capital Limited owns North Fingal Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Fingal Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Fingal Property Limited on 17 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Fairfax noted that a former employee alleged that North Fingal Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
