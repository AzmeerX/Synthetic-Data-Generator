Board meeting minutes of North Lowmoor Holdings Limited

Date: 9 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00672.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Hale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Sable, Daria Ulric and Cairn & Hale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Lowmoor Holdings Limited owns Harrow Bay Dock.Daria Sable is the chair of North Lowmoor Holdings Limited.

Daria Yarrow is a shareholder of North Lowmoor Holdings Limited.

Daria Pell is a family member of Daria Quade.

Alder Commercial Bank Limited lent North Lowmoor Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 8 August 2023.

North Lowmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Lowmoor Holdings Limited.

North Lowmoor Holdings Limited owes Alder Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 13 August 2023.

Alden & Fairfax LLP represents North Lowmoor Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Lowmoor Holdings Limited owns Harrow Bay Office Park.

Harrow Bay Office Park is located in Lowmoor Bridge.

North Lowmoor Holdings Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-384.Daria Quade signed the Side Letter LEG-384.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Marek Wyndham, chair of North Lowmoor Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire Lowmoor Logistics Limited on 9 February 2027.

Marek Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Lowmoor Logistics Limited by North Lowmoor Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Lowmoor Logistics Limited by North Lowmoor Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
