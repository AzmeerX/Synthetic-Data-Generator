Case notes for North Osterley Property Limited against Quintrell Partners Limited

Date: 11 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01362.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Osterley Property Limited against Quintrell Partners Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Osterley Property Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-399.

Quin Cairn signed the Power of Attorney LEG-399.Quin Ellery is the chair of North Osterley Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Bank Limited lent North Osterley Property Limited GBP 44 million on 8 September 2023.

North Osterley Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Osterley Property Limited.

North Osterley Property Limited owes Quill Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 13 September 2023.

Quin Jarvis is a shareholder of North Osterley Property Limited.

Quin Briar is a family member of Quin Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Osterley Property Limited owns North Osterley Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Osterley Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Quintrell Partners Limited on 11 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Anika Zouch noted that a former employee alleged that North Osterley Property Limited misstated its revenue.
