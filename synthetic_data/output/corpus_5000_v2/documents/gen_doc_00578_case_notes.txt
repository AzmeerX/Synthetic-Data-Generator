Case notes for North Dunmere Ventures Limited against North Elmsworth Industries Limited

Date: 22 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00578.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Dunmere Ventures Limited against North Elmsworth Industries Limited in Fairmouth Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Dunmere Ventures Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-298.

Bastian Everard signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-298.Bastian Grenville is the chair of North Dunmere Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Merchant Bank Limited lent North Dunmere Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 31 August 2025.

North Elmsworth Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunmere Ventures Limited.

North Dunmere Ventures Limited owes Inver Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 5 September 2025.

Bastian Langford is a shareholder of North Dunmere Ventures Limited.

Bastian Delacourt is a family member of Bastian Everard.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Dunmere Ventures Limited owns North Dunmere Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Dunmere Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Elmsworth Industries Limited on 22 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Alden noted that a former employee alleged that North Dunmere Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
