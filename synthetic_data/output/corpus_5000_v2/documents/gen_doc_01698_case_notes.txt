Case notes for Draycott Partners Limited against Draycott Energy Limited

Date: 22 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01698.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Draycott Partners Limited against Draycott Energy Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Draycott Partners Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-148.

Gideon Ulric signed the Board Resolution LEG-148.Gideon Ward is the chair of Draycott Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Commercial Bank Limited lent Draycott Partners Limited GBP 38 million on 7 June 2023.

Draycott Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Draycott Partners Limited.

Draycott Partners Limited owes Fair Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 12 June 2023.

Gideon Cavendish is a shareholder of Draycott Partners Limited.

Gideon Thorne is a family member of Gideon Ulric.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Draycott Partners Limited owns Draycott Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Draycott Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Draycott Energy Limited on 22 January 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
