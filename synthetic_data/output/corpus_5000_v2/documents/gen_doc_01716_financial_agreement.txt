Facility agreement between Northbank Bank Limited and Eastmoor Property Limited

Date: 13 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01716.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Northbank Bank Limited and the borrower is Eastmoor Property Limited.

Eastmoor Property Limited owns Eastmoor Ventures Limited.Fenwick Bank Limited lent Eastmoor Property Limited GBP 30 million on 30 November 2023.

Eastmoor Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Eastmoor Property Limited.

Eastmoor Property Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 5 December 2023.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Eastmoor Property Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-164.

Gideon Langford signed the Board Resolution LEG-164.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Eastmoor Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Fallow Holdings Limited on 13 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
