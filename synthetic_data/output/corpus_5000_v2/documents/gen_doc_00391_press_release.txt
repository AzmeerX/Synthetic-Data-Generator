Press release concerning Nairn Ventures Limited

Publication date: 3 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00391.

1. Corporate statement

Nairn Ventures Limited today issued the following statement.

Nairn Ventures Limited owns Nairn Systems Limited.

Anika Ingram is a shareholder of Nairn Ventures Limited.

Anika Ashby is a family member of Anika Balfour.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Nairn Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 5 April 2025.

Orkney Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Nairn Ventures Limited.

Nairn Ventures Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 10 April 2025.

Anika Balfour signed the Facility Agreement LEG-130.
