Board meeting minutes of North Redmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 7 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00756.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Vale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Underwood, Elias Whitlock and Briar & Vale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Redmoor Logistics Limited owns North Redmoor Trading Limited.Elias Underwood is the chair of North Redmoor Logistics Limited.

Elias Zouch is a shareholder of North Redmoor Logistics Limited.

Elias Ravenscroft is a family member of Elias Sinclair.

Nether Bank Limited lent North Redmoor Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 10 November 2025.

North Redmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Redmoor Logistics Limited.

North Redmoor Logistics Limited owes Nether Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 15 November 2025.

Cairn & Hale LLP represents North Redmoor Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Redmoor Logistics Limited owns Lowmoor Mill.

Lowmoor Mill is located in Vicarage.

North Redmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-459.Elias Sinclair signed the Debenture LEG-459.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Jeffreys, chair of North Redmoor Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Redmoor Energy Limited on 7 June 2025.

Oren Jeffreys confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Redmoor Energy Limited by North Redmoor Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Redmoor Energy Limited by North Redmoor Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
