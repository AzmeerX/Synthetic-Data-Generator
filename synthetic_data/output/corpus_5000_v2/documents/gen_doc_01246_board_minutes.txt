Board meeting minutes of North Gorse Holdings Limited

Date: 19 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01246.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Pemberton LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Whitlock, Nadia Yardley and Alden & Pemberton LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Gorse Holdings Limited owns Denmoor Dock.Nadia Whitlock is the chair of North Gorse Holdings Limited.

Oren Briar is a shareholder of North Gorse Holdings Limited.

Nadia Tremayne is a family member of Nadia Underwood.

Uldale Commercial Bank Limited lent North Gorse Holdings Limited GBP 34 million on 6 March 2025.

North Gorse Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Gorse Holdings Limited.

North Gorse Holdings Limited owes Uldale Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 11 March 2025.

Alden & Balfour LLP represents North Gorse Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Gorse Holdings Limited owns Denmoor Office Park.

Denmoor Office Park is located in Inglemere Moor.

North Gorse Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-294.Nadia Underwood signed the Loan Note LEG-294.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Hastings, chair of North Gorse Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Gorse Partners Limited on 19 December 2024.

Yara Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Gorse Partners Limited by North Gorse Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Gorse Partners Limited by North Gorse Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
