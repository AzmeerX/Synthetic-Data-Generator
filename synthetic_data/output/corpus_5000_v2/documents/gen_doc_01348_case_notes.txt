Case notes for North Norbury Energy Limited against North Norbury Systems Limited

Date: 3 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01348.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Norbury Energy Limited against North Norbury Systems Limited in Glenport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Norbury Energy Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-386.
