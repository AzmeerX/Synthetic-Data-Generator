Case notes for North Elmstead Ventures Limited against Pemberton Partners Limited

Date: 22 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01229.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Elmstead Ventures Limited against Pemberton Partners Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Elmstead Ventures Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-280.

Nadia Fairfax signed the Lease LEG-280.Nadia Hastings is the chair of North Elmstead Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Merchant Bank Limited lent North Elmstead Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 3 October 2024.

North Farnley Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmstead Ventures Limited.

North Elmstead Ventures Limited owes Stan Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 8 October 2024.

Nadia Montague is a shareholder of North Elmstead Ventures Limited.

Nadia Everard is a family member of Nadia Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Elmstead Ventures Limited owns North Elmstead Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Elmstead Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Pemberton Partners Limited on 22 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Orrick noted that a former employee alleged that North Elmstead Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
