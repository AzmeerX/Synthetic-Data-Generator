Board meeting minutes of North Upton Logistics Limited

Date: 21 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01435.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Yarrow & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Yarrow, Rhea Ashby and Yarrow & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Upton Logistics Limited owns North Upton Trading Limited.Rhea Yarrow is the chair of North Upton Logistics Limited.

Rhea Delacourt is a shareholder of North Upton Logistics Limited.

Rhea Thorne is a family member of Rhea Vale.

Fair Bank Limited lent North Upton Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 3 September 2025.

North Upton Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Upton Logistics Limited.

North Upton Logistics Limited owes Fair Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 8 September 2025.

Briar & Sable LLP represents North Upton Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Upton Logistics Limited owns Merrivale Marina.

Merrivale Marina is located in Harrowden Port.

North Upton Logistics Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-465.Rhea Vale signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-465.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Garrow, chair of North Upton Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Upton Energy Limited on 21 April 2025.

Amina Garrow confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Upton Energy Limited by North Upton Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Upton Energy Limited by North Upton Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
