Board meeting minutes of Fallow Logistics Limited

Date: 26 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01722.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Quade LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Underwood, Gideon Whitlock and Cairn & Quade LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
