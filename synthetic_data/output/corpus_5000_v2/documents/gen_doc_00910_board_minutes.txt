Board meeting minutes of Netherby Holdings Limited

Date: 5 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00910.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Harrow & Finch LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Underwood, Calder Whitlock and Harrow & Finch LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Netherby Holdings Limited owns Netherby Industries Limited.Calder Underwood is the chair of Netherby Holdings Limited.

Calder Zouch is a shareholder of Netherby Holdings Limited.

Calder Ravenscroft is a family member of Calder Sinclair.

Carrow Bank Limited lent Netherby Holdings Limited GBP 12 million on 16 November 2022.

Netherby Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Netherby Holdings Limited.

Netherby Holdings Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 21 November 2022.

Harrow & Finch LLP represents Netherby Holdings Limited in the matter.

Netherby Holdings Limited owns Eastmere Silo.

Eastmere Silo is located in Juniper Cross.

Netherby Holdings Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-055.Calder Sinclair signed the Services Agreement LEG-055.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Tremayne, chair of Netherby Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Fallow Group Limited on 5 February 2025.

Rhea Tremayne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Fallow Group Limited by Netherby Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Fallow Group Limited by Netherby Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
