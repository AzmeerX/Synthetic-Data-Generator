Board meeting minutes of Lorne Ventures Limited

Date: 28 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00364.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Ellery, Anika Garrow and Briar & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Lorne Ventures Limited owns Lorne Systems Limited.Anika Ellery is the chair of Lorne Ventures Limited.

Anika Jarvis is a shareholder of Lorne Ventures Limited.

Anika Alden is a family member of Anika Briar.

Uldale Merchant Bank Limited lent Lorne Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 15 July 2024.

Moray Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Lorne Ventures Limited.

Lorne Ventures Limited owes Uldale Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 20 July 2024.

Alden & Mercer LLP represents Lorne Ventures Limited in the matter.

Lorne Ventures Limited owns Harrowgate Plant.

Harrowgate Plant is located in Elmstead.

Lorne Ventures Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-106.Anika Briar signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-106.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Ingram, chair of Lorne Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Moray Group Limited on 28 June 2024.

Gideon Ingram confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Moray Group Limited by Lorne Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Moray Group Limited by Lorne Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
