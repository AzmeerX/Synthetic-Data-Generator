Board meeting minutes of North Lambley Group Limited

Date: 16 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00994.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Yardley LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Juno Orrick, Juno Quade and Alden & Yardley LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Lambley Group Limited owns North Lambley Logistics Limited.Juno Orrick is the chair of North Lambley Group Limited.

Juno Thorne is a shareholder of North Lambley Group Limited.

Juno Lorne is a family member of Juno Mercer.

Elm International Bank Limited lent North Lambley Group Limited GBP 22 million on 10 January 2023.

North Lambley Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Lambley Group Limited.

North Lambley Group Limited owes Elm International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 15 January 2023.

Briar & Lorne LLP represents North Lambley Group Limited in the matter.

North Lambley Group Limited owns Greyhaven Data Centre.

Greyhaven Data Centre is located in Carrow Green.

North Lambley Group Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-068.Juno Mercer signed the Board Resolution LEG-068.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Tamsin Delacourt, chair of North Lambley Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Lambley Capital Limited on 16 September 2026.

Tamsin Delacourt confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Lambley Capital Limited by North Lambley Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Lambley Capital Limited by North Lambley Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
