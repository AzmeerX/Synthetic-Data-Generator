Date: 12 June 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00466.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Jarrow Energy Limited against Trossachs Energy Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

Jarrow Energy Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-015.

Calder Briar is a family member of Calder Cairn.4. Findings and allegations
