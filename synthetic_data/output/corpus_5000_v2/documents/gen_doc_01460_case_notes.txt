Case notes for North Wexford Industries Limited against North Wexford Trading Limited

Date: 13 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01460.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Wexford Industries Limited against North Wexford Trading Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Wexford Industries Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-487.

Rhea Tremayne signed the Services Agreement LEG-487.Rhea Villiers is the chair of North Wexford Industries Limited.
