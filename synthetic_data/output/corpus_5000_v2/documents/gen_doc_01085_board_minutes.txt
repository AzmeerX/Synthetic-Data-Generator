Board meeting minutes of North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited

Date: 2 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01085.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Wyndham, Kellan Zouch and Alden & Jarvis LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited owns Kestrelford Data Centre.Kellan Wyndham is the chair of North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited.

Lena Cairn is a shareholder of North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited.

Kellan Underwood is a family member of Kellan Villiers.

Stan Commercial Bank Limited lent North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 30 June 2025.

North Saltmarsh Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited.

North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited owes Stan Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 5 July 2025.

Alden & Mercer LLP represents North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited owns Kestrelford Yard.

Kestrelford Yard is located in Netherby Bay.

North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-150.Kellan Villiers signed the Loan Note LEG-150.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Thorne, chair of North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Saltmarsh Trading Limited on 2 March 2025.

Vera Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Saltmarsh Trading Limited by North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Saltmarsh Trading Limited by North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
