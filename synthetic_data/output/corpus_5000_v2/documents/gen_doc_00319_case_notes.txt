Case notes for Isbourne Capital Limited against Isbourne Energy Limited

Date: 30 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00319.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Isbourne Capital Limited against Isbourne Energy Limited in Alderport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Isbourne Capital Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-066.

Zane Lorne signed the Facility Agreement LEG-066.Zane Nyle is the chair of Isbourne Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Commercial Bank Limited lent Isbourne Capital Limited GBP 46 million on 2 May 2023.

Isbourne Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Isbourne Capital Limited.

Isbourne Capital Limited owes Nether Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 7 May 2023.

Zane Sable is a shareholder of Isbourne Capital Limited.

Zane Kerr is a family member of Zane Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Isbourne Capital Limited owns Isbourne Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
