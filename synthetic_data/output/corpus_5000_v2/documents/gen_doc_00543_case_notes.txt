Case notes for Kelbrook Logistics Limited against North Belmont Capital Limited

Date: 20 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00543.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kelbrook Logistics Limited against North Belmont Capital Limited in Alderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kelbrook Logistics Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-022.

Calder Jarvis signed the Lease LEG-022.Calder Lorne is the chair of Kelbrook Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Kelbrook Logistics Limited GBP 26 million on 15 July 2026.

Kelbrook Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Kelbrook Logistics Limited.

Kelbrook Logistics Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 20 July 2026.

Calder Quade is a shareholder of Kelbrook Logistics Limited.

Calder Ivers is a family member of Calder Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kelbrook Logistics Limited owns Calderport Mill.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kelbrook Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Belmont Capital Limited on 20 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Kelbrook Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
