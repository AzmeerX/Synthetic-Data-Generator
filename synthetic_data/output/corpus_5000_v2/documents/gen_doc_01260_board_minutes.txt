Board meeting minutes of North Hampstead Industries Limited

Date: 27 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01260.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Kerr, Oren Mercer and Briar & Fenwick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Hampstead Industries Limited owns North Hampstead Group Limited.Oren Kerr is the chair of North Hampstead Industries Limited.

Oren Pell is a shareholder of North Hampstead Industries Limited.

Oren Garrow is a family member of Oren Hale.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent North Hampstead Industries Limited GBP 20 million on 27 July 2025.

North Hampstead Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Hampstead Industries Limited.

North Hampstead Industries Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 1 August 2025.

Alden & Osborne LLP represents North Hampstead Industries Limited in the matter.

North Hampstead Industries Limited owns Elmford Yard.

Elmford Yard is located in Kelbrook Cross.

North Hampstead Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-307.Oren Hale signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-307.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Whitlock, chair of North Hampstead Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Pemberton Energy Limited on 27 March 2025.

Yara Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Pemberton Energy Limited by North Hampstead Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Pemberton Energy Limited by North Hampstead Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
