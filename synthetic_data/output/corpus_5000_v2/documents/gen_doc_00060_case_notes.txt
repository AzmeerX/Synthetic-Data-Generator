Case notes for Neston Holdings Limited against Neston Logistics Limited

Date: 27 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00060.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Neston Holdings Limited against Neston Logistics Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Neston Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-438.

Ulric Ashby signed the Loan Note LEG-438.Ulric Cavendish is the chair of Neston Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Commercial Bank Limited lent Neston Holdings Limited GBP 18 million on 5 August 2025.

Neston Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Neston Holdings Limited.

Neston Holdings Limited owes Stan Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 10 August 2025.

Ulric Hastings is a shareholder of Neston Holdings Limited.

Ulric Zell is a family member of Ulric Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Neston Holdings Limited owns Jarrow Cross Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Neston Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Neston Logistics Limited on 27 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Zell noted that a former employee alleged that Neston Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
