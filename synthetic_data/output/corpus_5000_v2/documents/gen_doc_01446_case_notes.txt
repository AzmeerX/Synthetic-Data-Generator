Case notes for North Vinehall Industries Limited against North Vinehall Trading Limited

Date: 7 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01446.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Vinehall Industries Limited against North Vinehall Trading Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Vinehall Industries Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-475.

Rhea Grenville signed the Debenture LEG-475.Rhea Ingram is the chair of North Vinehall Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Private Bank Limited lent North Vinehall Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 22 December 2025.

North Vinehall Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Vinehall Industries Limited.

North Vinehall Industries Limited owes Glen Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 27 December 2025.

Rhea Neville is a shareholder of North Vinehall Industries Limited.

Rhea Fairfax is a family member of Rhea Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Vinehall Industries Limited owns North Vinehall Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Vinehall Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Vinehall Trading Limited on 7 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Sable noted that a former employee alleged that North Vinehall Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
