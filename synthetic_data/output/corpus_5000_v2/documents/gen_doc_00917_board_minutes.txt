Board meeting minutes of North Fallow Trading Limited

Date: 26 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00917.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Cairn LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Sinclair, Hana Underwood and Alden & Cairn LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Fallow Trading Limited owns Calderport Marina.Hana Sinclair is the chair of North Fallow Trading Limited.

Hana Wyndham is a shareholder of North Fallow Trading Limited.

Hana Osborne is a family member of Hana Pemberton.

Pember Merchant Bank Limited lent North Fallow Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 28 July 2025.

North Fallow Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fallow Trading Limited.

North Fallow Trading Limited owes Pember Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 2 August 2025.

Cairn & Yarrow LLP represents North Fallow Trading Limited in the matter.

North Fallow Trading Limited owns Calderport Mill.

Calderport Mill is located in Kelbrook.

North Fallow Trading Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-604.Hana Pemberton signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-604.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Silas Alden, chair of North Fallow Trading Limited, proposed to acquire North Fallow Energy Limited on 26 March 2025.

Silas Alden confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Fallow Energy Limited by North Fallow Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Fallow Energy Limited by North Fallow Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
