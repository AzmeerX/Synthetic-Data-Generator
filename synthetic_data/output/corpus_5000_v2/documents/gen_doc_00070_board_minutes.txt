Board meeting minutes of Neston Property Limited

Date: 5 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00070.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Jeffreys LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Langford, Ulric Neville and Alden & Jeffreys LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Ulric Ingram is a family member of Ulric Jeffreys.

Alden & Hale LLP represents Neston Property Limited in the matter.

Neston Property Limited owns Kelbrook Yard.

The acquisition of Ormsby Group Limited by Neston Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
