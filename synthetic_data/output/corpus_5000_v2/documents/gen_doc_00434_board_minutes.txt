Board meeting minutes of Rannoch Industries Limited

Date: 31 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00434.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ivers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bram Thorne, Bram Vale and Alden & Ivers LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Rannoch Industries Limited owns Rannoch Group Limited.Bram Thorne is the chair of Rannoch Industries Limited.

Bram Zell is a shareholder of Rannoch Industries Limited.

Bram Quade is a family member of Bram Rook.

Inver Private Bank Limited lent Rannoch Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 8 June 2026.

Rannoch Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Rannoch Industries Limited.

Rannoch Industries Limited owes Inver Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 13 June 2026.

Briar & Balfour LLP represents Rannoch Industries Limited in the matter.

Rannoch Industries Limited owns Larkspur Rail Depot.

Larkspur Rail Depot is located in Harrowden Bay.

Rannoch Industries Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-169.Bram Rook signed the Trust Deed LEG-169.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ivo Ellery, chair of Rannoch Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Rannoch Capital Limited on 31 October 2025.

Ivo Ellery confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Rannoch Capital Limited by Rannoch Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Rannoch Capital Limited by Rannoch Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
