Case notes for North Quintrell Capital Limited against North Quintrell Property Limited

Date: 29 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00746.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quintrell Capital Limited against North Quintrell Property Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quintrell Capital Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-450.

Elias Ingram signed the Facility Agreement LEG-450.Elias Kingsley is the chair of North Quintrell Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low Commercial Bank Limited lent North Quintrell Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 3 August 2025.

North Quintrell Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quintrell Capital Limited.

North Quintrell Capital Limited owes Low Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 8 August 2025.

Elias Pemberton is a shareholder of North Quintrell Capital Limited.

Elias Hastings is a family member of Elias Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quintrell Capital Limited owns North Quintrell Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quintrell Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quintrell Property Limited on 29 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Zell noted that a former employee alleged that North Quintrell Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
