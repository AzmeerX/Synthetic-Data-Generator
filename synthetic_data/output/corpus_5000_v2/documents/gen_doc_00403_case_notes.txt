Case notes for Orkney Property Limited against Pembroke Holdings Limited

Date: 28 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00403.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Orkney Property Limited against Pembroke Holdings Limited in Glenport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Orkney Property Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-141.

Anika Montague signed the Supply Agreement LEG-141.Anika Osborne is the chair of Orkney Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Bank Limited lent Orkney Property Limited GBP 36 million on 4 August 2025.

Orkney Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Orkney Property Limited.

Orkney Property Limited owes Elm Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 9 August 2025.

Anika Underwood is a shareholder of Orkney Property Limited.

Anika Langford is a family member of Anika Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Orkney Property Limited owns Orkney Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Orkney Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Pembroke Holdings Limited on 28 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bram Nyle noted that a former employee alleged that Orkney Property Limited misstated its revenue.
