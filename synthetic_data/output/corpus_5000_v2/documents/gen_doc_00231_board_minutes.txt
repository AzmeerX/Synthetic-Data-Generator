Board meeting minutes of Borrowdale Systems Limited

Date: 24 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00231.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Quade LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Ingram, Xander Kingsley and Briar & Quade LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Borrowdale Systems Limited owns Culm Holdings Limited.Xander Ingram is the chair of Borrowdale Systems Limited.

Xander Neville is a shareholder of Borrowdale Systems Limited.

Xander Fairfax is a family member of Xander Grenville.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Borrowdale Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 10 August 2025.

Culm Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Borrowdale Systems Limited.

Borrowdale Systems Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 15 August 2025.

Alden & Yarrow LLP represents Borrowdale Systems Limited in the matter.

Borrowdale Systems Limited owns Pinehurst Silo.

Pinehurst Silo is located in Glenmoor Hill.

Borrowdale Systems Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-593.Xander Grenville signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-593.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Whitlock, chair of Borrowdale Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Culm Group Limited on 24 March 2025.

Daria Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Culm Group Limited by Borrowdale Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Culm Group Limited by Borrowdale Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
