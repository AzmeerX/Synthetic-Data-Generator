Facility agreement between Tarrow Trust Bank Limited and North Saltmarsh Marine Limited

Date: 27 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01093.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Tarrow Trust Bank Limited and the borrower is North Saltmarsh Marine Limited.

North Saltmarsh Marine Limited owns North Saltmarsh Energy Limited.Tarrow Private Bank Limited lent North Saltmarsh Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 15 September 2025.

North Saltmarsh Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Saltmarsh Marine Limited.

North Saltmarsh Marine Limited owes Tarrow Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 20 September 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Saltmarsh Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-157.

Lena Cairn signed the Supply Agreement LEG-157.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Saltmarsh Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited on 27 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
