Board meeting minutes of North Barrowfield Group Limited

Date: 28 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00861.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Villiers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Sinclair, Gideon Underwood and Alden & Villiers LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Barrowfield Group Limited owns North Barrowfield Logistics Limited.Gideon Sinclair is the chair of North Barrowfield Group Limited.

Gideon Wyndham is a shareholder of North Barrowfield Group Limited.

Gideon Osborne is a family member of Gideon Pemberton.

Glen International Bank Limited lent North Barrowfield Group Limited GBP 22 million on 25 January 2024.

North Barrowfield Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Barrowfield Group Limited.

North Barrowfield Group Limited owes Glen International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 30 January 2024.

Briar & Vale LLP represents North Barrowfield Group Limited in the matter.

North Barrowfield Group Limited owns Quintrell Quay.

Quintrell Quay is located in Kelbrook Port.

North Barrowfield Group Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-554.Gideon Pemberton signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-554.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Quin Sinclair, chair of North Barrowfield Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Barrowfield Capital Limited on 28 February 2024.

Quin Sinclair confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Barrowfield Capital Limited by North Barrowfield Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Barrowfield Capital Limited by North Barrowfield Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
