Case notes for Lorne Marine Limited against Lorne Ventures Limited

Date: 7 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00361.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Lorne Marine Limited against Lorne Ventures Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Lorne Marine Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-103.

Zane Yardley signed the Services Agreement LEG-103.Anika Alden is the chair of Lorne Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Private Bank Limited lent Lorne Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 12 June 2024.

Lorne Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Lorne Marine Limited.

Lorne Marine Limited owes Tarrow Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 17 June 2024.

Anika Garrow is a shareholder of Lorne Marine Limited.

Zane Wyndham is a family member of Zane Yardley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Lorne Marine Limited owns Lorne Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Lorne Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Lorne Ventures Limited on 7 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
