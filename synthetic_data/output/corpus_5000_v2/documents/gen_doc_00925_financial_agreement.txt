Facility agreement between Silvergate Bank Limited and North Fallow Systems Limited

Date: 21 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00925.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Silvergate Bank Limited and the borrower is North Fallow Systems Limited.

North Fallow Systems Limited owns North Grimsdale Holdings Limited.Quill Trust Bank Limited lent North Fallow Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 13 October 2025.

North Grimsdale Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fallow Systems Limited.

North Fallow Systems Limited owes Quill Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 18 October 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Fallow Systems Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-005.

Hana Wyndham signed the Facility Agreement LEG-005.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Fallow Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Grimsdale Group Limited on 21 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
