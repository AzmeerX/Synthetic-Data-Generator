Board meeting minutes of Uldale Logistics Limited

Date: 28 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01603.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Cavendish LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ingram, Elias Kingsley and Cairn & Cavendish LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Uldale Logistics Limited owns Eastmere Plant.Elias Ingram is the chair of Uldale Logistics Limited.

Elias Neville is a shareholder of Uldale Logistics Limited.

Elias Fairfax is a family member of Elias Grenville.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Uldale Logistics Limited GBP 26 million on 30 June 2025.

Uldale Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Logistics Limited.

Uldale Logistics Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 5 July 2025.

Alden & Mercer LLP represents Uldale Logistics Limited in the matter.

Uldale Logistics Limited owns Fenwick Bay Wharf.

Fenwick Bay Wharf is located in Ashcombe Port.

Uldale Logistics Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-062.Elias Grenville signed the Licence LEG-062.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Balfour, chair of Uldale Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Uldale Marine Limited on 28 March 2025.

Daria Balfour confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Uldale Marine Limited by Uldale Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Uldale Marine Limited by Uldale Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
