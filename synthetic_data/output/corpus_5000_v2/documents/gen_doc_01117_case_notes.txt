Case notes for North Underhill Partners Limited against Hollowbrook Holdings Limited

Date: 12 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01117.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Underhill Partners Limited against Hollowbrook Holdings Limited in Fairmouth Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Underhill Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-179.

Lena Ashby signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-179.Lena Cavendish is the chair of North Underhill Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent North Underhill Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 15 May 2026.

North Underhill Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Underhill Partners Limited.

North Underhill Partners Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 20 May 2026.

Lena Hastings is a shareholder of North Underhill Partners Limited.

Lena Zell is a family member of Lena Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Underhill Partners Limited owns North Underhill Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Underhill Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hollowbrook Holdings Limited on 12 October 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
