Case notes for North Dunkeld Partners Limited against North Dunkeld Marine Limited

Date: 15 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01530.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Dunkeld Partners Limited against North Dunkeld Marine Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Dunkeld Partners Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-551.

Tamsin Hale signed the Services Agreement LEG-551.Tamsin Jarvis is the chair of North Dunkeld Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Trust Bank Limited lent North Dunkeld Partners Limited GBP 28 million on 11 August 2023.

North Dunkeld Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunkeld Partners Limited.

North Dunkeld Partners Limited owes Tarrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 16 August 2023.

Tamsin Pell is a shareholder of North Dunkeld Partners Limited.

Tamsin Garrow is a family member of Tamsin Hale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Dunkeld Partners Limited owns North Dunkeld Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Dunkeld Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Dunkeld Marine Limited on 15 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that North Dunkeld Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
