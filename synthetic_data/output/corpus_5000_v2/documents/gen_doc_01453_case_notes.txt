Case notes for Westmarch Logistics Limited against North Vinehall Property Limited

Date: 25 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01453.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Westmarch Logistics Limited against North Vinehall Property Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Westmarch Logistics Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-024.

Rhea Rook signed the Lease LEG-024.Nadia Jarvis is the chair of Westmarch Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Westmarch Logistics Limited GBP 58 million on 20 October 2022.

Quarrystone Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Westmarch Logistics Limited.

Westmarch Logistics Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 27 million as at 25 October 2022.

Xander Garrow is a shareholder of Westmarch Logistics Limited.

Nadia Jarvis is a family member of Rhea Rook.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Westmarch Logistics Limited owns Fenwick Cold Store.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Westmarch Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Vinehall Property Limited on 25 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Ashby noted that a former employee alleged that Westmarch Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
