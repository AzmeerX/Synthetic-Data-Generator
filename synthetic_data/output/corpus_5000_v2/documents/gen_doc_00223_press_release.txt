Publication date: 27 January 2025.

1. Corporate statement

Borrowdale Logistics Limited today issued the following statement.

Borrowdale Logistics Limited owns Borrowdale Trading Limited.

Xander Fairfax is a shareholder of Borrowdale Logistics Limited.
