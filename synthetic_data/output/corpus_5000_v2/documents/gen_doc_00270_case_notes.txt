Case notes for Ennerdale Property Limited against Fermanagh Holdings Limited

Date: 22 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00270.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ennerdale Property Limited against Fermanagh Holdings Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ennerdale Property Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-021.

Yara Pell signed the Services Agreement LEG-021.Yara Sable is the chair of Ennerdale Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Bank Limited lent Ennerdale Property Limited GBP 36 million on 19 August 2026.

Ennerdale Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Ennerdale Property Limited.

Ennerdale Property Limited owes Glen Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 24 August 2026.

Yara Yarrow is a shareholder of Ennerdale Property Limited.

Yara Orrick is a family member of Yara Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ennerdale Property Limited owns Ennerdale Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ennerdale Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Fermanagh Holdings Limited on 22 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Ennerdale Property Limited misstated its revenue.
