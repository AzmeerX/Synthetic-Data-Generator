Case notes for Draycott Systems Limited against Eastmoor Group Limited

Date: 12 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01705.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Draycott Systems Limited against Eastmoor Group Limited in Glenport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Draycott Systems Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-154.

Gideon Balfour signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-154.Gideon Delacourt is the chair of Draycott Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Commercial Bank Limited lent Draycott Systems Limited GBP 50 million on 12 August 2023.

Eastmoor Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Draycott Systems Limited.

Draycott Systems Limited owes Glen Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 17 August 2023.

Gideon Ingram is a shareholder of Draycott Systems Limited.

Gideon Ashby is a family member of Gideon Balfour.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Draycott Systems Limited owns Juniper Cross Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Draycott Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Eastmoor Group Limited on 12 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that Draycott Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
