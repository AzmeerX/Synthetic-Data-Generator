Case notes for Alderney Marine Limited against Alderney Ventures Limited

Date: 25 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00214.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Alderney Marine Limited against Alderney Ventures Limited in Fairmouth High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Alderney Marine Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-577.

Xander Pell signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-577.Xander Rook is the chair of Alderney Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Private Bank Limited lent Alderney Marine Limited GBP 16 million on 15 February 2025.

Alderney Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Alderney Marine Limited.

Alderney Marine Limited owes Tarrow Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 20 February 2025.

Xander Ward is a shareholder of Alderney Marine Limited.

Xander Orrick is a family member of Xander Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Alderney Marine Limited owns Alderney Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Alderney Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Alderney Ventures Limited on 25 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Alderney Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
