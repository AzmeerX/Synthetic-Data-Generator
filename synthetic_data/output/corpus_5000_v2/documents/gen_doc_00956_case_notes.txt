Case notes for North Ivybridge Logistics Limited against North Ivybridge Capital Limited

Date: 24 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00956.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ivybridge Logistics Limited against North Ivybridge Capital Limited in Bexhaven Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ivybridge Logistics Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-033.

Ivo Cavendish signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-033.Ivo Everard is the chair of North Ivybridge Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent North Ivybridge Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 17 August 2026.

North Ivybridge Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ivybridge Logistics Limited.

North Ivybridge Logistics Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 22 August 2026.

Ivo Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Ivybridge Logistics Limited.

Ivo Balfour is a family member of Ivo Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ivybridge Logistics Limited owns North Ivybridge Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ivybridge Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Ivybridge Capital Limited on 24 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Silas Osborne noted that a former employee alleged that North Ivybridge Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
