Case notes for Aldergate Property Limited against Barrowfield Holdings Limited

Date: 22 May 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01663.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Aldergate Property Limited against Barrowfield Holdings Limited in Glenport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Aldergate Property Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-116.

Farah Montague signed the Board Resolution LEG-116.Farah Osborne is the chair of Aldergate Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Aldergate Property Limited GBP 14 million on 20 June 2022.

Aldergate Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Property Limited.

Aldergate Property Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 25 June 2022.

Farah Underwood is a shareholder of Aldergate Property Limited.

Farah Langford is a family member of Farah Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Aldergate Property Limited owns Aldergate Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Aldergate Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Barrowfield Holdings Limited on 22 May 2026.
