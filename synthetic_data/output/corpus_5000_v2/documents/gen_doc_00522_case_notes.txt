Case notes for Yorkshire Energy Limited against Yorkshire Systems Limited

Date: 26 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00522.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yorkshire Energy Limited against Yorkshire Systems Limited in Elmford Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yorkshire Energy Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-248.

Amina Everard signed the Lease LEG-248.Amina Grenville is the chair of Yorkshire Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Yorkshire Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 28 February 2024.

Yorkshire Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Yorkshire Energy Limited.

Yorkshire Energy Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 4 March 2024.

Amina Langford is a shareholder of Yorkshire Energy Limited.

Amina Delacourt is a family member of Amina Everard.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yorkshire Energy Limited owns Alderport Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yorkshire Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yorkshire Systems Limited on 26 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Yorkshire Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
