Case notes for North Redmoor Industries Limited against North Redmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 17 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00753.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Redmoor Industries Limited against North Redmoor Logistics Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Redmoor Industries Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-457.

Elias Pemberton signed the Trust Deed LEG-457.Elias Sinclair is the chair of North Redmoor Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Private Bank Limited lent North Redmoor Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 19 October 2025.

North Redmoor Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Redmoor Industries Limited.

North Redmoor Industries Limited owes Merri Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 24 October 2025.

Elias Wyndham is a shareholder of North Redmoor Industries Limited.

Elias Osborne is a family member of Elias Pemberton.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Redmoor Industries Limited owns North Redmoor Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Redmoor Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Redmoor Logistics Limited on 17 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that North Redmoor Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
