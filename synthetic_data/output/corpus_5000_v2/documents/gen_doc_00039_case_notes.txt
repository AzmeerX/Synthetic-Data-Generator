Case notes for Langton Partners Limited against Langton Energy Limited

Date: 31 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00039.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Langton Partners Limited against Langton Energy Limited in Glenport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Langton Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-419.

Ulric Garrow signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-419.Ulric Ivers is the chair of Langton Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Trust Bank Limited lent Langton Partners Limited GBP 20 million on 8 January 2025.

Langton Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Langton Partners Limited.

Langton Partners Limited owes Pember Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 13 January 2025.

Ulric Nyle is a shareholder of Langton Partners Limited.

Ulric Fenwick is a family member of Ulric Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Langton Partners Limited owns Langton Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Langton Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Langton Energy Limited on 31 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that Langton Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
