Case notes for Tavistock Ventures Limited against Ulverston Industries Limited

Date: 24 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00151.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Tavistock Ventures Limited against Ulverston Industries Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Tavistock Ventures Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-520.

Wren Hale signed the Lease LEG-520.Wren Jarvis is the chair of Tavistock Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kel Merchant Bank Limited lent Tavistock Ventures Limited GBP 22 million on 30 May 2023.

Ulverston Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Tavistock Ventures Limited.

Tavistock Ventures Limited owes Kel Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 4 June 2023.

Wren Orrick is a shareholder of Tavistock Ventures Limited.

Wren Garrow is a family member of Wren Hale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Tavistock Ventures Limited owns Tavistock Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Tavistock Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ulverston Industries Limited on 24 December 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Tavistock Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
