Case notes for North Trentvale Trading Limited against North Trentvale Marine Limited

Date: 6 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01103.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Trentvale Trading Limited against North Trentvale Marine Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Trentvale Trading Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-166.

Lena Lorne signed the Loan Note LEG-166.Lena Nyle is the chair of North Trentvale Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent North Trentvale Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 23 December 2025.

North Trentvale Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Trentvale Trading Limited.

North Trentvale Trading Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 28 December 2025.

Lena Thorne is a shareholder of North Trentvale Trading Limited.

Lena Kerr is a family member of Lena Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Trentvale Trading Limited owns Larkspur Fleet.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Trentvale Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Trentvale Marine Limited on 6 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Montague noted that a former employee alleged that North Trentvale Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
