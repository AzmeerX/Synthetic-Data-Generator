Board meeting minutes of North Dunkeld Marine Limited

Date: 8 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01533.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Sable LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Lorne, Tamsin Orrick and Briar & Sable LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Dunkeld Marine Limited owns North Dunkeld Energy Limited.Tamsin Lorne is the chair of North Dunkeld Marine Limited.

Tamsin Rook is a shareholder of North Dunkeld Marine Limited.

Tamsin Ivers is a family member of Tamsin Jarvis.

Tarrow Private Bank Limited lent North Dunkeld Marine Limited GBP 32 million on 2 September 2023.

North Dunkeld Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunkeld Marine Limited.

North Dunkeld Marine Limited owes Tarrow Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 7 September 2023.

Alden & Ashby LLP represents North Dunkeld Marine Limited in the matter.

North Dunkeld Marine Limited owns Quintrell Farm.

Quintrell Farm is located in Barrowfield.

North Dunkeld Marine Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-553.Tamsin Jarvis signed the Trust Deed LEG-553.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Fenwick, chair of North Dunkeld Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Eversley Holdings Limited on 8 March 2027.

Calder Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Eversley Holdings Limited by North Dunkeld Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Eversley Holdings Limited by North Dunkeld Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
