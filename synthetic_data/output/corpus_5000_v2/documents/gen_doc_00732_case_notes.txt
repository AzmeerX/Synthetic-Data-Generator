Case notes for Lowmoor Energy Limited against North Pemberton Energy Limited

Date: 21 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00732.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Lowmoor Energy Limited against North Pemberton Energy Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Lowmoor Energy Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-039.

Calder Balfour signed the Services Agreement LEG-039.Calder Delacourt is the chair of Lowmoor Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Energy Limited GBP 20 million on 24 May 2022.

Lowmoor Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Energy Limited.

Lowmoor Energy Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 29 May 2022.

Calder Ingram is a shareholder of Lowmoor Energy Limited.

Calder Ashby is a family member of Calder Balfour.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Lowmoor Energy Limited owns Lowmoor Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Lowmoor Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Pemberton Energy Limited on 21 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Kerr noted that a former employee alleged that Lowmoor Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
