Board meeting minutes of North Gorse Marine Limited

Date: 6 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01253.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Wyndham LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Dunmore, Oren Fenwick and Alden & Wyndham LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Gorse Marine Limited owns North Gorse Energy Limited.Oren Dunmore is the chair of North Gorse Marine Limited.

Oren Ivers is a shareholder of North Gorse Marine Limited.

Oren Alden is a family member of Oren Briar.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent North Gorse Marine Limited GBP 48 million on 22 May 2025.

North Gorse Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Gorse Marine Limited.

North Gorse Marine Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 27 May 2025.

Alden & Ingram LLP represents North Gorse Marine Limited in the matter.

North Gorse Marine Limited owns Elmford Foundry.

Elmford Foundry is located in Jarrow Moor.

North Gorse Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-301.Oren Briar signed the Supply Agreement LEG-301.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Osborne, chair of North Gorse Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Gorse Systems Limited on 6 February 2025.

Yara Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Gorse Systems Limited by North Gorse Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Gorse Systems Limited by North Gorse Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
