Case notes for Westerby Energy Limited against Ashcombe Partners Limited

Date: 7 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01635.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Westerby Energy Limited against Ashcombe Partners Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Westerby Energy Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-091.

Farah Kerr signed the Debenture LEG-091.Farah Mercer is the chair of Westerby Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Private Bank Limited lent Westerby Energy Limited GBP 44 million on 15 May 2026.

Westerby Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Westerby Energy Limited.

Westerby Energy Limited owes Elm Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 20 May 2026.

Farah Thorne is a shareholder of Westerby Energy Limited.

Farah Jarvis is a family member of Farah Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Westerby Energy Limited owns Westerby Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Westerby Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ashcombe Partners Limited on 7 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Westerby Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
