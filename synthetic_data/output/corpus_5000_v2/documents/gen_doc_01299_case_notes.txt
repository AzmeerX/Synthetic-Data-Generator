Case notes for North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited against North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 25 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01299.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited against North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited in Glenport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-342.

Oren Tremayne signed the Loan Note LEG-342.Oren Villiers is the chair of North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Commercial Bank Limited lent North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 16 August 2026.

North Kingsmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited.

North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited owes Glen Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 21 August 2026.

Petra Briar is a shareholder of North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited.

Oren Sinclair is a family member of Oren Tremayne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Kingsmoor Holdings Limited owns Glenport Cold Store.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
