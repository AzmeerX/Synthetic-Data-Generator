Board meeting minutes of North Mossgrove Energy Limited

Date: 10 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01015.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ulric LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Juno Hastings, Juno Jeffreys and Briar & Ulric LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Mossgrove Energy Limited owns Harrowgate Rail Depot.Juno Hastings is the chair of North Mossgrove Energy Limited.

Juno Montague is a shareholder of North Mossgrove Energy Limited.

Juno Everard is a family member of Juno Fairfax.

Harrow International Bank Limited lent North Mossgrove Energy Limited GBP 18 million on 27 July 2023.

North Mossgrove Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Mossgrove Energy Limited.

North Mossgrove Energy Limited owes Harrow International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 1 August 2023.

Briar & Everard LLP represents North Mossgrove Energy Limited in the matter.

North Mossgrove Energy Limited owns Harrowgate Foundry.

Harrowgate Foundry is located in Elmsworth Vale.

North Mossgrove Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-086.Juno Fairfax signed the Loan Note LEG-086.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Tamsin Zouch, chair of North Mossgrove Energy Limited, proposed to acquire North Norwood Industries Limited on 10 February 2027.

Tamsin Zouch confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Norwood Industries Limited by North Mossgrove Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Norwood Industries Limited by North Mossgrove Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
