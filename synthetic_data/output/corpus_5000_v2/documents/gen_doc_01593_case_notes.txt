Case notes for Tarrow Capital Limited against Tarrow Energy Limited

Date: 17 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01593.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Tarrow Capital Limited against Tarrow Energy Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Tarrow Capital Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-053.

Elias Ward signed the Security Agreement LEG-053.Elias Zell is the chair of Tarrow Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Bank Limited lent Tarrow Capital Limited GBP 48 million on 23 March 2025.

Tarrow Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Capital Limited.

Tarrow Capital Limited owes Harrow Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 28 March 2025.

Elias Everard is a shareholder of Tarrow Capital Limited.

Elias Vale is a family member of Elias Ward.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Tarrow Capital Limited owns Tarrow Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Tarrow Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tarrow Energy Limited on 17 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Quade noted that a former employee alleged that Tarrow Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
