Board meeting minutes of North Ravensby Industries Limited

Date: 1 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01393.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ivers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Ingram, Quin Kingsley and Briar & Ivers LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Ravensby Industries Limited owns North Ravensby Group Limited.Quin Ingram is the chair of North Ravensby Industries Limited.

Quin Neville is a shareholder of North Ravensby Industries Limited.

Quin Fairfax is a family member of Quin Grenville.

Northbank Bank Limited lent North Ravensby Industries Limited GBP 20 million on 12 July 2024.

North Ravensby Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ravensby Industries Limited.

North Ravensby Industries Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 17 July 2024.

Alden & Everard LLP represents North Ravensby Industries Limited in the matter.

North Ravensby Industries Limited owns Kelbrook Yard.

Kelbrook Yard is located in Carrow Bridge.

North Ravensby Industries Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-427.Quin Grenville signed the Debenture LEG-427.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Petra Hale, chair of North Ravensby Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Quintrell Energy Limited on 1 July 2024.

Petra Hale confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Quintrell Energy Limited by North Ravensby Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Quintrell Energy Limited by North Ravensby Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
