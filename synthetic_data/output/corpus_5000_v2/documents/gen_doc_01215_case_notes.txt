Case notes for North Denholm Property Limited against North Elmstead Holdings Limited

Date: 16 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01215.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Denholm Property Limited against North Elmstead Holdings Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Denholm Property Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-267.

Nadia Quade signed the Debenture LEG-267.Nadia Sable is the chair of North Denholm Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Bank Limited lent North Denholm Property Limited GBP 20 million on 13 May 2024.

North Denholm Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Denholm Property Limited.

North Denholm Property Limited owes Quill Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 18 May 2024.

Nadia Yarrow is a shareholder of North Denholm Property Limited.

Nadia Pell is a family member of Nadia Quade.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Denholm Property Limited owns North Denholm Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Denholm Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Elmstead Holdings Limited on 16 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Alden noted that a former employee alleged that North Denholm Property Limited misstated its revenue.
