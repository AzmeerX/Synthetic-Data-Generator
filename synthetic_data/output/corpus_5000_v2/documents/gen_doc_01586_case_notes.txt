Case notes for Stanbrook Systems Limited against Tarrow Group Limited

Date: 29 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01586.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Stanbrook Systems Limited against Tarrow Group Limited in Bexhaven Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Stanbrook Systems Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-046.

Elias Pell signed the Licence LEG-046.Elias Rook is the chair of Stanbrook Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair International Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Systems Limited GBP 34 million on 5 January 2025.

Tarrow Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Systems Limited.

Stanbrook Systems Limited owes Fair International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 10 January 2025.

Elias Ward is a shareholder of Stanbrook Systems Limited.

Elias Nyle is a family member of Elias Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Stanbrook Systems Limited owns Dunhollow Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Stanbrook Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tarrow Group Limited on 29 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that Stanbrook Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
