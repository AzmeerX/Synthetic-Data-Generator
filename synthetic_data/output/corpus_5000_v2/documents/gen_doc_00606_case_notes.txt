Case notes for North Glenmoor Holdings Limited against North Glenmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 4 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00606.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Glenmoor Holdings Limited against North Glenmoor Logistics Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Glenmoor Holdings Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-324.

Calder Fenwick signed the Board Resolution LEG-324.Calder Hale is the chair of North Glenmoor Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Commercial Bank Limited lent North Glenmoor Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 13 June 2026.

North Glenmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Glenmoor Holdings Limited.

North Glenmoor Holdings Limited owes Merri Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 18 June 2026.

Calder Mercer is a shareholder of North Glenmoor Holdings Limited.

Calder Ellery is a family member of Calder Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Glenmoor Holdings Limited owns Elmford Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Glenmoor Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Glenmoor Logistics Limited on 4 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Everard noted that a former employee alleged that North Glenmoor Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
