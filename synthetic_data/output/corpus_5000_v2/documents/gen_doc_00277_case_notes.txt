Case notes for Inglemere Logistics Limited against Fermanagh Capital Limited

Date: 9 February 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00277.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Inglemere Logistics Limited against Fermanagh Capital Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Inglemere Logistics Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.

Bastian Kingsley signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.Bastian Montague is the chair of Inglemere Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited lent Inglemere Logistics Limited GBP 18 million on 24 October 2025.

Inglemere Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Logistics Limited.

Inglemere Logistics Limited owes Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 29 October 2025.

Bastian Sinclair is a shareholder of Inglemere Logistics Limited.

Bastian Jeffreys is a family member of Bastian Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Inglemere Logistics Limited owns Marrow Bay Marina.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Inglemere Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Fermanagh Capital Limited on 9 February 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Inglemere Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
