Case notes for Redmoor Capital Limited against North Cavendish Holdings Limited

Date: 21 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01509.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Redmoor Capital Limited against North Cavendish Holdings Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Redmoor Capital Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-029.

Daria Wyndham signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-029.Daria Zouch is the chair of Redmoor Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent Redmoor Capital Limited GBP 40 million on 2 July 2024.

Redmoor Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Capital Limited.

Redmoor Capital Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 7 July 2024.

Elias Ellery is a shareholder of Redmoor Capital Limited.

Daria Whitlock is a family member of Daria Wyndham.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Redmoor Capital Limited owns Redmoor Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Redmoor Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Cavendish Holdings Limited on 21 September 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that Redmoor Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
