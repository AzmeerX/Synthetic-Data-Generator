Case notes for North Kelbrook Property Limited against North Lowmoor Holdings Limited

Date: 19 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00669.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Kelbrook Property Limited against North Lowmoor Holdings Limited in Glenport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Kelbrook Property Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-381.

Daria Nyle signed the Supply Agreement LEG-381.Daria Pell is the chair of North Kelbrook Property Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Daria Ulric is a shareholder of North Kelbrook Property Limited.

Daria Mercer is a family member of Daria Nyle.4. Findings and allegations
