Board meeting minutes of Jervis Capital Limited

Date: 9 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00014.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Zouch LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Kingsley, Tamsin Montague and Briar & Zouch LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Jervis Capital Limited owns Jervis Marine Limited.Tamsin Kingsley is the chair of Jervis Capital Limited.

Tamsin Pemberton is a shareholder of Jervis Capital Limited.

Tamsin Hastings is a family member of Tamsin Ingram.

Low Commercial Bank Limited lent Jervis Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 30 April 2024.

Jervis Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Jervis Capital Limited.

Jervis Capital Limited owes Low Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 5 May 2024.

Briar & Montague LLP represents Jervis Capital Limited in the matter.

Jervis Capital Limited owns Invermere Rail Depot.

Invermere Rail Depot is located in Elmsworth Green.

Jervis Capital Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-396.Tamsin Ingram signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-396.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Nyle, chair of Jervis Capital Limited, proposed to acquire Jervis Systems Limited on 9 May 2024.

Bram Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Jervis Systems Limited by Jervis Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Jervis Systems Limited by Jervis Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
