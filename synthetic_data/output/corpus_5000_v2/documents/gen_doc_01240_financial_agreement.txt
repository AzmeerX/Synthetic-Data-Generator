Facility agreement between Bexley International Bank Limited and North Farnley Marine Limited

Date: 7 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01240.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Bexley International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Farnley Marine Limited.

North Farnley Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Farnley Marine Limited.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
