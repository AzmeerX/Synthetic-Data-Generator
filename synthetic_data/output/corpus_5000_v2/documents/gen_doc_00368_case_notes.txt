Case notes for Moray Industries Limited against Moray Trading Limited

Date: 26 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00368.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Moray Industries Limited against Moray Trading Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Moray Industries Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-109.

Anika Fenwick signed the Supply Agreement LEG-109.Anika Hale is the chair of Moray Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Moray Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 17 August 2024.

Moray Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Moray Industries Limited.

Moray Industries Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 22 August 2024.

Anika Mercer is a shareholder of Moray Industries Limited.

Anika Ellery is a family member of Anika Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Moray Industries Limited owns Moray Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Moray Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Moray Trading Limited on 26 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Montague noted that a former employee alleged that Moray Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
