Board meeting minutes of Stanbrook Industries Limited

Date: 13 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01575.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Alden LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Garrow, Elias Ivers and Cairn & Alden LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Stanbrook Industries Limited owns Stanbrook Group Limited.Elias Garrow is the chair of Stanbrook Industries Limited.

Elias Lorne is a shareholder of Stanbrook Industries Limited.

Elias Dunmore is a family member of Elias Ellery.

Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Industries Limited GBP 14 million on 17 September 2024.

Stanbrook Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Industries Limited.

Stanbrook Industries Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 22 September 2024.

Alden & Langford LLP represents Stanbrook Industries Limited in the matter.

Stanbrook Industries Limited owns Dunhollow Silo.

Dunhollow Silo is located in Mossgrove.

Stanbrook Industries Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-036.Elias Ellery signed the Board Resolution LEG-036.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Wyndham, chair of Stanbrook Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Stanbrook Capital Limited on 13 September 2024.

Calder Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Stanbrook Capital Limited by Stanbrook Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Stanbrook Capital Limited by Stanbrook Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
