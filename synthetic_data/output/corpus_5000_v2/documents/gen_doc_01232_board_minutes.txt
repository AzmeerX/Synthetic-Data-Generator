Board meeting minutes of Pemberton Partners Limited

Date: 12 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01232.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Balfour LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Zell, Daria Balfour and Alden & Balfour LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Pemberton Partners Limited owns Pemberton Capital Limited.Daria Zell is the chair of Pemberton Partners Limited.

Daria Everard is a shareholder of Pemberton Partners Limited.

Daria Vale is a family member of Daria Ward.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Pemberton Partners Limited GBP 30 million on 1 October 2023.

Pemberton Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Pemberton Partners Limited.

Pemberton Partners Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 6 October 2023.

Alden & Ellery LLP represents Pemberton Partners Limited in the matter.

Pemberton Partners Limited owns Calderport Wharf.

Calderport Wharf is located in Elmford.

Pemberton Partners Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-004.Daria Ward signed the Facility Agreement LEG-004.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Sable, chair of Pemberton Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Farnley Trading Limited on 12 September 2024.

Yara Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Farnley Trading Limited by Pemberton Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Farnley Trading Limited by Pemberton Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
