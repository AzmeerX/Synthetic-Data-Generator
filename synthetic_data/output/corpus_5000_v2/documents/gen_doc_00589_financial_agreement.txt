Facility agreement between Denmoor Trust Bank Limited and North Elmsworth Energy Limited

Date: 8 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00589.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Denmoor Trust Bank Limited and the borrower is North Elmsworth Energy Limited.

North Elmsworth Energy Limited owns Denmoor Office Park.Jarrow International Bank Limited lent North Elmsworth Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 19 December 2025.

North Elmsworth Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmsworth Energy Limited.

North Elmsworth Energy Limited owes Jarrow International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 24 December 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Elmsworth Energy Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-308.

Bastian Osborne signed the Board Resolution LEG-308.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Elmsworth Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Elmsworth Systems Limited on 8 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
