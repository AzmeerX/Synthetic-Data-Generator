Case notes for Ulverston Trading Limited against Ulverston Marine Limited

Date: 11 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00158.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ulverston Trading Limited against Ulverston Marine Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ulverston Trading Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-526.

Wren Nyle signed the Licence LEG-526.Wren Pell is the chair of Ulverston Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low Merchant Bank Limited lent Ulverston Trading Limited GBP 34 million on 4 August 2023.

Ulverston Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Ulverston Trading Limited.

Ulverston Trading Limited owes Low Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 9 August 2023.

Wren Ulric is a shareholder of Ulverston Trading Limited.

Wren Mercer is a family member of Wren Nyle.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ulverston Trading Limited owns Oakhurst Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ulverston Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ulverston Marine Limited on 11 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Ulverston Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
