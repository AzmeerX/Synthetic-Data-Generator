Case notes for North Cresswell Holdings Limited against North Cresswell Logistics Limited

Date: 15 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00872.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Cresswell Holdings Limited against North Cresswell Logistics Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Cresswell Holdings Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-564.

Hana Alden signed the Board Resolution LEG-564.Hana Cairn is the chair of North Cresswell Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Commercial Bank Limited lent North Cresswell Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 14 May 2024.

North Cresswell Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cresswell Holdings Limited.

North Cresswell Holdings Limited owes Inver Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 19 May 2024.

Hana Hale is a shareholder of North Cresswell Holdings Limited.

Gideon Zouch is a family member of Hana Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Cresswell Holdings Limited owns Quintrell Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Cresswell Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Cresswell Logistics Limited on 15 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Dunmore noted that a former employee alleged that North Cresswell Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
