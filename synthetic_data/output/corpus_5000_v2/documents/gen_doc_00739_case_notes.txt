Case notes for North Quintrell Holdings Limited against North Quintrell Logistics Limited

Date: 8 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00739.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quintrell Holdings Limited against North Quintrell Logistics Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quintrell Holdings Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-444.

Elias Cavendish signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-444.Elias Everard is the chair of North Quintrell Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kel Commercial Bank Limited lent North Quintrell Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 29 May 2025.

North Quintrell Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quintrell Holdings Limited.

North Quintrell Holdings Limited owes Kel Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 3 June 2025.

Elias Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Quintrell Holdings Limited.

Elias Balfour is a family member of Elias Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quintrell Holdings Limited owns Kelbrook Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quintrell Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quintrell Logistics Limited on 8 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Rook noted that a former employee alleged that North Quintrell Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
