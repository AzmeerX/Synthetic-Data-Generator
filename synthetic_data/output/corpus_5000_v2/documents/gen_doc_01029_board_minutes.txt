Board meeting minutes of North Norwood Property Limited

Date: 4 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01029.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Jeffreys LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Juno Villiers, Juno Wyndham and Briar & Jeffreys LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Norwood Property Limited owns North Norwood Ventures Limited.Juno Villiers is the chair of North Norwood Property Limited.

Kellan Alden is a shareholder of North Norwood Property Limited.

Juno Sinclair is a family member of Juno Tremayne.

Kel Bank Limited lent North Norwood Property Limited GBP 44 million on 17 December 2023.

North Norwood Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norwood Property Limited.

North Norwood Property Limited owes Kel Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 22 December 2023.

Briar & Sinclair LLP represents North Norwood Property Limited in the matter.

North Norwood Property Limited owns Harrowgate Office Park.

Harrowgate Office Park is located in Glenmoor Bridge.

North Norwood Property Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-099.Juno Tremayne signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-099.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Nyle, chair of North Norwood Property Limited, proposed to acquire North Overdale Industries Limited on 4 February 2024.

Ulric Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Overdale Industries Limited by North Norwood Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Overdale Industries Limited by North Norwood Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
