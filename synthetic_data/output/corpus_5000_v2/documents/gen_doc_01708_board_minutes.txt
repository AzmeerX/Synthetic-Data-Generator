Board meeting minutes of Eastmoor Group Limited

Date: 2 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01708.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Ellery LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Grenville, Gideon Ingram and Cairn & Ellery LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Eastmoor Group Limited owns Eastmoor Logistics Limited.Gideon Grenville is the chair of Eastmoor Group Limited.

Gideon Langford is a shareholder of Eastmoor Group Limited.

Gideon Delacourt is a family member of Gideon Everard.

Harrow Bank Limited lent Eastmoor Group Limited GBP 16 million on 14 September 2023.

Eastmoor Group Limited owes Harrow Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 19 September 2023.

Alden & Hale LLP represents Eastmoor Group Limited in the matter.

Eastmoor Group Limited owns Juniper Cross Farm.

Juniper Cross Farm is located in Kingsmoor.

Eastmoor Group Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-157.Gideon Everard signed the Supply Agreement LEG-157.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Farah Jeffreys, chair of Eastmoor Group Limited, proposed to acquire Eastmoor Marine Limited on 2 April 2027.

Farah Jeffreys confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Eastmoor Marine Limited by Eastmoor Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Eastmoor Marine Limited by Eastmoor Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
