Board meeting minutes of Moray Ventures Limited

Date: 4 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00378.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ingram LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Quade, Anika Sable and Briar & Ingram LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Moray Ventures Limited owns Moray Systems Limited.Anika Quade is the chair of Moray Ventures Limited.

Anika Vale is a shareholder of Moray Ventures Limited.

Anika Nyle is a family member of Anika Orrick.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Moray Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 24 November 2024.

Nairn Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Moray Ventures Limited.

Moray Ventures Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 29 November 2024.

Alden & Zell LLP represents Moray Ventures Limited in the matter.

Moray Ventures Limited owns Ironside Quay.

Ironside Quay is located in Ashcombe Bridge.

Moray Ventures Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-118.Anika Orrick signed the Loan Note LEG-118.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Wyndham, chair of Moray Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Nairn Logistics Limited on 4 October 2024.

Gideon Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Nairn Logistics Limited by Moray Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Nairn Logistics Limited by Moray Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
