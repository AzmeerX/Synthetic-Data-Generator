Board meeting minutes of Oakhurst Holdings Limited

Date: 12 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01043.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Pell & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Hale, Daria Jarvis and Pell & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Oakhurst Holdings Limited owns Oakhurst Industries Limited.Daria Hale is the chair of Oakhurst Holdings Limited.

Daria Mercer is a shareholder of Oakhurst Holdings Limited.

Daria Dunmore is a family member of Daria Ellery.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhurst Holdings Limited GBP 36 million on 28 March 2023.

Oakhurst Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Oakhurst Holdings Limited.

Oakhurst Holdings Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 2 April 2023.

Alden & Hale LLP represents Oakhurst Holdings Limited in the matter.

Oakhurst Holdings Limited owns Fenwick Bay Warehouse.

Fenwick Bay Warehouse is located in Harrow Bay.

Oakhurst Holdings Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-067.Daria Ellery signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-067.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Cavendish, chair of Oakhurst Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Penhurst Group Limited on 12 May 2024.

Ulric Cavendish confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Penhurst Group Limited by Oakhurst Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Penhurst Group Limited by Oakhurst Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
