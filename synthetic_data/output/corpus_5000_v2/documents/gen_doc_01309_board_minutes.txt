Board meeting minutes of Quintrell Holdings Limited

Date: 5 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01309.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ellery LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Grenville, Daria Ingram and Alden & Ellery LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
