Board meeting minutes of Neston Logistics Limited

Date: 17 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00063.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Cavendish LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Fairfax, Ulric Hastings and Alden & Cavendish LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Neston Logistics Limited owns Neston Trading Limited.Ulric Fairfax is the chair of Neston Logistics Limited.

Ulric Kingsley is a shareholder of Neston Logistics Limited.

Ulric Cavendish is a family member of Ulric Delacourt.

Tarrow Bank Limited lent Neston Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 7 September 2025.

Neston Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Neston Logistics Limited.

Neston Logistics Limited owes Tarrow Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 12 September 2025.

Alden & Briar LLP represents Neston Logistics Limited in the matter.

Neston Logistics Limited owns Kelbrook Foundry.

Kelbrook Foundry is located in Kelbrook Moor.

Neston Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-441.Ulric Delacourt signed the Trust Deed LEG-441.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Cavendish, chair of Neston Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Neston Marine Limited on 17 April 2025.

Amina Cavendish confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Neston Marine Limited by Neston Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Neston Marine Limited by Neston Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
