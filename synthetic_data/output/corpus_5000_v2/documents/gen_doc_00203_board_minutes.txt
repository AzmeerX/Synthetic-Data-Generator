Board meeting minutes of Yelverton Property Limited

Date: 9 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00203.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Montague LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Hale, Xander Jarvis and Alden & Montague LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Yelverton Property Limited owns Yelverton Ventures Limited.Xander Hale is the chair of Yelverton Property Limited.

Xander Mercer is a shareholder of Yelverton Property Limited.

Xander Dunmore is a family member of Xander Ellery.

Stan Bank Limited lent Yelverton Property Limited GBP 36 million on 28 October 2024.

Yelverton Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Yelverton Property Limited.

Yelverton Property Limited owes Stan Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 2 November 2024.

Cairn & Sable LLP represents Yelverton Property Limited in the matter.

Yelverton Property Limited owns Quintrell Yard.

Quintrell Yard is located in Dunmere Bridge.

Yelverton Property Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-567.Xander Ellery signed the Services Agreement LEG-567.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Sable, chair of Yelverton Property Limited, proposed to acquire Alderney Group Limited on 9 September 2024.

Daria Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Alderney Group Limited by Yelverton Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Alderney Group Limited by Yelverton Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
