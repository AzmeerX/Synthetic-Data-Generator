Case notes for Netherby Industries Limited against North Fallow Ventures Limited

Date: 23 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00921.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Netherby Industries Limited against North Fallow Ventures Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Netherby Industries Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-056.

Calder Tremayne signed the Lease LEG-056.Calder Villiers is the chair of Netherby Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Netherby Industries Limited GBP 14 million on 27 November 2022.

Netherby Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Netherby Industries Limited.

Netherby Industries Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 2 December 2022.

Daria Alden is a shareholder of Netherby Industries Limited.

Calder Sinclair is a family member of Calder Tremayne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Netherby Industries Limited owns Netherby Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Netherby Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Fallow Ventures Limited on 23 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Silas Ellery noted that a former employee alleged that Netherby Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
