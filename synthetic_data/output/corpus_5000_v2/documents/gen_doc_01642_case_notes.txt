Case notes for Yarmouth Group Limited against Yarmouth Partners Limited

Date: 26 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01642.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yarmouth Group Limited against Yarmouth Partners Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yarmouth Group Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-097.

Farah Rook signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-097.Farah Ulric is the chair of Yarmouth Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Private Bank Limited lent Yarmouth Group Limited GBP 16 million on 20 July 2026.

Yarmouth Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Yarmouth Group Limited.

Yarmouth Group Limited owes Fair Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 25 July 2026.

Farah Ashby is a shareholder of Yarmouth Group Limited.

Farah Quade is a family member of Farah Rook.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yarmouth Group Limited owns Yarmouth Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yarmouth Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yarmouth Partners Limited on 26 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Quade noted that a former employee alleged that Yarmouth Group Limited misstated its revenue.
