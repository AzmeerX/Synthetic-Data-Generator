Case notes for Kintyre Industries Limited against Greyhaven Industries Limited

Date: 26 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00340.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kintyre Industries Limited against Greyhaven Industries Limited in Denmoor Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kintyre Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-085.

Zane Fairfax signed the Security Agreement LEG-085.Zane Hastings is the chair of Kintyre Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Private Bank Limited lent Kintyre Industries Limited GBP 44 million on 27 November 2023.

Kintyre Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Kintyre Industries Limited.

Kintyre Industries Limited owes Quill Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 2 December 2023.

Zane Montague is a shareholder of Kintyre Industries Limited.

Zane Everard is a family member of Zane Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kintyre Industries Limited owns Kintyre Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kintyre Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Greyhaven Industries Limited on 26 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Kintyre Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
