Facility agreement between Denmoor Private Bank Limited and North Aldergate Energy Limited

Date: 1 May 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00855.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Denmoor Private Bank Limited and the borrower is North Aldergate Energy Limited.

North Aldergate Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Aldergate Energy Limited.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
