Case notes for Ashcombe Capital Limited against Yarmouth Systems Limited

Date: 13 February 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01649.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ashcombe Capital Limited against Yarmouth Systems Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ashcombe Capital Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-031.

Xander Garrow signed the Power of Attorney LEG-031.Lena Orrick is the chair of Ashcombe Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Capital Limited GBP 32 million on 5 January 2023.

Ashcombe Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Capital Limited.

Ashcombe Capital Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 10 January 2023.

Bram Kerr is a shareholder of Ashcombe Capital Limited.

Elias Orrick is a family member of Xander Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ashcombe Capital Limited owns Ashcombe Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ashcombe Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yarmouth Systems Limited on 13 February 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Ashcombe Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
