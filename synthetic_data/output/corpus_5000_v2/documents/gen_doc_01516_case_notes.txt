Case notes for North Cavendish Trading Limited against North Cavendish Marine Limited

Date: 9 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01516.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Cavendish Trading Limited against North Cavendish Marine Limited in Elmford Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Cavendish Trading Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-538.

Silas Underwood signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-538.Silas Whitlock is the chair of North Cavendish Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Merchant Bank Limited lent North Cavendish Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 21 March 2023.

North Cavendish Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cavendish Trading Limited.

North Cavendish Trading Limited owes Red Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 26 March 2023.

Tamsin Briar is a shareholder of North Cavendish Trading Limited.

Silas Tremayne is a family member of Silas Underwood.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Cavendish Trading Limited owns Pemberton Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Cavendish Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Cavendish Marine Limited on 9 November 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Neville noted that a former employee alleged that North Cavendish Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
