Case notes for Dartmoor Group Limited against Dartmoor Partners Limited

Date: 28 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00249.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Dartmoor Group Limited against Dartmoor Partners Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Dartmoor Group Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-002.

Xander Whitlock signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-002.Xander Yardley is the chair of Dartmoor Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Dartmoor Group Limited GBP 38 million on 22 January 2026.

Dartmoor Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Dartmoor Group Limited.

Dartmoor Group Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 27 January 2026.

Yara Dunmore is a shareholder of Dartmoor Group Limited.

Xander Villiers is a family member of Xander Whitlock.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Dartmoor Group Limited owns Dartmoor Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Dartmoor Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Dartmoor Partners Limited on 28 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Dartmoor Group Limited misstated its revenue.
