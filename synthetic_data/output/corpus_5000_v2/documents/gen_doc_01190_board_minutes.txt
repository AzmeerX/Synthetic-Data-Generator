Board meeting minutes of North Bexley Ventures Limited

Date: 7 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01190.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Marek Underwood, Marek Whitlock and Alden & Fenwick LLP.

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
