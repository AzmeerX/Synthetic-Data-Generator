Board meeting minutes of North Saltmarsh Capital Limited

Date: 20 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01092.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Quade LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Lena Dunmore, Lena Fenwick and Alden & Quade LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Saltmarsh Capital Limited owns North Saltmarsh Marine Limited.Lena Dunmore is the chair of North Saltmarsh Capital Limited.

Lena Ivers is a shareholder of North Saltmarsh Capital Limited.

Lena Alden is a family member of Lena Briar.

Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Saltmarsh Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 4 September 2025.

North Saltmarsh Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Saltmarsh Capital Limited.

North Saltmarsh Capital Limited owes Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 9 September 2025.

Alden & Sable LLP represents North Saltmarsh Capital Limited in the matter.

North Saltmarsh Capital Limited owns Kestrelford Farm.

Kestrelford Farm is located in Calderport.

North Saltmarsh Capital Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-156.Lena Briar signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-156.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Balfour, chair of North Saltmarsh Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Saltmarsh Systems Limited on 20 April 2025.

Vera Balfour confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Saltmarsh Systems Limited by North Saltmarsh Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Saltmarsh Systems Limited by North Saltmarsh Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
