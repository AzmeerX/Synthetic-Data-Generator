Case notes for North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited against North Trentvale Holdings Limited

Date: 18 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01096.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited against North Trentvale Holdings Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-160.

Lena Fenwick signed the Side Letter LEG-160.Lena Hale is the chair of North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Uldale Merchant Bank Limited lent North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 18 October 2025.

North Trentvale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited.

North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited owes Uldale Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 23 October 2025.

Lena Mercer is a shareholder of North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited.

Lena Ellery is a family member of Lena Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited owns North Saltmarsh Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Trentvale Holdings Limited on 18 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Fairfax noted that a former employee alleged that North Saltmarsh Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
