Board meeting minutes of North Vicarage Industries Limited

Date: 21 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01127.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Cairn LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Lena Kingsley, Lena Montague and Briar & Cairn LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Vicarage Industries Limited owns North Vicarage Group Limited.Lena Kingsley is the chair of North Vicarage Industries Limited.

Lena Pemberton is a shareholder of North Vicarage Industries Limited.

Lena Hastings is a family member of Lena Ingram.

Carrow Private Bank Limited lent North Vicarage Industries Limited GBP 20 million on 11 August 2026.

North Vicarage Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Vicarage Industries Limited.

North Vicarage Industries Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 16 August 2026.

Alden & Zouch LLP represents North Vicarage Industries Limited in the matter.

North Vicarage Industries Limited owns Marrow Bay Works.

Marrow Bay Works is located in Tarrow.

North Vicarage Industries Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-187.Lena Ingram signed the Debenture LEG-187.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Wren Lorne, chair of North Vicarage Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Oakhurst Energy Limited on 21 December 2025.

Wren Lorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Oakhurst Energy Limited by North Vicarage Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Oakhurst Energy Limited by North Vicarage Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
