Board meeting minutes of North Barrowfield Energy Limited

Date: 17 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00868.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Dunmore LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Yardley, Hana Alden and Briar & Dunmore LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Barrowfield Energy Limited owns Quintrell Works.Gideon Yardley is the chair of North Barrowfield Energy Limited.

Hana Dunmore is a shareholder of North Barrowfield Energy Limited.

Gideon Villiers is a family member of Gideon Whitlock.

Harrow International Bank Limited lent North Barrowfield Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 31 March 2024.

North Barrowfield Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Barrowfield Energy Limited.

North Barrowfield Energy Limited owes Harrow International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 5 April 2024.

Briar & Cavendish LLP represents North Barrowfield Energy Limited in the matter.

North Barrowfield Energy Limited owns Quintrell Dock.

Quintrell Dock is located in Lowmoor Bay.

North Barrowfield Energy Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-560.Gideon Whitlock signed the Side Letter LEG-560.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Quin Zouch, chair of North Barrowfield Energy Limited, proposed to acquire North Cresswell Industries Limited on 17 April 2024.

Quin Zouch confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Cresswell Industries Limited by North Barrowfield Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Cresswell Industries Limited by North Barrowfield Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
