Board meeting minutes of North Quintrell Logistics Limited

Date: 1 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00742.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Hale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Hastings, Elias Jeffreys and Briar & Hale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quintrell Logistics Limited owns North Quintrell Trading Limited.Elias Hastings is the chair of North Quintrell Logistics Limited.

Elias Montague is a shareholder of North Quintrell Logistics Limited.

Elias Everard is a family member of Elias Fairfax.

Low Bank Limited lent North Quintrell Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 1 July 2025.

North Quintrell Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quintrell Logistics Limited.

North Quintrell Logistics Limited owes Low Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 6 July 2025.

Briar & Underwood LLP represents North Quintrell Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Quintrell Logistics Limited owns Lowmoor Terminal.

Lowmoor Terminal is located in Jesmond.

North Quintrell Logistics Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-447.Elias Fairfax signed the Power of Attorney LEG-447.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Ulric, chair of North Quintrell Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Quintrell Marine Limited on 1 March 2025.

Oren Ulric confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quintrell Marine Limited by North Quintrell Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quintrell Marine Limited by North Quintrell Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
