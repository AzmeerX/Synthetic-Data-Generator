Case notes for North Upton Marine Limited against Redmoor Holdings Limited

Date: 19 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01439.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Upton Marine Limited against Redmoor Holdings Limited in Glenport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Upton Marine Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-469.

Rhea Ashby signed the Security Agreement LEG-469.Rhea Cavendish is the chair of North Upton Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Private Bank Limited lent North Upton Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 17 October 2025.

North Upton Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Upton Marine Limited.

North Upton Marine Limited owes Fair Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 22 October 2025.

Rhea Hastings is a shareholder of North Upton Marine Limited.

Rhea Zell is a family member of Rhea Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Upton Marine Limited owns North Upton Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Upton Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Redmoor Holdings Limited on 19 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Lorne noted that a former employee alleged that North Upton Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
