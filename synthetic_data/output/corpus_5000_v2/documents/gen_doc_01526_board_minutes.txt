Board meeting minutes of North Dunkeld Industries Limited

Date: 18 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01526.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Lorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Fenwick, Tamsin Hale and Briar & Lorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Dunkeld Industries Limited owns North Dunkeld Group Limited.Tamsin Fenwick is the chair of North Dunkeld Industries Limited.

Tamsin Kerr is a shareholder of North Dunkeld Industries Limited.

Tamsin Cairn is a family member of Tamsin Dunmore.

Stan Private Bank Limited lent North Dunkeld Industries Limited GBP 20 million on 28 June 2023.

North Dunkeld Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunkeld Industries Limited.

North Dunkeld Industries Limited owes Stan Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 3 July 2023.

Alden & Thorne LLP represents North Dunkeld Industries Limited in the matter.

North Dunkeld Industries Limited owns Quintrell Yard.

Quintrell Yard is located in Tarrow.

North Dunkeld Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-547.Tamsin Dunmore signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-547.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bastian Yardley, chair of North Dunkeld Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Redmoor Energy Limited on 18 January 2027.

Bastian Yardley confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Redmoor Energy Limited by North Dunkeld Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Redmoor Energy Limited by North Dunkeld Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
