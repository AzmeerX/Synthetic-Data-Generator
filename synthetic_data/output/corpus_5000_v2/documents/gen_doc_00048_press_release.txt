Press release concerning Marlowe Industries Limited

Publication date: 2 January 2025.

1. Corporate statement

Marlowe Industries Limited today issued the following statement.

Marlowe Industries Limited owns Marlowe Group Limited.

Ulric Vale is a shareholder of Marlowe Industries Limited.
