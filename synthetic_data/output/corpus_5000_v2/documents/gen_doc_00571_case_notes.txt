Case notes for North Dunmere Trading Limited against North Dunmere Marine Limited

Date: 4 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00571.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Dunmere Trading Limited against North Dunmere Marine Limited in Elmford Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Dunmere Trading Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-292.

Bastian Yarrow signed the Board Resolution LEG-292.Bastian Ashby is the chair of North Dunmere Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Merchant Bank Limited lent North Dunmere Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 26 June 2025.

North Dunmere Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunmere Trading Limited.

North Dunmere Trading Limited owes Harrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 1 July 2025.

Bastian Fairfax is a shareholder of North Dunmere Trading Limited.

Bastian Ward is a family member of Bastian Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Dunmere Trading Limited owns Denmoor Cold Store.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Dunmere Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Dunmere Marine Limited on 4 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that North Dunmere Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
