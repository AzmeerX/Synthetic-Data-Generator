Case notes for Wear Energy Limited against Wear Ventures Limited

Date: 2 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00508.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Wear Energy Limited against Wear Ventures Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Wear Energy Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-236.

Amina Rook signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-236.Amina Thorne is the chair of Wear Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow International Bank Limited lent Wear Energy Limited GBP 26 million on 19 October 2023.

Wear Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Wear Energy Limited.

Wear Energy Limited owes Tarrow International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 24 October 2023.

Amina Zell is a shareholder of Wear Energy Limited.

Amina Quade is a family member of Amina Rook.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Wear Energy Limited owns Alderport Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Wear Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Wear Ventures Limited on 2 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Wear Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
