Case notes for Dartmoor Energy Limited against Dartmoor Systems Limited

Date: 15 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00256.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Dartmoor Energy Limited against Dartmoor Systems Limited in Elmford Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Dartmoor Energy Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.

Yara Cairn signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.Yara Ellery is the chair of Dartmoor Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor International Bank Limited lent Dartmoor Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 29 March 2026.

Dartmoor Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Dartmoor Energy Limited.

Dartmoor Energy Limited owes Denmoor International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 April 2026.

Yara Jarvis is a shareholder of Dartmoor Energy Limited.

Yara Briar is a family member of Yara Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Dartmoor Energy Limited owns Calderport Marina.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Dartmoor Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Dartmoor Systems Limited on 15 September 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Dartmoor Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
