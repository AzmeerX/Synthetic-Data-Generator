Case notes for Pemberton Industries Limited against North Bexley Ventures Limited

Date: 14 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01187.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pemberton Industries Limited against North Bexley Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pemberton Industries Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-080.

Daria Sable signed the Side Letter LEG-080.Daria Ulric is the chair of Pemberton Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder International Bank Limited lent Pemberton Industries Limited GBP 22 million on 18 August 2023.

Pemberton Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Pemberton Industries Limited.

Pemberton Industries Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 23 August 2023.

Daria Ashby is a shareholder of Pemberton Industries Limited.

Daria Rook is a family member of Daria Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pemberton Industries Limited owns Pemberton Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pemberton Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Bexley Ventures Limited on 14 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Xander Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Pemberton Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
