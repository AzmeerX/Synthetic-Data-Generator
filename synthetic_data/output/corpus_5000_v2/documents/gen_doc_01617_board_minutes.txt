Board meeting minutes of Vellacott Logistics Limited

Date: 4 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01617.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Vale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Villiers, Elias Wyndham and Alden & Vale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Vellacott Logistics Limited owns Fenwick Bay Hangar.Elias Villiers is the chair of Vellacott Logistics Limited.

Farah Alden is a shareholder of Vellacott Logistics Limited.

Elias Sinclair is a family member of Elias Tremayne.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Vellacott Logistics Limited GBP 50 million on 9 November 2025.

Vellacott Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Logistics Limited.

Vellacott Logistics Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 14 November 2025.

Alden & Zell LLP represents Vellacott Logistics Limited in the matter.

Vellacott Logistics Limited owns Fenwick Bay Yard.

Fenwick Bay Yard is located in Belmont Hill.

Vellacott Logistics Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-074.Elias Tremayne signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-074.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Pemberton, chair of Vellacott Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Vellacott Energy Limited on 4 July 2025.

Daria Pemberton confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Vellacott Energy Limited by Vellacott Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Vellacott Energy Limited by Vellacott Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
