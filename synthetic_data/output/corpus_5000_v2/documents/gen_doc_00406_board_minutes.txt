Board meeting minutes of Pembroke Holdings Limited

Date: 18 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00406.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Briar LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Sinclair, Anika Underwood and Cairn & Briar LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Pembroke Holdings Limited owns Juniper Cross Works.Anika Sinclair is the chair of Pembroke Holdings Limited.

Anika Wyndham is a shareholder of Pembroke Holdings Limited.

Anika Osborne is a family member of Anika Pemberton.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent Pembroke Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 6 September 2025.

Pembroke Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Pembroke Holdings Limited.

Pembroke Holdings Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 11 September 2025.

Briar & Alden LLP represents Pembroke Holdings Limited in the matter.

Pembroke Holdings Limited owns Juniper Cross Dock.

Juniper Cross Dock is located in Dunmere Vale.

Pembroke Holdings Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-144.Anika Pemberton signed the Side Letter LEG-144.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Farah Orrick, chair of Pembroke Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire Pembroke Trading Limited on 18 April 2025.

Farah Orrick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Pembroke Trading Limited by Pembroke Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Pembroke Trading Limited by Pembroke Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
