Board meeting minutes of Nairn Systems Limited

Date: 10 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00392.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Sable LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Everard, Anika Grenville and Cairn & Sable LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Nairn Systems Limited owns Orkney Holdings Limited.Anika Everard is the chair of Nairn Systems Limited.

Anika Jeffreys is a shareholder of Nairn Systems Limited.

Anika Balfour is a family member of Anika Cavendish.

Carrow Trust Bank Limited lent Nairn Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 16 April 2025.

Orkney Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Nairn Systems Limited.

Nairn Systems Limited owes Carrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 21 April 2025.

Alden & Montague LLP represents Nairn Systems Limited in the matter.

Nairn Systems Limited owns Juniper Cross Terminal.

Juniper Cross Terminal is located in Carrow Port.

Nairn Systems Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.Anika Cavendish signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Marek Nyle, chair of Nairn Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Orkney Trading Limited on 10 January 2025.

Marek Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Orkney Trading Limited by Nairn Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Orkney Trading Limited by Nairn Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
