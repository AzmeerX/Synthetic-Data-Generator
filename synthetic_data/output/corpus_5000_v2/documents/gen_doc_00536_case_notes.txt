Case notes for North Ashcombe Property Limited against North Belmont Holdings Limited

Date: 2 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00536.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ashcombe Property Limited against North Belmont Holdings Limited in Glenport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ashcombe Property Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-261.

Amina Sinclair signed the Security Agreement LEG-261.Amina Underwood is the chair of North Ashcombe Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Bank Limited lent North Ashcombe Property Limited GBP 36 million on 20 July 2024.

North Ashcombe Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ashcombe Property Limited.

North Ashcombe Property Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 25 July 2024.

Amina Zouch is a shareholder of North Ashcombe Property Limited.

Amina Ravenscroft is a family member of Amina Sinclair.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ashcombe Property Limited owns North Ashcombe Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ashcombe Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Belmont Holdings Limited on 2 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Hale noted that a former employee alleged that North Ashcombe Property Limited misstated its revenue.
