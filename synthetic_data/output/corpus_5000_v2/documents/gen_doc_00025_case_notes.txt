Case notes for Kendal Trading Limited against Kendal Marine Limited

Date: 25 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00025.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kendal Trading Limited against Kendal Marine Limited in Denmoor Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kendal Trading Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-406.

Tamsin Tremayne signed the Loan Note LEG-406.Tamsin Villiers is the chair of Kendal Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Merchant Bank Limited lent Kendal Trading Limited GBP 34 million on 18 August 2024.

Kendal Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Kendal Trading Limited.

Kendal Trading Limited owes Nether Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 23 August 2024.

Ulric Alden is a shareholder of Kendal Trading Limited.

Tamsin Sinclair is a family member of Tamsin Tremayne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kendal Trading Limited owns Invermere Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kendal Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kendal Marine Limited on 25 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lorne Sable noted that a former employee alleged that Kendal Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
