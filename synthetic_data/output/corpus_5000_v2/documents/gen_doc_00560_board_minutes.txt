Board meeting minutes of North Carrow Marine Limited

Date: 17 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00560.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Ashby LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Quade, Bastian Sable and Cairn & Ashby LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Carrow Marine Limited owns North Carrow Energy Limited.Bastian Quade is the chair of North Carrow Marine Limited.

Bastian Vale is a shareholder of North Carrow Marine Limited.

Bastian Nyle is a family member of Bastian Orrick.

Fair Private Bank Limited lent North Carrow Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 19 March 2025.

North Carrow Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Carrow Marine Limited.

North Carrow Marine Limited owes Fair Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 24 March 2025.

Briar & Kerr LLP represents North Carrow Marine Limited in the matter.

North Carrow Marine Limited owns Carrow Bay Farm.

Carrow Bay Farm is located in Bexley.

North Carrow Marine Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-283.Bastian Orrick signed the Debenture LEG-283.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Hastings, chair of North Carrow Marine Limited, proposed to acquire Zephyr Logistics Limited on 17 December 2024.

Kellan Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Zephyr Logistics Limited by North Carrow Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Zephyr Logistics Limited by North Carrow Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
