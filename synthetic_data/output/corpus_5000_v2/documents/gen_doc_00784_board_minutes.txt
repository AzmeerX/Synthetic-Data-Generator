Board meeting minutes of North Tarrow Partners Limited

Date: 20 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00784.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Quade & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Ward, Farah Zell and Quade & Thorne LLP.
