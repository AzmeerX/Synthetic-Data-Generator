Board meeting minutes of Culm Trading Limited

Date: 12 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00238.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Yarrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Neville, Xander Pemberton and Briar & Yarrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Culm Trading Limited owns Quarrystone Mill.Xander Neville is the chair of Culm Trading Limited.

Xander Tremayne is a shareholder of Culm Trading Limited.

Xander Kingsley is a family member of Xander Langford.

Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent Culm Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 4 October 2025.

Culm Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Culm Trading Limited.

Culm Trading Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 9 October 2025.

Alden & Delacourt LLP represents Culm Trading Limited in the matter.

Culm Trading Limited owns Valebrook Farm.

Valebrook Farm is located in Harrowden Moor.

Culm Trading Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-598.Xander Langford signed the Loan Note LEG-598.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Dunmore, chair of Culm Trading Limited, proposed to acquire Culm Property Limited on 12 May 2025.

Elias Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Culm Property Limited by Culm Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Culm Property Limited by Culm Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
