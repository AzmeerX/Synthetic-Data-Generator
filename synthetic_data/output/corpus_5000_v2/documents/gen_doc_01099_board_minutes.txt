Board meeting minutes of North Trentvale Holdings Limited

Date: 8 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01099.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Yarrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Lena Jarvis, Lena Lorne and Alden & Yarrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Trentvale Holdings Limited owns Larkspur Wharf.Lena Jarvis is the chair of North Trentvale Holdings Limited.

Lena Pell is a shareholder of North Trentvale Holdings Limited.

Lena Garrow is a family member of Lena Hale.

Uldale Commercial Bank Limited lent North Trentvale Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 9 November 2025.

North Trentvale Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Trentvale Holdings Limited.

North Trentvale Holdings Limited owes Uldale Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 14 November 2025.

Alden & Zell LLP represents North Trentvale Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Trentvale Holdings Limited owns Larkspur Fleet.

Larkspur Fleet is located in Ironside.

North Trentvale Holdings Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-162.Lena Hale signed the Facility Agreement LEG-162.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Ingram, chair of North Trentvale Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Trentvale Partners Limited on 8 June 2025.

Vera Ingram confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Trentvale Partners Limited by North Trentvale Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Trentvale Partners Limited by North Trentvale Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
