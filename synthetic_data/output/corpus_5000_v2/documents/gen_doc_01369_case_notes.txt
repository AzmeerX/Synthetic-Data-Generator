Case notes for North Pinewood Logistics Limited against North Pinewood Capital Limited

Date: 29 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01369.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Pinewood Logistics Limited against North Pinewood Capital Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Pinewood Logistics Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-405.

Quin Ivers signed the Security Agreement LEG-405.Quin Kerr is the chair of North Pinewood Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Bank Limited lent North Pinewood Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 13 November 2023.

North Pinewood Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Pinewood Logistics Limited.

North Pinewood Logistics Limited owes Red Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 18 November 2023.

Quin Quade is a shareholder of North Pinewood Logistics Limited.

Quin Hale is a family member of Quin Ivers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Pinewood Logistics Limited owns North Pinewood Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Pinewood Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Pinewood Capital Limited on 29 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bram Garrow noted that a former employee alleged that North Pinewood Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
