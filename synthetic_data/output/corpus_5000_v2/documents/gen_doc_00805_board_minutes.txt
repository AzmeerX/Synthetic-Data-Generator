Board meeting minutes of North Uldale Systems Limited

Date: 16 May 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00805.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Kerr LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Pemberton, Farah Sinclair and Cairn & Kerr LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Alden & Thorne LLP represents North Uldale Systems Limited in the matter.

Netherby Farm is located in Dunmere Hill.

North Uldale Systems Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-503.Farah Neville signed the Services Agreement LEG-503.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of Merrivale Logistics Limited by North Uldale Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
