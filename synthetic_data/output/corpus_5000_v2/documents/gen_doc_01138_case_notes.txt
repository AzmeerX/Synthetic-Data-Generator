Case notes for North Vicarage Systems Limited against North Whitbourne Group Limited

Date: 8 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01138.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Vicarage Systems Limited against North Whitbourne Group Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Vicarage Systems Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-197.

Lena Tremayne signed the Security Agreement LEG-197.Lena Villiers is the chair of North Vicarage Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Trust Bank Limited lent North Vicarage Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 4 April 2022.

North Whitbourne Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Vicarage Systems Limited.

North Vicarage Systems Limited owes Elm Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 9 April 2022.

Marek Alden is a shareholder of North Vicarage Systems Limited.

Lena Sinclair is a family member of Lena Tremayne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Vicarage Systems Limited owns North Whitbourne Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Vicarage Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Whitbourne Group Limited on 8 March 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Wren Ward noted that a former employee alleged that North Vicarage Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
