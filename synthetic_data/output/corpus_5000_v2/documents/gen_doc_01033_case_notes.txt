Case notes for North Overdale Holdings Limited against North Overdale Logistics Limited

Date: 3 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01033.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Overdale Holdings Limited against North Overdale Logistics Limited in Glenport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Overdale Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-102.

Juno Whitlock signed the Loan Note LEG-102.Juno Yardley is the chair of North Overdale Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kel Commercial Bank Limited lent North Overdale Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 19 January 2024.

North Overdale Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Overdale Holdings Limited.

North Overdale Holdings Limited owes Kel Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 24 January 2024.

Kellan Dunmore is a shareholder of North Overdale Holdings Limited.

Juno Villiers is a family member of Juno Whitlock.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Overdale Holdings Limited owns Harrowgate Farm.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Overdale Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Overdale Logistics Limited on 3 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Rook noted that a former employee alleged that North Overdale Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
