Board meeting minutes of North Oakhurst Industries Limited

Date: 17 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00714.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Delacourt LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Fenwick, Elias Hale and Alden & Delacourt LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Oakhurst Industries Limited owns North Oakhurst Group Limited.Elias Fenwick is the chair of North Oakhurst Industries Limited.

Elias Kerr is a shareholder of North Oakhurst Industries Limited.

Elias Cairn is a family member of Elias Dunmore.

Glen Private Bank Limited lent North Oakhurst Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 18 September 2024.

North Oakhurst Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Oakhurst Industries Limited.

North Oakhurst Industries Limited owes Glen Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 23 September 2024.

Briar & Sable LLP represents North Oakhurst Industries Limited in the matter.

North Oakhurst Industries Limited owns Jarrow Cross Plant.

Jarrow Cross Plant is located in Harrow Bay.

North Oakhurst Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-421.Elias Dunmore signed the Security Agreement LEG-421.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Ravenscroft, chair of North Oakhurst Industries Limited, proposed to acquire North Oakhurst Capital Limited on 17 August 2024.

Nadia Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Oakhurst Capital Limited by North Oakhurst Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Oakhurst Capital Limited by North Oakhurst Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
