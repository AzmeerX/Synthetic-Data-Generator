Board meeting minutes of North Belmont Holdings Limited

Date: 23 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00539.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Wyndham, Amina Zouch and Cairn & Fenwick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Belmont Holdings Limited owns Bexhaven Dock.Amina Wyndham is the chair of North Belmont Holdings Limited.

Bastian Cairn is a shareholder of North Belmont Holdings Limited.

Amina Underwood is a family member of Amina Villiers.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Belmont Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 22 August 2024.

North Belmont Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Belmont Holdings Limited.

North Belmont Holdings Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 27 August 2024.

Alden & Pemberton LLP represents North Belmont Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Belmont Holdings Limited owns Bexhaven Office Park.

Bexhaven Office Park is located in Grimsdale.

North Belmont Holdings Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-264.Amina Villiers signed the Lease LEG-264.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Kerr, chair of North Belmont Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Belmont Trading Limited on 23 July 2024.

Kellan Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Belmont Trading Limited by North Belmont Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Belmont Trading Limited by North Belmont Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
