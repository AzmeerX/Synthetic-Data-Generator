Case notes for North Elmsworth Partners Limited against Kelbrook Marine Limited

Date: 10 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00585.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Elmsworth Partners Limited against Kelbrook Marine Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Elmsworth Partners Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-305.

Bastian Langford signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-305.Bastian Neville is the chair of North Elmsworth Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Jarrow Trust Bank Limited lent North Elmsworth Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 16 November 2025.

North Elmsworth Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmsworth Partners Limited.

North Elmsworth Partners Limited owes Jarrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 21 November 2025.

Bastian Tremayne is a shareholder of North Elmsworth Partners Limited.

Bastian Kingsley is a family member of Bastian Langford.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Elmsworth Partners Limited owns North Elmsworth Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Elmsworth Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kelbrook Marine Limited on 10 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Hale noted that a former employee alleged that North Elmsworth Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
