Board meeting minutes of North Elmsworth Industries Limited

Date: 13 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00581.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ashby LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Jeffreys, Bastian Langford and Alden & Ashby LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Elmsworth Industries Limited owns North Elmsworth Group Limited.Bastian Jeffreys is the chair of North Elmsworth Industries Limited.

Bastian Osborne is a shareholder of North Elmsworth Industries Limited.

Bastian Grenville is a family member of Bastian Hastings.

Inver Private Bank Limited lent North Elmsworth Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 3 October 2025.

North Elmsworth Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmsworth Industries Limited.

North Elmsworth Industries Limited owes Inver Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 8 October 2025.

Briar & Delacourt LLP represents North Elmsworth Industries Limited in the matter.

North Elmsworth Industries Limited owns Denmoor Plant.

Denmoor Plant is located in Ashcombe Vale.

North Elmsworth Industries Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-301.Bastian Hastings signed the Supply Agreement LEG-301.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Lena Dunmore, chair of North Elmsworth Industries Limited, proposed to acquire North Elmsworth Capital Limited on 13 May 2025.

Lena Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Elmsworth Capital Limited by North Elmsworth Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Elmsworth Capital Limited by North Elmsworth Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
