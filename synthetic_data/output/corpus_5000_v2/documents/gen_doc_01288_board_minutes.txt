Board meeting minutes of North Juniper Group Limited

Date: 9 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01288.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ingram LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Kingsley, Oren Montague and Briar & Ingram LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Oren Hastings is a family member of Oren Ingram.

North Juniper Group Limited owes Elm International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 May 2026.

Briar & Pell LLP represents North Juniper Group Limited in the matter.

North Juniper Group Limited owns Fairmouth Estate.

Fairmouth Estate is located in Netherby Port.

North Juniper Group Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-332.Oren Ingram signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-332.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of North Juniper Marine Limited by North Juniper Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
