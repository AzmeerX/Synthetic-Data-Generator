Case notes for North Ashcombe Logistics Limited against Kelbrook Group Limited

Date: 14 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00529.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ashcombe Logistics Limited against Kelbrook Group Limited in Fairmouth High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ashcombe Logistics Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-255.

Amina Langford signed the Power of Attorney LEG-255.Amina Neville is the chair of North Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Bank Limited lent North Ashcombe Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 15 May 2024.

North Ashcombe Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

North Ashcombe Logistics Limited owes Bexley Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 20 May 2024.

Amina Tremayne is a shareholder of North Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

Amina Kingsley is a family member of Amina Langford.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ashcombe Logistics Limited owns North Ashcombe Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ashcombe Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kelbrook Group Limited on 14 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Alden noted that a former employee alleged that North Ashcombe Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
