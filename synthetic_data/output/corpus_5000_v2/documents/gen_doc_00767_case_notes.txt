Case notes for North Stanbrook Industries Limited against North Stanbrook Trading Limited

Date: 23 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00767.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Stanbrook Industries Limited against North Stanbrook Trading Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Stanbrook Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-469.

Farah Cairn signed the Security Agreement LEG-469.Farah Ellery is the chair of North Stanbrook Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Private Bank Limited lent North Stanbrook Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 28 February 2026.

North Stanbrook Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Stanbrook Industries Limited.

North Stanbrook Industries Limited owes Oak Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 5 March 2026.

Farah Jarvis is a shareholder of North Stanbrook Industries Limited.

Farah Briar is a family member of Farah Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Stanbrook Industries Limited owns North Stanbrook Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Stanbrook Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Stanbrook Trading Limited on 23 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that North Stanbrook Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
