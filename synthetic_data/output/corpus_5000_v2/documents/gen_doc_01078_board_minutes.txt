Board meeting minutes of North Rushmere Partners Limited

Date: 12 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01078.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Yarrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Pemberton, Kellan Sinclair and Cairn & Yarrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Rushmere Partners Limited owns North Rushmere Capital Limited.Kellan Pemberton is the chair of North Rushmere Partners Limited.

Kellan Villiers is a shareholder of North Rushmere Partners Limited.

Kellan Montague is a family member of Kellan Neville.

Red Trust Bank Limited lent North Rushmere Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 14 April 2025.

North Rushmere Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Rushmere Partners Limited.

North Rushmere Partners Limited owes Red Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 19 April 2025.

Alden & Fenwick LLP represents North Rushmere Partners Limited in the matter.

North Rushmere Partners Limited owns Kestrelford Fleet.

Kestrelford Fleet is located in Merrivale Cross.

North Rushmere Partners Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-143.Kellan Neville signed the Power of Attorney LEG-143.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Mercer, chair of North Rushmere Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Rushmere Ventures Limited on 12 January 2025.

Vera Mercer confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Rushmere Ventures Limited by North Rushmere Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Rushmere Ventures Limited by North Rushmere Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
