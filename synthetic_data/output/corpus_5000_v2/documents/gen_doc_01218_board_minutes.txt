Board meeting minutes of North Elmstead Holdings Limited

Date: 6 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01218.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Mercer LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Vale, Nadia Yarrow and Alden & Mercer LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Elmstead Holdings Limited owns Carrow Bay Works.Nadia Vale is the chair of North Elmstead Holdings Limited.

Nadia Cavendish is a shareholder of North Elmstead Holdings Limited.

Nadia Sable is a family member of Nadia Thorne.

Quill Commercial Bank Limited lent North Elmstead Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 15 June 2024.

North Elmstead Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmstead Holdings Limited.

North Elmstead Holdings Limited owes Quill Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 20 June 2024.

Alden & Cairn LLP represents North Elmstead Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Elmstead Holdings Limited owns Carrow Bay Dock.

Carrow Bay Dock is located in Fairhaven Moor.

North Elmstead Holdings Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-270.Nadia Thorne signed the Licence LEG-270.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Dunmore, chair of North Elmstead Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Elmstead Trading Limited on 6 June 2024.

Yara Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Elmstead Trading Limited by North Elmstead Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Elmstead Trading Limited by North Elmstead Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
