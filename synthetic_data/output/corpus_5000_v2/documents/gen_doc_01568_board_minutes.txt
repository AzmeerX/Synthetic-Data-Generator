Board meeting minutes of North Grafton Logistics Limited

Date: 26 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01568.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Dunmore LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Underwood, Tamsin Whitlock and Alden & Dunmore LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Grafton Logistics Limited owns North Grafton Trading Limited.Tamsin Underwood is the chair of North Grafton Logistics Limited.

Tamsin Zouch is a shareholder of North Grafton Logistics Limited.

Tamsin Ravenscroft is a family member of Tamsin Sinclair.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent North Grafton Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 19 August 2024.

North Grafton Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grafton Logistics Limited.

North Grafton Logistics Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 24 August 2024.

Briar & Ivers LLP represents North Grafton Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Grafton Logistics Limited owns Calderport Warehouse.

Calderport Warehouse is located in Kingsmoor.

North Grafton Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-585.Tamsin Sinclair signed the Trust Deed LEG-585.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Pemberton, chair of North Grafton Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Grafton Energy Limited on 26 July 2024.

Calder Pemberton confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Grafton Energy Limited by North Grafton Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Grafton Energy Limited by North Grafton Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
