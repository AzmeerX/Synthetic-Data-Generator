Case notes for Kendal Systems Limited against Langton Industries Limited

Date: 12 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00032.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kendal Systems Limited against Langton Industries Limited in Elmford Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kendal Systems Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-413.

Ulric Alden signed the Supply Agreement LEG-413.Ulric Cairn is the chair of Kendal Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Trust Bank Limited lent Kendal Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 3 November 2024.

Langton Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Kendal Systems Limited.

Kendal Systems Limited owes Oak Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 8 November 2024.

Ulric Hale is a shareholder of Kendal Systems Limited.

Tamsin Zouch is a family member of Ulric Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kendal Systems Limited owns Langton Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kendal Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Langton Industries Limited on 12 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Sable noted that a former employee alleged that Kendal Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
