Board meeting minutes of North Osterley Partners Limited

Date: 11 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01358.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Alden, Quin Cairn and Alden & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Osterley Partners Limited owns North Osterley Capital Limited.Quin Alden is the chair of North Osterley Partners Limited.

Quin Fenwick is a shareholder of North Osterley Partners Limited.

Petra Wyndham is a family member of Petra Yardley.

Pember Trust Bank Limited lent North Osterley Partners Limited GBP 36 million on 26 July 2023.

North Osterley Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Osterley Partners Limited.

North Osterley Partners Limited owes Pember Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 31 July 2023.

Cairn & Sable LLP represents North Osterley Partners Limited in the matter.

North Osterley Partners Limited owns Jarrow Cross Wharf.

Jarrow Cross Wharf is located in Yewdale.

North Osterley Partners Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-395.Petra Yardley signed the Debenture LEG-395.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Anika Villiers, chair of North Osterley Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Osterley Ventures Limited on 11 February 2027.

Anika Villiers confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Osterley Ventures Limited by North Osterley Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Osterley Ventures Limited by North Osterley Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
