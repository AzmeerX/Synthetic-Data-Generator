Case notes for North Clifford Energy Limited against North Clifford Systems Limited

Date: 8 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01201.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Clifford Energy Limited against North Clifford Systems Limited in Elmford Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Clifford Energy Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-254.

Nadia Cairn signed the Licence LEG-254.Nadia Ellery is the chair of North Clifford Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether International Bank Limited lent North Clifford Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 22 December 2023.

North Clifford Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Clifford Energy Limited.

North Clifford Energy Limited owes Nether International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 27 December 2023.

Nadia Kerr is a shareholder of North Clifford Energy Limited.

Nadia Briar is a family member of Nadia Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Clifford Energy Limited owns Bexhaven Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Clifford Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Clifford Systems Limited on 8 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Xander Langford noted that a former employee alleged that North Clifford Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
