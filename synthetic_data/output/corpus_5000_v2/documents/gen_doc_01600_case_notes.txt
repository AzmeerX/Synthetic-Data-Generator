Case notes for Uldale Holdings Limited against Uldale Logistics Limited

Date: 7 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01600.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Uldale Holdings Limited against Uldale Logistics Limited in Denmoor Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Uldale Holdings Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-059.

Elias Delacourt signed the Debenture LEG-059.Elias Fairfax is the chair of Uldale Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Uldale Holdings Limited GBP 20 million on 28 May 2025.

Uldale Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Holdings Limited.

Uldale Holdings Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 2 June 2025.

Elias Kingsley is a shareholder of Uldale Holdings Limited.

Elias Cavendish is a family member of Elias Delacourt.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Uldale Holdings Limited owns Uldale Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Uldale Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Uldale Logistics Limited on 7 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Uldale Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
