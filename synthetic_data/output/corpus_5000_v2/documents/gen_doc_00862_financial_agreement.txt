Facility agreement between Elm International Bank Limited and North Barrowfield Logistics Limited

Date: 6 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00862.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Barrowfield Logistics Limited.

North Barrowfield Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Barrowfield Logistics Limited.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
