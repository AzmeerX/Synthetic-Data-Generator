Board meeting minutes of Pembroke Capital Limited

Date: 6 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00413.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Ivers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Yardley, Bram Alden and Cairn & Ivers LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Pembroke Capital Limited owns Pembroke Marine Limited.Anika Yardley is the chair of Pembroke Capital Limited.

Bram Dunmore is a shareholder of Pembroke Capital Limited.

Anika Villiers is a family member of Anika Whitlock.

Fair Commercial Bank Limited lent Pembroke Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 11 November 2025.

Pembroke Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Pembroke Capital Limited.

Pembroke Capital Limited owes Fair Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 16 November 2025.

Briar & Hale LLP represents Pembroke Capital Limited in the matter.

Pembroke Capital Limited owns Kestrelford Rail Depot.

Kestrelford Rail Depot is located in Elmsworth Green.

Pembroke Capital Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-150.Anika Whitlock signed the Loan Note LEG-150.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Hana Hastings, chair of Pembroke Capital Limited, proposed to acquire Pembroke Systems Limited on 6 June 2025.

Hana Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Pembroke Systems Limited by Pembroke Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Pembroke Systems Limited by Pembroke Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
