Board meeting minutes of Kelbrook Marine Limited

Date: 1 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00588.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Hastings LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Pell, Calder Rook and Alden & Hastings LLP.
