Board meeting minutes of Jura Property Limited

Date: 29 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00336.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Pemberton LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Zane Delacourt, Zane Fairfax and Alden & Pemberton LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

The acquisition of Kintyre Group Limited by Jura Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
