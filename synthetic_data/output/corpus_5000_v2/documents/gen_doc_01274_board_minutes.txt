Board meeting minutes of North Inkberrow Group Limited

Date: 3 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01274.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Yarrow, Oren Ashby and Briar & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Inkberrow Group Limited owns North Inkberrow Logistics Limited.Oren Yarrow is the chair of North Inkberrow Group Limited.

Oren Delacourt is a shareholder of North Inkberrow Group Limited.

Oren Ulric is a family member of Oren Vale.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent North Inkberrow Group Limited GBP 46 million on 17 December 2025.

North Inkberrow Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Inkberrow Group Limited.

North Inkberrow Group Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 22 December 2025.

Briar & Dunmore LLP represents North Inkberrow Group Limited in the matter.

North Inkberrow Group Limited owns Fairmouth Warehouse.

Fairmouth Warehouse is located in Lowmoor Hill.

North Inkberrow Group Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-320.Oren Vale signed the Side Letter LEG-320.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Zane Kerr, chair of North Inkberrow Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Inkberrow Capital Limited on 3 July 2025.

Zane Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Inkberrow Capital Limited by North Inkberrow Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Inkberrow Capital Limited by North Inkberrow Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
