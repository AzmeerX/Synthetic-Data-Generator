Board meeting minutes of Moray Trading Limited

Date: 16 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00371.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Balfour LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Kerr, Anika Mercer and Briar & Balfour LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Anika Pell is a shareholder of Moray Trading Limited.

Anika Hale is a family member of Anika Ivers.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Moray Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 19 September 2024.

Moray Trading Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 24 September 2024.

Alden & Sable LLP represents Moray Trading Limited in the matter.

Moray Trading Limited owns Ironside Terminal.

Ironside Terminal is located in Linden.

Moray Trading Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-112.Anika Ivers signed the Side Letter LEG-112.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of Moray Property Limited by Moray Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
