Board meeting minutes of North Cresswell Property Limited

Date: 24 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00882.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Rook LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Lorne, Hana Nyle and Briar & Rook LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Cresswell Property Limited owns North Cresswell Ventures Limited.Hana Lorne is the chair of North Cresswell Property Limited.

Hana Quade is a shareholder of North Cresswell Property Limited.

Hana Ivers is a family member of Hana Jarvis.

Kel Bank Limited lent North Cresswell Property Limited GBP 20 million on 21 August 2024.

North Cresswell Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cresswell Property Limited.

North Cresswell Property Limited owes Kel Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 26 August 2024.

Briar & Pemberton LLP represents North Cresswell Property Limited in the matter.

North Cresswell Property Limited owns Fenwick Cold Store.

Fenwick Cold Store is located in Merrivale Green.

North Cresswell Property Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-573.Hana Jarvis signed the Supply Agreement LEG-573.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Nyle, chair of North Cresswell Property Limited, proposed to acquire Merrivale Ventures Limited on 24 July 2024.

Rhea Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Merrivale Ventures Limited by North Cresswell Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Merrivale Ventures Limited by North Cresswell Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
