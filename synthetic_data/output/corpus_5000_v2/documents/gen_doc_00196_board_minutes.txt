Board meeting minutes of Yelverton Logistics Limited

Date: 22 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00196.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Fairfax LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Alden, Xander Cairn and Alden & Fairfax LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Yelverton Logistics Limited owns Yelverton Trading Limited.Xander Alden is the chair of Yelverton Logistics Limited.

Xander Fenwick is a shareholder of Yelverton Logistics Limited.

Wren Wyndham is a family member of Wren Yardley.

Red Bank Limited lent Yelverton Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 23 August 2024.

Yelverton Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Yelverton Logistics Limited.

Yelverton Logistics Limited owes Red Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 28 August 2024.

Cairn & Zell LLP represents Yelverton Logistics Limited in the matter.

Yelverton Logistics Limited owns Quintrell Foundry.

Quintrell Foundry is located in Carrow Hill.

Yelverton Logistics Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-561.Wren Yardley signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-561.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Lorne, chair of Yelverton Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Yelverton Marine Limited on 22 July 2024.

Daria Lorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Yelverton Marine Limited by Yelverton Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Yelverton Marine Limited by Yelverton Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
