Case notes for North Tarrow Group Limited against North Tarrow Partners Limited

Date: 29 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00781.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Tarrow Group Limited against North Tarrow Partners Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Tarrow Group Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-482.

Farah Quade signed the Facility Agreement LEG-482.Farah Thorne is the chair of North Tarrow Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill International Bank Limited lent North Tarrow Group Limited GBP 38 million on 21 July 2026.

North Tarrow Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Tarrow Group Limited.

North Tarrow Group Limited owes Quill International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 26 July 2026.

Farah Zell is a shareholder of North Tarrow Group Limited.

Farah Pell is a family member of Farah Quade.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Tarrow Group Limited owns North Tarrow Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Tarrow Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Tarrow Partners Limited on 29 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Petra Lorne noted that a former employee alleged that North Tarrow Group Limited misstated its revenue.
