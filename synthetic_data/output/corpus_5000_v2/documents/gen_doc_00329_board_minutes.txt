Board meeting minutes of Jura Logistics Limited

Date: 8 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00329.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ingram LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Zane Ward, Zane Zell and Alden & Ingram LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Jura Logistics Limited owns Jura Trading Limited.Zane Ward is the chair of Jura Logistics Limited.

Zane Cavendish is a shareholder of Jura Logistics Limited.

Zane Thorne is a family member of Zane Ulric.

Pember Bank Limited lent Jura Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 9 August 2023.

Jura Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Jura Logistics Limited.

Jura Logistics Limited owes Pember Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 14 August 2023.

Cairn & Nyle LLP represents Jura Logistics Limited in the matter.

Jura Logistics Limited owns Greyhaven Cold Store.

Greyhaven Cold Store is located in Vellacott.

Jura Logistics Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-075.Zane Ulric signed the Debenture LEG-075.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Farah Wyndham, chair of Jura Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Jura Marine Limited on 8 February 2027.

Farah Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Jura Marine Limited by Jura Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Jura Marine Limited by Jura Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
