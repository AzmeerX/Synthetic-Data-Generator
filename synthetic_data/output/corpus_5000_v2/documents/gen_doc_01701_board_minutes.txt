Board meeting minutes of Draycott Energy Limited

Date: 12 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01701.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Whitlock LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Ashby, Gideon Cavendish and Briar & Whitlock LLP.
