Case notes for Westerby Industries Limited against Westerby Trading Limited

Date: 19 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01628.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Westerby Industries Limited against Westerby Trading Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Westerby Industries Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-084.

Farah Dunmore signed the Board Resolution LEG-084.Farah Fenwick is the chair of Westerby Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent Westerby Industries Limited GBP 30 million on 27 February 2026.

Westerby Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Westerby Industries Limited.

Westerby Industries Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 4 March 2026.

Farah Kerr is a shareholder of Westerby Industries Limited.

Farah Cairn is a family member of Farah Dunmore.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Westerby Industries Limited owns Westerby Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Westerby Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Westerby Trading Limited on 19 September 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Westerby Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
