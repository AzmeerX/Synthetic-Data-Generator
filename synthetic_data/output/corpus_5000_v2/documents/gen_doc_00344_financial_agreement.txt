Facility agreement between Glen Commercial Bank Limited and Inglemere Property Limited

Date: 9 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00344.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Glen Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is Inglemere Property Limited.

Inglemere Property Limited owns Inglemere Ventures Limited.Silvergate Bank Limited lent Inglemere Property Limited GBP 30 million on 29 December 2025.

Inglemere Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Property Limited.

Inglemere Property Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 January 2026.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Inglemere Property Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-004.

Bastian Ravenscroft signed the Facility Agreement LEG-004.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
