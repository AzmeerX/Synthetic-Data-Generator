Case notes for Selby Property Limited against Tavistock Holdings Limited

Date: 17 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00137.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Selby Property Limited against Tavistock Holdings Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Selby Property Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-507.

Vera Underwood signed the Debenture LEG-507.Vera Whitlock is the chair of Selby Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Bank Limited lent Selby Property Limited GBP 36 million on 7 January 2023.

Selby Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Selby Property Limited.

Selby Property Limited owes Inver Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 12 January 2023.

Wren Briar is a shareholder of Selby Property Limited.

Vera Tremayne is a family member of Vera Underwood.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Selby Property Limited owns Selby Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Selby Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tavistock Holdings Limited on 17 September 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Selby Property Limited misstated its revenue.
