Board meeting minutes of Nairn Partners Limited

Date: 22 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00385.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Pemberton LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Anika Yarrow, Anika Ashby and Briar & Pemberton LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Nairn Partners Limited owns Nairn Capital Limited.Anika Yarrow is the chair of Nairn Partners Limited.

Anika Delacourt is a shareholder of Nairn Partners Limited.

Anika Ulric is a family member of Anika Vale.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent Nairn Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 9 February 2025.

Nairn Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Nairn Partners Limited.

Nairn Partners Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 14 February 2025.

Alden & Grenville LLP represents Nairn Partners Limited in the matter.

Nairn Partners Limited owns Ironside Plant.

Ironside Plant is located in Belmont Moor.

Nairn Partners Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-125.Anika Vale signed the Supply Agreement LEG-125.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Hana Ellery, chair of Nairn Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Nairn Property Limited on 22 November 2024.

Hana Ellery confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Nairn Property Limited by Nairn Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Nairn Property Limited by Nairn Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
