Case notes for Moray Energy Limited against Moray Ventures Limited

Date: 13 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00375.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Moray Energy Limited against Moray Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Moray Energy Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-116.

Anika Mercer signed the Board Resolution LEG-116.Anika Orrick is the chair of Moray Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Moray Energy Limited GBP 26 million on 2 November 2024.

Moray Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Moray Energy Limited.

Moray Energy Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 7 November 2024.

Anika Thorne is a shareholder of Moray Energy Limited.

Anika Lorne is a family member of Anika Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Moray Energy Limited owns Ironside Terminal.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Moray Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Moray Ventures Limited on 13 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Moray Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
