Board meeting minutes of Grimsdale Ventures Limited

Date: 20 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01743.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Rook LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ines Alden, Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department and Alden & Rook LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Grimsdale Ventures Limited owns Grimsdale Systems Limited.Ines Alden is the chair of Grimsdale Ventures Limited.

Kira Kerr is a shareholder of Grimsdale Ventures Limited.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department is a family member of Marek Nyle.

Denmoor Private Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Ventures Limited GBP 40 million on 31 August 2024.

Hazelmere Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Ventures Limited.

Grimsdale Ventures Limited owes Denmoor Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 5 September 2024.

Alden & Osborne LLP represents Grimsdale Ventures Limited in the matter.

Grimsdale Ventures Limited owns Larkspur Quay.

Larkspur Quay is located in Dunhollow.

Grimsdale Ventures Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-189.Marek Nyle signed the Supply Agreement LEG-189.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Ulric, chair of Grimsdale Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Belmont Logistics Limited on 20 August 2024.

Gideon Ulric confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Belmont Logistics Limited by Grimsdale Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Belmont Logistics Limited by Grimsdale Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
