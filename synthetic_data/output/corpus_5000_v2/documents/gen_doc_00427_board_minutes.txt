Board meeting minutes of Quantock Marine Limited

Date: 12 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00427.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bram Mercer, Bram Pell and Cairn & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Quantock Marine Limited owns Quantock Energy Limited.Bram Mercer is the chair of Quantock Marine Limited.

Bram Sable is a shareholder of Quantock Marine Limited.

Bram Ivers is a family member of Bram Jarvis.

Harrow Private Bank Limited lent Quantock Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 3 April 2026.

Quantock Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Quantock Marine Limited.

Quantock Marine Limited owes Harrow Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 8 April 2026.

Briar & Ulric LLP represents Quantock Marine Limited in the matter.

Quantock Marine Limited owns Kestrelford Dock.

Kestrelford Dock is located in Glenmoor Port.

Quantock Marine Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.Bram Jarvis signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Hana Whitlock, chair of Quantock Marine Limited, proposed to acquire Jarrow Partners Limited on 12 September 2025.

Hana Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Jarrow Partners Limited by Quantock Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Jarrow Partners Limited by Quantock Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
