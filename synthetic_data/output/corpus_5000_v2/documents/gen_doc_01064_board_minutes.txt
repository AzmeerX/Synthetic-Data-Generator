Board meeting minutes of North Quarrymere Partners Limited

Date: 6 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01064.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Delacourt, Kellan Fairfax and Cairn & Jarvis LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quarrymere Partners Limited owns North Quarrymere Capital Limited.Kellan Delacourt is the chair of North Quarrymere Partners Limited.

Kellan Ingram is a shareholder of North Quarrymere Partners Limited.

Kellan Ashby is a family member of Kellan Balfour.

Pember Trust Bank Limited lent North Quarrymere Partners Limited GBP 28 million on 3 December 2024.

North Quarrymere Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quarrymere Partners Limited.

North Quarrymere Partners Limited owes Pember Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 8 December 2024.

Cairn & Balfour LLP represents North Quarrymere Partners Limited in the matter.

North Quarrymere Partners Limited owns Juniper Cross Yard.

Juniper Cross Yard is located in Kelbrook Green.

North Quarrymere Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.Kellan Balfour signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Yardley, chair of North Quarrymere Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Quarrymere Property Limited on 6 October 2024.

Ulric Yardley confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quarrymere Property Limited by North Quarrymere Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quarrymere Property Limited by North Quarrymere Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
