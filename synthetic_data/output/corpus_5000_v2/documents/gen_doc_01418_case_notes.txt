Case notes for North Thurlow Holdings Limited against North Thurlow Group Limited

Date: 23 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01418.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Thurlow Holdings Limited against North Thurlow Group Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Thurlow Holdings Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-450.

Rhea Ellery signed the Facility Agreement LEG-450.Rhea Garrow is the chair of North Thurlow Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Thurlow Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 22 March 2025.

North Thurlow Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Thurlow Holdings Limited.

North Thurlow Holdings Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 27 March 2025.

Rhea Lorne is a shareholder of North Thurlow Holdings Limited.

Rhea Dunmore is a family member of Rhea Ellery.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Thurlow Holdings Limited owns Lowmoor Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Thurlow Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Thurlow Group Limited on 23 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nolan Quade noted that a former employee alleged that North Thurlow Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
