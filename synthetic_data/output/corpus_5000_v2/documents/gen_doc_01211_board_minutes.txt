Board meeting minutes of North Denholm Partners Limited

Date: 18 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01211.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Balfour LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Orrick, Nadia Quade and Cairn & Balfour LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Denholm Partners Limited owns North Denholm Capital Limited.Nadia Orrick is the chair of North Denholm Partners Limited.

Nadia Thorne is a shareholder of North Denholm Partners Limited.

Nadia Lorne is a family member of Nadia Mercer.

Pember Trust Bank Limited lent North Denholm Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 30 March 2024.

North Denholm Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Denholm Partners Limited.

North Denholm Partners Limited owes Pember Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 4 April 2024.

Harrow & Finch LLP represents North Denholm Partners Limited in the matter.

North Denholm Partners Limited owns Carrow Bay Hangar.

Carrow Bay Hangar is located in Elmsworth Bridge.

North Denholm Partners Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-263.Nadia Mercer signed the Services Agreement LEG-263.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Xander Whitlock, chair of North Denholm Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Denholm Ventures Limited on 18 April 2024.

Xander Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Denholm Ventures Limited by North Denholm Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Denholm Ventures Limited by North Denholm Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
