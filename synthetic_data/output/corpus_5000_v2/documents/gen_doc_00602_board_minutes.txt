Board meeting minutes of North Fairhaven Energy Limited

Date: 7 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00602.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Whitlock LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Dunmore, Calder Fenwick and Alden & Whitlock LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
