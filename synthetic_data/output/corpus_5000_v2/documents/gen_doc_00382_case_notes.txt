Case notes for Nairn Group Limited against Nairn Partners Limited

Date: 1 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00382.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Nairn Group Limited against Nairn Partners Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Nairn Group Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-122.

Anika Sable signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-122.Anika Ulric is the chair of Nairn Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder International Bank Limited lent Nairn Group Limited GBP 38 million on 7 January 2025.

Nairn Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Nairn Group Limited.

Nairn Group Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 12 January 2025.

Anika Ashby is a shareholder of Nairn Group Limited.

Anika Rook is a family member of Anika Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Nairn Group Limited owns Nairn Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Nairn Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Nairn Partners Limited on 1 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Hana Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Nairn Group Limited misstated its revenue.
