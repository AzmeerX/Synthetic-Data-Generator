Facility agreement between Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited and North Quenington Industries Limited

Date: 1 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01380.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is North Quenington Industries Limited.

North Quenington Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quenington Industries Limited.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
