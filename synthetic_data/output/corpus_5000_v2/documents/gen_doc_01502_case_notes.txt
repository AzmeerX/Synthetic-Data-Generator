Case notes for North Bramley Logistics Limited against North Bramley Capital Limited

Date: 3 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01502.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Bramley Logistics Limited against North Bramley Capital Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Bramley Logistics Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-525.

Silas Grenville signed the Supply Agreement LEG-525.Silas Ingram is the chair of North Bramley Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Bank Limited lent North Bramley Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 29 October 2022.

North Bramley Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Bramley Logistics Limited.

North Bramley Logistics Limited owes Pember Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 3 November 2022.

Silas Neville is a shareholder of North Bramley Logistics Limited.

Silas Fairfax is a family member of Silas Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Bramley Logistics Limited owns North Bramley Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Bramley Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Bramley Capital Limited on 3 August 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Zell noted that a former employee alleged that North Bramley Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
