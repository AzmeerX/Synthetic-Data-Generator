Board meeting minutes of Quantock Industries Limited

Date: 25 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00420.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Pell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bram Fenwick, Bram Hale and Cairn & Pell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Quantock Industries Limited owns Quantock Group Limited.Bram Fenwick is the chair of Quantock Industries Limited.

Bram Lorne is a shareholder of Quantock Industries Limited.

Bram Cairn is a family member of Bram Dunmore.

Glen Private Bank Limited lent Quantock Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 27 January 2026.

Quantock Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Quantock Industries Limited.

Quantock Industries Limited owes Glen Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 1 February 2026.

Briar & Orrick LLP represents Quantock Industries Limited in the matter.

Quantock Industries Limited owns Kestrelford Quay.

Kestrelford Quay is located in Fairhaven Bridge.

Quantock Industries Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-157.Bram Dunmore signed the Supply Agreement LEG-157.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Hana Osborne, chair of Quantock Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Quantock Partners Limited on 25 July 2025.

Hana Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Quantock Partners Limited by Quantock Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Quantock Partners Limited by Quantock Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
