Board meeting minutes of Wear Ventures Limited

Date: 23 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00511.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Langford LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Vale, Amina Yarrow and Briar & Langford LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Wear Ventures Limited owns Wear Systems Limited.Amina Vale is the chair of Wear Ventures Limited.

Amina Balfour is a shareholder of Wear Ventures Limited.

Amina Sable is a family member of Amina Thorne.

Uldale Merchant Bank Limited lent Wear Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 10 November 2023.

Yorkshire Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Wear Ventures Limited.

Wear Ventures Limited owes Uldale Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 15 November 2023.

Alden & Orrick LLP represents Wear Ventures Limited in the matter.

Wear Ventures Limited owns Alderport Silo.

Alderport Silo is located in Elmford.

Wear Ventures Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-238.Amina Thorne signed the Licence LEG-238.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Juno Hastings, chair of Wear Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Yorkshire Logistics Limited on 23 April 2027.

Juno Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Yorkshire Logistics Limited by Wear Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Yorkshire Logistics Limited by Wear Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
