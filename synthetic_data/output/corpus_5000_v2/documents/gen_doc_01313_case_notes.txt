Case notes for North Linden Industries Limited against North Linden Trading Limited

Date: 2 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01313.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Linden Industries Limited against North Linden Trading Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Linden Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-355.

Petra Ivers signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-355.Petra Kerr is the chair of North Linden Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Private Bank Limited lent North Linden Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 12 May 2022.

North Linden Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Linden Industries Limited.

North Linden Industries Limited owes Inver Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 17 May 2022.

Petra Pell is a shareholder of North Linden Industries Limited.

Petra Garrow is a family member of Petra Ivers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Linden Industries Limited owns North Linden Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Linden Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Linden Trading Limited on 2 April 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
