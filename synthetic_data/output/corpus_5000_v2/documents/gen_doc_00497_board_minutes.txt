Board meeting minutes of Vale Ventures Limited

Date: 15 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00497.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Ivers, Amina Lorne and Briar & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Vale Ventures Limited owns Vale Systems Limited.Amina Ivers is the chair of Vale Ventures Limited.

Amina Orrick is a shareholder of Vale Ventures Limited.

Amina Fenwick is a family member of Amina Garrow.

Stan Merchant Bank Limited lent Vale Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 1 July 2023.

Wear Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Vale Ventures Limited.

Vale Ventures Limited owes Stan Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 6 July 2023.

Alden & Cairn LLP represents Vale Ventures Limited in the matter.

Vale Ventures Limited owns Alderport Rail Depot.

Alderport Rail Depot is located in Greyhaven.

Vale Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-226.Amina Garrow signed the Facility Agreement LEG-226.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Juno Sable, chair of Vale Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Wear Group Limited on 15 January 2027.

Juno Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Wear Group Limited by Vale Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Wear Group Limited by Vale Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
