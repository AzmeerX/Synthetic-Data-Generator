Case notes for North Quenington Trading Limited against North Quenington Marine Limited

Date: 22 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01383.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quenington Trading Limited against North Quenington Marine Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quenington Trading Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-418.

Quin Ward signed the Facility Agreement LEG-418.Quin Zell is the chair of North Quenington Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Merchant Bank Limited lent North Quenington Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 4 April 2024.

North Quenington Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quenington Trading Limited.

North Quenington Trading Limited owes Tarrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 9 April 2024.

Quin Everard is a shareholder of North Quenington Trading Limited.

Quin Vale is a family member of Quin Ward.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quenington Trading Limited owns Jarrow Cross Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quenington Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quenington Marine Limited on 22 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bram Ward noted that a former employee alleged that North Quenington Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
