Case notes for Oakhurst Industries Limited against North Penhurst Ventures Limited

Date: 28 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01054.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Oakhurst Industries Limited against North Penhurst Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Oakhurst Industries Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-068.

Daria Fenwick signed the Board Resolution LEG-068.Daria Ivers is the chair of Oakhurst Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Oakhurst Industries Limited GBP 38 million on 8 April 2023.

Oakhurst Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Oakhurst Industries Limited.

Oakhurst Industries Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 13 April 2023.

Daria Nyle is a shareholder of Oakhurst Industries Limited.

Daria Ellery is a family member of Daria Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Oakhurst Industries Limited owns Oakhurst Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Oakhurst Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Penhurst Ventures Limited on 28 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Neville noted that a former employee alleged that Oakhurst Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
