Case notes for Wilton Capital Limited against Wilton Energy Limited

Date: 13 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00186.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Wilton Capital Limited against Wilton Energy Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Wilton Capital Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-552.

Wren Osborne signed the Lease LEG-552.Wren Ravenscroft is the chair of Wilton Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Commercial Bank Limited lent Wilton Capital Limited GBP 46 million on 16 May 2024.

Wilton Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Wilton Capital Limited.

Wilton Capital Limited owes Pember Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 21 May 2024.

Wren Whitlock is a shareholder of Wilton Capital Limited.

Wren Neville is a family member of Wren Osborne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Wilton Capital Limited owns Wilton Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Wilton Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Wilton Energy Limited on 13 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Alden noted that a former employee alleged that Wilton Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
