Case notes for Ulverston Systems Limited against Vernon Industries Limited

Date: 1 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00165.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ulverston Systems Limited against Vernon Industries Limited in Elmford County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ulverston Systems Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-533.

Wren Ulric signed the Security Agreement LEG-533.Wren Ward is the chair of Ulverston Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Trust Bank Limited lent Ulverston Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 20 October 2023.

Vernon Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Ulverston Systems Limited.

Ulverston Systems Limited owes Merri Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 25 October 2023.

Wren Cavendish is a shareholder of Ulverston Systems Limited.

Wren Thorne is a family member of Wren Ulric.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ulverston Systems Limited owns Vernon Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ulverston Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vernon Industries Limited on 1 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Ulverston Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
