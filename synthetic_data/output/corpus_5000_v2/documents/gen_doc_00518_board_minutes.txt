Board meeting minutes of Yorkshire Partners Limited

Date: 27 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00518.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Tremayne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Delacourt, Amina Fairfax and Briar & Tremayne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Yorkshire Partners Limited owns Yorkshire Capital Limited.Amina Delacourt is the chair of Yorkshire Partners Limited.

Amina Ingram is a shareholder of Yorkshire Partners Limited.

Amina Ashby is a family member of Amina Balfour.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Yorkshire Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 26 January 2024.

Yorkshire Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Yorkshire Partners Limited.

Yorkshire Partners Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 31 January 2024.

Alden & Vale LLP represents Yorkshire Partners Limited in the matter.

Yorkshire Partners Limited owns Bexhaven Wharf.

Bexhaven Wharf is located in Lowmoor.

Yorkshire Partners Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-245.Amina Balfour signed the Security Agreement LEG-245.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Juno Osborne, chair of Yorkshire Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Yorkshire Property Limited on 27 February 2024.

Juno Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Yorkshire Property Limited by Yorkshire Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Yorkshire Property Limited by Yorkshire Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
