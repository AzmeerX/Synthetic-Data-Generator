Board meeting minutes of Tarrow Group Limited

Date: 20 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01589.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Orrick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ulric, Elias Ward and Cairn & Orrick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Tarrow Group Limited owns Tarrow Logistics Limited.Elias Ulric is the chair of Tarrow Group Limited.

Elias Ashby is a shareholder of Tarrow Group Limited.

Elias Rook is a family member of Elias Sable.

Glen Trust Bank Limited lent Tarrow Group Limited GBP 40 million on 7 February 2025.

Tarrow Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Group Limited.

Tarrow Group Limited owes Glen Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 12 February 2025.

Quade & Thorne LLP represents Tarrow Group Limited in the matter.

Tarrow Group Limited owns Eastmere Data Centre.

Eastmere Data Centre is located in Bexley.

Tarrow Group Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-049.Elias Sable signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-049.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Mercer, chair of Tarrow Group Limited, proposed to acquire Ashcombe Industries Limited on 20 December 2024.

Daria Mercer confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Ashcombe Industries Limited by Tarrow Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Ashcombe Industries Limited by Tarrow Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
