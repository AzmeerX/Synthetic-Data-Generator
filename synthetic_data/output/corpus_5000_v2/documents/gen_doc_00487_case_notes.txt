Case notes for Vale Industries Limited against Vale Logistics Limited

Date: 6 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00487.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vale Industries Limited against Vale Logistics Limited in Fairmouth Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vale Industries Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-217.

Lorne Ward signed the Trust Deed LEG-217.Yara Rook is the chair of Vale Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Private Bank Limited lent Vale Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 24 March 2023.

Vale Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Vale Industries Limited.

Vale Industries Limited owes Quill Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 29 March 2023.

Amina Ellery is a shareholder of Vale Industries Limited.

Gideon Nyle is a family member of Lorne Ward.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vale Industries Limited owns Vale Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vale Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vale Logistics Limited on 6 November 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Vale Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
