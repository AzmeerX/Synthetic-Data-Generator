Board meeting minutes of Oakhaven Holdings Limited

Date: 1 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00000.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Harrow & Finch LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Kerr, Marek Nyle and Harrow & Finch LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Oakhaven Holdings Limited owns Quarrystone Industries Limited.Jarrah Dunmore served as chair of Oakhaven Holdings Limited until 30 June 2023.

Kellan Lorne is a shareholder of Oakhaven Holdings Limited.

Jarrah Dunmore is a family member of Ines Hale.

Oakhaven Holdings Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 14 February 2022.

Juniper Data Centre is located in Juniper Cross.

Oakhaven Holdings Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-001.Ines Hale signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-001.

The acquisition of Juniper Industries Limited by Oakhaven Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.
