Press release concerning Wilton Industries Limited

Publication date: 8 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00181.

1. Corporate statement

Wilton Industries Limited today issued the following statement.

Wilton Industries Limited owns Wilton Group Limited.

Wren Ravenscroft is a shareholder of Wilton Industries Limited.

Wren Ingram is a family member of Wren Jeffreys.

Oak Private Bank Limited lent Wilton Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 22 March 2024.

Wilton Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Wilton Industries Limited.

Wilton Industries Limited owes Oak Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 27 March 2024.
