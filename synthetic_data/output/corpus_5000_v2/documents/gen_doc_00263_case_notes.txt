Case notes for Ennerdale Logistics Limited against Inglemere Group Limited

Date: 3 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00263.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ennerdale Logistics Limited against Inglemere Group Limited in Fairmouth Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ennerdale Logistics Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-015.

Yara Jarvis signed the Security Agreement LEG-015.Yara Lorne is the chair of Ennerdale Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Bank Limited lent Ennerdale Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 14 June 2026.

Ennerdale Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Ennerdale Logistics Limited.

Ennerdale Logistics Limited owes Fair Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 19 June 2026.

Yara Quade is a shareholder of Ennerdale Logistics Limited.

Yara Ivers is a family member of Yara Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ennerdale Logistics Limited owns Ennerdale Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ennerdale Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Inglemere Group Limited on 3 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Ennerdale Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
