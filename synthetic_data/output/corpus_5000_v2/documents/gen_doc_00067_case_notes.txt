Case notes for Neston Capital Limited against Neston Property Limited

Date: 15 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00067.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Neston Capital Limited against Neston Property Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Neston Capital Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-444.

Ulric Grenville signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-444.Ulric Ingram is the chair of Neston Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited lent Neston Capital Limited GBP 30 million on 10 October 2025.

Neston Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Neston Capital Limited.

Neston Capital Limited owes Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 15 October 2025.

Ulric Neville is a shareholder of Neston Capital Limited.

Ulric Fairfax is a family member of Ulric Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Neston Capital Limited owns Neston Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Neston Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Neston Property Limited on 15 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that Neston Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
