Case notes for North Farnley Logistics Limited against North Farnley Capital Limited

Date: 10 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01236.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Farnley Logistics Limited against North Farnley Capital Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Farnley Logistics Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-285.

Nadia Kingsley signed the Supply Agreement LEG-285.Nadia Montague is the chair of North Farnley Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Bank Limited lent North Farnley Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 27 November 2024.

North Farnley Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Farnley Logistics Limited.

North Farnley Logistics Limited owes Tarrow Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 2 December 2024.

Nadia Sinclair is a shareholder of North Farnley Logistics Limited.

Nadia Jeffreys is a family member of Nadia Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Farnley Logistics Limited owns North Farnley Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Farnley Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Farnley Capital Limited on 10 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Ward noted that a former employee alleged that North Farnley Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
