Board meeting minutes of North Aldergate Industries Limited

Date: 6 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00847.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Grenville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Everard, Gideon Grenville and Alden & Grenville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Aldergate Industries Limited owns North Aldergate Group Limited.Gideon Everard is the chair of North Aldergate Industries Limited.

Gideon Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Aldergate Industries Limited.

Gideon Balfour is a family member of Gideon Cavendish.

Elm Private Bank Limited lent North Aldergate Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 4 September 2023.

North Aldergate Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Aldergate Industries Limited.

North Aldergate Industries Limited owes Elm Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 9 September 2023.

Briar & Ivers LLP represents North Aldergate Industries Limited in the matter.

North Aldergate Industries Limited owns Pemberton Plant.

Pemberton Plant is located in Inglemere Bridge.

North Aldergate Industries Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-541.Gideon Cavendish signed the Supply Agreement LEG-541.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Quin Delacourt, chair of North Aldergate Industries Limited, proposed to acquire North Aldergate Capital Limited on 6 March 2027.

Quin Delacourt confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Aldergate Capital Limited by North Aldergate Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Aldergate Capital Limited by North Aldergate Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
