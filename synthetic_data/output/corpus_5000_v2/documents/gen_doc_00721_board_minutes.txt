Board meeting minutes of Lowmoor Marine Limited

Date: 5 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00721.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Kingsley LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Cavendish, Calder Everard and Alden & Kingsley LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Lowmoor Marine Limited owns Dunhollow Works.Calder Cavendish is the chair of Lowmoor Marine Limited.

Calder Hastings is a shareholder of Lowmoor Marine Limited.

Calder Zell is a family member of Calder Ashby.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 13 May 2022.

Lowmoor Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Marine Limited.

Lowmoor Marine Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 18 May 2022.

Alden & Ivers LLP represents Lowmoor Marine Limited in the matter.

Lowmoor Marine Limited owns Dunhollow Dock.

Dunhollow Dock is located in Overdale.

Lowmoor Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-038.Calder Ashby signed the Loan Note LEG-038.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Yardley, chair of Lowmoor Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Pemberton Holdings Limited on 5 October 2024.

Nadia Yardley confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Pemberton Holdings Limited by Lowmoor Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Pemberton Holdings Limited by Lowmoor Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
