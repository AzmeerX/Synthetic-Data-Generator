Board meeting minutes of North Cavendish Marine Limited

Date: 30 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01519.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ellery LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Silas Zouch, Tamsin Briar and Briar & Ellery LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Cavendish Marine Limited owns North Cavendish Energy Limited.Silas Zouch is the chair of North Cavendish Marine Limited.

Tamsin Ellery is a shareholder of North Cavendish Marine Limited.

Silas Whitlock is a family member of Silas Wyndham.

Red Private Bank Limited lent North Cavendish Marine Limited GBP 48 million on 23 April 2023.

North Cavendish Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cavendish Marine Limited.

North Cavendish Marine Limited owes Red Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 28 April 2023.

Alden & Nyle LLP represents North Cavendish Marine Limited in the matter.

North Cavendish Marine Limited owns Quintrell Foundry.

Quintrell Foundry is located in Netherby.

North Cavendish Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-541.Silas Wyndham signed the Supply Agreement LEG-541.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bastian Ravenscroft, chair of North Cavendish Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Cavendish Systems Limited on 30 November 2026.

Bastian Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Cavendish Systems Limited by North Cavendish Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Cavendish Systems Limited by North Cavendish Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
