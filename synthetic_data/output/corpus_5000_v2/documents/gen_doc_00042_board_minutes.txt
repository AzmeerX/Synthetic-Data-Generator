Board meeting minutes of Langton Energy Limited

Date: 21 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00042.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Hale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Lorne, Ulric Nyle and Alden & Hale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
