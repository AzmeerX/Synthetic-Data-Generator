Case notes for North Yarmouth Ventures Limited against North Aldergate Industries Limited

Date: 13 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00844.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Yarmouth Ventures Limited against North Aldergate Industries Limited in Fairmouth High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Yarmouth Ventures Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-538.

Gideon Zell signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-538.Gideon Balfour is the chair of North Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent North Yarmouth Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 2 August 2023.

North Aldergate Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

North Yarmouth Ventures Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 7 August 2023.

Gideon Grenville is a shareholder of North Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

Gideon Yarrow is a family member of Gideon Zell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Yarmouth Ventures Limited owns North Yarmouth Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Yarmouth Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Aldergate Industries Limited on 13 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Quin Ashby noted that a former employee alleged that North Yarmouth Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
