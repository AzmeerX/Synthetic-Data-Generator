Board meeting minutes of North Grimsdale Partners Limited

Date: 2 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00931.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Garrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ivo Garrow, Ivo Ivers and Cairn & Garrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Grimsdale Partners Limited owns North Grimsdale Capital Limited.Ivo Garrow is the chair of North Grimsdale Partners Limited.

Ivo Lorne is a shareholder of North Grimsdale Partners Limited.

Ivo Cairn is a family member of Ivo Ellery.

Red Trust Bank Limited lent North Grimsdale Partners Limited GBP 28 million on 18 December 2025.

North Grimsdale Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grimsdale Partners Limited.

North Grimsdale Partners Limited owes Red Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 23 December 2025.

Alden & Dunmore LLP represents North Grimsdale Partners Limited in the matter.

North Grimsdale Partners Limited owns Dunhollow Hangar.

Dunhollow Hangar is located in Yarmouth.

North Grimsdale Partners Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-011.Ivo Ellery signed the Board Resolution LEG-011.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Silas Orrick, chair of North Grimsdale Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Grimsdale Property Limited on 2 July 2025.

Silas Orrick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Grimsdale Property Limited by North Grimsdale Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Grimsdale Property Limited by North Grimsdale Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
