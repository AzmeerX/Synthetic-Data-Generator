Board meeting minutes of Harrowden Property Limited

Date: 28 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00210.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Underwood LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Grenville, Bastian Ingram and Alden & Underwood LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Harrowden Property Limited owns Harrowden Ventures Limited.Bastian Grenville is the chair of Harrowden Property Limited.

Bastian Langford is a shareholder of Harrowden Property Limited.

Bastian Delacourt is a family member of Bastian Everard.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Harrowden Property Limited GBP 46 million on 19 August 2025.

Harrowden Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Property Limited.

Harrowden Property Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 24 August 2025.

Alden & Ward LLP represents Harrowden Property Limited in the matter.

Harrowden Property Limited owns Kestrelford Hangar.

Kestrelford Hangar is located in Greyhaven.

Harrowden Property Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-118.Bastian Everard signed the Loan Note LEG-118.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Ashby, chair of Harrowden Property Limited, proposed to acquire Alderney Energy Limited on 28 October 2024.

Daria Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Alderney Energy Limited by Harrowden Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Alderney Energy Limited by Harrowden Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
