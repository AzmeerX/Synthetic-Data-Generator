Case notes for Quantock Trading Limited against Quantock Marine Limited

Date: 22 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00424.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quantock Trading Limited against Quantock Marine Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quantock Trading Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-160.

Bram Garrow signed the Side Letter LEG-160.Bram Ivers is the chair of Quantock Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Quantock Trading Limited GBP 34 million on 1 March 2026.

Quantock Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Quantock Trading Limited.

Quantock Trading Limited owes Harrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 6 March 2026.

Bram Pell is a shareholder of Quantock Trading Limited.

Bram Fenwick is a family member of Bram Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quantock Trading Limited owns Kestrelford Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Quantock Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Quantock Marine Limited on 22 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Hana Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Quantock Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
