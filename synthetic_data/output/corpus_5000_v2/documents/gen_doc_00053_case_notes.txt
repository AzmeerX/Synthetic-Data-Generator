Case notes for Marlowe Capital Limited against Marlowe Energy Limited

Date: 6 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00053.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Marlowe Capital Limited against Marlowe Energy Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Marlowe Capital Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-432.

Ulric Thorne signed the Side Letter LEG-432.Ulric Vale is the chair of Marlowe Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Commercial Bank Limited lent Marlowe Capital Limited GBP 46 million on 31 May 2025.

Marlowe Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Marlowe Capital Limited.

Marlowe Capital Limited owes Red Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 5 June 2025.

Ulric Balfour is a shareholder of Marlowe Capital Limited.

Ulric Sable is a family member of Ulric Thorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Marlowe Capital Limited owns Marlowe Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Marlowe Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Marlowe Energy Limited on 6 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Rook noted that a former employee alleged that Marlowe Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
