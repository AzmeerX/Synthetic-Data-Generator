Case notes for Culm Energy Limited against Culm Ventures Limited

Date: 9 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00242.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Culm Energy Limited against Culm Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Culm Energy Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-602.

Xander Pemberton signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-602.Xander Sinclair is the chair of Culm Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Culm Energy Limited GBP 26 million on 17 November 2025.

Culm Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Culm Energy Limited.

Culm Energy Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 22 November 2025.

Xander Wyndham is a shareholder of Culm Energy Limited.

Xander Osborne is a family member of Xander Pemberton.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Culm Energy Limited owns Valebrook Farm.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Culm Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Culm Ventures Limited on 9 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Hale noted that a former employee alleged that Culm Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
