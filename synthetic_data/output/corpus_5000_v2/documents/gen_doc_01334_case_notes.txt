Case notes for North Maplebury Energy Limited against North Maplebury Systems Limited

Date: 27 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01334.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Maplebury Energy Limited against North Maplebury Systems Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Maplebury Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-374.

Petra Cavendish signed the Loan Note LEG-374.Petra Everard is the chair of North Maplebury Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low International Bank Limited lent North Maplebury Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 7 December 2022.

North Maplebury Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Maplebury Energy Limited.

North Maplebury Energy Limited owes Low International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 12 December 2022.

Petra Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Maplebury Energy Limited.

Petra Balfour is a family member of Petra Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Maplebury Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Maplebury Systems Limited on 27 August 2026.
