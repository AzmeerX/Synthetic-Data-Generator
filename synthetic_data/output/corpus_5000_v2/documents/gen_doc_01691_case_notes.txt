Case notes for Cresswell Systems Limited against Draycott Industries Limited

Date: 4 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01691.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Cresswell Systems Limited against Draycott Industries Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Cresswell Systems Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-142.

Gideon Orrick signed the Licence LEG-142.Gideon Quade is the chair of Cresswell Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent Cresswell Systems Limited GBP 26 million on 2 April 2023.

Draycott Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Cresswell Systems Limited.

Cresswell Systems Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 7 April 2023.

Gideon Vale is a shareholder of Cresswell Systems Limited.

Gideon Mercer is a family member of Gideon Orrick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
