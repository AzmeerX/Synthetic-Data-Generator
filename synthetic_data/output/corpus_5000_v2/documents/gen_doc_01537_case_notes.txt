Case notes for North Dunkeld Systems Limited against North Eversley Group Limited

Date: 5 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01537.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Dunkeld Systems Limited against North Eversley Group Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Dunkeld Systems Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-557.

Tamsin Orrick signed the Supply Agreement LEG-557.Tamsin Quade is the chair of North Dunkeld Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Uldale Trust Bank Limited lent North Dunkeld Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 16 October 2023.

North Eversley Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunkeld Systems Limited.

North Dunkeld Systems Limited owes Uldale Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 21 October 2023.

Tamsin Vale is a shareholder of North Dunkeld Systems Limited.

Tamsin Nyle is a family member of Tamsin Orrick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Dunkeld Systems Limited owns North Eversley Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Dunkeld Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Eversley Group Limited on 5 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that North Dunkeld Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
