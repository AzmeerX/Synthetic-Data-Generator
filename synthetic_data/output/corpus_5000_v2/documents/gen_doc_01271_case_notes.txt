Case notes for North Hampstead Systems Limited against North Inkberrow Group Limited

Date: 12 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01271.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Hampstead Systems Limited against North Inkberrow Group Limited in Bexhaven Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Hampstead Systems Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-317.

Oren Sable signed the Supply Agreement LEG-317.Oren Ulric is the chair of North Hampstead Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Trust Bank Limited lent North Hampstead Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 14 November 2025.

North Inkberrow Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Hampstead Systems Limited.

North Hampstead Systems Limited owes Carrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 19 November 2025.

Oren Ashby is a shareholder of North Hampstead Systems Limited.

Oren Rook is a family member of Oren Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Hampstead Systems Limited owns North Inkberrow Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Hampstead Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Inkberrow Group Limited on 12 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Zane Hale noted that a former employee alleged that North Hampstead Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
