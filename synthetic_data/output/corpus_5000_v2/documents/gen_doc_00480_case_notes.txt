Case notes for Uist Capital Limited against Uist Property Limited

Date: 18 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00480.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Uist Capital Limited against Uist Property Limited in Elmford County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Uist Capital Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-210.

Elias Orrick signed the Facility Agreement LEG-210.Freya Sable is the chair of Uist Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Commercial Bank Limited lent Uist Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 6 January 2023.

Uist Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Uist Capital Limited.

Uist Capital Limited owes Pember Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 11 January 2023.

Lorne Ward is a shareholder of Uist Capital Limited.

Nolan Quade is a family member of Elias Orrick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Uist Capital Limited owns Uist Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Uist Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Uist Property Limited on 18 September 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Uist Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
