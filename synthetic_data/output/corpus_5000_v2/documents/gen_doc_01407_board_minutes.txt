Board meeting minutes of North Southwold Group Limited

Date: 7 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01407.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Whitlock, Quin Yardley and Briar & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Southwold Group Limited owns North Southwold Logistics Limited.Quin Whitlock is the chair of North Southwold Group Limited.

Rhea Briar is a shareholder of North Southwold Group Limited.

Quin Tremayne is a family member of Quin Underwood.

Alder International Bank Limited lent North Southwold Group Limited GBP 46 million on 2 December 2024.

North Southwold Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Southwold Group Limited.

North Southwold Group Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 7 December 2024.

Alden & Sinclair LLP represents North Southwold Group Limited in the matter.

North Southwold Group Limited owns Lowmoor Warehouse.

Lowmoor Warehouse is located in Elmsworth Cross.

North Southwold Group Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-440.Quin Underwood signed the Lease LEG-440.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Marek Orrick, chair of North Southwold Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Southwold Capital Limited on 7 October 2024.

Marek Orrick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Southwold Capital Limited by North Southwold Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Southwold Capital Limited by North Southwold Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
