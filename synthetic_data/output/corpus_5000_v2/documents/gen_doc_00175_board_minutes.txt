Board meeting minutes of Vernon Energy Limited

Date: 26 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00175.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Wren Grenville, Wren Ingram and Alden & Jarvis LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.
