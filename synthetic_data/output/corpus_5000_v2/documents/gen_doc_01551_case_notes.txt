Case notes for North Fingal Holdings Limited against North Fingal Group Limited

Date: 29 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01551.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Fingal Holdings Limited against North Fingal Group Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Fingal Holdings Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-570.

Tamsin Cavendish signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-570.Tamsin Everard is the chair of North Fingal Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Commercial Bank Limited lent North Fingal Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 7 March 2024.

North Fingal Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fingal Holdings Limited.

North Fingal Holdings Limited owes Alder Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 12 March 2024.

Tamsin Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Fingal Holdings Limited.

Tamsin Balfour is a family member of Tamsin Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Fingal Holdings Limited owns Ironside Foundry.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Fingal Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Fingal Group Limited on 29 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that North Fingal Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
