Board meeting minutes of North Penhurst Trading Limited

Date: 30 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01050.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Villiers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Pell, Kellan Rook and Briar & Villiers LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Penhurst Trading Limited owns Ironside Dock.Kellan Pell is the chair of North Penhurst Trading Limited.

Kellan Ulric is a shareholder of North Penhurst Trading Limited.

Kellan Mercer is a family member of Kellan Nyle.

Nether Merchant Bank Limited lent North Penhurst Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 13 July 2024.

North Penhurst Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Penhurst Trading Limited.

North Penhurst Trading Limited owes Nether Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 18 July 2024.

Cairn & Mercer LLP represents North Penhurst Trading Limited in the matter.

North Penhurst Trading Limited owns Ironside Office Park.

Ironside Office Park is located in Inglemere Vale.

North Penhurst Trading Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-118.Kellan Nyle signed the Loan Note LEG-118.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Jeffreys, chair of North Penhurst Trading Limited, proposed to acquire North Penhurst Energy Limited on 30 June 2024.

Ulric Jeffreys confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Penhurst Energy Limited by North Penhurst Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Penhurst Energy Limited by North Penhurst Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
