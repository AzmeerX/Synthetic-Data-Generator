Case notes for Yarrowfield Holdings Limited against North Lowmoor Partners Limited

Date: 9 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00676.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yarrowfield Holdings Limited against North Lowmoor Partners Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yarrowfield Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-017.

Farah Orrick signed the Loan Note LEG-017.Tamsin Mercer is the chair of Yarrowfield Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Yarrowfield Holdings Limited GBP 44 million on 4 August 2022.

Brackenwold Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Yarrowfield Holdings Limited.

Yarrowfield Holdings Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 20 million as at 9 August 2022.

Rhea Rook is a shareholder of Yarrowfield Holdings Limited.

Tamsin Mercer is a family member of Farah Orrick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yarrowfield Holdings Limited owns Kestrel Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yarrowfield Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Lowmoor Partners Limited on 9 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Yarrowfield Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
