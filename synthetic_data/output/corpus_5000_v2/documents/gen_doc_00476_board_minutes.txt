Board meeting minutes of Uist Logistics Limited

Date: 21 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00476.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Alden LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nolan Quade, Xander Garrow and Briar & Alden LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Cairn & Pell LLP represents Uist Logistics Limited in the matter.

The acquisition of Uist Marine Limited by Uist Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
