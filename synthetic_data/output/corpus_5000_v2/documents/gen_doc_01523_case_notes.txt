Case notes for North Cavendish Ventures Limited against North Dunkeld Industries Limited

Date: 28 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01523.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Cavendish Ventures Limited against North Dunkeld Industries Limited in Fairmouth Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Cavendish Ventures Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-544.

Tamsin Alden signed the Side Letter LEG-544.Tamsin Cairn is the chair of North Cavendish Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Merchant Bank Limited lent North Cavendish Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 26 May 2023.

North Dunkeld Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cavendish Ventures Limited.

North Cavendish Ventures Limited owes Stan Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 31 May 2023.

Tamsin Hale is a shareholder of North Cavendish Ventures Limited.

Silas Zouch is a family member of Tamsin Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Cavendish Ventures Limited owns North Cavendish Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Cavendish Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Dunkeld Industries Limited on 28 December 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that North Cavendish Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
