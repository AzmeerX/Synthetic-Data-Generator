Case notes for Kintyre Capital Limited against Kintyre Property Limited

Date: 1 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00347.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kintyre Capital Limited against Kintyre Property Limited in Elmford Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kintyre Capital Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-090.

Zane Kingsley signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-090.Zane Montague is the chair of Kintyre Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Commercial Bank Limited lent Kintyre Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 21 January 2024.

Kintyre Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Kintyre Capital Limited.

Kintyre Capital Limited owes Red Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 26 January 2024.

Zane Sinclair is a shareholder of Kintyre Capital Limited.

Zane Jeffreys is a family member of Zane Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kintyre Capital Limited owns Kintyre Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kintyre Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kintyre Property Limited on 1 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Quade noted that a former employee alleged that Kintyre Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
