Case notes for North Trentvale Ventures Limited against North Underhill Industries Limited

Date: 24 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01110.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Trentvale Ventures Limited against North Underhill Industries Limited in Elmford County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Trentvale Ventures Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-172.

Lena Sable signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-172.Lena Ulric is the chair of North Trentvale Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent North Trentvale Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 27 February 2026.

North Underhill Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Trentvale Ventures Limited.

North Trentvale Ventures Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 4 March 2026.

Lena Ashby is a shareholder of North Trentvale Ventures Limited.

Lena Rook is a family member of Lena Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Trentvale Ventures Limited owns North Trentvale Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Trentvale Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Underhill Industries Limited on 24 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that North Trentvale Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
