Case notes for North Oakhurst Partners Limited against Lowmoor Marine Limited

Date: 14 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00718.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Oakhurst Partners Limited against Lowmoor Marine Limited in Glenport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Oakhurst Partners Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-425.

Elias Hale signed the Trust Deed LEG-425.Elias Jarvis is the chair of North Oakhurst Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Trust Bank Limited lent North Oakhurst Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 1 November 2024.

North Oakhurst Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Oakhurst Partners Limited.

North Oakhurst Partners Limited owes Harrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 6 November 2024.

Elias Pell is a shareholder of North Oakhurst Partners Limited.

Elias Garrow is a family member of Elias Hale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Oakhurst Partners Limited owns North Oakhurst Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Oakhurst Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Lowmoor Marine Limited on 14 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that North Oakhurst Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
