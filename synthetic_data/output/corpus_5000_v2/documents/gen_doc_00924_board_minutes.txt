Board meeting minutes of North Fallow Ventures Limited

Date: 14 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00924.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Zouch LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Yardley, Ivo Alden and Briar & Zouch LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Fallow Ventures Limited owns North Fallow Systems Limited.Hana Yardley is the chair of North Fallow Ventures Limited.

Ivo Ellery is a shareholder of North Fallow Ventures Limited.

Hana Villiers is a family member of Hana Whitlock.

Quill Merchant Bank Limited lent North Fallow Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 2 October 2025.

North Grimsdale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fallow Ventures Limited.

North Fallow Ventures Limited owes Quill Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 7 October 2025.

Marlowe & Reed LLP represents North Fallow Ventures Limited in the matter.

North Fallow Ventures Limited owns Dunhollow Wharf.

Dunhollow Wharf is located in Quintrell.

North Fallow Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-004.Hana Whitlock signed the Facility Agreement LEG-004.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Silas Hale, chair of North Fallow Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire North Grimsdale Logistics Limited on 14 May 2025.

Silas Hale confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Grimsdale Logistics Limited by North Fallow Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Grimsdale Logistics Limited by North Fallow Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
