Case notes for Selby Logistics Limited against Harrowden Group Limited

Date: 30 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00130.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Selby Logistics Limited against Harrowden Group Limited in Glenport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Selby Logistics Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-501.

Vera Neville signed the Security Agreement LEG-501.Vera Pemberton is the chair of Selby Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Bank Limited lent Selby Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 2 November 2022.

Selby Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Selby Logistics Limited.

Selby Logistics Limited owes Harrow Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 7 November 2022.

Vera Villiers is a shareholder of Selby Logistics Limited.

Vera Montague is a family member of Vera Neville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Selby Logistics Limited owns Selby Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Selby Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Harrowden Group Limited on 30 July 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Selby Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
