Case notes for North Juniper Holdings Limited against North Juniper Group Limited

Date: 18 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01285.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Juniper Holdings Limited against North Juniper Group Limited in Denmoor Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Juniper Holdings Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-330.

Oren Grenville signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-330.Oren Ingram is the chair of North Juniper Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent North Juniper Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 6 April 2026.

North Juniper Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Juniper Holdings Limited.

North Juniper Holdings Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 11 April 2026.

Oren Neville is a shareholder of North Juniper Holdings Limited.

Oren Fairfax is a family member of Oren Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Juniper Holdings Limited owns Fairmouth Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Juniper Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Juniper Group Limited on 18 September 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
