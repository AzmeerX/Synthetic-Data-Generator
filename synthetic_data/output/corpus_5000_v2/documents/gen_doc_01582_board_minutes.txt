Board meeting minutes of Stanbrook Energy Limited

Date: 1 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01582.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Hale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Nyle, Elias Quade and Cairn & Hale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Stanbrook Energy Limited owns Stanbrook Property Limited.Elias Nyle is the chair of Stanbrook Energy Limited.

Elias Thorne is a shareholder of Stanbrook Energy Limited.

Elias Kerr is a family member of Elias Lorne.

Fair Trust Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Energy Limited GBP 28 million on 3 December 2024.

Stanbrook Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Energy Limited.

Stanbrook Energy Limited owes Fair Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 8 December 2024.

Alden & Tremayne LLP represents Stanbrook Energy Limited in the matter.

Stanbrook Energy Limited owns Eastmere Wharf.

Eastmere Wharf is located in Trentvale.

Stanbrook Energy Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-043.Elias Lorne signed the Debenture LEG-043.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Ellery, chair of Stanbrook Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Tarrow Holdings Limited on 1 November 2024.

Daria Ellery confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Tarrow Holdings Limited by Stanbrook Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Tarrow Holdings Limited by Stanbrook Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
