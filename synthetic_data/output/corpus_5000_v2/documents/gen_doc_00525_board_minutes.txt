Board meeting minutes of Yorkshire Systems Limited

Date: 16 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00525.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Yarrow & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Jeffreys, Amina Langford and Yarrow & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Yorkshire Systems Limited owns North Ashcombe Holdings Limited.Amina Jeffreys is the chair of Yorkshire Systems Limited.

Amina Osborne is a shareholder of Yorkshire Systems Limited.

Amina Grenville is a family member of Amina Hastings.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Yorkshire Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 1 April 2024.

North Ashcombe Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Yorkshire Systems Limited.

Yorkshire Systems Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 6 April 2024.

Alden & Cavendish LLP represents Yorkshire Systems Limited in the matter.

Yorkshire Systems Limited owns Bexhaven Foundry.

Bexhaven Foundry is located in Redmoor.

Yorkshire Systems Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-251.Amina Hastings signed the Debenture LEG-251.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Juno Whitlock, chair of Yorkshire Systems Limited, proposed to acquire North Ashcombe Trading Limited on 16 April 2024.

Juno Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Ashcombe Trading Limited by Yorkshire Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Ashcombe Trading Limited by Yorkshire Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
