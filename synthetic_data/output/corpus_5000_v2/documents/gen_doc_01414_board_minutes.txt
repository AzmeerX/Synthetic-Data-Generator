Board meeting minutes of North Southwold Energy Limited

Date: 25 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01414.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Everard LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Cairn, Rhea Ellery and Briar & Everard LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
