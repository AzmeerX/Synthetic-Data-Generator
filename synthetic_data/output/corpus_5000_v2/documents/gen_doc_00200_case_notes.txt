Case notes for Yelverton Capital Limited against Yelverton Property Limited

Date: 19 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00200.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yelverton Capital Limited against Yelverton Property Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yelverton Capital Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-564.

Xander Briar signed the Board Resolution LEG-564.Xander Dunmore is the chair of Yelverton Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Commercial Bank Limited lent Yelverton Capital Limited GBP 30 million on 25 September 2024.

Yelverton Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Yelverton Capital Limited.

Yelverton Capital Limited owes Red Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 30 September 2024.

Xander Jarvis is a shareholder of Yelverton Capital Limited.

Xander Alden is a family member of Xander Briar.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yelverton Capital Limited owns Yelverton Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yelverton Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yelverton Property Limited on 19 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Yelverton Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
