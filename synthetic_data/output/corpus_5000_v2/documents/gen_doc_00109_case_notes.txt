Case notes for Quill Property Limited against Quill Systems Limited

Date: 5 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00109.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quill Property Limited against Quill Systems Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quill Property Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-483.

Vera Ulric signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-483.Vera Ward is the chair of Quill Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Bank Limited lent Quill Property Limited GBP 28 million on 18 April 2022.

Quill Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Quill Property Limited.

Quill Property Limited owes Elm Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 23 April 2022.

Vera Cavendish is a shareholder of Quill Property Limited.

Vera Thorne is a family member of Vera Ulric.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quill Property Limited owns Quill Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
