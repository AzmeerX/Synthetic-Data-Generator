Facility agreement between Alder Bank Limited and Silverbeck Holdings Limited

Date: 28 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00008.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Alder Bank Limited and the borrower is Silverbeck Holdings Limited.

Silverbeck Holdings Limited owns Ironside Industries Limited.Northbank Bank Limited lent Silverbeck Holdings Limited GBP 28 million on 8 May 2022.

Elderglen Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Silverbeck Holdings Limited.

Silverbeck Holdings Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 13 May 2022.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Silverbeck Holdings Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.

Lorne Vale signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Silverbeck Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Glenmoor Logistics Limited on 28 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
