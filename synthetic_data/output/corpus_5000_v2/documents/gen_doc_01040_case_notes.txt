Case notes for North Overdale Marine Limited against Oakhurst Holdings Limited

Date: 21 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01040.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Overdale Marine Limited against Oakhurst Holdings Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Overdale Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-109.

Kellan Dunmore signed the Supply Agreement LEG-109.Kellan Fenwick is the chair of North Overdale Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low Private Bank Limited lent North Overdale Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 5 April 2024.

North Overdale Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Overdale Marine Limited.

North Overdale Marine Limited owes Low Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 10 April 2024.

Kellan Kerr is a shareholder of North Overdale Marine Limited.

Kellan Cairn is a family member of Kellan Dunmore.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Overdale Marine Limited owns North Overdale Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Overdale Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Oakhurst Holdings Limited on 21 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Zell noted that a former employee alleged that North Overdale Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
