Board meeting minutes of Langton Industries Limited

Date: 3 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00035.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Ulric LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Ellery, Ulric Garrow and Cairn & Ulric LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Langton Industries Limited owns Langton Group Limited.Ulric Ellery is the chair of Langton Industries Limited.

Ulric Jarvis is a shareholder of Langton Industries Limited.

Ulric Briar is a family member of Ulric Cairn.

Oak Private Bank Limited lent Langton Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 25 November 2024.

Langton Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Langton Industries Limited.

Langton Industries Limited owes Oak Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 30 November 2024.

Cairn & Hale LLP represents Langton Industries Limited in the matter.

Langton Industries Limited owns Jarrow Cross Wharf.

Jarrow Cross Wharf is located in Harrowden Bay.

Langton Industries Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-415.Ulric Cairn signed the Power of Attorney LEG-415.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Kerr, chair of Langton Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Langton Capital Limited on 3 October 2024.

Bram Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Langton Capital Limited by Langton Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Langton Capital Limited by Langton Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
