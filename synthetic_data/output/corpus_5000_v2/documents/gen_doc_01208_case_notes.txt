Case notes for North Denholm Logistics Limited against North Denholm Partners Limited

Date: 28 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01208.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Denholm Logistics Limited against North Denholm Partners Limited in Fairmouth Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Denholm Logistics Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-261.

Nadia Kerr signed the Security Agreement LEG-261.Nadia Mercer is the chair of North Denholm Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Bank Limited lent North Denholm Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 8 March 2024.

North Denholm Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Denholm Logistics Limited.

North Denholm Logistics Limited owes Pember Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 13 March 2024.

Nadia Rook is a shareholder of North Denholm Logistics Limited.

Nadia Ivers is a family member of Nadia Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Denholm Logistics Limited owns North Denholm Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Denholm Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Denholm Partners Limited on 28 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Xander Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that North Denholm Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
