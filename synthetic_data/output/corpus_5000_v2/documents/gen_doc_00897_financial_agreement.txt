Facility agreement between Kel Private Bank Limited and North Draycott Systems Limited

Date: 6 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00897.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Kel Private Bank Limited and the borrower is North Draycott Systems Limited.

North Draycott Systems Limited owns North Eastmoor Holdings Limited.Merri Trust Bank Limited lent North Draycott Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 22 January 2025.

North Eastmoor Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Draycott Systems Limited.

North Draycott Systems Limited owes Merri Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 27 January 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Draycott Systems Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-587.

Hana Yarrow signed the Debenture LEG-587.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Draycott Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Eastmoor Holdings Limited on 6 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
