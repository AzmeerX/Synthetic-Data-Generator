Case notes for Culm Industries Limited against Culm Trading Limited

Date: 21 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00235.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Culm Industries Limited against Culm Trading Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Culm Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-595.

Xander Ingram signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-595.Xander Kingsley is the chair of Culm Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent Culm Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 1 September 2025.

Culm Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Culm Industries Limited.

Culm Industries Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 6 September 2025.

Xander Pemberton is a shareholder of Culm Industries Limited.

Xander Hastings is a family member of Xander Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Culm Industries Limited owns Culm Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Culm Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Culm Trading Limited on 21 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Alden noted that a former employee alleged that Culm Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
