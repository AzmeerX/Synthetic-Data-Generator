Facility agreement between Denmoor Bank Limited and North Gorse Industries Limited

Date: 26 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01247.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

The lender is Denmoor Bank Limited and the borrower is North Gorse Industries Limited.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
