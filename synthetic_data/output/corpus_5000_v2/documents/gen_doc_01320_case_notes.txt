Case notes for Quintrell Industries Limited against North Linden Ventures Limited

Date: 21 May 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01320.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quintrell Industries Limited against North Linden Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quintrell Industries Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-012.

Daria Fairfax signed the Board Resolution LEG-012.Daria Hastings is the chair of Quintrell Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Quintrell Industries Limited GBP 46 million on 28 December 2023.

Quintrell Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Quintrell Industries Limited.

Quintrell Industries Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 2 January 2024.

Daria Montague is a shareholder of Quintrell Industries Limited.

Daria Everard is a family member of Daria Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quintrell Industries Limited owns Quintrell Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Quintrell Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Linden Ventures Limited on 21 May 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Anika Hale noted that a former employee alleged that Quintrell Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
