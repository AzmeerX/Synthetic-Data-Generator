Case notes for North Grimsdale Group Limited against North Grimsdale Partners Limited

Date: 11 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00928.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Grimsdale Group Limited against North Grimsdale Partners Limited in Elmford High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Grimsdale Group Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.

Ivo Alden signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.Ivo Cairn is the chair of North Grimsdale Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill International Bank Limited lent North Grimsdale Group Limited GBP 22 million on 15 November 2025.

North Grimsdale Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grimsdale Group Limited.

North Grimsdale Group Limited owes Quill International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 20 November 2025.

Ivo Ivers is a shareholder of North Grimsdale Group Limited.

Hana Zouch is a family member of Ivo Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Grimsdale Group Limited owns North Grimsdale Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Grimsdale Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Grimsdale Partners Limited on 11 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Silas Lorne noted that a former employee alleged that North Grimsdale Group Limited misstated its revenue.
