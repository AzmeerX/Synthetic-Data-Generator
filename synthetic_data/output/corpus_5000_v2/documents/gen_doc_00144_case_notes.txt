Case notes for Harrowden Logistics Limited against Tavistock Capital Limited

Date: 5 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00144.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Harrowden Logistics Limited against Tavistock Capital Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Harrowden Logistics Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-112.

Bastian Yarrow signed the Side Letter LEG-112.Bastian Ashby is the chair of Harrowden Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent Harrowden Logistics Limited GBP 34 million on 14 June 2025.

Harrowden Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Logistics Limited.

Harrowden Logistics Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 19 June 2025.

Bastian Fairfax is a shareholder of Harrowden Logistics Limited.

Bastian Ward is a family member of Bastian Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Harrowden Logistics Limited owns Greyhaven Estate.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Harrowden Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tavistock Capital Limited on 5 November 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Harrowden Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
