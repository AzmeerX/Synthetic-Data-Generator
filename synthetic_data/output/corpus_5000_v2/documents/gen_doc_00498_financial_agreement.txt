Facility agreement between Kel Merchant Bank Limited and Vale Systems Limited

Date: 22 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00498.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Kel Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Vale Systems Limited.

Vale Systems Limited owns Wear Holdings Limited.Stan Trust Bank Limited lent Vale Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 12 July 2023.

Wear Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Vale Systems Limited.

Vale Systems Limited owes Stan Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 17 July 2023.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
