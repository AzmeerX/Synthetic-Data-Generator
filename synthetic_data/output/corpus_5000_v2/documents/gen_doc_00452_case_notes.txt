Case notes for Skye Capital Limited against Jarrow Marine Limited

Date: 6 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00452.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Skye Capital Limited against Jarrow Marine Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Skye Capital Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-186.

Lorne Cairn signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-186.Kellan Lorne is the chair of Skye Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low Commercial Bank Limited lent Skye Capital Limited GBP 46 million on 17 April 2022.

Skye Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Skye Capital Limited.

Skye Capital Limited owes Low Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 22 April 2022.

Daria Garrow is a shareholder of Skye Capital Limited.

Ines Alden is a family member of Lorne Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Skye Capital Limited owns Skye Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Skye Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Jarrow Marine Limited on 6 March 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ivo Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Skye Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
