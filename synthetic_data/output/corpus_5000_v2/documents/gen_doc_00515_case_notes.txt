Case notes for Yorkshire Group Limited against Yorkshire Partners Limited

Date: 6 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00515.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yorkshire Group Limited against Yorkshire Partners Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yorkshire Group Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-242.

Amina Yarrow signed the Facility Agreement LEG-242.Amina Ashby is the chair of Yorkshire Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Yorkshire Group Limited GBP 38 million on 24 December 2023.

Yorkshire Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Yorkshire Group Limited.

Yorkshire Group Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 29 December 2023.

Amina Fairfax is a shareholder of Yorkshire Group Limited.

Amina Ward is a family member of Amina Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yorkshire Group Limited owns Yorkshire Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yorkshire Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yorkshire Partners Limited on 6 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Yorkshire Group Limited misstated its revenue.
