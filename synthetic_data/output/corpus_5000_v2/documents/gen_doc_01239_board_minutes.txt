Board meeting minutes of North Farnley Capital Limited

Date: 31 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01239.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ingram LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Pemberton, Nadia Sinclair and Alden & Ingram LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Farnley Capital Limited owns North Farnley Marine Limited.Nadia Pemberton is the chair of North Farnley Capital Limited.

Nadia Villiers is a shareholder of North Farnley Capital Limited.

Nadia Montague is a family member of Nadia Neville.

Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Farnley Capital Limited GBP 22 million on 30 December 2024.

North Farnley Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Farnley Capital Limited.

North Farnley Capital Limited owes Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 4 January 2025.

Alden & Ulric LLP represents North Farnley Capital Limited in the matter.

North Farnley Capital Limited owns Denmoor Silo.

Denmoor Silo is located in Harrowden Green.

North Farnley Capital Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-288.Nadia Neville signed the Side Letter LEG-288.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Ashby, chair of North Farnley Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Farnley Ventures Limited on 31 October 2024.

Yara Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Farnley Ventures Limited by North Farnley Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Farnley Ventures Limited by North Farnley Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
