Case notes for North Osterley Group Limited against North Osterley Partners Limited

Date: 21 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01355.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Osterley Group Limited against North Osterley Partners Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Osterley Group Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-392.
