Case notes for North Gorse Trading Limited against North Gorse Marine Limited

Date: 16 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01250.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Gorse Trading Limited against North Gorse Marine Limited in Fairmouth Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Gorse Trading Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-298.

Nadia Yardley signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-298.Oren Alden is the chair of North Gorse Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent North Gorse Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 19 April 2025.

North Gorse Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Gorse Trading Limited.

North Gorse Trading Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 24 April 2025.

Oren Fenwick is a shareholder of North Gorse Trading Limited.

Nadia Wyndham is a family member of Nadia Yardley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Gorse Trading Limited owns Denmoor Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Gorse Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Gorse Marine Limited on 16 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Langford noted that a former employee alleged that North Gorse Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
