Board meeting minutes of North Redmoor Ventures Limited

Date: 26 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00763.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Delacourt LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Briar, Farah Dunmore and Briar & Delacourt LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Redmoor Ventures Limited owns North Redmoor Systems Limited.Farah Briar is the chair of North Redmoor Ventures Limited.

Farah Garrow is a shareholder of North Redmoor Ventures Limited.

Elias Yardley is a family member of Elias Zouch.

Oak Merchant Bank Limited lent North Redmoor Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 26 January 2026.

North Stanbrook Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Redmoor Ventures Limited.

North Redmoor Ventures Limited owes Oak Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 31 January 2026.

Cairn & Orrick LLP represents North Redmoor Ventures Limited in the matter.

North Redmoor Ventures Limited owns Merrivale Rail Depot.

Merrivale Rail Depot is located in Elmstead.

North Redmoor Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-466.Elias Zouch signed the Facility Agreement LEG-466.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Ravenscroft, chair of North Redmoor Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire North Stanbrook Group Limited on 26 July 2025.

Oren Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Stanbrook Group Limited by North Redmoor Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Stanbrook Group Limited by North Redmoor Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
