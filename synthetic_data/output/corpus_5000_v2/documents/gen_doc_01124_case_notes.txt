Case notes for North Underhill Ventures Limited against North Vicarage Industries Limited

Date: 30 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01124.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Underhill Ventures Limited against North Vicarage Industries Limited in Glenport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Underhill Ventures Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-184.

Lena Fairfax signed the Lease LEG-184.Lena Hastings is the chair of North Underhill Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent North Underhill Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 9 July 2026.

North Vicarage Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Underhill Ventures Limited.

North Underhill Ventures Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 14 July 2026.

Lena Montague is a shareholder of North Underhill Ventures Limited.

Lena Everard is a family member of Lena Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Underhill Ventures Limited owns North Underhill Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Underhill Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Vicarage Industries Limited on 30 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Wren Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that North Underhill Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
