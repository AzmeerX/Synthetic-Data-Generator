Case notes for North Quarrymere Group Limited against North Quarrymere Partners Limited

Date: 15 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01061.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quarrymere Group Limited against North Quarrymere Partners Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quarrymere Group Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-128.

Kellan Yarrow signed the Side Letter LEG-128.Kellan Ashby is the chair of North Quarrymere Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak International Bank Limited lent North Quarrymere Group Limited GBP 22 million on 31 October 2024.

North Quarrymere Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quarrymere Group Limited.

North Quarrymere Group Limited owes Oak International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 5 November 2024.

Kellan Fairfax is a shareholder of North Quarrymere Group Limited.

Kellan Ward is a family member of Kellan Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quarrymere Group Limited owns North Quarrymere Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quarrymere Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quarrymere Partners Limited on 15 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that North Quarrymere Group Limited misstated its revenue.
