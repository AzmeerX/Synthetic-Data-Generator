Facility agreement between Kel International Bank Limited and North Norwood Ventures Limited

Date: 11 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01030.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Kel International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Norwood Ventures Limited.

North Norwood Ventures Limited owns North Norwood Systems Limited.Kel Merchant Bank Limited lent North Norwood Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 28 December 2023.

North Overdale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norwood Ventures Limited.

North Norwood Ventures Limited owes Kel Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 2 January 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Norwood Ventures Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-100.

Juno Underwood signed the Board Resolution LEG-100.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Norwood Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Overdale Holdings Limited on 11 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
