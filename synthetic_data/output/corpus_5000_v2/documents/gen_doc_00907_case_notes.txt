Case notes for North Eastmoor Marine Limited against Netherby Holdings Limited

Date: 15 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00907.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Eastmoor Marine Limited against Netherby Holdings Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Eastmoor Marine Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-595.

Hana Grenville signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-595.Hana Ingram is the chair of North Eastmoor Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Private Bank Limited lent North Eastmoor Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 20 April 2025.

North Eastmoor Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eastmoor Marine Limited.

North Eastmoor Marine Limited owes Nether Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 25 April 2025.

Hana Neville is a shareholder of North Eastmoor Marine Limited.

Hana Fairfax is a family member of Hana Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Eastmoor Marine Limited owns North Eastmoor Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Eastmoor Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Netherby Holdings Limited on 15 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Pemberton noted that a former employee alleged that North Eastmoor Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
