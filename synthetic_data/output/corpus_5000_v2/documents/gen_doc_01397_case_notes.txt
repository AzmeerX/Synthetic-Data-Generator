Case notes for North Ravensby Partners Limited against North Ravensby Marine Limited

Date: 29 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01397.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ravensby Partners Limited against North Ravensby Marine Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ravensby Partners Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-431.

Quin Kingsley signed the Power of Attorney LEG-431.Quin Montague is the chair of North Ravensby Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent North Ravensby Partners Limited GBP 28 million on 25 August 2024.

North Ravensby Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ravensby Partners Limited.

North Ravensby Partners Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 30 August 2024.

Quin Sinclair is a shareholder of North Ravensby Partners Limited.

Quin Jeffreys is a family member of Quin Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ravensby Partners Limited owns North Ravensby Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ravensby Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Ravensby Marine Limited on 29 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Lorne noted that a former employee alleged that North Ravensby Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
