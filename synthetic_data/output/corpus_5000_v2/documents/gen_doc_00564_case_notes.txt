Case notes for North Carrow Systems Limited against North Dunmere Holdings Limited

Date: 14 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00564.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Carrow Systems Limited against North Dunmere Holdings Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Carrow Systems Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-287.

Bastian Sable signed the Power of Attorney LEG-287.Bastian Ulric is the chair of North Carrow Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Trust Bank Limited lent North Carrow Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 2 May 2025.

North Dunmere Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Carrow Systems Limited.

North Carrow Systems Limited owes Glen Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 7 May 2025.

Bastian Ashby is a shareholder of North Carrow Systems Limited.

Bastian Rook is a family member of Bastian Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Carrow Systems Limited owns North Dunmere Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Carrow Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Dunmere Holdings Limited on 14 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Langford noted that a former employee alleged that North Carrow Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
