Case notes for North Draycott Marine Limited against North Draycott Ventures Limited

Date: 9 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00893.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Draycott Marine Limited against North Draycott Ventures Limited in Fairmouth Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Draycott Marine Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-583.

Hana Thorne signed the Services Agreement LEG-583.Hana Vale is the chair of North Draycott Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low Private Bank Limited lent North Draycott Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 9 December 2024.

North Draycott Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Draycott Marine Limited.

North Draycott Marine Limited owes Low Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 14 December 2024.

Hana Balfour is a shareholder of North Draycott Marine Limited.

Hana Sable is a family member of Hana Thorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Draycott Marine Limited owns North Draycott Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Draycott Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Draycott Ventures Limited on 9 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Balfour noted that a former employee alleged that North Draycott Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
