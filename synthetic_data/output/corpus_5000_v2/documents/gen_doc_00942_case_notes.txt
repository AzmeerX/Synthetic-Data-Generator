Case notes for North Hazelmere Logistics Limited against North Hazelmere Partners Limited

Date: 17 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00942.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Hazelmere Logistics Limited against North Hazelmere Partners Limited in Glenport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Hazelmere Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-021.

Ivo Pell signed the Services Agreement LEG-021.Ivo Rook is the chair of North Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Bank Limited lent North Hazelmere Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 7 April 2026.

North Hazelmere Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

North Hazelmere Logistics Limited owes Tarrow Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 12 April 2026.

Ivo Ward is a shareholder of North Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

Ivo Nyle is a family member of Ivo Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
