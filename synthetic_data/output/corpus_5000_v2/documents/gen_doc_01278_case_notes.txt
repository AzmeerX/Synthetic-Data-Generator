Case notes for North Inkberrow Partners Limited against North Inkberrow Energy Limited

Date: 31 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01278.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Inkberrow Partners Limited against North Inkberrow Energy Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Inkberrow Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-323.

Oren Zell signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-323.Oren Balfour is the chair of North Inkberrow Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Trust Bank Limited lent North Inkberrow Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 19 January 2026.

North Inkberrow Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Inkberrow Partners Limited.

North Inkberrow Partners Limited owes Denmoor Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 24 January 2026.

Oren Grenville is a shareholder of North Inkberrow Partners Limited.

Oren Yarrow is a family member of Oren Zell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Inkberrow Partners Limited owns North Inkberrow Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Inkberrow Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Inkberrow Energy Limited on 31 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Zane Orrick noted that a former employee alleged that North Inkberrow Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
