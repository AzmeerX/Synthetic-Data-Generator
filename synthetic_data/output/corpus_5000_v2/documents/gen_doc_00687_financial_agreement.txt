Facility agreement between Red International Bank Limited and North Merrivale Industries Limited

Date: 10 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00687.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Red International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Merrivale Industries Limited.

North Merrivale Industries Limited owns North Merrivale Group Limited.Carrow Private Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 29 December 2023.

North Merrivale Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Industries Limited.

North Merrivale Industries Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 3 January 2024.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
