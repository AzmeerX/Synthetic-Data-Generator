Board meeting minutes of Vellacott Ventures Limited

Date: 22 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01624.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Delacourt LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Cairn, Farah Ellery and Alden & Delacourt LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Vellacott Ventures Limited owns Vellacott Systems Limited.Farah Cairn is the chair of Vellacott Ventures Limited.

Farah Hale is a shareholder of Vellacott Ventures Limited.

Elias Zouch is a family member of Farah Alden.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent Vellacott Ventures Limited GBP 24 million on 25 January 2026.

Westerby Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Ventures Limited.

Vellacott Ventures Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 30 January 2026.

Alden & Grenville LLP represents Vellacott Ventures Limited in the matter.

Vellacott Ventures Limited owns Fenwick Bay Office Park.

Fenwick Bay Office Park is located in Carrow Hill.

Vellacott Ventures Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-081.Farah Alden signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-081.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Wyndham, chair of Vellacott Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Westerby Group Limited on 22 August 2025.

Daria Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Westerby Group Limited by Vellacott Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Westerby Group Limited by Vellacott Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
