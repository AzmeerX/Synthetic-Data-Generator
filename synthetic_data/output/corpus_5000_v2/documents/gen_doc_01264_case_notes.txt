Case notes for North Hampstead Partners Limited against North Hampstead Marine Limited

Date: 24 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01264.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Hampstead Partners Limited against North Hampstead Marine Limited in Alderport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Hampstead Partners Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-311.

Oren Mercer signed the Services Agreement LEG-311.Oren Orrick is the chair of North Hampstead Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent North Hampstead Partners Limited GBP 28 million on 9 September 2025.

North Hampstead Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Hampstead Partners Limited.

North Hampstead Partners Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 14 September 2025.

Oren Thorne is a shareholder of North Hampstead Partners Limited.

Oren Lorne is a family member of Oren Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Hampstead Partners Limited owns North Hampstead Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Hampstead Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Hampstead Marine Limited on 24 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Zane Alden noted that a former employee alleged that North Hampstead Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
