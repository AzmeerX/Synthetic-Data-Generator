Press release concerning Yelverton Energy Limited

Publication date: 2 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00202.

1. Corporate statement

Yelverton Energy Limited today issued the following statement.

Yelverton Energy Limited owns Quintrell Data Centre.

Xander Lorne is a shareholder of Yelverton Energy Limited.

Xander Cairn is a family member of Xander Dunmore.

Yelverton Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Yelverton Energy Limited.

Yelverton Energy Limited owes Red International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 22 October 2024.
