Board meeting minutes of Ulverston Marine Limited

Date: 4 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00161.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Garrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Wren Sable, Wren Ulric and Alden & Garrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Ulverston Marine Limited owns Ulverston Energy Limited.Wren Sable is the chair of Ulverston Marine Limited.

Wren Yarrow is a shareholder of Ulverston Marine Limited.

Wren Pell is a family member of Wren Quade.

Ulverston Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Ulverston Marine Limited.

Ulverston Marine Limited owes Low Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 11 September 2023.

Briar & Pemberton LLP represents Ulverston Marine Limited in the matter.

Ulverston Marine Limited owns Oakhurst Mill.

Oakhurst Mill is located in Bexley.

Ulverston Marine Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-529.Wren Quade signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-529.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Harrowden Partners Limited by Ulverston Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Harrowden Partners Limited by Ulverston Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
