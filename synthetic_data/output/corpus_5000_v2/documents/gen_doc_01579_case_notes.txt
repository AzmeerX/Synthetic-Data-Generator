Case notes for Stanbrook Partners Limited against Stanbrook Energy Limited

Date: 11 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01579.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Stanbrook Partners Limited against Stanbrook Energy Limited in Alderport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Stanbrook Partners Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-040.

Elias Ivers signed the Lease LEG-040.Elias Kerr is the chair of Stanbrook Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm International Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Partners Limited GBP 22 million on 31 October 2024.

Stanbrook Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Partners Limited.

Stanbrook Partners Limited owes Elm International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 5 November 2024.

Elias Quade is a shareholder of Stanbrook Partners Limited.

Elias Hale is a family member of Elias Ivers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Stanbrook Partners Limited owns Stanbrook Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Stanbrook Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Stanbrook Energy Limited on 11 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Stanbrook Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
