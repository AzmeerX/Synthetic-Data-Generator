Board meeting minutes of North Dunmere Holdings Limited

Date: 4 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00567.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Lorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Vale, Bastian Yarrow and Alden & Lorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Dunmere Holdings Limited owns Carrow Bay Office Park.Bastian Vale is the chair of North Dunmere Holdings Limited.

Bastian Balfour is a shareholder of North Dunmere Holdings Limited.

Bastian Sable is a family member of Bastian Thorne.

Glen Commercial Bank Limited lent North Dunmere Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 13 May 2025.

North Dunmere Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Dunmere Holdings Limited.

North Dunmere Holdings Limited owes Glen Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 18 May 2025.

Briar & Pell LLP represents North Dunmere Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Dunmere Holdings Limited owns Denmoor Cold Store.

Denmoor Cold Store is located in Gorse.

North Dunmere Holdings Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-288.Bastian Thorne signed the Side Letter LEG-288.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Osborne, chair of North Dunmere Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Dunmere Partners Limited on 4 February 2025.

Kellan Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Dunmere Partners Limited by North Dunmere Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Dunmere Partners Limited by North Dunmere Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
