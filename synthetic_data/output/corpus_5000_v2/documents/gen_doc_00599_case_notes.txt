Case notes for Kelbrook Energy Limited against North Fairhaven Energy Limited

Date: 16 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00599.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kelbrook Energy Limited against North Fairhaven Energy Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kelbrook Energy Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-027.

Calder Orrick signed the Trust Deed LEG-027.Calder Quade is the chair of Kelbrook Energy Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kelbrook Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Kelbrook Energy Limited.

Kelbrook Energy Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 13 September 2026.

Calder Vale is a shareholder of Kelbrook Energy Limited.

Calder Nyle is a family member of Calder Orrick.4. Findings and allegations
