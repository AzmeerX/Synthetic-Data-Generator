Board meeting minutes of Merrivale Marine Limited

Date: 24 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00854.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Neville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Osborne, Calder Ravenscroft and Alden & Neville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Merrivale Marine Limited owns Eastmere Fleet.Calder Osborne is the chair of Merrivale Marine Limited.

Calder Underwood is a shareholder of Merrivale Marine Limited.

Calder Langford is a family member of Calder Montague.

Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent Merrivale Marine Limited GBP 42 million on 22 September 2022.

Merrivale Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Marine Limited.

Merrivale Marine Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 27 September 2022.

Alden & Ulric LLP represents Merrivale Marine Limited in the matter.

Merrivale Marine Limited owns Eastmere Hangar.

Eastmere Hangar is located in Eastmere.

Merrivale Marine Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-050.Calder Montague signed the Facility Agreement LEG-050.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Quin Kingsley, chair of Merrivale Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Barrowfield Holdings Limited on 24 April 2027.

Quin Kingsley confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Barrowfield Holdings Limited by Merrivale Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Barrowfield Holdings Limited by Merrivale Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
