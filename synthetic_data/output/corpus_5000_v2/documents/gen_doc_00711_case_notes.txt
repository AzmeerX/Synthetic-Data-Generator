Case notes for North Netherby Ventures Limited against North Oakhurst Industries Limited

Date: 27 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00711.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Netherby Ventures Limited against North Oakhurst Industries Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Netherby Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-418.

Elias Alden signed the Facility Agreement LEG-418.Elias Cairn is the chair of North Netherby Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Merchant Bank Limited lent North Netherby Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 16 August 2024.

North Oakhurst Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Netherby Ventures Limited.

North Netherby Ventures Limited owes Glen Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 21 August 2024.

Elias Hale is a shareholder of North Netherby Ventures Limited.

Daria Zouch is a family member of Elias Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Netherby Ventures Limited owns North Netherby Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Netherby Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Oakhurst Industries Limited on 27 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Neville noted that a former employee alleged that North Netherby Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
