Case notes for North Ravensby Systems Limited against North Southwold Group Limited

Date: 16 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01404.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ravensby Systems Limited against North Southwold Group Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ravensby Systems Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-437.

Quin Ravenscroft signed the Security Agreement LEG-437.Quin Tremayne is the chair of North Ravensby Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent North Ravensby Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 30 October 2024.

North Southwold Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ravensby Systems Limited.

North Ravensby Systems Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 4 November 2024.

Quin Yardley is a shareholder of North Ravensby Systems Limited.

Quin Pemberton is a family member of Quin Ravenscroft.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ravensby Systems Limited owns North Southwold Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ravensby Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Southwold Group Limited on 16 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Mira Mercer noted that a former employee alleged that North Ravensby Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
