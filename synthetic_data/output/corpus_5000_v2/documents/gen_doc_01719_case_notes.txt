Case notes for Fallow Holdings Limited against Fallow Logistics Limited

Date: 5 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01719.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Fallow Holdings Limited against Fallow Logistics Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Fallow Holdings Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-167.

Gideon Osborne signed the Services Agreement LEG-167.Gideon Ravenscroft is the chair of Fallow Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Bank Limited lent Fallow Holdings Limited GBP 36 million on 2 January 2024.

Fallow Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Fallow Holdings Limited.

Fallow Holdings Limited owes Alder Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 7 January 2024.

Gideon Whitlock is a shareholder of Fallow Holdings Limited.

Gideon Neville is a family member of Gideon Osborne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Fallow Holdings Limited owns Fallow Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Fallow Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Fallow Logistics Limited on 5 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that Fallow Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
