Board meeting minutes of North Inkberrow Energy Limited

Date: 21 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01281.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Balfour LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Everard, Oren Grenville and Briar & Balfour LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Inkberrow Energy Limited owns Fairmouth Data Centre.Oren Everard is the chair of North Inkberrow Energy Limited.

Oren Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Inkberrow Energy Limited.

Oren Balfour is a family member of Oren Cavendish.

Denmoor International Bank Limited lent North Inkberrow Energy Limited GBP 18 million on 21 February 2026.

North Inkberrow Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Inkberrow Energy Limited.

North Inkberrow Energy Limited owes Denmoor International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 26 February 2026.

Briar & Jarvis LLP represents North Inkberrow Energy Limited in the matter.

North Inkberrow Energy Limited owns Fairmouth Works.

Fairmouth Works is located in Merrivale Bridge.

North Inkberrow Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-326.Oren Cavendish signed the Loan Note LEG-326.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Zane Rook, chair of North Inkberrow Energy Limited, proposed to acquire North Juniper Industries Limited on 21 August 2025.

Zane Rook confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Juniper Industries Limited by North Inkberrow Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Juniper Industries Limited by North Inkberrow Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
