Board meeting minutes of Dartmoor Partners Limited

Date: 18 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00252.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Montague LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Yara Briar, Yara Dunmore and Briar & Montague LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Dartmoor Partners Limited owns Dartmoor Capital Limited.Yara Briar is the chair of Dartmoor Partners Limited.

Yara Garrow is a shareholder of Dartmoor Partners Limited.

Xander Yardley is a family member of Xander Zouch.

Denmoor Trust Bank Limited lent Dartmoor Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 24 February 2026.

Dartmoor Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Dartmoor Partners Limited.

Dartmoor Partners Limited owes Denmoor Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 1 March 2026.

Alden & Ravenscroft LLP represents Dartmoor Partners Limited in the matter.

Dartmoor Partners Limited owns Calderport Works.

Calderport Works is located in Jarrow Bay.

Dartmoor Partners Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-005.Xander Zouch signed the Facility Agreement LEG-005.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Sable, chair of Dartmoor Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Dartmoor Property Limited on 18 August 2025.

Elias Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Dartmoor Property Limited by Dartmoor Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Dartmoor Property Limited by Dartmoor Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
