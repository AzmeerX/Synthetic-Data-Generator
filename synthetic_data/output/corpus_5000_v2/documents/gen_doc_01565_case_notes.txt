Case notes for North Grafton Holdings Limited against North Grafton Logistics Limited

Date: 5 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01565.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Grafton Holdings Limited against North Grafton Logistics Limited in Fairmouth Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Grafton Holdings Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-582.

Tamsin Osborne signed the Loan Note LEG-582.Tamsin Ravenscroft is the chair of North Grafton Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Grafton Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 17 July 2024.

North Grafton Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grafton Holdings Limited.

North Grafton Holdings Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 22 July 2024.

Tamsin Whitlock is a shareholder of North Grafton Holdings Limited.

Tamsin Neville is a family member of Tamsin Osborne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Grafton Holdings Limited owns Valebrook Farm.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Grafton Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Grafton Logistics Limited on 5 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Montague noted that a former employee alleged that North Grafton Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
