Case notes for North Belmont Ventures Limited against North Carrow Industries Limited

Date: 8 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00550.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Belmont Ventures Limited against North Carrow Industries Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Belmont Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-274.

Bastian Fenwick signed the Facility Agreement LEG-274.Bastian Hale is the chair of North Belmont Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent North Belmont Ventures Limited GBP 22 million on 10 December 2024.

North Carrow Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Belmont Ventures Limited.

North Belmont Ventures Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 15 December 2024.

Bastian Mercer is a shareholder of North Belmont Ventures Limited.

Bastian Ellery is a family member of Bastian Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Belmont Ventures Limited owns North Belmont Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Belmont Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Carrow Industries Limited on 8 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Ward noted that a former employee alleged that North Belmont Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
