Board meeting minutes of North Juniper Property Limited

Date: 27 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01295.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Pemberton LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Sinclair, Oren Underwood and Briar & Pemberton LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
