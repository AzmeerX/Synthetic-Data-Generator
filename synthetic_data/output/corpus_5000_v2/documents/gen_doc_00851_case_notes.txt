Case notes for North Aldergate Partners Limited against Merrivale Marine Limited

Date: 3 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00851.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Aldergate Partners Limited against Merrivale Marine Limited in Glenport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Aldergate Partners Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-545.

Gideon Grenville signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-545.Gideon Ingram is the chair of North Aldergate Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Trust Bank Limited lent North Aldergate Partners Limited GBP 44 million on 18 October 2023.

North Aldergate Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Aldergate Partners Limited.

North Aldergate Partners Limited owes Fair Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 23 October 2023.

Gideon Neville is a shareholder of North Aldergate Partners Limited.

Gideon Fairfax is a family member of Gideon Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Aldergate Partners Limited owns North Aldergate Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Aldergate Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Merrivale Marine Limited on 3 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Quin Hastings noted that a former employee alleged that North Aldergate Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
