Board meeting minutes of North Glenmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 25 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00609.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ellery LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Kerr, Calder Mercer and Briar & Ellery LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Glenmoor Logistics Limited owns North Glenmoor Trading Limited.Calder Kerr is the chair of North Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

Calder Pell is a shareholder of North Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

Calder Hale is a family member of Calder Ivers.

Nether Bank Limited lent North Glenmoor Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 16 July 2026.

North Glenmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

North Glenmoor Logistics Limited owes Nether Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 21 July 2026.

Cairn & Fenwick LLP represents North Glenmoor Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Glenmoor Logistics Limited owns Fairmouth Terminal.

Fairmouth Terminal is located in Elmsworth Port.

North Glenmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-327.Calder Ivers signed the Services Agreement LEG-327.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Lena Hastings, chair of North Glenmoor Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Glenmoor Marine Limited on 25 November 2025.

Lena Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Glenmoor Marine Limited by North Glenmoor Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Glenmoor Marine Limited by North Glenmoor Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
