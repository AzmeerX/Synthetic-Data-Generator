Case notes for Merrivale Energy Limited against North Barrowfield Energy Limited

Date: 27 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00865.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Merrivale Energy Limited against North Barrowfield Energy Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Merrivale Energy Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-051.

Calder Neville signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-051.Calder Pemberton is the chair of Merrivale Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent Merrivale Energy Limited GBP 44 million on 3 October 2022.

Merrivale Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Energy Limited.

Merrivale Energy Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 8 October 2022.

Calder Villiers is a shareholder of Merrivale Energy Limited.

Calder Montague is a family member of Calder Neville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Merrivale Energy Limited owns Merrivale Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Merrivale Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Barrowfield Energy Limited on 27 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Quin Whitlock noted that a former employee alleged that Merrivale Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
