Case notes for North Norwood Capital Limited against North Norwood Property Limited

Date: 28 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01026.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Norwood Capital Limited against North Norwood Property Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Norwood Capital Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-096.

Juno Pemberton signed the Side Letter LEG-096.Juno Sinclair is the chair of North Norwood Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Jarrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Norwood Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 14 November 2023.

North Norwood Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norwood Capital Limited.

North Norwood Capital Limited owes Jarrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 19 November 2023.

Juno Wyndham is a shareholder of North Norwood Capital Limited.

Juno Osborne is a family member of Juno Pemberton.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Norwood Capital Limited owns North Norwood Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Norwood Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Norwood Property Limited on 28 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Kerr noted that a former employee alleged that North Norwood Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
