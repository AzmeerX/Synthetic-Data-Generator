Board meeting minutes of North Draycott Logistics Limited

Date: 11 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00889.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Rook, Hana Thorne and Briar & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Draycott Logistics Limited owns North Draycott Trading Limited.Hana Rook is the chair of North Draycott Logistics Limited.

Hana Ward is a shareholder of North Draycott Logistics Limited.

Hana Orrick is a family member of Hana Pell.

Low Bank Limited lent North Draycott Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 26 October 2024.

North Draycott Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Draycott Logistics Limited.

North Draycott Logistics Limited owes Low Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 31 October 2024.

Briar & Whitlock LLP represents North Draycott Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Draycott Logistics Limited owns Larkspur Quay.

Larkspur Quay is located in Netherby Moor.

North Draycott Logistics Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-579.Hana Pell signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-579.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Ward, chair of North Draycott Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Draycott Energy Limited on 11 September 2024.

Rhea Ward confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Draycott Energy Limited by North Draycott Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Draycott Energy Limited by North Draycott Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
