Case notes for North Eastmoor Holdings Limited against North Eastmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 27 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00900.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Eastmoor Holdings Limited against North Eastmoor Logistics Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Eastmoor Holdings Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-588.

Hana Zell signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-588.Hana Balfour is the chair of North Eastmoor Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Commercial Bank Limited lent North Eastmoor Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 2 February 2025.

North Eastmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eastmoor Holdings Limited.

North Eastmoor Holdings Limited owes Merri Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 7 February 2025.

Hana Grenville is a shareholder of North Eastmoor Holdings Limited.

Hana Yarrow is a family member of Hana Zell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Eastmoor Holdings Limited owns Quarrystone Mill.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Eastmoor Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Eastmoor Logistics Limited on 27 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Ingram noted that a former employee alleged that North Eastmoor Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
