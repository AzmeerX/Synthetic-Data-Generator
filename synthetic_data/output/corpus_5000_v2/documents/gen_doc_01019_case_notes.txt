Case notes for North Norwood Holdings Limited against North Norwood Group Limited

Date: 10 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01019.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Norwood Holdings Limited against North Norwood Group Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Norwood Holdings Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-090.

Juno Jeffreys signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-090.Juno Langford is the chair of North Norwood Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Commercial Bank Limited lent North Norwood Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 9 September 2023.

North Norwood Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norwood Holdings Limited.

North Norwood Holdings Limited owes Inver Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 14 September 2023.

Juno Ravenscroft is a shareholder of North Norwood Holdings Limited.

Juno Ingram is a family member of Juno Jeffreys.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Norwood Holdings Limited owns Harrowgate Foundry.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Norwood Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Norwood Group Limited on 10 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Dunmore noted that a former employee alleged that North Norwood Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
