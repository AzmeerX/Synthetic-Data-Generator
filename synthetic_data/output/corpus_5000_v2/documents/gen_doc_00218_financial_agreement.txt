Facility agreement between Harrow Commercial Bank Limited and Alderney Systems Limited

Date: 23 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00218.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Harrow Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is Alderney Systems Limited.

Alderney Systems Limited owns Borrowdale Holdings Limited.Uldale Trust Bank Limited lent Alderney Systems Limited GBP 24 million on 31 March 2025.

Borrowdale Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Alderney Systems Limited.

Alderney Systems Limited owes Uldale Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 5 April 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Alderney Systems Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-581.

Xander Thorne signed the Security Agreement LEG-581.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Alderney Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Harrowden Ventures Limited on 23 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
