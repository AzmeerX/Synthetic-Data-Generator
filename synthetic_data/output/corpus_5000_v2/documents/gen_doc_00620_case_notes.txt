Case notes for North Harrowden Industries Limited against North Harrowden Logistics Limited

Date: 10 February 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00620.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Harrowden Industries Limited against North Harrowden Logistics Limited in Fairmouth Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Harrowden Industries Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-337.

Calder Sable signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-337.Calder Ulric is the chair of North Harrowden Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Private Bank Limited lent North Harrowden Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 9 March 2022.

North Harrowden Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Harrowden Industries Limited.

North Harrowden Industries Limited owes Oak Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 14 March 2022.

Calder Ashby is a shareholder of North Harrowden Industries Limited.

Calder Rook is a family member of Calder Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Harrowden Industries Limited owns North Harrowden Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Harrowden Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Harrowden Logistics Limited on 10 February 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that North Harrowden Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
