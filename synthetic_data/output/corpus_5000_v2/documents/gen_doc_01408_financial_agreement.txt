Facility agreement between Harrow International Bank Limited and North Southwold Logistics Limited

Date: 14 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01408.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Harrow International Bank Limited and the borrower is North Southwold Logistics Limited.

North Southwold Logistics Limited owns North Southwold Trading Limited.Bexley Bank Limited lent North Southwold Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 13 December 2024.

North Southwold Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Southwold Logistics Limited.

North Southwold Logistics Limited owes Bexley Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 18 December 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Southwold Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-441.

Quin Villiers signed the Trust Deed LEG-441.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Southwold Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Southwold Partners Limited on 14 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
