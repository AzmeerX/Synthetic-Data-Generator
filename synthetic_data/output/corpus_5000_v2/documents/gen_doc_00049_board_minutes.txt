Board meeting minutes of Marlowe Group Limited

Date: 9 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00049.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Nyle LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Rook, Ulric Thorne and Alden & Nyle LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Marlowe Group Limited owns Marlowe Logistics Limited.Ulric Rook is the chair of Marlowe Group Limited.

Ulric Ward is a shareholder of Marlowe Group Limited.

Ulric Orrick is a family member of Ulric Pell.

Quill International Bank Limited lent Marlowe Group Limited GBP 38 million on 17 April 2025.

Marlowe Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Marlowe Group Limited.

Marlowe Group Limited owes Quill International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 22 April 2025.

Cairn & Vale LLP represents Marlowe Group Limited in the matter.

Marlowe Group Limited owns Jarrow Cross Silo.

Jarrow Cross Silo is located in Inglemere Green.

Marlowe Group Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-428.Ulric Pell signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-428.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Nyle, chair of Marlowe Group Limited, proposed to acquire Marlowe Marine Limited on 9 January 2025.

Amina Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Marlowe Marine Limited by Marlowe Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Marlowe Marine Limited by Marlowe Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
