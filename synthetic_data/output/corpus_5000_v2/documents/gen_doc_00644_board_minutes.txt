Board meeting minutes of North Inglemere Ventures Limited

Date: 28 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00644.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Osborne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Ravenscroft, Calder Tremayne and Briar & Osborne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Calder Neville is a family member of Calder Osborne.

North Inglemere Ventures Limited owns Glenport Silo.

Glenport Silo is located in Inglemere Cross.

North Inglemere Ventures Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-358.Calder Osborne signed the Loan Note LEG-358.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of North Jarrow Logistics Limited by North Inglemere Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
