Board meeting minutes of North Netherby Holdings Limited

Date: 11 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00700.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Orrick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Sinclair, Daria Underwood and Alden & Orrick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Netherby Holdings Limited owns Invermere Office Park.Daria Sinclair is the chair of North Netherby Holdings Limited.

Daria Wyndham is a shareholder of North Netherby Holdings Limited.

Daria Osborne is a family member of Daria Pemberton.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent North Netherby Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 28 April 2024.

North Netherby Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Netherby Holdings Limited.

North Netherby Holdings Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 May 2024.

Briar & Fenwick LLP represents North Netherby Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Netherby Holdings Limited owns Jarrow Cross Cold Store.

Jarrow Cross Cold Store is located in Ironside.

North Netherby Holdings Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-408.Daria Pemberton signed the Lease LEG-408.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Cavendish, chair of North Netherby Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Netherby Partners Limited on 11 May 2024.

Nadia Cavendish confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Netherby Partners Limited by North Netherby Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Netherby Partners Limited by North Netherby Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
