Case notes for Vernon Partners Limited against Vernon Energy Limited

Date: 5 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00172.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vernon Partners Limited against Vernon Energy Limited in Fairmouth Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vernon Partners Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-539.

Wren Balfour signed the Debenture LEG-539.Wren Delacourt is the chair of Vernon Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Trust Bank Limited lent Vernon Partners Limited GBP 20 million on 25 December 2023.

Vernon Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Vernon Partners Limited.

Vernon Partners Limited owes Nether Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 30 December 2023.

Wren Ingram is a shareholder of Vernon Partners Limited.

Wren Ashby is a family member of Wren Balfour.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vernon Partners Limited owns Vernon Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vernon Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vernon Energy Limited on 5 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Vernon Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
