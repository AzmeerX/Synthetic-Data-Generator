Case notes for Ormsby Marine Limited against Ormsby Ventures Limited

Date: 21 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00081.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ormsby Marine Limited against Ormsby Ventures Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ormsby Marine Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-457.

Ulric Underwood signed the Trust Deed LEG-457.Ulric Whitlock is the chair of Ormsby Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Ormsby Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Ormsby Marine Limited.

Ormsby Marine Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 7 March 2026.

Vera Briar is a shareholder of Ormsby Marine Limited.

Ulric Tremayne is a family member of Ulric Underwood.4. Findings and allegations
