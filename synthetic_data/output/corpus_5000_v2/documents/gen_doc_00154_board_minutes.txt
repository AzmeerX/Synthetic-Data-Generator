Board meeting minutes of Ulverston Industries Limited

Date: 14 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00154.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Wren Mercer, Wren Orrick and Cairn & Jarvis LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
