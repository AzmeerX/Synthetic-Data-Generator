Case notes for North Redmoor Marine Limited against North Redmoor Ventures Limited

Date: 5 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00760.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Redmoor Marine Limited against North Redmoor Ventures Limited in Glenport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Redmoor Marine Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-463.

Elias Whitlock signed the Power of Attorney LEG-463.Elias Yardley is the chair of North Redmoor Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Private Bank Limited lent North Redmoor Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 24 December 2025.

North Redmoor Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Redmoor Marine Limited.

North Redmoor Marine Limited owes Nether Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 29 December 2025.

Farah Dunmore is a shareholder of North Redmoor Marine Limited.

Elias Villiers is a family member of Elias Whitlock.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Redmoor Marine Limited owns North Redmoor Energy Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Redmoor Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Redmoor Ventures Limited on 5 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Neville noted that a former employee alleged that North Redmoor Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
