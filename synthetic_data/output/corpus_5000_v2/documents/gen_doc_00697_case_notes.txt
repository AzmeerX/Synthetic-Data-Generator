Case notes for North Merrivale Ventures Limited against North Netherby Holdings Limited

Date: 20 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00697.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Merrivale Ventures Limited against North Netherby Holdings Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Merrivale Ventures Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-406.

Daria Neville signed the Loan Note LEG-406.Daria Pemberton is the chair of North Merrivale Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 6 April 2024.

North Netherby Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Ventures Limited.

North Merrivale Ventures Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 11 April 2024.

Daria Villiers is a shareholder of North Merrivale Ventures Limited.

Daria Montague is a family member of Daria Neville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Merrivale Ventures Limited owns North Merrivale Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Merrivale Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Netherby Holdings Limited on 20 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that North Merrivale Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
