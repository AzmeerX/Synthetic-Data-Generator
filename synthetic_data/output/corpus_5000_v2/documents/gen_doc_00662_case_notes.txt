Date: 1 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00662.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Kelbrook Logistics Limited against Lowmoor Group Limited in Elmford Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

North Kelbrook Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-375.
