Board meeting minutes of Redmoor Holdings Limited

Date: 9 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01442.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Wyndham LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Tremayne, Daria Villiers and Briar & Wyndham LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Redmoor Holdings Limited owns Redmoor Industries Limited.Daria Tremayne is the chair of Redmoor Holdings Limited.

Daria Yardley is a shareholder of Redmoor Holdings Limited.

Daria Pemberton is a family member of Daria Ravenscroft.

Carrow Bank Limited lent Redmoor Holdings Limited GBP 28 million on 27 April 2024.

Redmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Holdings Limited.

Redmoor Holdings Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 2 May 2024.

Alden & Yarrow LLP represents Redmoor Holdings Limited in the matter.

Redmoor Holdings Limited owns Calderport Office Park.

Calderport Office Park is located in Yarmouth.

Redmoor Holdings Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-023.Daria Ravenscroft signed the Lease LEG-023.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Orrick, chair of Redmoor Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Vinehall Group Limited on 9 June 2025.

Amina Orrick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Vinehall Group Limited by Redmoor Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Vinehall Group Limited by Redmoor Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
