Board meeting minutes of Borrowdale Trading Limited

Date: 3 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00224.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Balfour, Xander Delacourt and Briar & Jarvis LLP.

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
