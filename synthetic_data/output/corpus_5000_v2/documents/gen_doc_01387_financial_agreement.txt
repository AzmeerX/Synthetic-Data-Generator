Facility agreement between Elm Trust Bank Limited and Quintrell Marine Limited

Date: 20 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01387.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Trust Bank Limited and the borrower is Quintrell Marine Limited.

Quintrell Marine Limited owns Calderport Marina.Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent Quintrell Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 3 March 2024.

Quintrell Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Quintrell Marine Limited.

Quintrell Marine Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 8 March 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Quintrell Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-018.

Daria Langford signed the Loan Note LEG-018.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Quintrell Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quenington Ventures Limited on 20 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
