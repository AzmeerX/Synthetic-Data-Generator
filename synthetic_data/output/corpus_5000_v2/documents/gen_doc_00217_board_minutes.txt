Board meeting minutes of Alderney Ventures Limited

Date: 16 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00217.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Cairn LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Ulric, Xander Ward and Briar & Cairn LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Alderney Ventures Limited owns Alderney Systems Limited.Xander Ulric is the chair of Alderney Ventures Limited.

Xander Ashby is a shareholder of Alderney Ventures Limited.

Xander Rook is a family member of Xander Sable.

Uldale Merchant Bank Limited lent Alderney Ventures Limited GBP 22 million on 20 March 2025.

Borrowdale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Alderney Ventures Limited.

Alderney Ventures Limited owes Uldale Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 25 March 2025.

Alden & Kerr LLP represents Alderney Ventures Limited in the matter.

Alderney Ventures Limited owns Kestrel Wharf.

Kestrel Wharf is located in Fairhaven Cross.

Alderney Ventures Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-580.Xander Sable signed the Board Resolution LEG-580.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Hastings, chair of Alderney Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Borrowdale Group Limited on 16 December 2024.

Daria Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Borrowdale Group Limited by Alderney Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Borrowdale Group Limited by Alderney Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
