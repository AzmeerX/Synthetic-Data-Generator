Case notes for Fermanagh Ventures Limited against Gower Industries Limited

Date: 30 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00284.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Fermanagh Ventures Limited against Gower Industries Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Fermanagh Ventures Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-034.

Yara Everard signed the Facility Agreement LEG-034.Yara Grenville is the chair of Fermanagh Ventures Limited.

Yara Langford is a shareholder of Fermanagh Ventures Limited.
