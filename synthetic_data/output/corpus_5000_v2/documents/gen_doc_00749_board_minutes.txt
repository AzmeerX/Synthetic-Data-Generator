Board meeting minutes of North Quintrell Property Limited

Date: 19 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00749.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Orrick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Neville, Elias Pemberton and Briar & Orrick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quintrell Property Limited owns North Quintrell Ventures Limited.Elias Neville is the chair of North Quintrell Property Limited.

Elias Tremayne is a shareholder of North Quintrell Property Limited.

Elias Kingsley is a family member of Elias Langford.

Merri Bank Limited lent North Quintrell Property Limited GBP 20 million on 5 September 2025.

North Quintrell Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quintrell Property Limited.

North Quintrell Property Limited owes Merri Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 10 September 2025.

Cairn & Alden LLP represents North Quintrell Property Limited in the matter.

North Quintrell Property Limited owns Lowmoor Hangar.

Lowmoor Hangar is located in Penhurst.

North Quintrell Property Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-453.Elias Langford signed the Security Agreement LEG-453.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Cavendish, chair of North Quintrell Property Limited, proposed to acquire Lowmoor Ventures Limited on 19 April 2025.

Oren Cavendish confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Lowmoor Ventures Limited by North Quintrell Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Lowmoor Ventures Limited by North Quintrell Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
