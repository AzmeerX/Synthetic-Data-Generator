Facility agreement between Elm Private Bank Limited and North Pemberton Logistics Limited

Date: 30 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00729.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Private Bank Limited and the borrower is North Pemberton Logistics Limited.

North Pemberton Logistics Limited owns North Pemberton Trading Limited.Jarrow Bank Limited lent North Pemberton Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 19 February 2025.

North Pemberton Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Pemberton Logistics Limited.

North Pemberton Logistics Limited owes Jarrow Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 24 February 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
