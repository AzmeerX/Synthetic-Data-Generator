Board meeting minutes of Lorne Logistics Limited

Date: 10 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00357.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Mercer LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Zane Whitlock, Zane Yardley and Briar & Mercer LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
