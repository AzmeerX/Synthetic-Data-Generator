Case notes for Barrowfield Trading Limited against Barrowfield Capital Limited

Date: 10 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01670.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Barrowfield Trading Limited against Barrowfield Capital Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Barrowfield Trading Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-123.

Farah Underwood signed the Debenture LEG-123.Farah Whitlock is the chair of Barrowfield Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent Barrowfield Trading Limited GBP 28 million on 5 September 2022.

Barrowfield Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Barrowfield Trading Limited.

Barrowfield Trading Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 10 September 2022.

Gideon Briar is a shareholder of Barrowfield Trading Limited.

Farah Tremayne is a family member of Farah Underwood.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Barrowfield Trading Limited owns Barrowfield Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Barrowfield Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Barrowfield Capital Limited on 10 July 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Barrowfield Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
