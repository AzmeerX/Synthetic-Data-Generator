Board meeting minutes of North Fairhaven Group Limited

Date: 19 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00595.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Osborne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Wyndham, Bastian Zouch and Alden & Osborne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Fairhaven Group Limited owns North Fairhaven Logistics Limited.Bastian Wyndham is the chair of North Fairhaven Group Limited.

Calder Cairn is a shareholder of North Fairhaven Group Limited.

Bastian Underwood is a family member of Bastian Villiers.

Kel International Bank Limited lent North Fairhaven Group Limited GBP 22 million on 23 February 2026.

North Fairhaven Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fairhaven Group Limited.

North Fairhaven Group Limited owes Kel International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 28 February 2026.

Briar & Ravenscroft LLP represents North Fairhaven Group Limited in the matter.

North Fairhaven Group Limited owns Elmford Quay.

Elmford Quay is located in Carrow Green.

North Fairhaven Group Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-314.Bastian Villiers signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-314.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Lena Sable, chair of North Fairhaven Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Fairhaven Capital Limited on 19 August 2025.

Lena Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Fairhaven Capital Limited by North Fairhaven Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Fairhaven Capital Limited by North Fairhaven Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
