Board meeting minutes of Kintyre Property Limited

Date: 22 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00350.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Zane Pemberton, Zane Sinclair and Briar & Fenwick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Kintyre Property Limited owns Kintyre Ventures Limited.Zane Pemberton is the chair of Kintyre Property Limited.

Zane Villiers is a shareholder of Kintyre Property Limited.

Zane Montague is a family member of Zane Neville.

Stan Bank Limited lent Kintyre Property Limited GBP 20 million on 23 February 2024.

Kintyre Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Kintyre Property Limited.

Kintyre Property Limited owes Stan Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 28 February 2024.

Quade & Thorne LLP represents Kintyre Property Limited in the matter.

Kintyre Property Limited owns Harrowgate Terminal.

Harrowgate Terminal is located in Penhurst.

Kintyre Property Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-093.Zane Neville signed the Supply Agreement LEG-093.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Thorne, chair of Kintyre Property Limited, proposed to acquire Inglemere Ventures Limited on 22 March 2024.

Gideon Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Inglemere Ventures Limited by Kintyre Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Inglemere Ventures Limited by Kintyre Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
