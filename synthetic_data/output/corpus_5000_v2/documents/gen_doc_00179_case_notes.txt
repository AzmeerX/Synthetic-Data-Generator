Case notes for Vernon Systems Limited against Wilton Group Limited

Date: 25 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00179.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vernon Systems Limited against Wilton Group Limited in Glenport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vernon Systems Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-545.

Wren Hastings signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-545.Wren Jeffreys is the chair of Vernon Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Trust Bank Limited lent Vernon Systems Limited GBP 32 million on 29 February 2024.

Wilton Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Vernon Systems Limited.

Vernon Systems Limited owes Oak Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 5 March 2024.

Wren Osborne is a shareholder of Vernon Systems Limited.

Wren Grenville is a family member of Wren Hastings.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vernon Systems Limited owns Wilton Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vernon Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Wilton Group Limited on 25 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Vernon Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
