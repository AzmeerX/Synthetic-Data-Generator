Case notes for Lorne Industries Limited against Lorne Logistics Limited

Date: 19 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00354.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Lorne Industries Limited against Lorne Logistics Limited in Glenport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Lorne Industries Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-097.

Zane Sinclair signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-097.Zane Underwood is the chair of Lorne Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Private Bank Limited lent Lorne Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 7 April 2024.

Lorne Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Lorne Industries Limited.

Lorne Industries Limited owes Stan Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 12 April 2024.

Zane Zouch is a shareholder of Lorne Industries Limited.

Zane Ravenscroft is a family member of Zane Sinclair.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Lorne Industries Limited owns Lorne Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Lorne Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Lorne Logistics Limited on 19 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Lorne Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
