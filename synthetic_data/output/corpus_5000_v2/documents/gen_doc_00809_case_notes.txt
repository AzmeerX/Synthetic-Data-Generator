Case notes for North Vellacott Logistics Limited against North Vellacott Partners Limited

Date: 13 June 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00809.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Vellacott Logistics Limited against North Vellacott Partners Limited in Glenport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Vellacott Logistics Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-507.

Farah Sinclair signed the Debenture LEG-507.Farah Underwood is the chair of North Vellacott Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent North Vellacott Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 26 August 2022.

North Vellacott Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Vellacott Logistics Limited.

North Vellacott Logistics Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 31 August 2022.

Farah Zouch is a shareholder of North Vellacott Logistics Limited.

Farah Ravenscroft is a family member of Farah Sinclair.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Vellacott Logistics Limited owns North Vellacott Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Vellacott Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Vellacott Partners Limited on 13 June 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
