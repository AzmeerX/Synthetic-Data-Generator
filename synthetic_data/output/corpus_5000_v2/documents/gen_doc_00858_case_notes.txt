Case notes for North Aldergate Systems Limited against North Barrowfield Group Limited

Date: 7 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00858.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Aldergate Systems Limited against North Barrowfield Group Limited in Alderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Aldergate Systems Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-551.

Gideon Montague signed the Services Agreement LEG-551.Gideon Osborne is the chair of North Aldergate Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Trust Bank Limited lent North Aldergate Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 23 December 2023.

North Barrowfield Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Aldergate Systems Limited.

North Aldergate Systems Limited owes Glen Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 28 December 2023.

Gideon Underwood is a shareholder of North Aldergate Systems Limited.

Gideon Langford is a family member of Gideon Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Aldergate Systems Limited owns North Barrowfield Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Aldergate Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Barrowfield Group Limited on 7 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Quin Osborne noted that a former employee alleged that North Aldergate Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
