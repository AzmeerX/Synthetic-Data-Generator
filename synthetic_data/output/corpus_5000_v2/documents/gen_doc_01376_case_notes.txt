Case notes for Quintrell Capital Limited against North Quenington Holdings Limited

Date: 4 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01376.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quintrell Capital Limited against North Quenington Holdings Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quintrell Capital Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-017.

Daria Kingsley signed the Loan Note LEG-017.Daria Montague is the chair of Quintrell Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Bank Limited lent Quintrell Capital Limited GBP 16 million on 21 February 2024.

Quintrell Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Quintrell Capital Limited.

Quintrell Capital Limited owes Bexley Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 26 February 2024.

Daria Sinclair is a shareholder of Quintrell Capital Limited.

Daria Jeffreys is a family member of Daria Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quintrell Capital Limited owns Quintrell Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Quintrell Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Quenington Holdings Limited on 4 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bram Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Quintrell Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
