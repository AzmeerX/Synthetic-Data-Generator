Facility agreement between Uldale Trust Bank Limited and North Trentvale Industries Limited

Date: 15 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01100.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Uldale Trust Bank Limited and the borrower is North Trentvale Industries Limited.

North Trentvale Industries Limited owns North Trentvale Group Limited.Northbank Bank Limited lent North Trentvale Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 20 November 2025.

North Trentvale Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Trentvale Industries Limited.

North Trentvale Industries Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 25 November 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Trentvale Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.

Lena Ivers signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

North Trentvale Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Trentvale Trading Limited on 15 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
