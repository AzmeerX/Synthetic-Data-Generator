Case notes for North Quenington Ventures Limited against North Ravensby Industries Limited

Date: 10 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01390.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Quenington Ventures Limited against North Ravensby Industries Limited in Glenport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Quenington Ventures Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-424.

Quin Delacourt signed the Lease LEG-424.Quin Fairfax is the chair of North Quenington Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Uldale Merchant Bank Limited lent North Quenington Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 9 June 2024.

North Ravensby Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quenington Ventures Limited.

North Quenington Ventures Limited owes Uldale Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 14 June 2024.

Quin Kingsley is a shareholder of North Quenington Ventures Limited.

Quin Cavendish is a family member of Quin Delacourt.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Quenington Ventures Limited owns North Quenington Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Quenington Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Ravensby Industries Limited on 10 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ines Hale noted that a former employee alleged that North Quenington Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
