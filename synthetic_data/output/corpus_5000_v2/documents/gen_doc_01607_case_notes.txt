Case notes for Uldale Capital Limited against Uldale Property Limited

Date: 25 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01607.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Uldale Capital Limited against Uldale Property Limited in Elmford Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Uldale Capital Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-065.

Elias Jeffreys signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-065.Elias Langford is the chair of Uldale Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Uldale Capital Limited GBP 32 million on 2 August 2025.

Uldale Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Capital Limited.

Uldale Capital Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 7 August 2025.

Elias Ravenscroft is a shareholder of Uldale Capital Limited.

Elias Ingram is a family member of Elias Jeffreys.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Uldale Capital Limited owns Uldale Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Uldale Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Uldale Property Limited on 25 April 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Fairfax noted that a former employee alleged that Uldale Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
