Case notes for Jarrow Logistics Limited against Pembroke Capital Limited

Date: 16 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00410.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Jarrow Logistics Limited against Pembroke Capital Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Jarrow Logistics Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-010.

Bastian Wyndham signed the Board Resolution LEG-010.Bastian Zouch is the chair of Jarrow Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Jarrow Logistics Limited GBP 42 million on 5 March 2026.

Jarrow Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Jarrow Logistics Limited.

Jarrow Logistics Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 10 March 2026.

Calder Ellery is a shareholder of Jarrow Logistics Limited.

Bastian Whitlock is a family member of Bastian Wyndham.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Jarrow Logistics Limited owns Calderport Terminal.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Jarrow Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Pembroke Capital Limited on 16 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Hana Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Jarrow Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
