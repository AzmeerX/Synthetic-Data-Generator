Board meeting minutes of Skye Group Limited

Date: 6 February 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00448.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Petra Hale, Lorne Cairn and Alden & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Skye Group Limited owns Skye Logistics Limited.Petra Hale is the chair of Skye Group Limited.

Lorne Vale is a shareholder of Skye Group Limited.

Ines Hale is a family member of Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department.

Kel International Bank Limited lent Skye Group Limited GBP 38 million on 4 March 2022.

Skye Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Skye Group Limited.

Skye Group Limited owes Kel International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 9 March 2022.

Briar & Osborne LLP represents Skye Group Limited in the matter.

Skye Group Limited owns Larkspur Dock.

Larkspur Dock is located in Inglemere Green.

Skye Group Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-182.Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department signed the Loan Note LEG-182.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ivo Thorne, chair of Skye Group Limited, proposed to acquire Skye Marine Limited on 6 February 2026.

Ivo Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Skye Marine Limited by Skye Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Skye Marine Limited by Skye Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
