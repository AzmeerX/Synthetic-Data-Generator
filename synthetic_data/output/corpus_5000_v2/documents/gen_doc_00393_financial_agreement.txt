Facility agreement between Oak Private Bank Limited and Orkney Holdings Limited

Date: 17 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00393.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Oak Private Bank Limited and the borrower is Orkney Holdings Limited.

Orkney Holdings Limited owns Ironside Office Park.Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent Orkney Holdings Limited GBP 18 million on 27 April 2025.

Orkney Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Orkney Holdings Limited.

Orkney Holdings Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 2 May 2025.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
