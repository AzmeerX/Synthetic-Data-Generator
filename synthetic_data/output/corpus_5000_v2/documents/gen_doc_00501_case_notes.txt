Case notes for Wear Industries Limited against Wear Trading Limited

Date: 12 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00501.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Wear Industries Limited against Wear Trading Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Wear Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-229.

Amina Jarvis signed the Security Agreement LEG-229.Amina Mercer is the chair of Wear Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Private Bank Limited lent Wear Industries Limited GBP 12 million on 3 August 2023.

Wear Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Wear Industries Limited.

Wear Industries Limited owes Stan Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 8 August 2023.

Amina Rook is a shareholder of Wear Industries Limited.

Amina Ivers is a family member of Amina Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Wear Industries Limited owns Wear Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Wear Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Wear Trading Limited on 12 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Juno Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Wear Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
