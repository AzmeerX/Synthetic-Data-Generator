Facility agreement between Red Bank Limited and Glenmoor Trading Limited

Date: 4 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00022.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Red Bank Limited and the borrower is Glenmoor Trading Limited.

Glenmoor Trading Limited owns Glenmoor Partners Limited.Eastmere Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Trading Limited GBP 12 million on 13 February 2025.

Glenmoor Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Trading Limited.

Glenmoor Trading Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 18 February 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Glenmoor Trading Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-101.

Bastian Mercer signed the Security Agreement LEG-101.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Glenmoor Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kendal Trading Limited on 4 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
