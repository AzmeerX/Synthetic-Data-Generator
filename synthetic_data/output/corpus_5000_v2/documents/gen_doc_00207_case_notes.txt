Case notes for Alderney Industries Limited against Harrowden Property Limited

Date: 7 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00207.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Alderney Industries Limited against Harrowden Property Limited in Elmford Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Alderney Industries Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-571.

Xander Jarvis signed the Debenture LEG-571.Xander Lorne is the chair of Alderney Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Private Bank Limited lent Alderney Industries Limited GBP 44 million on 11 December 2024.

Alderney Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Alderney Industries Limited.

Alderney Industries Limited owes Stan Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 16 December 2024.

Xander Quade is a shareholder of Alderney Industries Limited.

Xander Ivers is a family member of Xander Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Alderney Industries Limited owns Alderney Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Alderney Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Harrowden Property Limited on 7 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Alderney Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
