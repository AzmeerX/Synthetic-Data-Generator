Case notes for North Cresswell Capital Limited against North Cresswell Property Limited

Date: 3 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00879.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Cresswell Capital Limited against North Cresswell Property Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Cresswell Capital Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-570.

Hana Garrow signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-570.Hana Ivers is the chair of North Cresswell Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Jarrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Cresswell Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 19 July 2024.

North Cresswell Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cresswell Capital Limited.

North Cresswell Capital Limited owes Jarrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 24 July 2024.

Hana Nyle is a shareholder of North Cresswell Capital Limited.

Hana Fenwick is a family member of Hana Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Cresswell Capital Limited owns North Cresswell Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Cresswell Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Cresswell Property Limited on 3 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Kerr noted that a former employee alleged that North Cresswell Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
