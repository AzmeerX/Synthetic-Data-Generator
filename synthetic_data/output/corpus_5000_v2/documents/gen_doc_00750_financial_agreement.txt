Facility agreement between Inver Merchant Bank Limited and North Quintrell Ventures Limited

Date: 26 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00750.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

The lender is Inver Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is North Quintrell Ventures Limited.

4. Conditions precedent
