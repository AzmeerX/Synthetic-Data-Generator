Case notes for Grimsdale Marine Limited against Grimsdale Ventures Limited

Date: 30 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01740.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Grimsdale Marine Limited against Grimsdale Ventures Limited in Elmford County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Grimsdale Marine Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-186.

Jarrah Dunmore signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-186.Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department is the chair of Grimsdale Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Marine Limited GBP 34 million on 29 July 2024.

Grimsdale Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Marine Limited.

Grimsdale Marine Limited owes Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 3 August 2024.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department is a shareholder of Grimsdale Marine Limited.

Hana Hale is a family member of Jarrah Dunmore.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Grimsdale Marine Limited owns Eastmere Terminal.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Grimsdale Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Grimsdale Ventures Limited on 30 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Rook noted that a former employee alleged that Grimsdale Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
