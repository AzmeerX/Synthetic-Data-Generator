Board meeting minutes of Grimsdale Trading Limited

Date: 2 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01736.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Kerr LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Hale, Ines Hale and Alden & Kerr LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Grimsdale Trading Limited owns Grimsdale Partners Limited.Hana Hale is the chair of Grimsdale Trading Limited.

Petra Hale is a shareholder of Grimsdale Trading Limited.

Hana Ellery is a family member of Hana Fenwick.

Carrow Private Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Trading Limited GBP 28 million on 26 June 2024.

Grimsdale Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Trading Limited.

Grimsdale Trading Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 1 July 2024.

Alden & Ingram LLP represents Grimsdale Trading Limited in the matter.

Grimsdale Trading Limited owns Fenwick Cold Store.

Fenwick Cold Store is located in Dunmere Bay.

Grimsdale Trading Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-183.Hana Fenwick signed the Services Agreement LEG-183.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Mercer, chair of Grimsdale Trading Limited, proposed to acquire Grimsdale Energy Limited on 2 July 2024.

Gideon Mercer confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Grimsdale Energy Limited by Grimsdale Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Grimsdale Energy Limited by Grimsdale Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
