Board meeting minutes of North Quenington Holdings Limited

Date: 25 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01379.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Tremayne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Ulric, Quin Ward and Alden & Tremayne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quenington Holdings Limited owns Jarrow Cross Dock.Quin Ulric is the chair of North Quenington Holdings Limited.

Quin Ashby is a shareholder of North Quenington Holdings Limited.

Quin Rook is a family member of Quin Sable.

Stan Commercial Bank Limited lent North Quenington Holdings Limited GBP 34 million on 20 February 2024.

North Quenington Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quenington Holdings Limited.

North Quenington Holdings Limited owes Stan Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 25 February 2024.

Alden & Quade LLP represents North Quenington Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Quenington Holdings Limited owns Jarrow Cross Office Park.

Jarrow Cross Office Park is located in Ashcombe Hill.

North Quenington Holdings Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-414.Quin Sable signed the Licence LEG-414.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Sable, chair of North Quenington Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Quenington Partners Limited on 25 March 2024.

Bram Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quenington Partners Limited by North Quenington Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quenington Partners Limited by North Quenington Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
