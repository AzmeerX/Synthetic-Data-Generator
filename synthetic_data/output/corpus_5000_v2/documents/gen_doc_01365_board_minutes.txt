Board meeting minutes of Quintrell Partners Limited

Date: 1 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01365.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Everard LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Langford, Daria Neville and Alden & Everard LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Quintrell Partners Limited owns Quintrell Capital Limited.Daria Langford is the chair of Quintrell Partners Limited.

Daria Ravenscroft is a shareholder of Quintrell Partners Limited.

Daria Ingram is a family member of Daria Jeffreys.

Alder International Bank Limited lent Quintrell Partners Limited GBP 14 million on 10 February 2024.

Quintrell Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Quintrell Partners Limited.

Quintrell Partners Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 15 February 2024.

Alden & Quade LLP represents Quintrell Partners Limited in the matter.

Quintrell Partners Limited owns Calderport Yard.

Calderport Yard is located in Quintrell.

Quintrell Partners Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-016.Daria Jeffreys signed the Loan Note LEG-016.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Cairn, chair of Quintrell Partners Limited, proposed to acquire North Pinewood Trading Limited on 1 April 2027.

Bram Cairn confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Pinewood Trading Limited by Quintrell Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Pinewood Trading Limited by Quintrell Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
