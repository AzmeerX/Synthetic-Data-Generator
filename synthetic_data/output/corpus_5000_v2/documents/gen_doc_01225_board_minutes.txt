Board meeting minutes of North Elmstead Capital Limited

Date: 25 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01225.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Delacourt, Nadia Fairfax and Alden & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Elmstead Capital Limited owns North Elmstead Marine Limited.Nadia Delacourt is the chair of North Elmstead Capital Limited.

Nadia Ingram is a shareholder of North Elmstead Capital Limited.

Nadia Ashby is a family member of Nadia Balfour.

Red Commercial Bank Limited lent North Elmstead Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 20 August 2024.

North Elmstead Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmstead Capital Limited.

North Elmstead Capital Limited owes Red Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 25 August 2024.

Alden & Ivers LLP represents North Elmstead Capital Limited in the matter.

North Elmstead Capital Limited owns Denmoor Rail Depot.

Denmoor Rail Depot is located in Glenmoor Cross.

North Elmstead Capital Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-276.Nadia Balfour signed the Board Resolution LEG-276.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Kerr, chair of North Elmstead Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Elmstead Systems Limited on 25 July 2024.

Yara Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Elmstead Systems Limited by North Elmstead Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Elmstead Systems Limited by North Elmstead Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
