Case notes for North Yardley Energy Limited against North Yardley Systems Limited

Date: 9 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01481.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Yardley Energy Limited against North Yardley Systems Limited in Glenport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Yardley Energy Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-506.

Silas Mercer signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-506.Silas Orrick is the chair of North Yardley Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Low International Bank Limited lent North Yardley Energy Limited GBP 18 million on 3 April 2022.

North Yardley Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Yardley Energy Limited.

North Yardley Energy Limited owes Low International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 8 April 2022.

Silas Thorne is a shareholder of North Yardley Energy Limited.

Silas Lorne is a family member of Silas Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Yardley Energy Limited owns Oakhurst Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Yardley Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Yardley Systems Limited on 9 March 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Dunmore noted that a former employee alleged that North Yardley Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
