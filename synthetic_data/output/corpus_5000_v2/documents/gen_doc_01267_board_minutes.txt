Board meeting minutes of North Hampstead Marine Limited

Date: 15 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01267.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Mercer LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Quade, Oren Sable and Briar & Mercer LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Hampstead Marine Limited owns North Hampstead Energy Limited.Oren Quade is the chair of North Hampstead Marine Limited.

Oren Vale is a shareholder of North Hampstead Marine Limited.

Oren Nyle is a family member of Oren Orrick.

Bexley Private Bank Limited lent North Hampstead Marine Limited GBP 32 million on 1 October 2025.

North Hampstead Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Hampstead Marine Limited.

North Hampstead Marine Limited owes Bexley Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 6 October 2025.

Alden & Villiers LLP represents North Hampstead Marine Limited in the matter.

North Hampstead Marine Limited owns Elmford Farm.

Elmford Farm is located in Kelbrook Vale.

North Hampstead Marine Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-313.Oren Orrick signed the Trust Deed LEG-313.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Zane Dunmore, chair of North Hampstead Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Inkberrow Holdings Limited on 15 May 2025.

Zane Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Inkberrow Holdings Limited by North Hampstead Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Inkberrow Holdings Limited by North Hampstead Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
