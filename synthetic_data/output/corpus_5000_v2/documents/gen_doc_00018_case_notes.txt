Case notes for Jervis Ventures Limited against Kendal Industries Limited

Date: 6 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00018.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Jervis Ventures Limited against Kendal Industries Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Jervis Ventures Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-400.

Tamsin Montague signed the Side Letter LEG-400.Tamsin Osborne is the chair of Jervis Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Merchant Bank Limited lent Jervis Ventures Limited GBP 22 million on 13 June 2024.

Kendal Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Jervis Ventures Limited.

Jervis Ventures Limited owes Merri Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 18 June 2024.

Tamsin Underwood is a shareholder of Jervis Ventures Limited.

Tamsin Langford is a family member of Tamsin Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Jervis Ventures Limited owns Jervis Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Jervis Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kendal Industries Limited on 6 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Marek Orrick noted that a former employee alleged that Jervis Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
