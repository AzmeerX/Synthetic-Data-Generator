Board meeting minutes of North Norwood Group Limited

Date: 31 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01022.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Cavendish LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Juno Neville, Juno Pemberton and Briar & Cavendish LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Norwood Group Limited owns North Norwood Logistics Limited.Juno Neville is the chair of North Norwood Group Limited.

Juno Tremayne is a shareholder of North Norwood Group Limited.

Juno Kingsley is a family member of Juno Langford.

Inver International Bank Limited lent North Norwood Group Limited GBP 30 million on 1 October 2023.

North Norwood Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norwood Group Limited.

North Norwood Group Limited owes Inver International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 6 October 2023.

Briar & Kingsley LLP represents North Norwood Group Limited in the matter.

North Norwood Group Limited owns Harrowgate Yard.

Harrowgate Yard is located in Fairhaven Green.

North Norwood Group Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-092.Juno Langford signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-092.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Garrow, chair of North Norwood Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Norwood Marine Limited on 31 March 2027.

Ulric Garrow confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Norwood Marine Limited by North Norwood Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Norwood Marine Limited by North Norwood Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
