Date: 1 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00816.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Vellacott Property Limited against North Westerby Holdings Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Vellacott Property Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-513.
