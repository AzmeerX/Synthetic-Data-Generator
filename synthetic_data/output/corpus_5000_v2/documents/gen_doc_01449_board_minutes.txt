Board meeting minutes of North Vinehall Trading Limited

Date: 28 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01449.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Langford, Rhea Neville and Cairn & Fenwick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Vinehall Trading Limited owns Merrivale Office Park.Rhea Langford is the chair of North Vinehall Trading Limited.

Rhea Ravenscroft is a shareholder of North Vinehall Trading Limited.

Rhea Ingram is a family member of Rhea Jeffreys.

Harrow Merchant Bank Limited lent North Vinehall Trading Limited GBP 42 million on 24 January 2026.

North Vinehall Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Vinehall Trading Limited.

North Vinehall Trading Limited owes Harrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 29 January 2026.

Briar & Grenville LLP represents North Vinehall Trading Limited in the matter.

North Vinehall Trading Limited owns Netherby Cold Store.

Netherby Cold Store is located in Inglemere Vale.

North Vinehall Trading Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-478.Rhea Jeffreys signed the Licence LEG-478.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Vale, chair of North Vinehall Trading Limited, proposed to acquire Redmoor Industries Limited on 28 July 2025.

Amina Vale confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Redmoor Industries Limited by North Vinehall Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Redmoor Industries Limited by North Vinehall Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
