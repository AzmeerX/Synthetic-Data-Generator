Board meeting minutes of North Penhurst Ventures Limited

Date: 18 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01057.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Dunmore LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Vale, Kellan Yarrow and Cairn & Dunmore LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Penhurst Ventures Limited owns North Penhurst Systems Limited.Kellan Vale is the chair of North Penhurst Ventures Limited.

Kellan Balfour is a shareholder of North Penhurst Ventures Limited.

Kellan Sable is a family member of Kellan Thorne.

Oak Merchant Bank Limited lent North Penhurst Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 17 September 2024.

North Quarrymere Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Penhurst Ventures Limited.

North Penhurst Ventures Limited owes Oak Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 22 September 2024.

Cairn & Thorne LLP represents North Penhurst Ventures Limited in the matter.

North Penhurst Ventures Limited owns Juniper Cross Warehouse.

Juniper Cross Warehouse is located in Jarrow Hill.

North Penhurst Ventures Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.Kellan Thorne signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Ravenscroft, chair of North Penhurst Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire North Quarrymere Logistics Limited on 18 August 2024.

Ulric Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quarrymere Logistics Limited by North Penhurst Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quarrymere Logistics Limited by North Penhurst Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
