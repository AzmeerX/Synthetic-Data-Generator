Case notes for Eastmoor Capital Limited against Belmont Holdings Limited

Date: 30 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01712.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Eastmoor Capital Limited against Belmont Holdings Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Eastmoor Capital Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-161.

Gideon Ingram signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-161.Gideon Kingsley is the chair of Eastmoor Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent Eastmoor Capital Limited GBP 24 million on 28 October 2023.

Eastmoor Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Eastmoor Capital Limited.

Eastmoor Capital Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 2 November 2023.

Gideon Pemberton is a shareholder of Eastmoor Capital Limited.

Gideon Hastings is a family member of Gideon Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Eastmoor Capital Limited owns Eastmoor Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Eastmoor Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Belmont Holdings Limited on 30 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Neville noted that a former employee alleged that Eastmoor Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
