Case notes for North Elmsworth Systems Limited against North Fairhaven Group Limited

Date: 29 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00592.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Elmsworth Systems Limited against North Fairhaven Group Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Elmsworth Systems Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-311.

Bastian Sinclair signed the Services Agreement LEG-311.Bastian Underwood is the chair of North Elmsworth Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kel Trust Bank Limited lent North Elmsworth Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 21 January 2026.

North Fairhaven Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmsworth Systems Limited.

North Elmsworth Systems Limited owes Kel Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 26 January 2026.

Bastian Zouch is a shareholder of North Elmsworth Systems Limited.

Bastian Ravenscroft is a family member of Bastian Sinclair.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Elmsworth Systems Limited owns North Fairhaven Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Elmsworth Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Fairhaven Group Limited on 29 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Pell noted that a former employee alleged that North Elmsworth Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
