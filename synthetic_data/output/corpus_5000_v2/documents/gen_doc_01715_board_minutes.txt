Board meeting minutes of Belmont Holdings Limited

Date: 6 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01715.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Kerr LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Yara Rook, Amina Briar and Cairn & Kerr LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Belmont Holdings Limited owns Belmont Industries Limited.Yara Rook is the chair of Belmont Holdings Limited.

Amina Ellery is a shareholder of Belmont Holdings Limited.

Gideon Nyle is a family member of Lorne Ward.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Belmont Holdings Limited GBP 44 million on 12 March 2023.

Belmont Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Belmont Holdings Limited.

Belmont Holdings Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 17 March 2023.

Alden & Briar LLP represents Belmont Holdings Limited in the matter.

Belmont Holdings Limited owns Juniper Data Centre.

Juniper Data Centre is located in Fenwick Bay.

Belmont Holdings Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-037.Lorne Ward signed the Security Agreement LEG-037.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Farah Ravenscroft, chair of Belmont Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire Fallow Industries Limited on 6 February 2024.

Farah Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Fallow Industries Limited by Belmont Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Fallow Industries Limited by Belmont Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
