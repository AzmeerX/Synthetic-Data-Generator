Case notes for Yelverton Holdings Limited against Yelverton Logistics Limited

Date: 1 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00193.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yelverton Holdings Limited against Yelverton Logistics Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yelverton Holdings Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-558.

Wren Villiers signed the Licence LEG-558.Wren Wyndham is the chair of Yelverton Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Commercial Bank Limited lent Yelverton Holdings Limited GBP 18 million on 21 July 2024.

Yelverton Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Yelverton Holdings Limited.

Yelverton Holdings Limited owes Quill Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 26 July 2024.

Xander Cairn is a shareholder of Yelverton Holdings Limited.

Wren Underwood is a family member of Wren Villiers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yelverton Holdings Limited owns Pemberton Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yelverton Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yelverton Logistics Limited on 1 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Yelverton Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
