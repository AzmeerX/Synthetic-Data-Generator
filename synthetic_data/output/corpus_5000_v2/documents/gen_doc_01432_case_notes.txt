Case notes for North Upton Holdings Limited against North Upton Logistics Limited

Date: 31 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01432.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Upton Holdings Limited against North Upton Logistics Limited in Fairmouth Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Upton Holdings Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-462.

Rhea Quade signed the Licence LEG-462.Rhea Thorne is the chair of North Upton Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent North Upton Holdings Limited GBP 50 million on 1 August 2025.

North Upton Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Upton Holdings Limited.

North Upton Holdings Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 6 August 2025.

Rhea Ashby is a shareholder of North Upton Holdings Limited.

Rhea Pell is a family member of Rhea Quade.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Upton Holdings Limited owns Merrivale Cold Store.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Upton Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Upton Logistics Limited on 31 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Dunmore noted that a former employee alleged that North Upton Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
