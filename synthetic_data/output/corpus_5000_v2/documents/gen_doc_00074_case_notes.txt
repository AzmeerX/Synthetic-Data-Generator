Case notes for Ormsby Industries Limited against Glenmoor Property Limited

Date: 3 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00074.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ormsby Industries Limited against Glenmoor Property Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ormsby Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-451.

Ulric Neville signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-451.Ulric Pemberton is the chair of Ormsby Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Ormsby Industries Limited GBP 44 million on 26 December 2025.

Ormsby Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Ormsby Industries Limited.

Ormsby Industries Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 31 December 2025.

Ulric Villiers is a shareholder of Ormsby Industries Limited.

Ulric Montague is a family member of Ulric Neville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ormsby Industries Limited owns Ormsby Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ormsby Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Glenmoor Property Limited on 3 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Neville noted that a former employee alleged that Ormsby Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
