Case notes for Borrowdale Energy Limited against Borrowdale Systems Limited

Date: 3 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00228.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Borrowdale Energy Limited against Borrowdale Systems Limited in Alderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Borrowdale Energy Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-590.

Xander Delacourt signed the Licence LEG-590.Xander Fairfax is the chair of Borrowdale Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Borrowdale Energy Limited GBP 42 million on 8 July 2025.

Borrowdale Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Borrowdale Energy Limited.

Borrowdale Energy Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 13 July 2025.

Xander Kingsley is a shareholder of Borrowdale Energy Limited.

Xander Cavendish is a family member of Xander Delacourt.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Borrowdale Energy Limited owns Ironside Foundry.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Borrowdale Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Borrowdale Systems Limited on 3 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Borrowdale Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
