Date: 5 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00949.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Hazelmere Property Limited against North Ivybridge Holdings Limited in Alderport High Court.

2. Procedural history

North Hazelmere Property Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-027.
