Board meeting minutes of Fallow Property Limited

Date: 14 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01729.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Alden, Hana Cairn and Cairn & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Fallow Property Limited owns Fallow Ventures Limited.Hana Alden is the chair of Fallow Property Limited.

Hana Fenwick is a shareholder of Fallow Property Limited.

Gideon Wyndham is a family member of Gideon Yardley.

Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent Fallow Property Limited GBP 14 million on 10 April 2024.

Fallow Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Fallow Property Limited.

Fallow Property Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 15 April 2024.

Alden & Balfour LLP represents Fallow Property Limited in the matter.

Fallow Property Limited owns Kestrelford Farm.

Kestrelford Farm is located in Carrow Cross.

Fallow Property Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-176.Gideon Yardley signed the Side Letter LEG-176.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Fenwick, chair of Fallow Property Limited, proposed to acquire Grimsdale Group Limited on 14 May 2024.

Gideon Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Grimsdale Group Limited by Fallow Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Grimsdale Group Limited by Fallow Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
