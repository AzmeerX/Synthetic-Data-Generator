Press release concerning Alderney Property Limited

Publication date: 9 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00216.

1. Corporate statement

Alderney Property Limited today issued the following statement.

Alderney Property Limited owns Alderney Ventures Limited.

Xander Zell is a shareholder of Alderney Property Limited.

Xander Quade is a family member of Xander Rook.

Uldale Bank Limited lent Alderney Property Limited GBP 20 million on 9 March 2025.

Alderney Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Alderney Property Limited.

Alderney Property Limited owes Uldale Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 14 March 2025.
