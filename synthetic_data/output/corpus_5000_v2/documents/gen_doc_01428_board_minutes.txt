Board meeting minutes of North Thurlow Property Limited

Date: 3 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01428.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Tremayne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Pell, Rhea Sable and Briar & Tremayne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Thurlow Property Limited owns North Thurlow Ventures Limited.Rhea Pell is the chair of North Thurlow Property Limited.

Rhea Ward is a shareholder of North Thurlow Property Limited.

Rhea Mercer is a family member of Rhea Nyle.

Elm Bank Limited lent North Thurlow Property Limited GBP 44 million on 29 June 2025.

North Thurlow Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Thurlow Property Limited.

North Thurlow Property Limited owes Elm Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 4 July 2025.

Briar & Mercer LLP represents North Thurlow Property Limited in the matter.

North Thurlow Property Limited owns Merrivale Fleet.

Merrivale Fleet is located in Glenmoor Bridge.

North Thurlow Property Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-459.Rhea Nyle signed the Debenture LEG-459.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Yara Rook, chair of North Thurlow Property Limited, proposed to acquire North Upton Industries Limited on 3 March 2025.

Yara Rook confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Upton Industries Limited by North Thurlow Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Upton Industries Limited by North Thurlow Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
