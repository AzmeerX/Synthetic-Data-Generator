Board meeting minutes of North Pemberton Energy Limited

Date: 11 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00735.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Zouch LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ashby, Elias Cavendish and Alden & Zouch LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Pemberton Energy Limited owns Kelbrook Works.Elias Ashby is the chair of North Pemberton Energy Limited.

Elias Fairfax is a shareholder of North Pemberton Energy Limited.

Elias Ward is a family member of Elias Yarrow.

Jarrow International Bank Limited lent North Pemberton Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 15 April 2025.

North Pemberton Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Pemberton Energy Limited.

North Pemberton Energy Limited owes Jarrow International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 20 April 2025.

Briar & Montague LLP represents North Pemberton Energy Limited in the matter.

North Pemberton Energy Limited owns Kelbrook Dock.

Kelbrook Dock is located in Cresswell.

North Pemberton Energy Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-440.Elias Yarrow signed the Lease LEG-440.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Nyle, chair of North Pemberton Energy Limited, proposed to acquire North Quintrell Industries Limited on 11 January 2025.

Oren Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quintrell Industries Limited by North Pemberton Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quintrell Industries Limited by North Pemberton Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
