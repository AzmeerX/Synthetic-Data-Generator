Board meeting minutes of Tarrow Energy Limited

Date: 7 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01596.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Vale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Balfour, Elias Delacourt and Cairn & Vale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Tarrow Energy Limited owns Tarrow Property Limited.Elias Balfour is the chair of Tarrow Energy Limited.

Elias Grenville is a shareholder of Tarrow Energy Limited.

Elias Yarrow is a family member of Elias Zell.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Tarrow Energy Limited GBP 12 million on 14 April 2025.

Tarrow Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Energy Limited.

Tarrow Energy Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 19 April 2025.

Alden & Fenwick LLP represents Tarrow Energy Limited in the matter.

Tarrow Energy Limited owns Eastmere Silo.

Eastmere Silo is located in Hampstead.

Tarrow Energy Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-055.Elias Zell signed the Services Agreement LEG-055.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Thorne, chair of Tarrow Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Uldale Industries Limited on 7 February 2025.

Daria Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Uldale Industries Limited by Tarrow Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Uldale Industries Limited by Tarrow Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
