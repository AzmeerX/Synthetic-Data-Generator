Board meeting minutes of Wilton Energy Limited

Date: 3 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00189.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Yarrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Wren Tremayne, Wren Villiers and Alden & Yarrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Wilton Energy Limited owns Pemberton Dock.Wren Tremayne is the chair of Wilton Energy Limited.

Wren Yardley is a shareholder of Wilton Energy Limited.

Wren Pemberton is a family member of Wren Ravenscroft.

Pember International Bank Limited lent Wilton Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 7 June 2024.

Wilton Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Wilton Energy Limited.

Wilton Energy Limited owes Pember International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 12 June 2024.

Cairn & Quade LLP represents Wilton Energy Limited in the matter.

Wilton Energy Limited owns Pemberton Office Park.

Pemberton Office Park is located in Belmont Hill.

Wilton Energy Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-554.Wren Ravenscroft signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-554.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Dunmore, chair of Wilton Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Yelverton Industries Limited on 3 June 2024.

Daria Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Yelverton Industries Limited by Wilton Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Yelverton Industries Limited by Wilton Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
