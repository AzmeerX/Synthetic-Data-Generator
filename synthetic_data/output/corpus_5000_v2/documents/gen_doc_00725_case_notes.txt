Case notes for North Oakhurst Systems Limited against North Pemberton Group Limited

Date: 2 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00725.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Oakhurst Systems Limited against North Pemberton Group Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Oakhurst Systems Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-431.

Elias Nyle signed the Power of Attorney LEG-431.Elias Quade is the chair of North Oakhurst Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Trust Bank Limited lent North Oakhurst Systems Limited GBP 16 million on 6 January 2025.

North Pemberton Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Oakhurst Systems Limited.

North Oakhurst Systems Limited owes Inver Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 11 January 2025.

Elias Vale is a shareholder of North Oakhurst Systems Limited.

Elias Mercer is a family member of Elias Nyle.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Oakhurst Systems Limited owns North Pemberton Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Oakhurst Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Pemberton Group Limited on 2 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Oren Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that North Oakhurst Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
