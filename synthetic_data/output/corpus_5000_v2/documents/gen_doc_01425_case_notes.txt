Case notes for North Thurlow Capital Limited against North Thurlow Property Limited

Date: 10 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01425.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Thurlow Capital Limited against North Thurlow Property Limited in Elmford County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Thurlow Capital Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-456.

Rhea Kerr signed the Lease LEG-456.Rhea Mercer is the chair of North Thurlow Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent North Thurlow Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 27 May 2025.

North Thurlow Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Thurlow Capital Limited.

North Thurlow Capital Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 1 June 2025.

Rhea Sable is a shareholder of North Thurlow Capital Limited.

Rhea Jarvis is a family member of Rhea Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Thurlow Capital Limited owns North Thurlow Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Thurlow Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Thurlow Property Limited on 10 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Nyle noted that a former employee alleged that North Thurlow Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
