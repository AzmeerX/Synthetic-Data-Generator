Board meeting minutes of Greyhaven Industries Limited

Date: 2 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00343.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Wyndham LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Garrow, Tamsin Mercer and Alden & Wyndham LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Greyhaven Industries Limited owns Ravenmoor Group Limited.Daria Garrow is the chair of Greyhaven Industries Limited.

Anika Dunmore is a shareholder of Greyhaven Industries Limited.

Daria Garrow is a family member of Bram Nyle.

Fenwick Bank Limited lent Greyhaven Industries Limited GBP 38 million on 2 July 2022.

Zephyr Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Greyhaven Industries Limited.

Greyhaven Industries Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 17 million as at 7 July 2022.

Marlowe & Reed LLP represents Greyhaven Industries Limited in the matter.

Greyhaven Industries Limited owns Kestrel Wharf.

Kestrel Wharf is located in Kestrelford.

Greyhaven Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-014.Bram Nyle signed the Security Agreement LEG-014.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Lorne, chair of Greyhaven Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Kintyre Marine Limited on 2 February 2024.

Gideon Lorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Kintyre Marine Limited by Greyhaven Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Kintyre Marine Limited by Greyhaven Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
