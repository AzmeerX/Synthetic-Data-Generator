Board meeting minutes of North Underhill Industries Limited

Date: 14 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01113.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Montague LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Lena Yarrow, Lena Ashby and Alden & Montague LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Underhill Industries Limited owns North Underhill Group Limited.Lena Yarrow is the chair of North Underhill Industries Limited.

Lena Delacourt is a shareholder of North Underhill Industries Limited.

Lena Ulric is a family member of Lena Vale.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent North Underhill Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 1 April 2026.

North Underhill Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Underhill Industries Limited.

North Underhill Industries Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 6 April 2026.

Alden & Montague LLP represents North Underhill Industries Limited in the matter.

North Underhill Industries Limited owns Larkspur Farm.

Larkspur Farm is located in Harrow Bay.

North Underhill Industries Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-175.Lena Vale signed the Power of Attorney LEG-175.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Wyndham, chair of North Underhill Industries Limited, proposed to acquire North Underhill Capital Limited on 14 September 2025.

Vera Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Underhill Capital Limited by North Underhill Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Underhill Capital Limited by North Underhill Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
