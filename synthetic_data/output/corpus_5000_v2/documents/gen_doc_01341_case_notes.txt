Case notes for North Norbury Logistics Limited against North Norbury Trading Limited

Date: 15 October 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01341.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Norbury Logistics Limited against North Norbury Trading Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Norbury Logistics Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-381.

Petra Jeffreys signed the Supply Agreement LEG-381.Petra Langford is the chair of North Norbury Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Bank Limited lent North Norbury Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 22 February 2023.

North Norbury Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Norbury Logistics Limited.

North Norbury Logistics Limited owes Nether Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 27 February 2023.

Petra Ravenscroft is a shareholder of North Norbury Logistics Limited.

Petra Ingram is a family member of Petra Jeffreys.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Norbury Logistics Limited owns North Norbury Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Norbury Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Norbury Trading Limited on 15 October 2026.
