Case notes for North Rushmere Property Limited against North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited

Date: 9 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01082.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Rushmere Property Limited against North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Rushmere Property Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-147.

Kellan Sinclair signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-147.Kellan Underwood is the chair of North Rushmere Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Stan Bank Limited lent North Rushmere Property Limited GBP 20 million on 28 May 2025.

North Rushmere Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Rushmere Property Limited.

North Rushmere Property Limited owes Stan Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 2 June 2025.

Kellan Zouch is a shareholder of North Rushmere Property Limited.

Kellan Ravenscroft is a family member of Kellan Sinclair.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Rushmere Property Limited owns North Rushmere Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Rushmere Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Saltmarsh Holdings Limited on 9 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Quade noted that a former employee alleged that North Rushmere Property Limited misstated its revenue.
