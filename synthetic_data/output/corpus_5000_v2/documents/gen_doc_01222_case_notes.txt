Case notes for North Elmstead Logistics Limited against North Elmstead Capital Limited

Date: 4 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01222.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Elmstead Logistics Limited against North Elmstead Capital Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Elmstead Logistics Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-273.

Nadia Ward signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-273.Nadia Ashby is the chair of North Elmstead Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Bank Limited lent North Elmstead Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 18 July 2024.

North Elmstead Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmstead Logistics Limited.

North Elmstead Logistics Limited owes Red Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 23 July 2024.

Nadia Fairfax is a shareholder of North Elmstead Logistics Limited.

Nadia Vale is a family member of Nadia Ward.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Elmstead Logistics Limited owns North Elmstead Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Elmstead Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Elmstead Capital Limited on 4 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Hale noted that a former employee alleged that North Elmstead Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
