Board meeting minutes of North Quarrymere Systems Limited

Date: 24 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01071.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Garrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Jeffreys, Kellan Langford and Alden & Garrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quarrymere Systems Limited owns North Rushmere Holdings Limited.Kellan Jeffreys is the chair of North Quarrymere Systems Limited.

Kellan Osborne is a shareholder of North Quarrymere Systems Limited.

Kellan Grenville is a family member of Kellan Hastings.

Quill Trust Bank Limited lent North Quarrymere Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 7 February 2025.

North Rushmere Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quarrymere Systems Limited.

North Quarrymere Systems Limited owes Quill Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 12 February 2025.

Quade & Thorne LLP represents North Quarrymere Systems Limited in the matter.

North Quarrymere Systems Limited owns Juniper Cross Farm.

Juniper Cross Farm is located in Lowmoor Moor.

North Quarrymere Systems Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-137.Kellan Hastings signed the Trust Deed LEG-137.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Fenwick, chair of North Quarrymere Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Oakhurst Logistics Limited on 24 November 2024.

Vera Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Oakhurst Logistics Limited by North Quarrymere Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Oakhurst Logistics Limited by North Quarrymere Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
