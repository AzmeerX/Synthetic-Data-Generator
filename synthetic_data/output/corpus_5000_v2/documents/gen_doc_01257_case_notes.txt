Case notes for North Gorse Ventures Limited against North Hampstead Industries Limited

Date: 6 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01257.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Gorse Ventures Limited against North Hampstead Industries Limited in Glenport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Gorse Ventures Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-304.

Oren Ellery signed the Side Letter LEG-304.Oren Garrow is the chair of North Gorse Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent North Gorse Ventures Limited GBP 14 million on 24 June 2025.

North Hampstead Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Gorse Ventures Limited.

North Gorse Ventures Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 29 June 2025.

Oren Mercer is a shareholder of North Gorse Ventures Limited.

Oren Dunmore is a family member of Oren Ellery.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Gorse Ventures Limited owns North Gorse Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Gorse Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Hampstead Industries Limited on 6 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that North Gorse Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
