Case notes for North Merrivale Logistics Limited against North Merrivale Capital Limited

Date: 2 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00690.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Merrivale Logistics Limited against North Merrivale Capital Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Merrivale Logistics Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-399.

Daria Grenville signed the Power of Attorney LEG-399.Daria Ingram is the chair of North Merrivale Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 20 January 2024.

North Merrivale Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Logistics Limited.

North Merrivale Logistics Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 25 January 2024.

Daria Neville is a shareholder of North Merrivale Logistics Limited.

Daria Fairfax is a family member of Daria Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Merrivale Logistics Limited owns North Merrivale Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Merrivale Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Merrivale Capital Limited on 2 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Quade noted that a former employee alleged that North Merrivale Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
