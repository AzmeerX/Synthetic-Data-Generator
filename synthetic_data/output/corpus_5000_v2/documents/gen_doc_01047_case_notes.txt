Case notes for North Penhurst Industries Limited against North Penhurst Trading Limited

Date: 9 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01047.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Penhurst Industries Limited against North Penhurst Trading Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Penhurst Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-115.

Kellan Jarvis signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-115.Kellan Mercer is the chair of North Penhurst Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Merri Private Bank Limited lent North Penhurst Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 10 June 2024.

North Penhurst Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Penhurst Industries Limited.

North Penhurst Industries Limited owes Merri Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 15 June 2024.

Kellan Rook is a shareholder of North Penhurst Industries Limited.

Kellan Ivers is a family member of Kellan Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Penhurst Industries Limited owns North Penhurst Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Penhurst Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Penhurst Trading Limited on 9 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ulric Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that North Penhurst Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
