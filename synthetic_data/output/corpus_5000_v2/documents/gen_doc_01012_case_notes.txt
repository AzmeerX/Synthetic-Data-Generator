Case notes for North Mossgrove Partners Limited against North Mossgrove Energy Limited

Date: 20 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-01012.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Mossgrove Partners Limited against North Mossgrove Energy Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Mossgrove Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-083.

Juno Cavendish signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-083.Juno Everard is the chair of North Mossgrove Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Trust Bank Limited lent North Mossgrove Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 24 June 2023.

North Mossgrove Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Mossgrove Partners Limited.

North Mossgrove Partners Limited owes Harrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 29 June 2023.

Juno Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Mossgrove Partners Limited.

Juno Balfour is a family member of Juno Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Mossgrove Partners Limited owns North Mossgrove Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Mossgrove Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Mossgrove Energy Limited on 20 January 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Tamsin Whitlock noted that a former employee alleged that North Mossgrove Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
