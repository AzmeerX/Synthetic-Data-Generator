Board meeting minutes of North Trentvale Marine Limited

Date: 27 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01106.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Fairfax LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Lena Rook, Lena Thorne and Alden & Fairfax LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Trentvale Marine Limited owns North Trentvale Energy Limited.Lena Rook is the chair of North Trentvale Marine Limited.

Lena Ward is a shareholder of North Trentvale Marine Limited.

Lena Nyle is a family member of Lena Pell.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent North Trentvale Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 25 January 2026.

North Trentvale Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Trentvale Marine Limited.

North Trentvale Marine Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 30 January 2026.

Alden & Grenville LLP represents North Trentvale Marine Limited in the matter.

North Trentvale Marine Limited owns Larkspur Yard.

Larkspur Yard is located in Bexhaven.

North Trentvale Marine Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-169.Lena Pell signed the Trust Deed LEG-169.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Vera Pemberton, chair of North Trentvale Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Trentvale Systems Limited on 27 July 2025.

Vera Pemberton confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Trentvale Systems Limited by North Trentvale Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Trentvale Systems Limited by North Trentvale Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
