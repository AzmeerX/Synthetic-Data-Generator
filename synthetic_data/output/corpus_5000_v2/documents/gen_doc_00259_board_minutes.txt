Board meeting minutes of Dartmoor Systems Limited

Date: 6 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00259.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Underwood LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Yara Hale, Yara Jarvis and Briar & Underwood LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Yara Ellery is a family member of Yara Fenwick.

Alden & Wyndham LLP represents Dartmoor Systems Limited in the matter.

Dartmoor Systems Limited owns Calderport Estate.

Calderport Estate is located in Jarrow Vale.

The acquisition of Ennerdale Trading Limited by Dartmoor Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
