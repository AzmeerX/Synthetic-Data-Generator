Board meeting minutes of North Eversley Energy Limited

Date: 1 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01547.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Hastings LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Ashby, Tamsin Cavendish and Briar & Hastings LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Eversley Energy Limited owns Eastmere Terminal.Tamsin Ashby is the chair of North Eversley Energy Limited.

Tamsin Fairfax is a shareholder of North Eversley Energy Limited.

Tamsin Ward is a family member of Tamsin Yarrow.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent North Eversley Energy Limited GBP 18 million on 23 January 2024.

North Eversley Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eversley Energy Limited.

North Eversley Energy Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 28 January 2024.

Alden & Neville LLP represents North Eversley Energy Limited in the matter.

North Eversley Energy Limited owns Ironside Foundry.

Ironside Foundry is located in Overdale.

North Eversley Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-566.Tamsin Yarrow signed the Loan Note LEG-566.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Thorne, chair of North Eversley Energy Limited, proposed to acquire North Fingal Industries Limited on 1 March 2024.

Calder Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Fingal Industries Limited by North Eversley Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Fingal Industries Limited by North Eversley Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
