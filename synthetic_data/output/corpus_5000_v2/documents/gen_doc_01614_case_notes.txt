Case notes for Vellacott Industries Limited against Vellacott Logistics Limited

Date: 13 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01614.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vellacott Industries Limited against Vellacott Logistics Limited in Glenport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vellacott Industries Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-072.

Elias Ravenscroft signed the Lease LEG-072.Elias Tremayne is the chair of Vellacott Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent Vellacott Industries Limited GBP 46 million on 18 October 2025.

Vellacott Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Industries Limited.

Vellacott Industries Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 23 October 2025.

Elias Yardley is a shareholder of Vellacott Industries Limited.

Elias Pemberton is a family member of Elias Ravenscroft.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vellacott Industries Limited owns Vellacott Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vellacott Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vellacott Logistics Limited on 13 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Montague noted that a former employee alleged that Vellacott Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
