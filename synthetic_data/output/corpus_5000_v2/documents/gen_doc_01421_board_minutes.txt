Board meeting minutes of North Thurlow Group Limited

Date: 13 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01421.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Langford LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Ivers, Rhea Kerr and Briar & Langford LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Thurlow Group Limited owns North Thurlow Logistics Limited.Rhea Ivers is the chair of North Thurlow Group Limited.

Rhea Nyle is a shareholder of North Thurlow Group Limited.

Rhea Fenwick is a family member of Rhea Garrow.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent North Thurlow Group Limited GBP 30 million on 13 April 2025.

North Thurlow Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Thurlow Group Limited.

North Thurlow Group Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 18 April 2025.

Briar & Fenwick LLP represents North Thurlow Group Limited in the matter.

North Thurlow Group Limited owns Lowmoor Estate.

Lowmoor Estate is located in Fairhaven Green.

North Thurlow Group Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-452.Rhea Garrow signed the Board Resolution LEG-452.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Freya Sable, chair of North Thurlow Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Thurlow Marine Limited on 13 January 2025.

Freya Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Thurlow Marine Limited by North Thurlow Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Thurlow Marine Limited by North Thurlow Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
