Board meeting minutes of Inglemere Group Limited

Date: 24 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00266.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Briar LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Langford, Bastian Neville and Alden & Briar LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Inglemere Group Limited owns Inglemere Logistics Limited.Bastian Langford is the chair of Inglemere Group Limited.

Bastian Ravenscroft is a shareholder of Inglemere Group Limited.

Bastian Ingram is a family member of Bastian Jeffreys.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent Inglemere Group Limited GBP 16 million on 13 October 2025.

Inglemere Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Group Limited.

Inglemere Group Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 18 October 2025.

Cairn & Sable LLP represents Inglemere Group Limited in the matter.

Inglemere Group Limited owns Pinehurst Silo.

Pinehurst Silo is located in Larkspur.

Inglemere Group Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-123.Bastian Jeffreys signed the Debenture LEG-123.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Hastings, chair of Inglemere Group Limited, proposed to acquire Ennerdale Ventures Limited on 24 November 2025.

Elias Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Ennerdale Ventures Limited by Inglemere Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Ennerdale Ventures Limited by Inglemere Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
