Case notes for Pembroke Ventures Limited against Quantock Industries Limited

Date: 4 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00417.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pembroke Ventures Limited against Quantock Industries Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pembroke Ventures Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-154.

Bram Alden signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-154.Bram Cairn is the chair of Pembroke Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Merchant Bank Limited lent Pembroke Ventures Limited GBP 22 million on 25 December 2025.

Quantock Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Pembroke Ventures Limited.

Pembroke Ventures Limited owes Glen Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 30 December 2025.

Bram Hale is a shareholder of Pembroke Ventures Limited.

Anika Zouch is a family member of Bram Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pembroke Ventures Limited owns Pembroke Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pembroke Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Quantock Industries Limited on 4 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Hana Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Pembroke Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
