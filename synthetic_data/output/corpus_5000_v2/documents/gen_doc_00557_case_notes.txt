Case notes for North Carrow Trading Limited against North Carrow Marine Limited

Date: 26 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00557.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Carrow Trading Limited against North Carrow Marine Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Carrow Trading Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-280.

Bastian Lorne signed the Lease LEG-280.Bastian Nyle is the chair of North Carrow Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Merchant Bank Limited lent North Carrow Trading Limited GBP 34 million on 14 February 2025.

North Carrow Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Carrow Trading Limited.

North Carrow Trading Limited owes Fair Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 19 February 2025.

Bastian Sable is a shareholder of North Carrow Trading Limited.

Bastian Kerr is a family member of Bastian Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Carrow Trading Limited owns Carrow Bay Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Carrow Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Carrow Marine Limited on 26 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Kellan Everard noted that a former employee alleged that North Carrow Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
