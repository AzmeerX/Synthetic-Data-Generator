Case notes for Harrowden Ventures Limited against Borrowdale Trading Limited

Date: 13 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00221.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Harrowden Ventures Limited against Borrowdale Trading Limited in Glenport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Harrowden Ventures Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-119.

Bastian Fairfax signed the Services Agreement LEG-119.Bastian Hastings is the chair of Harrowden Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Trust Bank Limited lent Harrowden Ventures Limited GBP 48 million on 30 August 2025.

Inglemere Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Ventures Limited.

Harrowden Ventures Limited owes Carrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 4 September 2025.

Bastian Montague is a shareholder of Harrowden Ventures Limited.

Bastian Everard is a family member of Bastian Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Harrowden Ventures Limited owns Harrowden Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Harrowden Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Borrowdale Trading Limited on 13 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Harrowden Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
