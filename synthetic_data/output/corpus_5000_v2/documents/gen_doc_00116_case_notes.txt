Case notes for Rothbury Logistics Limited against Rothbury Capital Limited

Date: 23 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00116.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Rothbury Logistics Limited against Rothbury Capital Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Rothbury Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-489.

Vera Balfour signed the Trust Deed LEG-489.Vera Delacourt is the chair of Rothbury Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Bank Limited lent Rothbury Logistics Limited GBP 40 million on 23 June 2022.

Rothbury Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Rothbury Logistics Limited.

Rothbury Logistics Limited owes Fair Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 28 June 2022.

Vera Ingram is a shareholder of Rothbury Logistics Limited.

Vera Ashby is a family member of Vera Balfour.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Rothbury Logistics Limited owns Rothbury Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Rothbury Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Rothbury Capital Limited on 23 April 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
