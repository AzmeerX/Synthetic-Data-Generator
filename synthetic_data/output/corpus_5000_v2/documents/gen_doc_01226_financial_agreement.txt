Facility agreement between Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited and North Elmstead Marine Limited

Date: 1 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01226.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Tarrow Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is North Elmstead Marine Limited.

North Elmstead Marine Limited owns North Elmstead Energy Limited.Red Private Bank Limited lent North Elmstead Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 31 August 2024.

North Elmstead Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Elmstead Marine Limited.

North Elmstead Marine Limited owes Red Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 5 September 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Elmstead Marine Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-277.

Nadia Cavendish signed the Security Agreement LEG-277.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
