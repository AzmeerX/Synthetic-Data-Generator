Board meeting minutes of North Fingal Group Limited

Date: 19 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01554.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Osborne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Grenville, Tamsin Ingram and Briar & Osborne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Fingal Group Limited owns North Fingal Logistics Limited.Tamsin Grenville is the chair of North Fingal Group Limited.

Tamsin Langford is a shareholder of North Fingal Group Limited.

Tamsin Delacourt is a family member of Tamsin Everard.

Alder International Bank Limited lent North Fingal Group Limited GBP 30 million on 29 March 2024.

North Fingal Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fingal Group Limited.

North Fingal Group Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 April 2024.

Alden & Underwood LLP represents North Fingal Group Limited in the matter.

North Fingal Group Limited owns Oakhaven Yard.

Oakhaven Yard is located in Underhill.

North Fingal Group Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-572.Tamsin Everard signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-572.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Balfour, chair of North Fingal Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Fingal Marine Limited on 19 April 2024.

Calder Balfour confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Fingal Marine Limited by North Fingal Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Fingal Marine Limited by North Fingal Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
