Board meeting minutes of North Merrivale Holdings Limited

Date: 3 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00686.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Vale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Fairfax, Daria Hastings and Cairn & Vale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Merrivale Holdings Limited owns Invermere Data Centre.Daria Fairfax is the chair of North Merrivale Holdings Limited.

Daria Kingsley is a shareholder of North Merrivale Holdings Limited.

Daria Cavendish is a family member of Daria Delacourt.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Holdings Limited GBP 26 million on 18 December 2023.

North Merrivale Group Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Holdings Limited.

North Merrivale Holdings Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 23 December 2023.

Alden & Sinclair LLP represents North Merrivale Holdings Limited in the matter.

North Merrivale Holdings Limited owns Invermere Works.

Invermere Works is located in Netherby Bay.

North Merrivale Holdings Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-396.Daria Delacourt signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-396.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Mercer, chair of North Merrivale Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Merrivale Trading Limited on 3 February 2024.

Nadia Mercer confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Merrivale Trading Limited by North Merrivale Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Merrivale Trading Limited by North Merrivale Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
