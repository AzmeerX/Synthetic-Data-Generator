Board meeting minutes of Kendal Industries Limited

Date: 27 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00021.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Garrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Sinclair, Tamsin Underwood and Cairn & Garrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Kendal Industries Limited owns Kendal Group Limited.Tamsin Sinclair is the chair of Kendal Industries Limited.

Tamsin Wyndham is a shareholder of Kendal Industries Limited.

Tamsin Osborne is a family member of Tamsin Pemberton.

Merri Private Bank Limited lent Kendal Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 16 July 2024.

Kendal Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Kendal Industries Limited.

Kendal Industries Limited owes Merri Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 21 July 2024.

Briar & Underwood LLP represents Kendal Industries Limited in the matter.

Kendal Industries Limited owns Invermere Hangar.

Invermere Hangar is located in Fairhaven Bridge.

Kendal Industries Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-403.Tamsin Pemberton signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-403.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Anika Dunmore, chair of Kendal Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Kendal Partners Limited on 27 June 2024.

Anika Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Kendal Partners Limited by Kendal Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Kendal Partners Limited by Kendal Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
