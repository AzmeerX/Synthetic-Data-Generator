Board meeting minutes of North Draycott Ventures Limited

Date: 30 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00896.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Grenville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Zell, Hana Balfour and Briar & Grenville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Draycott Ventures Limited owns North Draycott Systems Limited.Hana Zell is the chair of North Draycott Ventures Limited.

Hana Everard is a shareholder of North Draycott Ventures Limited.

Hana Vale is a family member of Hana Ward.

Merri Merchant Bank Limited lent North Draycott Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 11 January 2025.

North Eastmoor Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Draycott Ventures Limited.

North Draycott Ventures Limited owes Merri Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 16 January 2025.

Cairn & Ellery LLP represents North Draycott Ventures Limited in the matter.

North Draycott Ventures Limited owns Silverbeck Plant.

Silverbeck Plant is located in Greyhaven.

North Draycott Ventures Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-586.Hana Ward signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-586.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Everard, chair of North Draycott Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire North Eastmoor Industries Limited on 30 October 2024.

Rhea Everard confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Eastmoor Industries Limited by North Draycott Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Eastmoor Industries Limited by North Draycott Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
