Board meeting minutes of North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 15 January 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01302.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Sable LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Oren Yardley, Petra Briar and Cairn & Sable LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited owns North Kingsmoor Trading Limited.Oren Yardley is the chair of North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited.

Petra Ellery is a shareholder of North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited.

Oren Villiers is a family member of Oren Whitlock.

Harrow Bank Limited lent North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 18 September 2026.

North Kingsmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited.

North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited owes Harrow Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 23 September 2026.

Briar & Delacourt LLP represents North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited owns Glenport Marina.

Glenport Marina is located in Larkspur.

North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-345.Oren Whitlock signed the Trust Deed LEG-345.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Zane Neville, chair of North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Kingsmoor Energy Limited on 15 January 2026.

Zane Neville confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Kingsmoor Energy Limited by North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Kingsmoor Energy Limited by North Kingsmoor Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
