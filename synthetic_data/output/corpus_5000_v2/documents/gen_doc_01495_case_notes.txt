Case notes for North Ashby Property Limited against Redmoor Partners Limited

Date: 15 June 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01495.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Ashby Property Limited against Redmoor Partners Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Ashby Property Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-519.

Silas Ashby signed the Services Agreement LEG-519.Silas Cavendish is the chair of North Ashby Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Bank Limited lent North Ashby Property Limited GBP 44 million on 24 August 2022.

North Ashby Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ashby Property Limited.

North Ashby Property Limited owes Oak Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 29 August 2022.

Silas Hastings is a shareholder of North Ashby Property Limited.

Silas Zell is a family member of Silas Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Ashby Property Limited owns North Ashby Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Ashby Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Redmoor Partners Limited on 15 June 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Rook noted that a former employee alleged that North Ashby Property Limited misstated its revenue.
