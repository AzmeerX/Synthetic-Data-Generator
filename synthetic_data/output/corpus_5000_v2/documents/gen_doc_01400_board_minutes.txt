Board meeting minutes of North Ravensby Marine Limited

Date: 19 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01400.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Pell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Osborne, Quin Ravenscroft and Briar & Pell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Ravensby Marine Limited owns North Ravensby Energy Limited.Quin Osborne is the chair of North Ravensby Marine Limited.

Quin Underwood is a shareholder of North Ravensby Marine Limited.

Quin Langford is a family member of Quin Montague.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent North Ravensby Marine Limited GBP 32 million on 16 September 2024.

North Ravensby Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Ravensby Marine Limited.

North Ravensby Marine Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 21 September 2024.

Alden & Kingsley LLP represents North Ravensby Marine Limited in the matter.

North Ravensby Marine Limited owns Kelbrook Farm.

Kelbrook Farm is located in Dunmere Port.

North Ravensby Marine Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-433.Quin Montague signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-433.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Ulric, chair of North Ravensby Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Southwold Holdings Limited on 19 August 2024.

Rhea Ulric confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Southwold Holdings Limited by North Ravensby Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Southwold Holdings Limited by North Ravensby Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
