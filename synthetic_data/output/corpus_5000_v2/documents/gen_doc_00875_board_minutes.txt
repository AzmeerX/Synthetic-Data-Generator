Board meeting minutes of North Cresswell Logistics Limited

Date: 5 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00875.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Kerr LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Fenwick, Hana Hale and Briar & Kerr LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Cresswell Logistics Limited owns North Cresswell Trading Limited.Hana Fenwick is the chair of North Cresswell Logistics Limited.

Hana Kerr is a shareholder of North Cresswell Logistics Limited.

Hana Cairn is a family member of Hana Dunmore.

Jarrow Bank Limited lent North Cresswell Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 16 June 2024.

North Cresswell Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Cresswell Logistics Limited.

North Cresswell Logistics Limited owes Jarrow Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 21 June 2024.

Briar & Jeffreys LLP represents North Cresswell Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Cresswell Logistics Limited owns Redmoor Terminal.

Redmoor Terminal is located in Lowmoor Vale.

North Cresswell Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-567.Hana Dunmore signed the Services Agreement LEG-567.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Garrow, chair of North Cresswell Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Cresswell Marine Limited on 5 June 2024.

Rhea Garrow confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Cresswell Marine Limited by North Cresswell Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Cresswell Marine Limited by North Cresswell Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
