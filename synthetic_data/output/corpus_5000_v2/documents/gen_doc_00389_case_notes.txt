Case notes for Nairn Energy Limited against Nairn Systems Limited

Date: 20 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00389.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Nairn Energy Limited against Nairn Systems Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Nairn Energy Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-128.

Anika Zell signed the Side Letter LEG-128.Anika Balfour is the chair of Nairn Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Nairn Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 14 March 2025.

Nairn Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Nairn Energy Limited.

Nairn Energy Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 19 March 2025.

Anika Grenville is a shareholder of Nairn Energy Limited.

Anika Yarrow is a family member of Anika Zell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Nairn Energy Limited owns Ironside Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Nairn Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Nairn Systems Limited on 20 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Jarrah Dunmore noted that a former employee alleged that Nairn Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
