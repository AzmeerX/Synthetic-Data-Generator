Facility agreement between Low Commercial Bank Limited and North Fingal Ventures Limited

Date: 14 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01562.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Low Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is North Fingal Ventures Limited.

North Fingal Ventures Limited owns North Fingal Systems Limited.Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent North Fingal Ventures Limited GBP 46 million on 25 June 2024.

North Grafton Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fingal Ventures Limited.

North Fingal Ventures Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 30 June 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

North Fingal Ventures Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-580.

Tamsin Montague signed the Board Resolution LEG-580.

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
