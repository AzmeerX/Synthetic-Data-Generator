Board meeting minutes of North Eastmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 18 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00903.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Neville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Hana Everard, Hana Grenville and Briar & Neville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Eastmoor Logistics Limited owns North Eastmoor Trading Limited.Hana Everard is the chair of North Eastmoor Logistics Limited.

Hana Jeffreys is a shareholder of North Eastmoor Logistics Limited.

Hana Balfour is a family member of Hana Cavendish.

Nether Bank Limited lent North Eastmoor Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 7 March 2025.

North Eastmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eastmoor Logistics Limited.

North Eastmoor Logistics Limited owes Nether Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 12 March 2025.

Cairn & Jarvis LLP represents North Eastmoor Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Eastmoor Logistics Limited owns Calderport Rail Depot.

Calderport Rail Depot is located in Larkspur.

North Eastmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-591.Hana Cavendish signed the Power of Attorney LEG-591.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Rhea Langford, chair of North Eastmoor Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Eastmoor Energy Limited on 18 December 2024.

Rhea Langford confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Eastmoor Energy Limited by North Eastmoor Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Eastmoor Energy Limited by North Eastmoor Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
