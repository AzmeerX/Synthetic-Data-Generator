Case notes for Grimsdale Industries Limited against Grimsdale Trading Limited

Date: 11 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01733.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Grimsdale Industries Limited against Grimsdale Trading Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Grimsdale Industries Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-180.

Hana Cairn signed the Board Resolution LEG-180.Hana Ellery is the chair of Grimsdale Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Industries Limited GBP 22 million on 24 May 2024.

Grimsdale Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Industries Limited.

Grimsdale Industries Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 29 May 2024.

Ines Hale is a shareholder of Grimsdale Industries Limited.

Hana Briar is a family member of Hana Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Grimsdale Industries Limited owns Grimsdale Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Grimsdale Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Grimsdale Trading Limited on 11 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that Grimsdale Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
