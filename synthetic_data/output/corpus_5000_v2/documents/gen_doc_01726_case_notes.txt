Case notes for Belmont Industries Limited against Fallow Property Limited

Date: 23 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01726.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Belmont Industries Limited against Fallow Property Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Belmont Industries Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-038.

Bram Kerr signed the Loan Note LEG-038.Amina Alden is the chair of Belmont Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Belmont Industries Limited GBP 46 million on 23 March 2023.

Belmont Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Belmont Industries Limited.

Belmont Industries Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 28 March 2023.

Amina Fenwick is a shareholder of Belmont Industries Limited.

Lorne Ward is a family member of Bram Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Belmont Industries Limited owns Belmont Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Belmont Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Fallow Property Limited on 23 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that Belmont Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
