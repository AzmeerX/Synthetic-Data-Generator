Board meeting minutes of North Merrivale Capital Limited

Date: 23 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00693.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Cavendish LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Langford, Daria Neville and Cairn & Cavendish LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Merrivale Capital Limited owns North Merrivale Marine Limited.Daria Langford is the chair of North Merrivale Capital Limited.

Daria Ravenscroft is a shareholder of North Merrivale Capital Limited.

Daria Ingram is a family member of Daria Jeffreys.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent North Merrivale Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 22 February 2024.

North Merrivale Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Merrivale Capital Limited.

North Merrivale Capital Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 27 February 2024.

Alden & Yardley LLP represents North Merrivale Capital Limited in the matter.

North Merrivale Capital Limited owns Invermere Estate.

Invermere Estate is located in Calderport.

North Merrivale Capital Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-402.Daria Jeffreys signed the Facility Agreement LEG-402.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Thorne, chair of North Merrivale Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Merrivale Systems Limited on 23 March 2024.

Nadia Thorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Merrivale Systems Limited by North Merrivale Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Merrivale Systems Limited by North Merrivale Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
