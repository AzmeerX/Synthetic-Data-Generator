Case notes for Jura Capital Limited against Jura Property Limited

Date: 8 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00333.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Jura Capital Limited against Jura Property Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Jura Capital Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-078.

Zane Yarrow signed the Licence LEG-078.Zane Ashby is the chair of Jura Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Pember Commercial Bank Limited lent Jura Capital Limited GBP 30 million on 11 September 2023.

Jura Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Jura Capital Limited.

Jura Capital Limited owes Pember Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 16 September 2023.

Zane Fairfax is a shareholder of Jura Capital Limited.

Zane Ward is a family member of Zane Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Jura Capital Limited owns Jura Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Jura Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Jura Property Limited on 8 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Jura Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
