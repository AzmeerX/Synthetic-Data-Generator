Case notes for North Fallow Industries Limited against North Fallow Trading Limited

Date: 5 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00914.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Fallow Industries Limited against North Fallow Trading Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Fallow Industries Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-601.

Hana Montague signed the Trust Deed LEG-601.Hana Osborne is the chair of North Fallow Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oak Private Bank Limited lent North Fallow Industries Limited GBP 36 million on 25 June 2025.

North Fallow Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fallow Industries Limited.

North Fallow Industries Limited owes Oak Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 30 June 2025.

Hana Underwood is a shareholder of North Fallow Industries Limited.

Hana Langford is a family member of Hana Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Fallow Industries Limited owns North Fallow Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Fallow Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Fallow Trading Limited on 5 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Wyndham noted that a former employee alleged that North Fallow Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
