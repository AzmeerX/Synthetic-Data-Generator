Case notes for Cresswell Trading Limited against Cresswell Marine Limited

Date: 16 October 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01684.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Cresswell Trading Limited against Cresswell Marine Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Cresswell Trading Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-135.

Gideon Garrow signed the Services Agreement LEG-135.Gideon Ivers is the chair of Cresswell Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Trust Bank Limited lent Cresswell Trading Limited GBP 12 million on 15 January 2023.

Cresswell Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Cresswell Trading Limited.

Cresswell Trading Limited owes Denmoor Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 20 January 2023.

Gideon Orrick is a shareholder of Cresswell Trading Limited.

Gideon Fenwick is a family member of Gideon Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Cresswell Trading Limited owns Cresswell Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Cresswell Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Cresswell Marine Limited on 16 October 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
