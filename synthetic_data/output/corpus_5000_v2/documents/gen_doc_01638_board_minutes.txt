Board meeting minutes of Ashcombe Partners Limited

Date: 28 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01638.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Sinclair LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Freya Sable, Mira Ivers and Alden & Sinclair LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Ashcombe Partners Limited owns Ashcombe Capital Limited.Freya Sable is the chair of Ashcombe Partners Limited.

Lorne Ward is a shareholder of Ashcombe Partners Limited.

Nolan Quade is a family member of Elias Orrick.

Fenwick Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Partners Limited GBP 30 million on 25 December 2022.

Ashcombe Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Partners Limited.

Ashcombe Partners Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 30 December 2022.

Alden & Ellery LLP represents Ashcombe Partners Limited in the matter.

Ashcombe Partners Limited owns Kestrel Wharf.

Kestrel Wharf is located in Elmford.

Ashcombe Partners Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-030.Elias Orrick signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-030.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Lorne, chair of Ashcombe Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Yarmouth Logistics Limited on 28 November 2025.

Elias Lorne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Yarmouth Logistics Limited by Ashcombe Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Yarmouth Logistics Limited by Ashcombe Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
