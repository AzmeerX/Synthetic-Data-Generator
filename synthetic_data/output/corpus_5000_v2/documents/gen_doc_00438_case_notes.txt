Case notes for Rannoch Partners Limited against Rannoch Energy Limited

Date: 28 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00438.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Rannoch Partners Limited against Rannoch Energy Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Rannoch Partners Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-173.

Bram Vale signed the Supply Agreement LEG-173.Bram Yarrow is the chair of Rannoch Partners Limited.

Jarrah Dunmore is a shareholder of Rannoch Partners Limited.

Bram Ulric is a family member of Bram Vale.4. Findings and allegations
