Case notes for Priors Energy Limited against Priors Systems Limited

Date: 27 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00095.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Priors Energy Limited against Priors Systems Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Priors Energy Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-470.

Vera Hale signed the Loan Note LEG-470.Vera Jarvis is the chair of Priors Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Priors Energy Limited GBP 42 million on 23 July 2026.

Priors Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Priors Energy Limited.

Priors Energy Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 28 July 2026.

Vera Orrick is a shareholder of Priors Energy Limited.

Vera Garrow is a family member of Vera Hale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Priors Energy Limited owns Lowmoor Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Priors Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Priors Systems Limited on 27 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
