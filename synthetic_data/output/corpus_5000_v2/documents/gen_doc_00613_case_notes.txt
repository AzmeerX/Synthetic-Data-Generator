Case notes for North Glenmoor Capital Limited against North Glenmoor Property Limited

Date: 23 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00613.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Glenmoor Capital Limited against North Glenmoor Property Limited in Elmford High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Glenmoor Capital Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-330.

Calder Lorne signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-330.Calder Nyle is the chair of North Glenmoor Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Nether Commercial Bank Limited lent North Glenmoor Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 18 August 2026.

North Glenmoor Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Glenmoor Capital Limited.

North Glenmoor Capital Limited owes Nether Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 23 August 2026.

Calder Sable is a shareholder of North Glenmoor Capital Limited.

Calder Kerr is a family member of Calder Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Glenmoor Capital Limited owns North Glenmoor Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Glenmoor Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Glenmoor Property Limited on 23 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lena Langford noted that a former employee alleged that North Glenmoor Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
