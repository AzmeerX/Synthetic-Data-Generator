Case notes for Vellacott Marine Limited against Vellacott Ventures Limited

Date: 1 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01621.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vellacott Marine Limited against Vellacott Ventures Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vellacott Marine Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-078.

Elias Wyndham signed the Licence LEG-078.Elias Zouch is the chair of Vellacott Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent Vellacott Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 23 December 2025.

Vellacott Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Marine Limited.

Vellacott Marine Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 28 December 2025.

Farah Ellery is a shareholder of Vellacott Marine Limited.

Elias Whitlock is a family member of Elias Wyndham.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vellacott Marine Limited owns Fenwick Bay Yard.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vellacott Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vellacott Ventures Limited on 1 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Vellacott Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
