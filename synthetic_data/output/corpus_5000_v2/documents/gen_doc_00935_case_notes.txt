Case notes for North Grimsdale Energy Limited against North Grimsdale Systems Limited

Date: 30 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00935.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Grimsdale Energy Limited against North Grimsdale Systems Limited in Fairmouth Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Grimsdale Energy Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-014.

Ivo Hale signed the Security Agreement LEG-014.Ivo Jarvis is the chair of North Grimsdale Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red International Bank Limited lent North Grimsdale Energy Limited GBP 34 million on 20 January 2026.

North Grimsdale Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grimsdale Energy Limited.

North Grimsdale Energy Limited owes Red International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 25 January 2026.

Ivo Pell is a shareholder of North Grimsdale Energy Limited.

Ivo Garrow is a family member of Ivo Hale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Grimsdale Energy Limited owns Dunhollow Data Centre.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Grimsdale Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Grimsdale Systems Limited on 30 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Silas Sable noted that a former employee alleged that North Grimsdale Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
