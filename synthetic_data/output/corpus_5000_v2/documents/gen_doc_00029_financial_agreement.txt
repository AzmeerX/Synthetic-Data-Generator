Facility agreement between Stan Merchant Bank Limited and Kendal Energy Limited

Date: 22 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00029.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Stan Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Kendal Energy Limited.

Kendal Energy Limited owns Invermere Works.Nether International Bank Limited lent Kendal Energy Limited GBP 42 million on 1 October 2024.

Kendal Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Kendal Energy Limited.

Kendal Energy Limited owes Nether International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 6 October 2024.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
