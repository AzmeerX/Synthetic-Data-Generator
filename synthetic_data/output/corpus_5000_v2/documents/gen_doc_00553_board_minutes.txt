Board meeting minutes of North Carrow Industries Limited

Date: 29 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00553.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Rook LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Kerr, Bastian Mercer and Cairn & Rook LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Carrow Industries Limited owns North Carrow Group Limited.Bastian Kerr is the chair of North Carrow Industries Limited.

Bastian Pell is a shareholder of North Carrow Industries Limited.

Bastian Hale is a family member of Bastian Ivers.

Elm Private Bank Limited lent North Carrow Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 12 January 2025.

North Carrow Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Carrow Industries Limited.

North Carrow Industries Limited owes Elm Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 17 January 2025.

Briar & Ellery LLP represents North Carrow Industries Limited in the matter.

North Carrow Industries Limited owns Carrow Bay Yard.

Carrow Bay Yard is located in Trentvale.

North Carrow Industries Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-277.Bastian Ivers signed the Security Agreement LEG-277.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Ashby, chair of North Carrow Industries Limited, proposed to acquire North Carrow Partners Limited on 29 October 2024.

Kellan Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Carrow Partners Limited by North Carrow Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Carrow Partners Limited by North Carrow Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
