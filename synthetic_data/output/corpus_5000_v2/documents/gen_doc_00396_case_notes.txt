Case notes for Orkney Logistics Limited against Jarrow Group Limited

Date: 7 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00396.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Orkney Logistics Limited against Jarrow Group Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Orkney Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-135.

Anika Grenville signed the Services Agreement LEG-135.Anika Ingram is the chair of Orkney Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Bank Limited lent Orkney Logistics Limited GBP 24 million on 30 May 2025.

Orkney Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Orkney Logistics Limited.

Orkney Logistics Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 4 June 2025.

Anika Neville is a shareholder of Orkney Logistics Limited.

Anika Fairfax is a family member of Anika Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Orkney Logistics Limited owns Orkney Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Orkney Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Jarrow Group Limited on 7 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department noted that a former employee alleged that Orkney Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
