Case notes for North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited against North Saltmarsh Capital Limited

Date: 30 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01089.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited against North Saltmarsh Capital Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-153.

Kellan Yardley signed the Trust Deed LEG-153.Lena Alden is the chair of North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Tarrow Bank Limited lent North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited GBP 32 million on 2 August 2025.

North Saltmarsh Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited.

North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited owes Tarrow Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 7 August 2025.

Lena Fenwick is a shareholder of North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited.

Kellan Wyndham is a family member of Kellan Yardley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited owns North Saltmarsh Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Saltmarsh Capital Limited on 30 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that North Saltmarsh Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
