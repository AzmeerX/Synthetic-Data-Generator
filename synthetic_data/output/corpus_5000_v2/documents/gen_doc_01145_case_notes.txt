Case notes for North Whitbourne Partners Limited against North Whitbourne Energy Limited

Date: 26 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01145.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Whitbourne Partners Limited against North Whitbourne Energy Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Whitbourne Partners Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-203.

Lena Zouch signed the Debenture LEG-203.Marek Briar is the chair of North Whitbourne Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Trust Bank Limited lent North Whitbourne Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 9 June 2022.

North Whitbourne Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Whitbourne Partners Limited.

North Whitbourne Partners Limited owes Fair Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 14 June 2022.

Marek Garrow is a shareholder of North Whitbourne Partners Limited.

Lena Yardley is a family member of Lena Zouch.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Whitbourne Partners Limited owns North Whitbourne Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Whitbourne Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Whitbourne Energy Limited on 26 April 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Wren Everard noted that a former employee alleged that North Whitbourne Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
