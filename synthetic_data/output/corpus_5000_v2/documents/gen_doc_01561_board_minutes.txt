Board meeting minutes of North Fingal Property Limited

Date: 7 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01561.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Marlowe & Reed LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Neville, Tamsin Pemberton and Marlowe & Reed LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Fingal Property Limited owns North Fingal Ventures Limited.Tamsin Neville is the chair of North Fingal Property Limited.

Tamsin Tremayne is a shareholder of North Fingal Property Limited.

Tamsin Kingsley is a family member of Tamsin Langford.

Carrow Bank Limited lent North Fingal Property Limited GBP 44 million on 14 June 2024.

North Fingal Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Fingal Property Limited.

North Fingal Property Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 19 June 2024.

Briar & Cairn LLP represents North Fingal Property Limited in the matter.

North Fingal Property Limited owns Westmarch Office Park.

Westmarch Office Park is located in Denholm.

North Fingal Property Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-579.Tamsin Langford signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-579.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Ingram, chair of North Fingal Property Limited, proposed to acquire North Grafton Industries Limited on 7 June 2024.

Calder Ingram confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Grafton Industries Limited by North Fingal Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Grafton Industries Limited by North Fingal Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
