Board meeting minutes of North Clifford Systems Limited

Date: 29 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01204.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Hale, Nadia Kerr and Cairn & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Clifford Systems Limited owns North Denholm Holdings Limited.Nadia Hale is the chair of North Clifford Systems Limited.

Nadia Nyle is a shareholder of North Clifford Systems Limited.

Nadia Ellery is a family member of Nadia Fenwick.

Oak Trust Bank Limited lent North Clifford Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 24 January 2024.

North Denholm Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Clifford Systems Limited.

North Clifford Systems Limited owes Oak Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 29 January 2024.

Cairn & Ward LLP represents North Clifford Systems Limited in the matter.

North Clifford Systems Limited owns Carrow Bay Terminal.

Carrow Bay Terminal is located in Dunmere Hill.

North Clifford Systems Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-257.Nadia Fenwick signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-257.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Xander Osborne, chair of North Clifford Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Pemberton Logistics Limited on 29 February 2024.

Xander Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Pemberton Logistics Limited by North Clifford Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Pemberton Logistics Limited by North Clifford Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
