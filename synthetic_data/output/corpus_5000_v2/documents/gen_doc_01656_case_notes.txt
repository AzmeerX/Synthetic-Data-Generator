Case notes for Aldergate Logistics Limited against Aldergate Capital Limited

Date: 3 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-01656.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Aldergate Logistics Limited against Aldergate Capital Limited in Fairmouth County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Aldergate Logistics Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-110.

Farah Grenville signed the Licence LEG-110.Farah Ingram is the chair of Aldergate Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Aldergate Logistics Limited GBP 42 million on 15 April 2022.

Aldergate Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Logistics Limited.

Aldergate Logistics Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 20 April 2022.

Farah Neville is a shareholder of Aldergate Logistics Limited.

Farah Fairfax is a family member of Farah Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Aldergate Logistics Limited owns Harrowgate Foundry.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Aldergate Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Aldergate Capital Limited on 3 April 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Fairfax noted that a former employee alleged that Aldergate Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
