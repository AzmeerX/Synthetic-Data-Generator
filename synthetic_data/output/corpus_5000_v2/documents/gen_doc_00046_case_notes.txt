Case notes for Langton Systems Limited against Marlowe Group Limited

Date: 19 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00046.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Langton Systems Limited against Marlowe Group Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Langton Systems Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-425.

Ulric Mercer signed the Trust Deed LEG-425.Ulric Orrick is the chair of Langton Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Quill Trust Bank Limited lent Langton Systems Limited GBP 32 million on 15 March 2025.

Marlowe Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Langton Systems Limited.

Langton Systems Limited owes Quill Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 20 March 2025.

Ulric Thorne is a shareholder of Langton Systems Limited.

Ulric Lorne is a family member of Ulric Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Langton Systems Limited owns Marlowe Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Langton Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Marlowe Group Limited on 19 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that Langton Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
