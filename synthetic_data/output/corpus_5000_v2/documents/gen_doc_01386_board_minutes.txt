Board meeting minutes of North Quenington Marine Limited

Date: 13 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01386.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Alden LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Quin Cavendish, Quin Everard and Briar & Alden LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Quenington Marine Limited owns North Quenington Energy Limited.Quin Cavendish is the chair of North Quenington Marine Limited.

Quin Hastings is a shareholder of North Quenington Marine Limited.

Quin Zell is a family member of Quin Ashby.

Tarrow Private Bank Limited lent North Quenington Marine Limited GBP 48 million on 7 May 2024.

North Quenington Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Quenington Marine Limited.

North Quenington Marine Limited owes Tarrow Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 12 May 2024.

Alden & Yarrow LLP represents North Quenington Marine Limited in the matter.

North Quenington Marine Limited owns Kelbrook Foundry.

Kelbrook Foundry is located in Belmont Green.

North Quenington Marine Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-421.Quin Ashby signed the Security Agreement LEG-421.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Ashby, chair of North Quenington Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Quenington Systems Limited on 13 May 2024.

Bram Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Quenington Systems Limited by North Quenington Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Quenington Systems Limited by North Quenington Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
