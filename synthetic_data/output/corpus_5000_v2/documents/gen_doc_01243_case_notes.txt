Case notes for Pemberton Capital Limited against North Gorse Holdings Limited

Date: 28 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01243.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pemberton Capital Limited against North Gorse Holdings Limited in Elmford High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pemberton Capital Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-005.

Daria Yarrow signed the Facility Agreement LEG-005.Daria Ashby is the chair of Pemberton Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent Pemberton Capital Limited GBP 32 million on 12 October 2023.

Pemberton Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Pemberton Capital Limited.

Pemberton Capital Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 17 October 2023.

Daria Fairfax is a shareholder of Pemberton Capital Limited.

Daria Ward is a family member of Daria Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pemberton Capital Limited owns Pemberton Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pemberton Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Gorse Holdings Limited on 28 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Yara Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Pemberton Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
