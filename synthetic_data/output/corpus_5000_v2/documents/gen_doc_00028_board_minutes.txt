Board meeting minutes of Kendal Marine Limited

Date: 15 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00028.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Nyle LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Tamsin Yardley, Ulric Alden and Cairn & Nyle LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Kendal Marine Limited owns Kendal Energy Limited.Tamsin Yardley is the chair of Kendal Marine Limited.

Ulric Dunmore is a shareholder of Kendal Marine Limited.

Tamsin Villiers is a family member of Tamsin Whitlock.

Nether Private Bank Limited lent Kendal Marine Limited GBP 40 million on 20 September 2024.

Kendal Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Kendal Marine Limited.

Kendal Marine Limited owes Nether Private Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 25 September 2024.

Cairn & Alden LLP represents Kendal Marine Limited in the matter.

Kendal Marine Limited owns Invermere Mill.

Invermere Mill is located in Glenmoor Port.

Kendal Marine Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-409.Tamsin Whitlock signed the Trust Deed LEG-409.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Xander Garrow, chair of Kendal Marine Limited, proposed to acquire Glenmoor Partners Limited on 15 August 2024.

Xander Garrow confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Glenmoor Partners Limited by Kendal Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Glenmoor Partners Limited by Kendal Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
