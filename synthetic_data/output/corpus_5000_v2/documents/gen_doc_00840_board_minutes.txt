Board meeting minutes of North Yarmouth Marine Limited

Date: 16 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00840.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Yarrow, Gideon Ashby and Alden & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Yarmouth Marine Limited owns North Yarmouth Energy Limited.Gideon Yarrow is the chair of North Yarmouth Marine Limited.

Gideon Delacourt is a shareholder of North Yarmouth Marine Limited.

Gideon Ulric is a family member of Gideon Vale.

Denmoor Private Bank Limited lent North Yarmouth Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 30 June 2023.

North Yarmouth Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Yarmouth Marine Limited.

North Yarmouth Marine Limited owes Denmoor Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 5 July 2023.

Briar & Cairn LLP represents North Yarmouth Marine Limited in the matter.

North Yarmouth Marine Limited owns Pemberton Marina.

Pemberton Marina is located in Harrowden Hill.

North Yarmouth Marine Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-535.Gideon Vale signed the Services Agreement LEG-535.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Quin Vale, chair of North Yarmouth Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Yarmouth Systems Limited on 16 January 2027.

Quin Vale confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Yarmouth Systems Limited by North Yarmouth Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Yarmouth Systems Limited by North Yarmouth Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
