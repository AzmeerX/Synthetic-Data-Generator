Case notes for North Eversley Partners Limited against North Eversley Energy Limited

Date: 9 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01544.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Eversley Partners Limited against North Eversley Energy Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Eversley Partners Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-563.

Tamsin Ulric signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-563.Tamsin Ward is the chair of North Eversley Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent North Eversley Partners Limited GBP 12 million on 21 December 2023.

North Eversley Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to North Eversley Partners Limited.

North Eversley Partners Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 26 December 2023.

Tamsin Cavendish is a shareholder of North Eversley Partners Limited.

Tamsin Thorne is a family member of Tamsin Ulric.4. Findings and allegations

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
