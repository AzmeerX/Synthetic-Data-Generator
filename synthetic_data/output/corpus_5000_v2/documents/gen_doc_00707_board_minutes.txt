Board meeting minutes of North Netherby Marine Limited

Date: 29 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00707.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Vale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Zouch, Elias Briar and Alden & Vale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Netherby Marine Limited owns North Netherby Energy Limited.Daria Zouch is the chair of North Netherby Marine Limited.

Elias Ellery is a shareholder of North Netherby Marine Limited.

Daria Whitlock is a family member of Daria Wyndham.

Fair Private Bank Limited lent North Netherby Marine Limited GBP 24 million on 14 July 2024.

North Netherby Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Netherby Marine Limited.

North Netherby Marine Limited owes Fair Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 19 July 2024.

Briar & Mercer LLP represents North Netherby Marine Limited in the matter.

North Netherby Marine Limited owns Jarrow Cross Marina.

Jarrow Cross Marina is located in Bexhaven.

North Netherby Marine Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-415.Daria Wyndham signed the Power of Attorney LEG-415.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Jeffreys, chair of North Netherby Marine Limited, proposed to acquire North Netherby Systems Limited on 29 June 2024.

Nadia Jeffreys confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Netherby Systems Limited by North Netherby Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Netherby Systems Limited by North Netherby Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
