Board meeting minutes of North Grimsdale Systems Limited

Date: 20 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00938.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Nyle LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ivo Mercer, Ivo Pell and Cairn & Nyle LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Grimsdale Systems Limited owns North Hazelmere Holdings Limited.Ivo Mercer is the chair of North Grimsdale Systems Limited.

Ivo Sable is a shareholder of North Grimsdale Systems Limited.

Ivo Jarvis is a family member of Ivo Kerr.

Stan Trust Bank Limited lent North Grimsdale Systems Limited GBP 40 million on 22 February 2026.

North Hazelmere Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Grimsdale Systems Limited.

North Grimsdale Systems Limited owes Stan Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 27 February 2026.

Alden & Jarvis LLP represents North Grimsdale Systems Limited in the matter.

North Grimsdale Systems Limited owns Dunhollow Mill.

Dunhollow Mill is located in Fallow.

North Grimsdale Systems Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-017.Ivo Kerr signed the Loan Note LEG-017.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Silas Vale, chair of North Grimsdale Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Netherby Logistics Limited on 20 August 2025.

Silas Vale confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Netherby Logistics Limited by North Grimsdale Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Netherby Logistics Limited by North Grimsdale Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
