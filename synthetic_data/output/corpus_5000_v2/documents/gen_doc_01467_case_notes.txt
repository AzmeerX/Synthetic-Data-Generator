Case notes for North Wexford Marine Limited against North Wexford Ventures Limited

Date: 1 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01467.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Wexford Marine Limited against North Wexford Ventures Limited in Elmford Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Wexford Marine Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-493.

Rhea Zouch signed the Supply Agreement LEG-493.Silas Briar is the chair of North Wexford Marine Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

North Wexford Property Limited guaranteed the loan to North Wexford Marine Limited.

Silas Garrow is a shareholder of North Wexford Marine Limited.

Rhea Yardley is a family member of Rhea Zouch.4. Findings and allegations
