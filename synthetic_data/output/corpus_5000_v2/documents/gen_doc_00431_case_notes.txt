Case notes for Quantock Systems Limited against Rannoch Industries Limited

Date: 10 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00431.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quantock Systems Limited against Rannoch Industries Limited in Denmoor Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quantock Systems Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-167.

Bram Pell signed the Services Agreement LEG-167.Bram Rook is the chair of Quantock Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Inver Trust Bank Limited lent Quantock Systems Limited GBP 48 million on 17 May 2026.

Rannoch Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Quantock Systems Limited.

Quantock Systems Limited owes Inver Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 22 May 2026.

Bram Ward is a shareholder of Quantock Systems Limited.

Bram Orrick is a family member of Bram Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quantock Systems Limited owns Rannoch Holdings Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Quantock Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Rannoch Industries Limited on 10 October 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Ivo Alden noted that a former employee alleged that Quantock Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
