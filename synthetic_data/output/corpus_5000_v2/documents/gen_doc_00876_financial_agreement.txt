Facility agreement between Harrow Merchant Bank Limited and Merrivale Property Limited

Date: 12 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00876.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Harrow Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Merrivale Property Limited.

Merrivale Property Limited owns Merrivale Ventures Limited.Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent Merrivale Property Limited GBP 46 million on 14 October 2022.

Merrivale Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Property Limited.

Merrivale Property Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 19 October 2022.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Merrivale Property Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-052.

Calder Osborne signed the Board Resolution LEG-052.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Merrivale Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Cresswell Capital Limited on 12 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
