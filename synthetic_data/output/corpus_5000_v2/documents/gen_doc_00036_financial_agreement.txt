Facility agreement between Tarrow Merchant Bank Limited and Langton Group Limited

Date: 10 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00036.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Tarrow Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Langton Group Limited.

Langton Group Limited owns Langton Logistics Limited.Oak International Bank Limited lent Langton Group Limited GBP 14 million on 6 December 2024.

Langton Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Langton Group Limited.

Langton Group Limited owes Oak International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 11 December 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Langton Group Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-416.

Ulric Dunmore signed the Side Letter LEG-416.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Langton Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Langton Partners Limited on 10 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
