Case notes for North Lowmoor Property Limited against North Merrivale Holdings Limited

Date: 27 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00683.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Lowmoor Property Limited against North Merrivale Holdings Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Lowmoor Property Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-393.

Daria Ashby signed the Trust Deed LEG-393.Daria Cavendish is the chair of North Lowmoor Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Bank Limited lent North Lowmoor Property Limited GBP 20 million on 15 November 2023.

North Lowmoor Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to North Lowmoor Property Limited.

North Lowmoor Property Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 20 November 2023.

Daria Hastings is a shareholder of North Lowmoor Property Limited.

Daria Zell is a family member of Daria Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Lowmoor Property Limited owns North Lowmoor Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Lowmoor Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Merrivale Holdings Limited on 27 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that North Lowmoor Property Limited misstated its revenue.
