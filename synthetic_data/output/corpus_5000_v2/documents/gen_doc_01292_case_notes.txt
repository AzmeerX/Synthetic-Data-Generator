Case notes for North Juniper Capital Limited against North Juniper Property Limited

Date: 6 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01292.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Juniper Capital Limited against North Juniper Property Limited in Elmford Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Juniper Capital Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-336.

Oren Montague signed the Side Letter LEG-336.Oren Osborne is the chair of North Juniper Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Commercial Bank Limited lent North Juniper Capital Limited GBP 38 million on 11 June 2026.

North Juniper Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Juniper Capital Limited.

North Juniper Capital Limited owes Fair Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 16 June 2026.

Oren Underwood is a shareholder of North Juniper Capital Limited.

Oren Langford is a family member of Oren Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Juniper Capital Limited owns North Juniper Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Juniper Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Juniper Property Limited on 6 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Zane Delacourt noted that a former employee alleged that North Juniper Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
