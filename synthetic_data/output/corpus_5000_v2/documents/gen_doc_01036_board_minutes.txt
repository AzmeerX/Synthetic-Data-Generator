Board meeting minutes of North Overdale Logistics Limited

Date: 24 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01036.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Ravenscroft LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Kellan Briar, Kellan Dunmore and Briar & Ravenscroft LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Overdale Logistics Limited owns North Overdale Trading Limited.Kellan Briar is the chair of North Overdale Logistics Limited.

Kellan Garrow is a shareholder of North Overdale Logistics Limited.

Juno Yardley is a family member of Juno Zouch.

Low Bank Limited lent North Overdale Logistics Limited GBP 16 million on 21 February 2024.

North Overdale Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Overdale Logistics Limited.

North Overdale Logistics Limited owes Low Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 26 February 2024.

Briar & Yardley LLP represents North Overdale Logistics Limited in the matter.

North Overdale Logistics Limited owns Ironside Warehouse.

Ironside Warehouse is located in Harrowden Port.

North Overdale Logistics Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-105.Juno Zouch signed the Trust Deed LEG-105.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Ulric Ulric, chair of North Overdale Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire North Overdale Energy Limited on 24 March 2024.

Ulric Ulric confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Overdale Energy Limited by North Overdale Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Overdale Energy Limited by North Overdale Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
