Board meeting minutes of Culm Ventures Limited

Date: 30 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00245.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Briar & Fairfax LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Xander Underwood, Xander Whitlock and Briar & Fairfax LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Culm Ventures Limited owns Culm Systems Limited.Xander Underwood is the chair of Culm Ventures Limited.

Xander Zouch is a shareholder of Culm Ventures Limited.

Xander Ravenscroft is a family member of Xander Sinclair.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Culm Ventures Limited GBP 30 million on 9 December 2025.

Dartmoor Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Culm Ventures Limited.

Culm Ventures Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 14 December 2025.

Alden & Jeffreys LLP represents Culm Ventures Limited in the matter.

Culm Ventures Limited owns Calderport Fleet.

Calderport Fleet is located in Inglemere Cross.

Culm Ventures Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-604.Xander Sinclair signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-604.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Kerr, chair of Culm Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Dartmoor Logistics Limited on 30 June 2025.

Elias Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Dartmoor Logistics Limited by Culm Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Dartmoor Logistics Limited by Culm Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
