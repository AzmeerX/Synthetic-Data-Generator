Board meeting minutes of North Pemberton Group Limited

Date: 23 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00728.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Sinclair LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Thorne, Elias Vale and Alden & Sinclair LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Pemberton Group Limited owns North Pemberton Logistics Limited.Elias Thorne is the chair of North Pemberton Group Limited.

Elias Zell is a shareholder of North Pemberton Group Limited.

Elias Quade is a family member of Elias Rook.

Inver International Bank Limited lent North Pemberton Group Limited GBP 22 million on 8 February 2025.

North Pemberton Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to North Pemberton Group Limited.

North Pemberton Group Limited owes Inver International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 13 February 2025.

Briar & Grenville LLP represents North Pemberton Group Limited in the matter.

North Pemberton Group Limited owns Kelbrook Quay.

Kelbrook Quay is located in Uldale.

North Pemberton Group Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-434.Elias Rook signed the Facility Agreement LEG-434.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Oren Fenwick, chair of North Pemberton Group Limited, proposed to acquire North Pemberton Capital Limited on 23 November 2024.

Oren Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Pemberton Capital Limited by North Pemberton Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Pemberton Capital Limited by North Pemberton Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
