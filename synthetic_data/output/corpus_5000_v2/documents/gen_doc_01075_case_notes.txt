Case notes for North Rushmere Logistics Limited against North Rushmere Partners Limited

Date: 22 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01075.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Rushmere Logistics Limited against North Rushmere Partners Limited in Glenport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Rushmere Logistics Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-141.

Kellan Langford signed the Supply Agreement LEG-141.Kellan Neville is the chair of North Rushmere Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Red Bank Limited lent North Rushmere Logistics Limited GBP 48 million on 23 March 2025.

North Rushmere Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to North Rushmere Logistics Limited.

North Rushmere Logistics Limited owes Red Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 28 March 2025.

Kellan Tremayne is a shareholder of North Rushmere Logistics Limited.

Kellan Kingsley is a family member of Kellan Langford.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Rushmere Logistics Limited owns North Rushmere Trading Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Rushmere Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Rushmere Partners Limited on 22 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Vera Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that North Rushmere Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
