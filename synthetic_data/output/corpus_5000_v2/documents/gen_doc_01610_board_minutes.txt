Board meeting minutes of Uldale Property Limited

Date: 16 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01610.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Orrick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Osborne, Elias Ravenscroft and Alden & Orrick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Uldale Property Limited owns Uldale Ventures Limited.Elias Osborne is the chair of Uldale Property Limited.

Elias Underwood is a shareholder of Uldale Property Limited.

Elias Langford is a family member of Elias Montague.

Alder International Bank Limited lent Uldale Property Limited GBP 38 million on 4 September 2025.

Uldale Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Property Limited.

Uldale Property Limited owes Alder International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 9 September 2025.

Alden & Sable LLP represents Uldale Property Limited in the matter.

Uldale Property Limited owns Fenwick Bay Foundry.

Fenwick Bay Foundry is located in Belmont Bay.

Uldale Property Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-068.Elias Montague signed the Board Resolution LEG-068.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Ingram, chair of Uldale Property Limited, proposed to acquire Vellacott Group Limited on 16 May 2025.

Daria Ingram confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Vellacott Group Limited by Uldale Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Vellacott Group Limited by Uldale Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
