Case notes for North Netherby Trading Limited against North Netherby Marine Limited

Date: 8 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00704.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Netherby Trading Limited against North Netherby Marine Limited in Elmford Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Netherby Trading Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-412.

Daria Underwood signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-412.Daria Whitlock is the chair of North Netherby Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Merchant Bank Limited lent North Netherby Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 11 June 2024.

North Netherby Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Netherby Trading Limited.

North Netherby Trading Limited owes Fair Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 16 June 2024.

Elias Briar is a shareholder of North Netherby Trading Limited.

Daria Tremayne is a family member of Daria Underwood.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Netherby Trading Limited owns Jarrow Cross Cold Store.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Netherby Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Netherby Marine Limited on 8 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Nadia Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that North Netherby Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
