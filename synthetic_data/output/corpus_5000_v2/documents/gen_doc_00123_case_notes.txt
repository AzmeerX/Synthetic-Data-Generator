Case notes for Rothbury Energy Limited against Rothbury Systems Limited

Date: 11 June 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00123.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Rothbury Energy Limited against Rothbury Systems Limited in Fairmouth Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Rothbury Energy Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-494.

Vera Grenville signed the Licence LEG-494.Vera Ingram is the chair of Rothbury Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair International Bank Limited lent Rothbury Energy Limited GBP 50 million on 17 August 2022.

Rothbury Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Rothbury Energy Limited.

Rothbury Energy Limited owes Fair International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 22 August 2022.

Vera Neville is a shareholder of Rothbury Energy Limited.

Vera Fairfax is a family member of Vera Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Rothbury Energy Limited owns Merrivale Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Rothbury Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Rothbury Systems Limited on 11 June 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Montague noted that a former employee alleged that Rothbury Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
