Board meeting minutes of Hollowbrook Holdings Limited

Date: 2 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-01120.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Underwood LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Kerr, Nadia Jarvis and Alden & Underwood LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Hollowbrook Holdings Limited owns Larkspur Industries Limited.Amina Kerr is the chair of Hollowbrook Holdings Limited.

Lorne Sable is a shareholder of Hollowbrook Holdings Limited.

Amina Kerr is a family member of Anika Dunmore.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Hollowbrook Holdings Limited GBP 52 million on 17 September 2022.

Northcliff Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Hollowbrook Holdings Limited.

Hollowbrook Holdings Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 24 million as at 22 September 2022.

Cairn & Sable LLP represents Hollowbrook Holdings Limited in the matter.

Hollowbrook Holdings Limited owns Juniper Data Centre.

Juniper Data Centre is located in Fenwick Bay.

Hollowbrook Holdings Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-021.Anika Dunmore signed the Services Agreement LEG-021.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Wren Ellery, chair of Hollowbrook Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire North Underhill Systems Limited on 2 November 2025.

Wren Ellery confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Underhill Systems Limited by Hollowbrook Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Underhill Systems Limited by Hollowbrook Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
