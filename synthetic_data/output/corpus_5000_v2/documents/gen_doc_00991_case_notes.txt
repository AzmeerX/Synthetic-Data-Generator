Case notes for North Kirkby Systems Limited against North Lambley Group Limited

Date: 26 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00991.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Kirkby Systems Limited against North Lambley Group Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Kirkby Systems Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-065.

Juno Jarvis signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-065.Juno Lorne is the chair of North Kirkby Systems Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

North Lambley Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to North Kirkby Systems Limited.

North Kirkby Systems Limited owes Elm Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 13 December 2022.

Juno Quade is a shareholder of North Kirkby Systems Limited.

Juno Ivers is a family member of Juno Jarvis.4. Findings and allegations
