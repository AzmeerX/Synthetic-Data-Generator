Board meeting minutes of North Belmont Capital Limited

Date: 10 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00546.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Lorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Dunmore, Bastian Fenwick and Cairn & Lorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

North Belmont Capital Limited owns North Belmont Marine Limited.Bastian Dunmore is the chair of North Belmont Capital Limited.

Bastian Ivers is a shareholder of North Belmont Capital Limited.

Bastian Alden is a family member of Bastian Briar.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent North Belmont Capital Limited GBP 14 million on 27 October 2024.

North Belmont Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to North Belmont Capital Limited.

North Belmont Capital Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 1 November 2024.

Alden & Whitlock LLP represents North Belmont Capital Limited in the matter.

North Belmont Capital Limited owns Carrow Bay Warehouse.

Carrow Bay Warehouse is located in Mossgrove.

North Belmont Capital Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-270.Bastian Briar signed the Licence LEG-270.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Kellan Sable, chair of North Belmont Capital Limited, proposed to acquire North Belmont Systems Limited on 10 September 2024.

Kellan Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of North Belmont Systems Limited by North Belmont Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of North Belmont Systems Limited by North Belmont Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
