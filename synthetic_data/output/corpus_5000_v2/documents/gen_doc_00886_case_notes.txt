Case notes for North Draycott Industries Limited against North Draycott Logistics Limited

Date: 21 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00886.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Draycott Industries Limited against North Draycott Logistics Limited in Elmford Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Draycott Industries Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-577.

Hana Nyle signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-577.Hana Pell is the chair of North Draycott Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Kel Private Bank Limited lent North Draycott Industries Limited GBP 28 million on 4 October 2024.

North Draycott Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to North Draycott Industries Limited.

North Draycott Industries Limited owes Kel Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 9 October 2024.

Hana Ulric is a shareholder of North Draycott Industries Limited.

Hana Mercer is a family member of Hana Nyle.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

North Draycott Industries Limited owns North Draycott Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

North Draycott Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for North Draycott Logistics Limited on 21 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Sable noted that a former employee alleged that North Draycott Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
