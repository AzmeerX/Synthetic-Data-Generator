Board meeting minutes of Glenmoor Property Limited

Date: 24 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00077.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ravenscroft LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Thorne, Bastian Vale and Alden & Ravenscroft LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Glenmoor Property Limited owns Glenmoor Ventures Limited.Bastian Thorne is the chair of Glenmoor Property Limited.

Bastian Zell is a shareholder of Glenmoor Property Limited.

Bastian Quade is a family member of Bastian Rook.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Property Limited GBP 22 million on 9 April 2025.

Glenmoor Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Property Limited.

Glenmoor Property Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 14 April 2025.

Alden & Kerr LLP represents Glenmoor Property Limited in the matter.

Glenmoor Property Limited owns Greyhaven Dock.

Greyhaven Dock is located in Quarrymere.

Glenmoor Property Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-106.Bastian Rook signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-106.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Ravenscroft, chair of Glenmoor Property Limited, proposed to acquire Ormsby Energy Limited on 24 July 2025.

Amina Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Ormsby Energy Limited by Glenmoor Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Ormsby Energy Limited by Glenmoor Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
