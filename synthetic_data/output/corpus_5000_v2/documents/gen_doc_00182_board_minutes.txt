Board meeting minutes of Wilton Group Limited

Date: 15 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00182.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Quade LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Wren Montague, Wren Osborne and Alden & Quade LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Wilton Group Limited owns Wilton Logistics Limited.Wren Montague is the chair of Wilton Group Limited.

Wren Sinclair is a shareholder of Wilton Group Limited.

Wren Jeffreys is a family member of Wren Kingsley.

Oak International Bank Limited lent Wilton Group Limited GBP 38 million on 2 April 2024.

Wilton Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Wilton Group Limited.

Wilton Group Limited owes Oak International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 7 April 2024.

Cairn & Kerr LLP represents Wilton Group Limited in the matter.

Wilton Group Limited owns Pemberton Silo.

Pemberton Silo is located in Belmont Bay.

Wilton Group Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-548.Wren Kingsley signed the Board Resolution LEG-548.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Whitlock, chair of Wilton Group Limited, proposed to acquire Wilton Marine Limited on 15 April 2024.

Calder Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Wilton Marine Limited by Wilton Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Wilton Marine Limited by Wilton Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
