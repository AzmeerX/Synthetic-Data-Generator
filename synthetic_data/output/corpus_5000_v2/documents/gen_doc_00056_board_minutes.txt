Board meeting minutes of Marlowe Energy Limited

Date: 27 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00056.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ulric LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Ulric Yarrow, Ulric Ashby and Alden & Ulric LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Marlowe Energy Limited owns Jarrow Cross Dock.Ulric Yarrow is the chair of Marlowe Energy Limited.

Ulric Delacourt is a shareholder of Marlowe Energy Limited.

Ulric Ulric is a family member of Ulric Vale.

Marlowe Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Marlowe Energy Limited.

Marlowe Energy Limited owes Red International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 27 June 2025.

Cairn & Cavendish LLP represents Marlowe Energy Limited in the matter.

Marlowe Energy Limited owns Jarrow Cross Office Park.

Jarrow Cross Office Park is located in Jarrow Bridge.

Marlowe Energy Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-434.Ulric Vale signed the Facility Agreement LEG-434.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of Neston Industries Limited by Marlowe Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
