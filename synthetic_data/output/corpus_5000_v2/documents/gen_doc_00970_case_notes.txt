Case notes for North Jesmond Trading Limited against North Jesmond Marine Limited

Date: 1 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00970.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by North Jesmond Trading Limited against North Jesmond Marine Limited in Denmoor Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

North Jesmond Trading Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-046.

Ivo Pemberton signed the Licence LEG-046.Ivo Sinclair is the chair of North Jesmond Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent North Jesmond Trading Limited GBP 18 million on 13 May 2022.

North Jesmond Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to North Jesmond Trading Limited.

North Jesmond Trading Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 18 May 2022.

Ivo Wyndham is a shareholder of North Jesmond Trading Limited.

Ivo Osborne is a family member of Ivo Pemberton.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

The court gave directions for the next hearing.
