Case notes for Hazelmere Group Limited against Hazelmere Trading Limited

Date: 17 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-01747.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Hazelmere Group Limited against Hazelmere Trading Limited in Fairmouth Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Hazelmere Group Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-193.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-193.Lorne Vale is the chair of Hazelmere Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Trust Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Group Limited GBP 48 million on 14 October 2024.

Hazelmere Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Group Limited.

Hazelmere Group Limited owes Elm Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 19 October 2024.

Bram Nyle is a shareholder of Hazelmere Group Limited.

Lorne Cairn is a family member of Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Hazelmere Group Limited owns Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Hazelmere Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hazelmere Trading Limited on 17 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Zell noted that a former employee alleged that Hazelmere Group Limited misstated its revenue.
