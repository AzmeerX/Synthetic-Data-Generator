Facility agreement between Silvergate Bank Limited and Quarrystone Industries Limited

Date: 8 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00001.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Silvergate Bank Limited and the borrower is Quarrystone Industries Limited.

Quarrystone Industries Limited owns Thornbury Group Limited.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Quarrystone Industries Limited GBP 14 million on 20 February 2022.Fenwick Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Quarrystone Industries Limited.

Quarrystone Industries Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 25 February 2022.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

Silvergate Bank Limited provided a facility of GBP 20 million to Quarrystone Industries Limited on 1 June 2022.

Silvergate Bank Limited increased the facility available to Quarrystone Industries Limited to GBP 35 million on 1 June 2023.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Quarrystone Industries Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-002.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-002.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Quarrystone Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Caldervale Holdings Limited on 8 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
