Case notes for Caldervale Holdings Limited against Marrowgate Logistics Limited

Date: 29 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00004.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Caldervale Holdings Limited against Marrowgate Logistics Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Caldervale Holdings Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-005.

Ines Alden signed the Facility Agreement LEG-005.Petra Hale served as chair of Caldervale Holdings Limited until 30 June 2023.

Rhea Rook became chair of Caldervale Holdings Limited on 1 July 2023.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Caldervale Holdings Limited GBP 20 million on 25 March 2022.

Valebrook Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Caldervale Holdings Limited.

Caldervale Holdings Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 30 March 2022.

Ivo Dunmore is a shareholder of Caldervale Holdings Limited.

Petra Hale is a family member of Ines Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Caldervale Holdings Limited owns Juniper Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Caldervale Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Marrowgate Logistics Limited on 29 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Rook noted that a former employee alleged that Caldervale Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
