Board meeting minutes of Jarrow Energy Limited

Date: 27 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00056.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Briar LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Ellery, Calder Garrow and Alden & Briar LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Jarrow Energy Limited owns Jarrow Property Limited.Calder Ellery is the chair of Jarrow Energy Limited.

Calder Jarvis is a shareholder of Jarrow Energy Limited.

Calder Briar is a family member of Calder Cairn.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent Jarrow Energy Limited GBP 12 million on 29 April 2026.

Jarrow Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Jarrow Energy Limited.

Jarrow Energy Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 4 May 2026.

Alden & Pell LLP represents Jarrow Energy Limited in the matter.

Jarrow Energy Limited owns Calderport Works.

Calderport Works is located in Pemberton.

Jarrow Energy Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-015.Calder Cairn signed the Security Agreement LEG-015.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Ulric, chair of Jarrow Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Kelbrook Industries Limited on 27 February 2025.

Amina Ulric confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Kelbrook Industries Limited by Jarrow Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Kelbrook Industries Limited by Jarrow Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
