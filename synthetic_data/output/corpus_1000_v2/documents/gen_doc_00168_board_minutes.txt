Board meeting minutes of Stanbrook Logistics Limited

Date: 22 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00168.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Nyle LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ivers, Elias Kerr and Alden & Nyle LLP.

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
