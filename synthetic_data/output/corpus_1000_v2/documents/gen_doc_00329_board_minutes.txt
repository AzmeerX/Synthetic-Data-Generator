Board meeting minutes of Grimsdale Partners Limited

Date: 8 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00329.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Jarrah Dunmore, Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Alden & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Grimsdale Partners Limited owns Grimsdale Capital Limited.Jarrah Dunmore is the chair of Grimsdale Partners Limited.

Ines Alden is a shareholder of Grimsdale Partners Limited.

Hana Fenwick is a family member of Hana Garrow.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Partners Limited GBP 30 million on 7 July 2024.

Grimsdale Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Partners Limited.

Grimsdale Partners Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 12 July 2024.

Alden & Jeffreys LLP represents Grimsdale Partners Limited in the matter.

Grimsdale Partners Limited owns Greyhaven Fleet.

Greyhaven Fleet is located in Dunmere Cross.

Grimsdale Partners Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-184.Hana Garrow signed the Lease LEG-184.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Farah Wyndham, chair of Grimsdale Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Grimsdale Ventures Limited on 8 February 2027.

Farah Wyndham confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Grimsdale Ventures Limited by Grimsdale Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Grimsdale Ventures Limited by Grimsdale Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
