Case notes for Vellacott Property Limited against Westerby Holdings Limited

Date: 25 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00214.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Vellacott Property Limited against Westerby Holdings Limited in Bexhaven Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Vellacott Property Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-080.

Elias Zouch signed the Side Letter LEG-080.Farah Briar is the chair of Vellacott Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Vellacott Property Limited GBP 22 million on 14 January 2026.

Vellacott Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Property Limited.

Vellacott Property Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 19 January 2026.

Farah Garrow is a shareholder of Vellacott Property Limited.

Elias Yardley is a family member of Elias Zouch.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Vellacott Property Limited owns Vellacott Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Vellacott Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Westerby Holdings Limited on 25 November 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
