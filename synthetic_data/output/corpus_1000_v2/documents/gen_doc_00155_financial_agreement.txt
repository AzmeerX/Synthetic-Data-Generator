Facility agreement between Harrow Merchant Bank Limited and Redmoor Group Limited

Date: 21 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00155.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Harrow Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Redmoor Group Limited.

Redmoor Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Group Limited.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
