Case notes for Eastmoor Energy Limited against Eastmoor Ventures Limited

Date: 24 August 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00305.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Eastmoor Energy Limited against Eastmoor Ventures Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Eastmoor Energy Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.

Gideon Kingsley signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-163.Gideon Montague is the chair of Eastmoor Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Eastmoor Energy Limited GBP 28 million on 19 November 2023.

Eastmoor Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Eastmoor Energy Limited.

Eastmoor Energy Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 24 November 2023.

Gideon Sinclair is a shareholder of Eastmoor Energy Limited.

Gideon Jeffreys is a family member of Gideon Kingsley.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Eastmoor Energy Limited owns Eastmoor Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Eastmoor Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Eastmoor Ventures Limited on 24 August 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Eastmoor Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
