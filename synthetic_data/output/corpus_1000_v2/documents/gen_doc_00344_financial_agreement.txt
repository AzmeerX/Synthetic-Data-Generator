Facility agreement between Elm Bank Limited and Hazelmere Marine Limited

Date: 9 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00344.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Bank Limited and the borrower is Hazelmere Marine Limited.

Hazelmere Marine Limited owns Quarrystone Mill.Fair Merchant Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 8 December 2024.

Hazelmere Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Marine Limited.

Hazelmere Marine Limited owes Fair Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 13 December 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Hazelmere Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-198.

Ivo Dunmore signed the Loan Note LEG-198.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Hazelmere Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hazelmere Ventures Limited on 9 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
