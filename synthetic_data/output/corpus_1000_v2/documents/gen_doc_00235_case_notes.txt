Case notes for Yarmouth Trading Limited against Yarmouth Marine Limited

Date: 21 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00235.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yarmouth Trading Limited against Yarmouth Marine Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yarmouth Trading Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-099.

Farah Ulric signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-099.Farah Ward is the chair of Yarmouth Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Bank Limited lent Yarmouth Trading Limited GBP 20 million on 11 August 2026.

Yarmouth Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Yarmouth Trading Limited.

Yarmouth Trading Limited owes Glen Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 16 August 2026.

Farah Cavendish is a shareholder of Yarmouth Trading Limited.

Farah Thorne is a family member of Farah Ulric.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.
