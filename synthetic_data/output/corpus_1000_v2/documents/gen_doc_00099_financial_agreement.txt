Facility agreement between Glen Commercial Bank Limited and Merrivale Systems Limited

Date: 25 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00099.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Glen Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is Merrivale Systems Limited.

Merrivale Systems Limited owns Eastmere Hangar.Bexley International Bank Limited lent Merrivale Systems Limited GBP 50 million on 5 November 2022.

Netherby Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Systems Limited.

Merrivale Systems Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 10 November 2022.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Merrivale Systems Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-054.

Calder Ravenscroft signed the Loan Note LEG-054.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Merrivale Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Netherby Group Limited on 25 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
