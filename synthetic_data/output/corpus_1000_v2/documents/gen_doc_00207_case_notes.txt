Case notes for Ashcombe Logistics Limited against Vellacott Partners Limited

Date: 7 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00207.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ashcombe Logistics Limited against Vellacott Partners Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ashcombe Logistics Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.

Lorne Sable signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.Elias Orrick is the chair of Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Logistics Limited GBP 26 million on 3 December 2022.

Ashcombe Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

Ashcombe Logistics Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 8 December 2022.

Farah Sable is a shareholder of Ashcombe Logistics Limited.

Ivo Orrick is a family member of Lorne Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ashcombe Logistics Limited owns Calderport Marina.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ashcombe Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Vellacott Partners Limited on 7 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Ashcombe Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
