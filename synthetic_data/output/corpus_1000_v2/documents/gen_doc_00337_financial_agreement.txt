Facility agreement between Carrow International Bank Limited and Hazelmere Industries Limited

Date: 5 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00337.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Carrow International Bank Limited and the borrower is Hazelmere Industries Limited.

Hazelmere Industries Limited owns Hazelmere Group Limited.Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Industries Limited GBP 46 million on 3 October 2024.

Hazelmere Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Industries Limited.

Hazelmere Industries Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 8 October 2024.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Hazelmere Industries Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-192.

Lorne Cairn signed the Side Letter LEG-192.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Hazelmere Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hazelmere Logistics Limited on 5 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
