Case notes for Redmoor Partners Limited against Redmoor Energy Limited

Date: 11 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00158.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Redmoor Partners Limited against Redmoor Energy Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Redmoor Partners Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.

Daria Whitlock signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.Daria Yardley is the chair of Redmoor Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Redmoor Partners Limited GBP 38 million on 21 June 2024.

Redmoor Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Partners Limited.

Redmoor Partners Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 26 June 2024.

Elias Dunmore is a shareholder of Redmoor Partners Limited.

Daria Villiers is a family member of Daria Whitlock.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Redmoor Partners Limited owns Redmoor Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Redmoor Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Redmoor Energy Limited on 11 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Redmoor Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
