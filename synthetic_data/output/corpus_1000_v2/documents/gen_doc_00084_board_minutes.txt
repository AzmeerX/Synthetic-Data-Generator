Board meeting minutes of Lowmoor Ventures Limited

Date: 11 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00084.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Everard LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Fairfax, Calder Hastings and Alden & Everard LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Lowmoor Ventures Limited owns Lowmoor Systems Limited.Calder Fairfax is the chair of Lowmoor Ventures Limited.

Calder Kingsley is a shareholder of Lowmoor Ventures Limited.

Calder Cavendish is a family member of Calder Delacourt.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Ventures Limited GBP 24 million on 15 June 2022.

Merrivale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Ventures Limited.

Lowmoor Ventures Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 20 June 2022.

Alden & Lorne LLP represents Lowmoor Ventures Limited in the matter.

Lowmoor Ventures Limited owns Dunhollow Farm.

Dunhollow Farm is located in Rushmere.

Lowmoor Ventures Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-041.Calder Delacourt signed the Trust Deed LEG-041.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Yardley, chair of Lowmoor Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Merrivale Group Limited on 11 September 2025.

Amina Yardley confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Merrivale Group Limited by Lowmoor Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Merrivale Group Limited by Lowmoor Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
