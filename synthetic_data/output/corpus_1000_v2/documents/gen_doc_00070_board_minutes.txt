Board meeting minutes of Kelbrook Property Limited

Date: 5 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00070.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Pell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Rook, Calder Thorne and Alden & Pell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Calder Ward is a shareholder of Kelbrook Property Limited.

Calder Orrick is a family member of Calder Pell.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Kelbrook Property Limited GBP 38 million on 19 September 2026.

Kelbrook Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Kelbrook Property Limited.

Kelbrook Property Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 24 September 2026.

Pell & Ward LLP represents Kelbrook Property Limited in the matter.

Kelbrook Property Limited owns Dunhollow Warehouse.

Dunhollow Warehouse is located in Eastmoor.

Kelbrook Property Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.Calder Pell signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-028.

Amina Jeffreys confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Lowmoor Group Limited by Kelbrook Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Lowmoor Group Limited by Kelbrook Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
