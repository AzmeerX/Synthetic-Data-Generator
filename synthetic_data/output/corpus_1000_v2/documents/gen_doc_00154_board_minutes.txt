Board meeting minutes of Redmoor Industries Limited

Date: 14 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00154.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Quade & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Underwood, Daria Whitlock and Quade & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Redmoor Industries Limited owns Redmoor Group Limited.Daria Underwood is the chair of Redmoor Industries Limited.

Daria Zouch is a shareholder of Redmoor Industries Limited.

Daria Ravenscroft is a family member of Daria Sinclair.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Redmoor Industries Limited GBP 30 million on 8 May 2024.

Redmoor Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Industries Limited.

Redmoor Industries Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 13 May 2024.

Alden & Zell LLP represents Redmoor Industries Limited in the matter.

Redmoor Industries Limited owns Dunhollow Wharf.

Dunhollow Wharf is located in Aldergate.

Redmoor Industries Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-024.Daria Sinclair signed the Lease LEG-024.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Sable, chair of Redmoor Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Redmoor Capital Limited on 14 January 2027.

Calder Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Redmoor Capital Limited by Redmoor Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Redmoor Capital Limited by Redmoor Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
