Board meeting minutes of Brackenwold Group Limited

Date: 18 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00098.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Tremayne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Marek Orrick, Anika Dunmore and Alden & Tremayne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Brackenwold Group Limited owns Pinehurst Logistics Limited.Marek Orrick is the chair of Brackenwold Group Limited.

Ewan Zell is a shareholder of Brackenwold Group Limited.

Marek Orrick is a family member of Oren Jarvis.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent Brackenwold Group Limited GBP 48 million on 26 August 2022.

Hollowbrook Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Brackenwold Group Limited.

Brackenwold Group Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 22 million as at 31 August 2022.

Harrow & Finch LLP represents Brackenwold Group Limited in the matter.

Brackenwold Group Limited owns Harrowgate Warehouse.

Harrowgate Warehouse is located in Dunhollow.

Brackenwold Group Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-019.Oren Jarvis signed the Services Agreement LEG-019.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bastian Mercer, chair of Brackenwold Group Limited, proposed to acquire Netherby Logistics Limited on 18 December 2025.

The acquisition of Netherby Logistics Limited by Brackenwold Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
