Case notes for Glenmoor Logistics Limited against Glenmoor Capital Limited

Date: 18 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00011.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Glenmoor Logistics Limited against Glenmoor Capital Limited in Bexhaven Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Glenmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-100.

Bastian Lorne signed the Board Resolution LEG-100.Bastian Nyle is the chair of Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Logistics Limited GBP 50 million on 2 February 2025.

Glenmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

Glenmoor Logistics Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 7 February 2025.

Bastian Sable is a shareholder of Glenmoor Logistics Limited.

Bastian Kerr is a family member of Bastian Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Glenmoor Logistics Limited owns Greyhaven Warehouse.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Glenmoor Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Glenmoor Capital Limited on 18 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Rhea Ulric noted that a former employee alleged that Glenmoor Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
