Facility agreement between Elm Merchant Bank Limited and Lowmoor Systems Limited

Date: 18 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00085.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Lowmoor Systems Limited.

Lowmoor Systems Limited owns Dunhollow Dock.Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Systems Limited GBP 26 million on 26 June 2022.

Merrivale Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Systems Limited.

Lowmoor Systems Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 1 July 2022.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.
