Facility agreement between Elm Trust Bank Limited and Tarrow Systems Limited

Date: 10 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00190.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Trust Bank Limited and the borrower is Tarrow Systems Limited.

Tarrow Systems Limited owns Eastmere Yard.Dunmore Bank Limited lent Tarrow Systems Limited GBP 18 million on 17 May 2025.

Uldale Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Systems Limited.

Tarrow Systems Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 22 May 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Tarrow Systems Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-058.

Elias Cavendish signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-058.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Tarrow Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Uldale Group Limited on 10 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
