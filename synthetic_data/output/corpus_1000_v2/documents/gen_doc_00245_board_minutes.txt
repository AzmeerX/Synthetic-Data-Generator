Board meeting minutes of Aldergate Industries Limited

Date: 30 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00245.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Pemberton LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Grenville, Farah Ingram and Alden & Pemberton LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Aldergate Industries Limited owns Aldergate Group Limited.Farah Grenville is the chair of Aldergate Industries Limited.

Farah Langford is a shareholder of Aldergate Industries Limited.

Farah Delacourt is a family member of Farah Everard.

Silvergate Bank Limited lent Aldergate Industries Limited GBP 38 million on 24 March 2022.

Aldergate Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Industries Limited.

Aldergate Industries Limited owes Silvergate Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 29 March 2022.

Alden & Ivers LLP represents Aldergate Industries Limited in the matter.

Aldergate Industries Limited owns Harrowgate Hangar.

Harrowgate Hangar is located in Invermere.

Aldergate Industries Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-108.Farah Everard signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-108.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Kerr, chair of Aldergate Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Aldergate Capital Limited on 30 June 2025.

Elias Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Aldergate Capital Limited by Aldergate Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Aldergate Capital Limited by Aldergate Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
