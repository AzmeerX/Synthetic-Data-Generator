Date: 18 May 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00291.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Draycott Marine Limited against Draycott Ventures Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Draycott Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-150.
