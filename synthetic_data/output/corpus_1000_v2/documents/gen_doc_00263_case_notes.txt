Case notes for Barrowfield Partners Limited against Barrowfield Energy Limited

Date: 3 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00263.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Barrowfield Partners Limited against Barrowfield Energy Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Barrowfield Partners Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.

Farah Villiers signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-124.Farah Wyndham is the chair of Barrowfield Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent Barrowfield Partners Limited GBP 30 million on 16 September 2022.

Barrowfield Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Barrowfield Partners Limited.

Barrowfield Partners Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 21 September 2022.

Gideon Cairn is a shareholder of Barrowfield Partners Limited.

Farah Underwood is a family member of Farah Villiers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Barrowfield Partners Limited owns Barrowfield Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Barrowfield Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Barrowfield Energy Limited on 3 November 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Barrowfield Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
