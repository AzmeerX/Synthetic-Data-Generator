Case notes for Eastmoor Industries Limited against Eastmoor Trading Limited

Date: 6 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00298.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Eastmoor Industries Limited against Eastmoor Trading Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Eastmoor Industries Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-156.

Gideon Delacourt signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-156.Gideon Fairfax is the chair of Eastmoor Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen International Bank Limited lent Eastmoor Industries Limited GBP 14 million on 3 September 2023.

Eastmoor Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Eastmoor Industries Limited.

Eastmoor Industries Limited owes Glen International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 8 September 2023.

Gideon Kingsley is a shareholder of Eastmoor Industries Limited.

Gideon Cavendish is a family member of Gideon Delacourt.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Eastmoor Industries Limited owns Eastmoor Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Eastmoor Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Eastmoor Trading Limited on 6 July 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Eastmoor Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
