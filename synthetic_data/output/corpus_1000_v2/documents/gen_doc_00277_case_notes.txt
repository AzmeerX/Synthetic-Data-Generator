Case notes for Cresswell Capital Limited against Cresswell Property Limited

Date: 9 February 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00277.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Cresswell Capital Limited against Cresswell Property Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Cresswell Capital Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-137.

Gideon Ivers signed the Trust Deed LEG-137.Gideon Kerr is the chair of Cresswell Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Private Bank Limited lent Cresswell Capital Limited GBP 16 million on 6 February 2023.

Cresswell Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Cresswell Capital Limited.

Cresswell Capital Limited owes Denmoor Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 11 February 2023.

Gideon Quade is a shareholder of Cresswell Capital Limited.

Gideon Hale is a family member of Gideon Ivers.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Cresswell Capital Limited owns Cresswell Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Cresswell Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Cresswell Property Limited on 9 February 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Cresswell Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
