Facility agreement between Elm Private Bank Limited and Inglemere Group Limited

Date: 10 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00036.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Elm Private Bank Limited and the borrower is Inglemere Group Limited.

Inglemere Group Limited owns Inglemere Logistics Limited.Denmoor Bank Limited lent Inglemere Group Limited GBP 16 million on 13 October 2025.

Inglemere Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Group Limited.

Inglemere Group Limited owes Denmoor Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 18 October 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Inglemere Group Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-123.

Bastian Jeffreys signed the Debenture LEG-123.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Inglemere Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Inglemere Partners Limited on 10 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
