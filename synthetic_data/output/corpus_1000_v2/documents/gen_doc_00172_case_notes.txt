Case notes for Stanbrook Marine Limited against Stanbrook Property Limited

Date: 5 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00172.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Stanbrook Marine Limited against Stanbrook Property Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Stanbrook Marine Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-042.

Elias Kerr signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-042.Elias Mercer is the chair of Stanbrook Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Merchant Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Marine Limited GBP 26 million on 22 November 2024.

Stanbrook Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Marine Limited.

Stanbrook Marine Limited owes Fair Merchant Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 27 November 2024.

Elias Sable is a shareholder of Stanbrook Marine Limited.

Elias Jarvis is a family member of Elias Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Stanbrook Marine Limited owns Dunhollow Dock.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Stanbrook Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Stanbrook Property Limited on 5 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Stanbrook Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
