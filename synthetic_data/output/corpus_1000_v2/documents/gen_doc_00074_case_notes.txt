Case notes for Lowmoor Industries Limited against Lowmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 3 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00074.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Lowmoor Industries Limited against Lowmoor Logistics Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Lowmoor Industries Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-032.

Calder Thorne signed the Side Letter LEG-032.Calder Vale is the chair of Lowmoor Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Industries Limited GBP 46 million on 8 March 2022.

Lowmoor Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Industries Limited.

Lowmoor Industries Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 13 March 2022.

Calder Balfour is a shareholder of Lowmoor Industries Limited.

Calder Sable is a family member of Calder Thorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Lowmoor Industries Limited owns Lowmoor Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Lowmoor Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Lowmoor Logistics Limited on 3 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Neville noted that a former employee alleged that Lowmoor Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
