Board meeting minutes of Aldergate Marine Limited

Date: 18 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00252.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Cairn & Sable LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Montague, Farah Osborne and Cairn & Sable LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Aldergate Marine Limited owns Harrowgate Marina.Farah Montague is the chair of Aldergate Marine Limited.

Farah Sinclair is a shareholder of Aldergate Marine Limited.

Farah Jeffreys is a family member of Farah Kingsley.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Aldergate Marine Limited GBP 50 million on 29 May 2022.

Aldergate Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Marine Limited.

Aldergate Marine Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 3 June 2022.

Alden & Orrick LLP represents Aldergate Marine Limited in the matter.

Aldergate Marine Limited owns Harrowgate Mill.

Harrowgate Mill is located in Oakhurst.

Aldergate Marine Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-114.Farah Kingsley signed the Facility Agreement LEG-114.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Sable, chair of Aldergate Marine Limited, proposed to acquire Barrowfield Holdings Limited on 18 August 2025.

Elias Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Barrowfield Holdings Limited by Aldergate Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Barrowfield Holdings Limited by Aldergate Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
