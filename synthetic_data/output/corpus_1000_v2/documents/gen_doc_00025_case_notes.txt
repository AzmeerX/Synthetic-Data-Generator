Case notes for Harrowden Trading Limited against Harrowden Marine Limited

Date: 25 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00025.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Harrowden Trading Limited against Harrowden Marine Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Harrowden Trading Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-113.

Bastian Zell signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-113.Bastian Balfour is the chair of Harrowden Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent Harrowden Trading Limited GBP 36 million on 25 June 2025.

Harrowden Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Trading Limited.

Harrowden Trading Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 30 June 2025.

Bastian Grenville is a shareholder of Harrowden Trading Limited.

Bastian Yarrow is a family member of Bastian Zell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Harrowden Trading Limited owns Harrowden Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Harrowden Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Harrowden Marine Limited on 25 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Lorne Sable noted that a former employee alleged that Harrowden Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
