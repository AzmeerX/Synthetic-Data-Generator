Case notes for Cresswell Holdings Limited against Ashcombe Property Limited

Date: 22 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00270.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Cresswell Holdings Limited against Ashcombe Property Limited in Carrow Bay Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Cresswell Holdings Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.

Gideon Cairn signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-131.Gideon Ellery is the chair of Cresswell Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Private Bank Limited lent Cresswell Holdings Limited GBP 44 million on 2 December 2022.

Cresswell Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Cresswell Holdings Limited.

Cresswell Holdings Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 7 December 2022.

Gideon Jarvis is a shareholder of Cresswell Holdings Limited.

Gideon Briar is a family member of Gideon Cairn.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Cresswell Holdings Limited owns Cresswell Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Cresswell Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ashcombe Property Limited on 22 December 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Cresswell Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
