Board meeting minutes of Tarrow Ventures Limited

Date: 3 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00189.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Jeffreys LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Delacourt, Elias Fairfax and Alden & Jeffreys LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Tarrow Ventures Limited owns Tarrow Systems Limited.Elias Delacourt is the chair of Tarrow Ventures Limited.

Elias Ingram is a shareholder of Tarrow Ventures Limited.

Elias Ashby is a family member of Elias Balfour.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent Tarrow Ventures Limited GBP 16 million on 6 May 2025.

Uldale Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Ventures Limited.

Tarrow Ventures Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 11 May 2025.

Alden & Hale LLP represents Tarrow Ventures Limited in the matter.

Tarrow Ventures Limited owns Eastmere Dock.

Eastmere Dock is located in Kingsmoor.

Tarrow Ventures Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-057.Elias Balfour signed the Trust Deed LEG-057.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Dunmore, chair of Tarrow Ventures Limited, proposed to acquire Uldale Logistics Limited on 3 June 2024.

Daria Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Uldale Logistics Limited by Tarrow Ventures Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Uldale Logistics Limited by Tarrow Ventures Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
