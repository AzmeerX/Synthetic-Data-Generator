Board meeting minutes of Barrowfield Group Limited

Date: 6 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00259.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ellery LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Underwood, Farah Whitlock and Alden & Ellery LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Barrowfield Group Limited owns Barrowfield Logistics Limited.Farah Underwood is the chair of Barrowfield Group Limited.

Farah Zouch is a shareholder of Barrowfield Group Limited.

Farah Ravenscroft is a family member of Farah Sinclair.

Bexley Bank Limited lent Barrowfield Group Limited GBP 24 million on 14 August 2022.

Barrowfield Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Barrowfield Group Limited.

Barrowfield Group Limited owes Bexley Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 19 August 2022.

Alden & Vale LLP represents Barrowfield Group Limited in the matter.

Barrowfield Group Limited owns Ironside Rail Depot.

Ironside Rail Depot is located in Vellacott.

Barrowfield Group Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-121.Farah Sinclair signed the Trust Deed LEG-121.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Ashby, chair of Barrowfield Group Limited, proposed to acquire Barrowfield Capital Limited on 6 October 2025.

Elias Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Barrowfield Capital Limited by Barrowfield Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Barrowfield Capital Limited by Barrowfield Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
