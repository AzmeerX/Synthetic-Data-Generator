Board meeting minutes of Barrowfield Energy Limited

Date: 24 November 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00266.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Lorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Alden, Gideon Cairn and Alden & Lorne LLP.

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
