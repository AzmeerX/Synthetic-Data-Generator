Case notes for Hazelmere Ventures Limited against Belmont Partners Limited

Date: 1 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00347.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Hazelmere Ventures Limited against Belmont Partners Limited in Denmoor High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Hazelmere Ventures Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-201.

Mira Mercer signed the Trust Deed LEG-201.Farah Orrick is the chair of Hazelmere Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Private Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Ventures Limited GBP 24 million on 10 January 2025.

Oakhaven Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Ventures Limited.

Hazelmere Ventures Limited owes Fair Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 15 January 2025.

Quin Pell is a shareholder of Hazelmere Ventures Limited.

Bram Nyle is a family member of Mira Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Hazelmere Ventures Limited owns Hazelmere Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Hazelmere Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Belmont Partners Limited on 1 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Quade noted that a former employee alleged that Hazelmere Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
