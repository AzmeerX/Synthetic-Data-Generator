Case notes for Glenmoor Ventures Limited against Ardenmore Logistics Limited

Date: 6 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00018.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Glenmoor Ventures Limited against Ardenmore Logistics Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Glenmoor Ventures Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-107.

Bastian Sable signed the Debenture LEG-107.Bastian Ulric is the chair of Glenmoor Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Ventures Limited GBP 24 million on 20 April 2025.

Harrowden Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Ventures Limited.

Glenmoor Ventures Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 25 April 2025.

Bastian Ashby is a shareholder of Glenmoor Ventures Limited.

Bastian Rook is a family member of Bastian Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Glenmoor Ventures Limited owns Glenmoor Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Glenmoor Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ardenmore Logistics Limited on 6 June 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Marek Orrick noted that a former employee alleged that Glenmoor Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
