Case notes for Dunhollow Holdings Limited against Inglemere Industries Limited

Date: 12 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00032.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Dunhollow Holdings Limited against Inglemere Industries Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Dunhollow Holdings Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-013.

Daria Garrow signed the Security Agreement LEG-013.Ivo Dunmore is the chair of Dunhollow Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Dunhollow Holdings Limited GBP 36 million on 21 June 2022.

Ravenmoor Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Dunhollow Holdings Limited.

Dunhollow Holdings Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 16 million as at 26 June 2022.

Amina Kerr is a shareholder of Dunhollow Holdings Limited.

Ivo Dunmore is a family member of Daria Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Dunhollow Holdings Limited owns Greyhaven Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Dunhollow Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Inglemere Industries Limited on 12 September 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Sable noted that a former employee alleged that Dunhollow Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
