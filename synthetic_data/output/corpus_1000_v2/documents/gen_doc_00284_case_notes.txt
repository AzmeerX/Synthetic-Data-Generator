Case notes for Ashcombe Ventures Limited against Draycott Logistics Limited

Date: 30 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00284.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ashcombe Ventures Limited against Draycott Logistics Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ashcombe Ventures Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-035.

Farah Sable signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-035.Lorne Ward is the chair of Ashcombe Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Ventures Limited GBP 40 million on 18 February 2023.

Belmont Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Ventures Limited.

Ashcombe Ventures Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 23 February 2023.

Amina Cairn is a shareholder of Ashcombe Ventures Limited.

Mira Ivers is a family member of Farah Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ashcombe Ventures Limited owns Ashcombe Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ashcombe Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Draycott Logistics Limited on 30 March 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Alden noted that a former employee alleged that Ashcombe Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
