Case notes for Kelbrook Holdings Limited against Kelbrook Logistics Limited

Date: 27 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00060.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kelbrook Holdings Limited against Kelbrook Logistics Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kelbrook Holdings Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-019.

Calder Garrow signed the Services Agreement LEG-019.Calder Ivers is the chair of Kelbrook Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Trust Bank Limited lent Kelbrook Holdings Limited GBP 20 million on 12 June 2026.

Kelbrook Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Kelbrook Holdings Limited.

Kelbrook Holdings Limited owes Bexley Trust Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 17 June 2026.

Calder Nyle is a shareholder of Kelbrook Holdings Limited.

Calder Fenwick is a family member of Calder Garrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kelbrook Holdings Limited owns Kelbrook Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kelbrook Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kelbrook Logistics Limited on 27 March 2025.
