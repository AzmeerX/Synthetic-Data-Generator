Board meeting minutes of Ashcombe Group Limited

Date: 22 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00196.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ravenscroft LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nolan Quade, Xander Garrow and Alden & Ravenscroft LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Ashcombe Group Limited owns Ashcombe Logistics Limited.Nolan Quade is the chair of Ashcombe Group Limited.

Mira Ivers is a shareholder of Ashcombe Group Limited.

Ewan Zell is a family member of Ivo Orrick.

Caldervale Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Group Limited GBP 24 million on 22 November 2022.

Ashcombe Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Group Limited.

Ashcombe Group Limited owes Caldervale Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 27 November 2022.

Alden & Briar LLP represents Ashcombe Group Limited in the matter.

Ashcombe Group Limited owns Calderport Silo.

Calderport Silo is located in Bexhaven.

Ashcombe Group Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-027.Ivo Orrick signed the Trust Deed LEG-027.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

The acquisition of Uldale Property Limited by Ashcombe Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
