Case notes for Inglemere Systems Limited against Jarrow Group Limited

Date: 19 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00046.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Inglemere Systems Limited against Jarrow Group Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Inglemere Systems Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-006.

Bastian Tremayne signed the Facility Agreement LEG-006.Bastian Villiers is the chair of Inglemere Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Inglemere Systems Limited GBP 34 million on 20 January 2026.

Jarrow Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Systems Limited.

Inglemere Systems Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 25 January 2026.

Calder Alden is a shareholder of Inglemere Systems Limited.

Bastian Sinclair is a family member of Bastian Tremayne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Inglemere Systems Limited owns Valebrook Farm.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Inglemere Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Jarrow Group Limited on 19 December 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Jarvis noted that a former employee alleged that Inglemere Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
