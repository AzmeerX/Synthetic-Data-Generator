Board meeting minutes of Lowmoor Logistics Limited

Date: 24 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00077.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Yarrow, Calder Ashby and Alden & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Lowmoor Logistics Limited owns Dunhollow Data Centre.Calder Yarrow is the chair of Lowmoor Logistics Limited.

Calder Delacourt is a shareholder of Lowmoor Logistics Limited.

Calder Ulric is a family member of Calder Vale.

Denmoor International Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Logistics Limited GBP 50 million on 30 March 2022.

Lowmoor Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Logistics Limited.

Lowmoor Logistics Limited owes Denmoor International Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 4 April 2022.

Alden & Ellery LLP represents Lowmoor Logistics Limited in the matter.

Lowmoor Logistics Limited owns Dunhollow Works.

Dunhollow Works is located in Kirkby.

Lowmoor Logistics Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-034.Calder Vale signed the Facility Agreement LEG-034.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Ravenscroft, chair of Lowmoor Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Lowmoor Energy Limited on 24 July 2025.

Amina Ravenscroft confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Lowmoor Energy Limited by Lowmoor Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Lowmoor Energy Limited by Lowmoor Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
