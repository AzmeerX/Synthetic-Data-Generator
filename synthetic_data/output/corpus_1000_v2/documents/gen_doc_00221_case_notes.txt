Case notes for Westerby Logistics Limited against Westerby Capital Limited

Date: 13 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00221.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Westerby Logistics Limited against Westerby Capital Limited in Denmoor Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Westerby Logistics Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-086.

Farah Fenwick signed the Loan Note LEG-086.Farah Hale is the chair of Westerby Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor International Bank Limited lent Westerby Logistics Limited GBP 34 million on 21 March 2026.

Westerby Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Westerby Logistics Limited.

Westerby Logistics Limited owes Denmoor International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 26 March 2026.

Farah Mercer is a shareholder of Westerby Logistics Limited.

Farah Ellery is a family member of Farah Fenwick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Westerby Logistics Limited owns Greyhaven Wharf.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Westerby Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Westerby Capital Limited on 13 January 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
