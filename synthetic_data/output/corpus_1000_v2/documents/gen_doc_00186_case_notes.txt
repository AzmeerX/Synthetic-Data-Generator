Case notes for Tarrow Marine Limited against Tarrow Ventures Limited

Date: 13 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00186.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Tarrow Marine Limited against Tarrow Ventures Limited in Bexhaven Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Tarrow Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-054.

Elias Yarrow signed the Loan Note LEG-054.Elias Ashby is the chair of Tarrow Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Tarrow Marine Limited GBP 50 million on 3 April 2025.

Tarrow Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Marine Limited.

Tarrow Marine Limited owes Harrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 8 April 2025.

Elias Fairfax is a shareholder of Tarrow Marine Limited.

Elias Ward is a family member of Elias Yarrow.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Tarrow Marine Limited owns Eastmere Hangar.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Tarrow Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tarrow Ventures Limited on 13 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Alden noted that a former employee alleged that Tarrow Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
