Case notes for Uldale Energy Limited against Uldale Systems Limited

Date: 19 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00200.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Uldale Energy Limited against Uldale Systems Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Uldale Energy Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-067.

Elias Langford signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-067.Elias Neville is the chair of Uldale Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent Uldale Energy Limited GBP 36 million on 24 August 2025.

Uldale Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Energy Limited.

Uldale Energy Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 29 August 2025.

Elias Tremayne is a shareholder of Uldale Energy Limited.

Elias Kingsley is a family member of Elias Langford.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Uldale Energy Limited owns Uldale Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Uldale Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Uldale Systems Limited on 19 August 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Uldale Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
