Case notes for Pemberton Ventures Limited against Quintrell Industries Limited

Date: 17 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00137.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pemberton Ventures Limited against Quintrell Industries Limited in Bexhaven County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pemberton Ventures Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.

Daria Cavendish signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.Daria Everard is the chair of Pemberton Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Pemberton Ventures Limited GBP 40 million on 25 November 2023.

Quintrell Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Pemberton Ventures Limited.

Pemberton Ventures Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 30 November 2023.

Daria Jeffreys is a shareholder of Pemberton Ventures Limited.

Daria Balfour is a family member of Daria Cavendish.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pemberton Ventures Limited owns Pemberton Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pemberton Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Quintrell Industries Limited on 17 September 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Pemberton Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
