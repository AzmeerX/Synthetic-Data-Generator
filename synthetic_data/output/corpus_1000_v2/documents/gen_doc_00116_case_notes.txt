Case notes for Oakhurst Logistics Limited against Oakhurst Capital Limited

Date: 23 April 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00116.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Oakhurst Logistics Limited against Oakhurst Capital Limited in Carrow Bay High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Oakhurst Logistics Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-070.

Daria Ivers signed the Loan Note LEG-070.Daria Kerr is the chair of Oakhurst Logistics Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Oakhurst Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Oakhurst Logistics Limited.

Daria Pell is a shareholder of Oakhurst Logistics Limited.

Daria Hale is a family member of Daria Ivers.4. Findings and allegations
