Board meeting minutes of Quintrell Industries Limited

Date: 8 October 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00140.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Kingsley LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Hastings, Daria Jeffreys and Alden & Kingsley LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

The acquisition of Quintrell Partners Limited by Quintrell Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
