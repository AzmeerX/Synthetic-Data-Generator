Board meeting minutes of Uldale Systems Limited

Date: 9 September 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00203.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Pell & Ward LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ravenscroft, Elias Tremayne and Pell & Ward LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Uldale Systems Limited owns Fenwick Bay Fleet.Elias Ravenscroft is the chair of Uldale Systems Limited.

Elias Whitlock is a shareholder of Uldale Systems Limited.

Elias Neville is a family member of Elias Osborne.

Bexley Merchant Bank Limited lent Uldale Systems Limited GBP 42 million on 26 September 2025.

Vellacott Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Systems Limited.

Uldale Systems Limited owes Bexley Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 1 October 2025.

Alden & Ulric LLP represents Uldale Systems Limited in the matter.

Uldale Systems Limited owns Fenwick Bay Hangar.

Fenwick Bay Hangar is located in Belmont Port.

Uldale Systems Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-070.Elias Osborne signed the Loan Note LEG-070.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Sable, chair of Uldale Systems Limited, proposed to acquire Vellacott Logistics Limited on 9 September 2024.

Daria Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Vellacott Logistics Limited by Uldale Systems Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Vellacott Logistics Limited by Uldale Systems Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
