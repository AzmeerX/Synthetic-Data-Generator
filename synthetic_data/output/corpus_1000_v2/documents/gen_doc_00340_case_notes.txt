Case notes for Hazelmere Logistics Limited against Hazelmere Capital Limited

Date: 26 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00340.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Hazelmere Logistics Limited against Hazelmere Capital Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Hazelmere Logistics Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-194.

Kellan Lorne signed the Facility Agreement LEG-194.Kira Kerr is the chair of Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Commercial Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Logistics Limited GBP 50 million on 25 October 2024.

Hazelmere Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

Hazelmere Logistics Limited owes Elm Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 30 October 2024.

Mira Mercer is a shareholder of Hazelmere Logistics Limited.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department is a family member of Kellan Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Hazelmere Logistics Limited owns Marrow Bay Marina.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Hazelmere Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hazelmere Capital Limited on 26 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Hazelmere Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
