Case notes for Yarmouth Ventures Limited against Aldergate Industries Limited

Date: 9 June 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00242.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Yarmouth Ventures Limited against Aldergate Industries Limited in Carrow Bay Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Yarmouth Ventures Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-105.

Farah Balfour signed the Trust Deed LEG-105.Farah Delacourt is the chair of Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Harrow Bank Limited lent Yarmouth Ventures Limited GBP 32 million on 19 February 2022.

Aldergate Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

Yarmouth Ventures Limited owes Harrow Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 24 February 2022.

Farah Ingram is a shareholder of Yarmouth Ventures Limited.

Farah Ashby is a family member of Farah Balfour.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Yarmouth Ventures Limited owns Yarmouth Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Yarmouth Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Aldergate Industries Limited on 9 June 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Hale noted that a former employee alleged that Yarmouth Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
