Case notes for Pemberton Trading Limited against Pemberton Capital Limited

Date: 30 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00130.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pemberton Trading Limited against Pemberton Capital Limited in Alderport Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pemberton Trading Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-003.

Daria Vale signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-003.Daria Yarrow is the chair of Pemberton Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Pemberton Trading Limited GBP 28 million on 20 September 2023.

Pemberton Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Pemberton Trading Limited.

Pemberton Trading Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 25 September 2023.

Daria Delacourt is a shareholder of Pemberton Trading Limited.

Daria Ulric is a family member of Daria Vale.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pemberton Trading Limited owns Pemberton Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pemberton Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Pemberton Capital Limited on 30 July 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Pemberton Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
