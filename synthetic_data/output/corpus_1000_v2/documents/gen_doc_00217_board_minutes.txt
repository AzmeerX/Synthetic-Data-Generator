Board meeting minutes of Westerby Holdings Limited

Date: 16 December 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00217.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Mercer LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Farah Ellery, Farah Garrow and Alden & Mercer LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Westerby Holdings Limited owns Westerby Industries Limited.Farah Ellery is the chair of Westerby Holdings Limited.

Farah Jarvis is a shareholder of Westerby Holdings Limited.

Farah Briar is a family member of Farah Cairn.

Denmoor Trust Bank Limited lent Westerby Holdings Limited GBP 28 million on 16 February 2026.

Westerby Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Westerby Holdings Limited.

Westerby Holdings Limited owes Denmoor Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 21 February 2026.

Alden & Ingram LLP represents Westerby Holdings Limited in the matter.

Westerby Holdings Limited owns Greyhaven Rail Depot.

Greyhaven Rail Depot is located in Dunmere Bay.

Westerby Holdings Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-083.Farah Cairn signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-083.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Hastings, chair of Westerby Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire Westerby Trading Limited on 16 December 2024.

Daria Hastings confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Westerby Trading Limited by Westerby Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Westerby Trading Limited by Westerby Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
