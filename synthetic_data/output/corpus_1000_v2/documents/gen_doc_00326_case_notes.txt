Case notes for Grimsdale Logistics Limited against Grimsdale Partners Limited

Date: 18 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00326.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Grimsdale Logistics Limited against Grimsdale Partners Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Grimsdale Logistics Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-182.

Hana Ellery signed the Loan Note LEG-182.Hana Garrow is the chair of Grimsdale Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Commercial Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Logistics Limited GBP 26 million on 15 June 2024.

Grimsdale Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Logistics Limited.

Grimsdale Logistics Limited owes Carrow Commercial Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 20 June 2024.

Marek Nyle is a shareholder of Grimsdale Logistics Limited.

Hana Dunmore is a family member of Hana Ellery.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Grimsdale Logistics Limited owns Larkspur Wharf.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Grimsdale Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Grimsdale Partners Limited on 18 January 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Farah Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Grimsdale Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
