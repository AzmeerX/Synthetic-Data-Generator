Case notes for Aldergate Partners Limited against Aldergate Marine Limited

Date: 28 July 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00249.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Aldergate Partners Limited against Aldergate Marine Limited in Denmoor County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Aldergate Partners Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-112.

Farah Ingram signed the Side Letter LEG-112.Farah Kingsley is the chair of Aldergate Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fenwick Bank Limited lent Aldergate Partners Limited GBP 46 million on 7 May 2022.

Aldergate Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Partners Limited.

Aldergate Partners Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 12 May 2022.

Farah Pemberton is a shareholder of Aldergate Partners Limited.

Farah Hastings is a family member of Farah Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Aldergate Partners Limited owns Aldergate Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Aldergate Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Aldergate Marine Limited on 28 July 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Pell noted that a former employee alleged that Aldergate Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
