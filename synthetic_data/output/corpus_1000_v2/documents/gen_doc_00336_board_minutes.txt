Board meeting minutes of Hazelmere Holdings Limited

Date: 29 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00336.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Grenville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department, Lorne Vale and Alden & Grenville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Hazelmere Holdings Limited owns Hazelmere Industries Limited.Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department is the chair of Hazelmere Holdings Limited.

Ivo Dunmore is a shareholder of Hazelmere Holdings Limited.

Petra Hale is a family member of Ines Alden.

Elm Bank Limited lent Hazelmere Holdings Limited GBP 44 million on 22 September 2024.

Hazelmere Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Hazelmere Holdings Limited.

Hazelmere Holdings Limited owes Elm Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 27 September 2024.

Alden & Ravenscroft LLP represents Hazelmere Holdings Limited in the matter.

Hazelmere Holdings Limited owns Northcliff Works.

Northcliff Works is located in Fenwick Bay.

Hazelmere Holdings Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-191.Ines Alden signed the Power of Attorney LEG-191.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Gideon Ellery, chair of Hazelmere Holdings Limited, proposed to acquire Hazelmere Trading Limited on 29 March 2027.

Gideon Ellery confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Hazelmere Trading Limited by Hazelmere Holdings Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Hazelmere Trading Limited by Hazelmere Holdings Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
