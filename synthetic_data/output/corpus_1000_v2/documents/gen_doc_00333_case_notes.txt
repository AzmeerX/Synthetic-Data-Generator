Case notes for Grimsdale Property Limited against Hazelmere Holdings Limited

Date: 8 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00333.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Grimsdale Property Limited against Hazelmere Holdings Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Grimsdale Property Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-188.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-188.Petra Hale is the chair of Grimsdale Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent Grimsdale Property Limited GBP 38 million on 20 August 2024.

Grimsdale Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Grimsdale Property Limited.

Grimsdale Property Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 25 August 2024.

Lorne Vale is a shareholder of Grimsdale Property Limited.

Ines Hale is a family member of Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Grimsdale Property Limited owns Grimsdale Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Grimsdale Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Hazelmere Holdings Limited on 8 March 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Gideon Briar noted that a former employee alleged that Grimsdale Property Limited misstated its revenue.
