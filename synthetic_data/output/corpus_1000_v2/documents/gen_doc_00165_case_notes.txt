Case notes for Stanbrook Holdings Limited against Stanbrook Logistics Limited

Date: 1 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00165.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Stanbrook Holdings Limited against Stanbrook Logistics Limited in Carrow Bay Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Stanbrook Holdings Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-035.

Elias Dunmore signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-035.Elias Fenwick is the chair of Stanbrook Holdings Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Elm Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Holdings Limited GBP 12 million on 6 September 2024.

Stanbrook Group Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Holdings Limited.

Stanbrook Holdings Limited owes Elm Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 11 September 2024.

Elias Kerr is a shareholder of Stanbrook Holdings Limited.

Elias Cairn is a family member of Elias Dunmore.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Stanbrook Holdings Limited owns Stanbrook Industries Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Stanbrook Holdings Limited did not guarantee the facility for Stanbrook Logistics Limited on 1 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Stanbrook Holdings Limited misstated its revenue.
