Case notes for Pinehurst Logistics Limited against Netherby Systems Limited

Date: 5 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00109.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Pinehurst Logistics Limited against Netherby Systems Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Pinehurst Logistics Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-020.

Amina Kerr signed the Services Agreement LEG-020.Oren Jarvis is the chair of Pinehurst Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Pinehurst Logistics Limited GBP 50 million on 6 September 2022.

Larkspur Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Pinehurst Logistics Limited.

Pinehurst Logistics Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 23 million as at 11 September 2022.

Ivo Orrick is a shareholder of Pinehurst Logistics Limited.

Oren Jarvis is a family member of Amina Kerr.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Pinehurst Logistics Limited owns Westmarch Office Park.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Pinehurst Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Netherby Systems Limited on 5 March 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Yarrow noted that a former employee alleged that Pinehurst Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
