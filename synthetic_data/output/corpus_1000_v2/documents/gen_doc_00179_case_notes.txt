Case notes for Tarrow Industries Limited against Tarrow Trading Limited

Date: 25 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00179.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Tarrow Industries Limited against Tarrow Trading Limited in Alderport High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Tarrow Industries Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-048.

Elias Rook signed the Side Letter LEG-048.Elias Thorne is the chair of Tarrow Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Glen Merchant Bank Limited lent Tarrow Industries Limited GBP 38 million on 27 January 2025.

Tarrow Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Industries Limited.

Tarrow Industries Limited owes Glen Merchant Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 1 February 2025.

Elias Zell is a shareholder of Tarrow Industries Limited.

Elias Quade is a family member of Elias Rook.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Tarrow Industries Limited owns Tarrow Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Tarrow Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tarrow Trading Limited on 25 March 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Tarrow Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
