Case notes for Quintrell Trading Limited against Quintrell Marine Limited

Date: 5 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00144.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Quintrell Trading Limited against Quintrell Marine Limited in Carrow Bay Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Quintrell Trading Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-015.

Daria Ingram signed the Security Agreement LEG-015.Daria Kingsley is the chair of Quintrell Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent Quintrell Trading Limited GBP 12 million on 30 January 2024.

Quintrell Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Quintrell Trading Limited.

Quintrell Trading Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 4 February 2024.

Daria Pemberton is a shareholder of Quintrell Trading Limited.

Daria Hastings is a family member of Daria Ingram.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Quintrell Trading Limited owns Quintrell Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Quintrell Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Quintrell Marine Limited on 5 November 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Quintrell Trading Limited misstated its revenue.
