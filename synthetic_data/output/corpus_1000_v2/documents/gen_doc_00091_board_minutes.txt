Board meeting minutes of Merrivale Trading Limited

Date: 30 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00091.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Langford LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Langford, Calder Neville and Alden & Langford LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Merrivale Trading Limited owns Merrivale Partners Limited.Calder Langford is the chair of Merrivale Trading Limited.

Calder Ravenscroft is a shareholder of Merrivale Trading Limited.

Calder Ingram is a family member of Calder Jeffreys.

Alder Private Bank Limited lent Merrivale Trading Limited GBP 36 million on 20 August 2022.

Merrivale Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Trading Limited.

Merrivale Trading Limited owes Alder Private Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 25 August 2022.

Alden & Rook LLP represents Merrivale Trading Limited in the matter.

Merrivale Trading Limited owns Eastmere Warehouse.

Eastmere Warehouse is located in Yewdale.

Merrivale Trading Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-047.Calder Jeffreys signed the Power of Attorney LEG-047.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bastian Fenwick, chair of Merrivale Trading Limited, proposed to acquire Merrivale Property Limited on 30 October 2025.

Bastian Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Merrivale Property Limited by Merrivale Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Merrivale Property Limited by Merrivale Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
