Case notes for Westerby Ventures Limited against Yarmouth Holdings Limited

Date: 3 March 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00228.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Westerby Ventures Limited against Yarmouth Holdings Limited in Alderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Westerby Ventures Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-093.

Farah Mercer signed the Supply Agreement LEG-093.Farah Pell is the chair of Westerby Ventures Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fair Bank Limited lent Westerby Ventures Limited GBP 48 million on 6 June 2026.

Yarmouth Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Westerby Ventures Limited.

Westerby Ventures Limited owes Fair Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 11 June 2026.

Farah Vale is a shareholder of Westerby Ventures Limited.

Farah Lorne is a family member of Farah Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Westerby Ventures Limited owns Westerby Systems Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Westerby Ventures Limited did not guarantee the facility for Yarmouth Holdings Limited on 3 March 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Tremayne noted that a former employee alleged that Westerby Ventures Limited misstated its revenue.
