Case notes for Oakhurst Property Limited against Pemberton Holdings Limited

Date: 11 June 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00123.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Oakhurst Property Limited against Pemberton Holdings Limited in Denmoor Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Oakhurst Property Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-076.

Daria Orrick signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-076.Daria Quade is the chair of Oakhurst Property Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Oakhurst Property Limited GBP 14 million on 5 July 2023.

Oakhurst Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Oakhurst Property Limited.

Oakhurst Property Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 10 July 2023.

Daria Vale is a shareholder of Oakhurst Property Limited.

Daria Nyle is a family member of Daria Orrick.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Oakhurst Property Limited owns Oakhurst Ventures Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Oakhurst Property Limited did not guarantee the facility for Pemberton Holdings Limited on 11 June 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
