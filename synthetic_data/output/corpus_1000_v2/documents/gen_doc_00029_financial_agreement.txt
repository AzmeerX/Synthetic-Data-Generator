Facility agreement between Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited and Harrowden Energy Limited

Date: 22 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00029.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is Harrowden Energy Limited.

Harrowden Energy Limited owns Harrowden Property Limited.Carrow Bank Limited lent Harrowden Energy Limited GBP 44 million on 8 August 2025.

Harrowden Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Energy Limited.

Harrowden Energy Limited owes Carrow Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 13 August 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Harrowden Energy Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-117.

Bastian Delacourt signed the Security Agreement LEG-117.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
