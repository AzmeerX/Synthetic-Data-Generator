Board meeting minutes of Redmoor Energy Limited

Date: 4 March 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00161.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Garrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Briar, Elias Dunmore and Alden & Garrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Redmoor Energy Limited owns Redmoor Property Limited.Elias Briar is the chair of Redmoor Energy Limited.

Elias Garrow is a shareholder of Redmoor Energy Limited.

Daria Yardley is a family member of Daria Zouch.

Denmoor Trust Bank Limited lent Redmoor Energy Limited GBP 44 million on 24 July 2024.

Redmoor Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Redmoor Energy Limited.

Redmoor Energy Limited owes Denmoor Trust Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 29 July 2024.

Alden & Grenville LLP represents Redmoor Energy Limited in the matter.

Redmoor Energy Limited owns Dunhollow Hangar.

Dunhollow Hangar is located in Hazelmere.

Redmoor Energy Limited is a party to the Power of Attorney LEG-031.Daria Zouch signed the Power of Attorney LEG-031.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Ashby, chair of Redmoor Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Stanbrook Industries Limited on 4 March 2027.

Calder Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Stanbrook Industries Limited by Redmoor Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Stanbrook Industries Limited by Redmoor Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
