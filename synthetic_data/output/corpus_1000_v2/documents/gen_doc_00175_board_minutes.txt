Board meeting minutes of Stanbrook Property Limited

Date: 26 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00175.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ulric LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Pell, Elias Rook and Alden & Ulric LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Stanbrook Property Limited owns Stanbrook Ventures Limited.Elias Pell is the chair of Stanbrook Property Limited.

Elias Ulric is a shareholder of Stanbrook Property Limited.

Elias Lorne is a family member of Elias Mercer.

Fair Commercial Bank Limited lent Stanbrook Property Limited GBP 30 million on 14 December 2024.

Stanbrook Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Stanbrook Property Limited.

Stanbrook Property Limited owes Fair Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 19 December 2024.

Alden & Underwood LLP represents Stanbrook Property Limited in the matter.

Stanbrook Property Limited owns Eastmere Rail Depot.

Eastmere Rail Depot is located in Underhill.

Stanbrook Property Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-044.Elias Mercer signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-044.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Osborne, chair of Stanbrook Property Limited, proposed to acquire Tarrow Group Limited on 26 February 2024.

Calder Osborne confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Tarrow Group Limited by Stanbrook Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Tarrow Group Limited by Stanbrook Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
