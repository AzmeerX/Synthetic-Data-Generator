Board meeting minutes of Vellacott Partners Limited

Date: 28 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00210.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Fenwick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Wyndham, Elias Zouch and Alden & Fenwick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Vellacott Partners Limited owns Vellacott Capital Limited.Elias Wyndham is the chair of Vellacott Partners Limited.

Farah Cairn is a shareholder of Vellacott Partners Limited.

Elias Underwood is a family member of Elias Villiers.

Carrow Merchant Bank Limited lent Vellacott Partners Limited GBP 14 million on 1 December 2025.

Vellacott Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Vellacott Partners Limited.

Vellacott Partners Limited owes Carrow Merchant Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 6 December 2025.

Alden & Balfour LLP represents Vellacott Partners Limited in the matter.

Vellacott Partners Limited owns Fenwick Bay Mill.

Fenwick Bay Mill is located in Carrow Cross.

Vellacott Partners Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-076.Elias Villiers signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-076.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Daria Ashby, chair of Vellacott Partners Limited, proposed to acquire Vellacott Ventures Limited on 28 October 2024.

Daria Ashby confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Vellacott Ventures Limited by Vellacott Partners Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Vellacott Ventures Limited by Vellacott Partners Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
