Facility agreement between Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited and Tarrow Partners Limited

Date: 22 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00183.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Denmoor Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Tarrow Partners Limited.

Tarrow Partners Limited owns Tarrow Capital Limited.Glen International Bank Limited lent Tarrow Partners Limited GBP 46 million on 12 March 2025.

Tarrow Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Partners Limited.

Tarrow Partners Limited owes Glen International Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 17 March 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Tarrow Partners Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-052.

Elias Vale signed the Board Resolution LEG-052.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Tarrow Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Tarrow Marine Limited on 22 April 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
