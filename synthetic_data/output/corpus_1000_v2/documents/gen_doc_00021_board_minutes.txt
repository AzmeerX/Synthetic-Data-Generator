Board meeting minutes of Ardenmore Logistics Limited

Date: 27 June 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00021.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Quade LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Rhea Ulric, Bram Nyle and Alden & Quade LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Ardenmore Logistics Limited owns Northcliff Works.Rhea Ulric is the chair of Ardenmore Logistics Limited.

Oren Jarvis is a shareholder of Ardenmore Logistics Limited.

Rhea Ulric is a family member of Ivo Dunmore.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Ardenmore Logistics Limited GBP 34 million on 10 June 2022.

Greyhaven Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Ardenmore Logistics Limited.

Ardenmore Logistics Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 15 million as at 15 June 2022.

Yarrow & Zell LLP represents Ardenmore Logistics Limited in the matter.

Ardenmore Logistics Limited owns Valebrook Farm.

Valebrook Farm is located in Ironside.

Ardenmore Logistics Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-012.Ivo Dunmore signed the Board Resolution LEG-012.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Anika Dunmore, chair of Ardenmore Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Harrowden Partners Limited on 27 June 2024.

Anika Dunmore confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Harrowden Partners Limited by Ardenmore Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Harrowden Partners Limited by Ardenmore Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
