Board meeting minutes of Tarrow Trading Limited

Date: 15 April 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00182.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Cavendish LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Elias Ward, Elias Zell and Alden & Cavendish LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Tarrow Trading Limited owns Tarrow Partners Limited.Elias Ward is the chair of Tarrow Trading Limited.

Elias Cavendish is a shareholder of Tarrow Trading Limited.

Elias Thorne is a family member of Elias Ulric.

Glen Private Bank Limited lent Tarrow Trading Limited GBP 44 million on 1 March 2025.

Tarrow Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Tarrow Trading Limited.

Tarrow Trading Limited owes Glen Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 6 March 2025.

Alden & Briar LLP represents Tarrow Trading Limited in the matter.

Tarrow Trading Limited owns Eastmere Quay.

Eastmere Quay is located in Denholm.

Tarrow Trading Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-051.Elias Ulric signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-051.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Whitlock, chair of Tarrow Trading Limited, proposed to acquire Tarrow Energy Limited on 15 April 2024.

Calder Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Tarrow Energy Limited by Tarrow Trading Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Tarrow Energy Limited by Tarrow Trading Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
