Board meeting minutes of Cresswell Property Limited

Date: 2 March 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00280.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ashby LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Gideon Orrick, Gideon Quade and Alden & Ashby LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Cresswell Property Limited owns Cresswell Ventures Limited.Gideon Orrick is the chair of Cresswell Property Limited.

Gideon Thorne is a shareholder of Cresswell Property Limited.

Gideon Kerr is a family member of Gideon Lorne.

Elm Merchant Bank Limited lent Cresswell Property Limited GBP 22 million on 11 March 2023.

Cresswell Systems Limited guaranteed the loan to Cresswell Property Limited.

Cresswell Property Limited owes Elm Merchant Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 16 March 2023.

Alden & Pemberton LLP represents Cresswell Property Limited in the matter.

Cresswell Property Limited owns Juniper Cross Rail Depot.

Juniper Cross Rail Depot is located in Quarrymere.

Cresswell Property Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-140.Gideon Lorne signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-140.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Elias Whitlock, chair of Cresswell Property Limited, proposed to acquire Draycott Industries Limited on 2 March 2026.

Elias Whitlock confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Draycott Industries Limited by Cresswell Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Draycott Industries Limited by Cresswell Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2026, which runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.
