Board meeting minutes of Kelbrook Logistics Limited

Date: 17 April 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00063.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Ivers LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Calder Lorne, Calder Nyle and Alden & Ivers LLP.

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.
