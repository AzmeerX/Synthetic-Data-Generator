Board meeting minutes of Hazelmere Capital Limited

Date: 2 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00343.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Neville LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Daria Garrow, Mira Mercer and Alden & Neville LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.
