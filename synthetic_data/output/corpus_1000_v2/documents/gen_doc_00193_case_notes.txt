Case notes for Uldale Group Limited against Ashcombe Group Limited

Date: 1 July 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00193.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Uldale Group Limited against Ashcombe Group Limited in Carrow Bay County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Uldale Group Limited is a party to the Supply Agreement LEG-061.

Elias Fairfax signed the Supply Agreement LEG-061.Elias Hastings is the chair of Uldale Group Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Uldale Group Limited GBP 24 million on 19 June 2025.

Uldale Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Uldale Group Limited.

Uldale Group Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 24 June 2025.

Elias Montague is a shareholder of Uldale Group Limited.

Elias Everard is a family member of Elias Fairfax.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Uldale Group Limited owns Uldale Logistics Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Uldale Group Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ashcombe Group Limited on 1 July 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Daria Ivers noted that a former employee alleged that Uldale Group Limited misstated its revenue.
