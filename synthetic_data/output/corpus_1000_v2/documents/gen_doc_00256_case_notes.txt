Case notes for Aldergate Systems Limited against Barrowfield Group Limited

Date: 15 September 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00256.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Aldergate Systems Limited against Barrowfield Group Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Aldergate Systems Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-118.

Farah Osborne signed the Loan Note LEG-118.Farah Ravenscroft is the chair of Aldergate Systems Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Commercial Bank Limited lent Aldergate Systems Limited GBP 18 million on 12 July 2022.

Barrowfield Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Aldergate Systems Limited.

Aldergate Systems Limited owes Alder Commercial Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 17 July 2022.

Farah Whitlock is a shareholder of Aldergate Systems Limited.

Farah Neville is a family member of Farah Osborne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Aldergate Systems Limited owns Harrowgate Mill.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Aldergate Systems Limited did not guarantee the facility for Barrowfield Group Limited on 15 September 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Elias Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Aldergate Systems Limited misstated its revenue.
