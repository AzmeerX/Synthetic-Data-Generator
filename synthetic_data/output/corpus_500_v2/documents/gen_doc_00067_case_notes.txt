Case notes for Kelbrook Capital Limited against Kelbrook Property Limited

Date: 15 May 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00067.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Kelbrook Capital Limited against Kelbrook Property Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Kelbrook Capital Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-025.

Calder Mercer signed the Trust Deed LEG-025.Calder Orrick is the chair of Kelbrook Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Trust Bank Limited lent Kelbrook Capital Limited GBP 32 million on 17 August 2026.

Kelbrook Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Kelbrook Capital Limited.

Kelbrook Capital Limited owes Carrow Trust Bank Limited GBP 4 million as at 22 August 2026.

Calder Thorne is a shareholder of Kelbrook Capital Limited.

Calder Lorne is a family member of Calder Mercer.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Kelbrook Capital Limited owns Kelbrook Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Kelbrook Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Kelbrook Property Limited on 15 May 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Grenville noted that a former employee alleged that Kelbrook Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
