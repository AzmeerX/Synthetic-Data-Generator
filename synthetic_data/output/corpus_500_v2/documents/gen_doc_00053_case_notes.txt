Case notes for Jarrow Capital Limited against Jarrow Energy Limited

Date: 6 February 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00053.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Jarrow Capital Limited against Jarrow Energy Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Jarrow Capital Limited is a party to the Security Agreement LEG-013.

Calder Alden signed the Security Agreement LEG-013.Calder Cairn is the chair of Jarrow Capital Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Trust Bank Limited lent Jarrow Capital Limited GBP 48 million on 7 April 2026.

Jarrow Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Jarrow Capital Limited.

Jarrow Capital Limited owes Alder Trust Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 12 April 2026.

Calder Hale is a shareholder of Jarrow Capital Limited.

Bastian Zouch is a family member of Calder Alden.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Jarrow Capital Limited owns Jarrow Marine Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Jarrow Capital Limited did not guarantee the facility for Jarrow Energy Limited on 6 February 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Rook noted that a former employee alleged that Jarrow Capital Limited misstated its revenue.
