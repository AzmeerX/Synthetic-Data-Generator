Board meeting minutes of Belmont Property Limited

Date: 14 January 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00154.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Quade & Thorne LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Amina Ivers, Amina Lorne and Quade & Thorne LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Belmont Property Limited owns Belmont Ventures Limited.Amina Ivers is the chair of Belmont Property Limited.

Amina Orrick is a shareholder of Belmont Property Limited.

Amina Fenwick is a family member of Amina Garrow.

Yarrow & Zell LLP represents Belmont Property Limited in the matter.

Belmont Property Limited owns Silverbeck Plant.

Silverbeck Plant is located in Alderport.

Belmont Property Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-046.Amina Garrow signed the Licence LEG-046.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Calder Sable confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Carrow Group Limited by Belmont Property Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Carrow Group Limited by Belmont Property Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2027, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
