Case notes for Inglemere Partners Limited against Inglemere Energy Limited

Date: 31 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00039.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Inglemere Partners Limited against Inglemere Energy Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Inglemere Partners Limited is a party to the Licence LEG-126.

Bastian Montague signed the Licence LEG-126.Bastian Osborne is the chair of Inglemere Partners Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited lent Inglemere Partners Limited GBP 22 million on 15 November 2025.

Inglemere Marine Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Partners Limited.

Inglemere Partners Limited owes Denmoor Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 20 November 2025.

Bastian Underwood is a shareholder of Inglemere Partners Limited.

Bastian Langford is a family member of Bastian Montague.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Inglemere Partners Limited owns Inglemere Capital Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Inglemere Partners Limited did not guarantee the facility for Inglemere Energy Limited on 31 October 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that Inglemere Partners Limited misstated its revenue.
