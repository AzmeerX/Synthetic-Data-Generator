Board meeting minutes of Marrowgate Logistics Limited

Date: 21 March 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00007.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Cairn LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department, Kira Kerr and Alden & Cairn LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Marrowgate Logistics Limited owns Juniper Data Centre.Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department is the chair of Marrowgate Logistics Limited.

Mira Mercer is a shareholder of Marrowgate Logistics Limited.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department is a family member of Kellan Lorne.

Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Marrowgate Logistics Limited GBP 26 million on 27 April 2022.

Ironside Industries Limited guaranteed the loan to Marrowgate Logistics Limited.

Marrowgate Logistics Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 11 million as at 2 May 2022.

Marlowe & Reed LLP represents Marrowgate Logistics Limited in the matter.

Marrowgate Logistics Limited owns Quarrystone Mill.

Quarrystone Mill is located in Eastmere.

Marrowgate Logistics Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.Kellan Lorne signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-008.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department, chair of Marrowgate Logistics Limited, proposed to acquire Glenmoor Trading Limited on 21 March 2024.

Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Glenmoor Trading Limited by Marrowgate Logistics Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Glenmoor Trading Limited by Marrowgate Logistics Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
