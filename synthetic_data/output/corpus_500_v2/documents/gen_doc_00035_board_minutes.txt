Board meeting minutes of Inglemere Industries Limited

Date: 3 October 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00035.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Yarrow & Zell LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Kingsley, Bastian Montague and Yarrow & Zell LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Inglemere Industries Limited owns Inglemere Group Limited.Bastian Kingsley is the chair of Inglemere Industries Limited.

Bastian Pemberton is a shareholder of Inglemere Industries Limited.

Bastian Hastings is a family member of Bastian Ingram.

Carrow International Bank Limited lent Inglemere Industries Limited GBP 14 million on 2 October 2025.

Inglemere Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Industries Limited.

Inglemere Industries Limited owes Carrow International Bank Limited GBP 5 million as at 7 October 2025.

Marlowe & Reed LLP represents Inglemere Industries Limited in the matter.

Inglemere Industries Limited owns Oakhaven Yard.

Oakhaven Yard is located in Kestrelford.

Inglemere Industries Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-122.Bastian Ingram signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-122.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Kerr, chair of Inglemere Industries Limited, proposed to acquire Inglemere Capital Limited on 3 October 2024.

Bram Kerr confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Inglemere Capital Limited by Inglemere Industries Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Inglemere Capital Limited by Inglemere Industries Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
