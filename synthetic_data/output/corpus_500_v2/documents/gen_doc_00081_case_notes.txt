Case notes for Lowmoor Marine Limited against Lowmoor Ventures Limited

Date: 21 August 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00081.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Lowmoor Marine Limited against Lowmoor Ventures Limited in Alderport County Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Lowmoor Marine Limited is a party to the Loan Note LEG-038.

Calder Ashby signed the Loan Note LEG-038.Calder Cavendish is the chair of Lowmoor Marine Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Dunmore Bank Limited lent Lowmoor Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 13 May 2022.

Lowmoor Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Lowmoor Marine Limited.

Lowmoor Marine Limited owes Dunmore Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 18 May 2022.

Calder Hastings is a shareholder of Lowmoor Marine Limited.

Calder Zell is a family member of Calder Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Lowmoor Marine Limited owns Dunhollow Works.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Lowmoor Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Lowmoor Ventures Limited on 21 August 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Amina Villiers noted that a former employee alleged that Lowmoor Marine Limited misstated its revenue.
