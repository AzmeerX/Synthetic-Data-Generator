Case notes for Carrow Energy Limited against Carrow Systems Limited

Date: 1 April 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00165.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Carrow Energy Limited against Carrow Systems Limited in Calderport Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Carrow Energy Limited is a party to the Trust Deed LEG-057.

Amina Sable signed the Trust Deed LEG-057.Amina Ulric is the chair of Carrow Energy Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Carrow Private Bank Limited lent Carrow Energy Limited GBP 44 million on 18 October 2023.

Carrow Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Carrow Energy Limited.

Carrow Energy Limited owes Carrow Private Bank Limited GBP 10 million as at 23 October 2023.

Amina Ashby is a shareholder of Carrow Energy Limited.

Amina Rook is a family member of Amina Sable.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Carrow Energy Limited owns Carrow Property Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Carrow Energy Limited did not guarantee the facility for Carrow Systems Limited on 1 April 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Everard noted that a former employee alleged that Carrow Energy Limited misstated its revenue.
