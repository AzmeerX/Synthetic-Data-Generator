Facility agreement between Bexley Private Bank Limited and Jarrow Logistics Limited

Date: 16 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00050.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

The lender is Bexley Private Bank Limited and the borrower is Jarrow Logistics Limited.

4. Conditions precedent
