Facility agreement between Alder Commercial Bank Limited and Greyhaven Industries Limited

Date: 28 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00043.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Alder Commercial Bank Limited and the borrower is Greyhaven Industries Limited.

Greyhaven Industries Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 17 million as at 7 July 2022.

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent
