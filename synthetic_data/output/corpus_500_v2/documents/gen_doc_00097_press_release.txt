Press release concerning Merrivale Ventures Limited

Publication date: 11 December 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00097.

1. Corporate statement

Merrivale Ventures Limited today issued the following statement.

Merrivale Ventures Limited owns Merrivale Systems Limited.

Calder Wyndham is a shareholder of Merrivale Ventures Limited.

Calder Osborne is a family member of Calder Pemberton.

Netherby Holdings Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Ventures Limited.

Merrivale Ventures Limited owes Bexley Private Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 30 October 2022.
