Board meeting minutes of Glenmoor Capital Limited

Date: 9 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00014.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Jarvis LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Quade, Bastian Sable and Alden & Jarvis LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Glenmoor Capital Limited owns Glenmoor Marine Limited.Bastian Quade is the chair of Glenmoor Capital Limited.

Bastian Vale is a shareholder of Glenmoor Capital Limited.

Bastian Nyle is a family member of Bastian Orrick.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Capital Limited GBP 16 million on 7 March 2025.

Glenmoor Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Capital Limited.

Glenmoor Capital Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 12 March 2025.

Alden & Hale LLP represents Glenmoor Capital Limited in the matter.

Glenmoor Capital Limited owns Greyhaven Yard.

Greyhaven Yard is located in Norwood.

Glenmoor Capital Limited is a party to the Services Agreement LEG-103.Bastian Orrick signed the Services Agreement LEG-103.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Bram Nyle, chair of Glenmoor Capital Limited, proposed to acquire Glenmoor Systems Limited on 9 May 2024.

Bram Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Glenmoor Systems Limited by Glenmoor Capital Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Glenmoor Systems Limited by Glenmoor Capital Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2024, which runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
