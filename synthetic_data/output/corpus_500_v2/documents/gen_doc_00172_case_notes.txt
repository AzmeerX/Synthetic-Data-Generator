Case notes for Dunmere Logistics Limited against Dunmere Capital Limited

Date: 5 February 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00172.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Dunmere Logistics Limited against Dunmere Capital Limited in Alderport Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Dunmere Logistics Limited is a party to the Side Letter LEG-064.

Amina Ashby signed the Side Letter LEG-064.Amina Cavendish is the chair of Dunmere Logistics Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Denmoor International Bank Limited lent Dunmere Logistics Limited GBP 18 million on 3 January 2024.

Dunmere Partners Limited guaranteed the loan to Dunmere Logistics Limited.

Dunmere Logistics Limited owes Denmoor International Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 8 January 2024.

Amina Hastings is a shareholder of Dunmere Logistics Limited.

Amina Zell is a family member of Amina Ashby.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Dunmere Logistics Limited owns Eastmere Hangar.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Dunmere Logistics Limited did not guarantee the facility for Dunmere Capital Limited on 5 February 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Langford noted that a former employee alleged that Dunmere Logistics Limited misstated its revenue.
