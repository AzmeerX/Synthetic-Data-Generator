Case notes for Ashcombe Systems Limited against Belmont Group Limited

Date: 5 November 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00144.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ashcombe Systems Limited against Belmont Group Limited in Bexhaven Commercial Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ashcombe Systems Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-036.

Gideon Nyle signed the Board Resolution LEG-036.Bram Kerr is the chair of Ashcombe Systems Limited.

Ashcombe Systems Limited owes Alder Commercial Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 6 March 2023.

Amina Dunmore is a shareholder of Ashcombe Systems Limited.

Farah Sable is a family member of Gideon Nyle.4. Findings and allegations
