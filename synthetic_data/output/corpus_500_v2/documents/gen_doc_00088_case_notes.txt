Case notes for Merrivale Industries Limited against Merrivale Trading Limited

Date: 9 October 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00088.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Merrivale Industries Limited against Merrivale Trading Limited in Bexhaven Appeal Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Merrivale Industries Limited is a party to the Shareholder Agreement LEG-044.

Calder Grenville signed the Shareholder Agreement LEG-044.Calder Ingram is the chair of Merrivale Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Alder Merchant Bank Limited lent Merrivale Industries Limited GBP 30 million on 18 July 2022.

Merrivale Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Merrivale Industries Limited.

Merrivale Industries Limited owes Alder Merchant Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 23 July 2022.

Calder Neville is a shareholder of Merrivale Industries Limited.

Calder Fairfax is a family member of Calder Grenville.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Merrivale Industries Limited owns Merrivale Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Merrivale Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Merrivale Trading Limited on 9 October 2025.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Cairn noted that a former employee alleged that Merrivale Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
