Case notes for Belmont Capital Limited against Belmont Property Limited

Date: 24 December 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00151.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Belmont Capital Limited against Belmont Property Limited in Dunhollow High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Belmont Capital Limited is a party to the Debenture LEG-043.

Amina Dunmore signed the Debenture LEG-043.Amina Fenwick is the chair of Belmont Capital Limited.

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Belmont Energy Limited guaranteed the loan to Belmont Capital Limited.

Belmont Capital Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 6 million as at 22 May 2023.

Amina Lorne is a shareholder of Belmont Capital Limited.

Amina Cairn is a family member of Amina Dunmore.4. Findings and allegations
