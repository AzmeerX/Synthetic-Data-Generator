Facility agreement between Bexley Merchant Bank Limited and Glenmoor Marine Limited

Date: 16 May 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00015.

1. Recitals

The borrower requested a term facility for working capital and refinancing.

2. Parties

The lender is Bexley Merchant Bank Limited and the borrower is Glenmoor Marine Limited.

Glenmoor Marine Limited owns Greyhaven Quay.Harrowgate Bank Limited lent Glenmoor Marine Limited GBP 18 million on 18 March 2025.

Glenmoor Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Glenmoor Marine Limited.

Glenmoor Marine Limited owes Harrowgate Bank Limited GBP 7 million as at 23 March 2025.

3. Facility terms

The facility is available subject to the conditions below.

4. Conditions precedent

The conditions precedent are set out in the schedule to the agreement.

5. RepaymentThe facility is repayable on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Glenmoor Marine Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-104.

Bastian Pell signed the Lease LEG-104.

6. Execution

The parties executed the agreement on the date stated above.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Oakhaven Holdings Limited GBP 42 million on 15 January 2024.

Glenmoor Marine Limited did not guarantee the facility for Glenmoor Ventures Limited on 16 May 2024.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
