Case notes for Carrow Industries Limited against Carrow Trading Limited

Date: 11 February 2027.

Document reference: GEN-00158.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Carrow Industries Limited against Carrow Trading Limited in Alderport Arbitration Tribunal.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Carrow Industries Limited is a party to the Facility Agreement LEG-050.

Amina Lorne signed the Facility Agreement LEG-050.Amina Nyle is the chair of Carrow Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Bexley Commercial Bank Limited lent Carrow Industries Limited GBP 30 million on 2 August 2023.

Carrow Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Carrow Industries Limited.

Carrow Industries Limited owes Bexley Commercial Bank Limited GBP 13 million as at 7 August 2023.

Amina Sable is a shareholder of Carrow Industries Limited.

Amina Jarvis is a family member of Amina Lorne.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Carrow Industries Limited owns Carrow Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Carrow Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Carrow Trading Limited on 11 February 2027.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Calder Ward noted that a former employee alleged that Carrow Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
