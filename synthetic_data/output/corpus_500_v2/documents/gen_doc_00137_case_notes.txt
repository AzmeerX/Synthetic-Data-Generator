Case notes for Ashcombe Trading Limited against Ashcombe Marine Limited

Date: 17 September 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00137.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Ashcombe Trading Limited against Ashcombe Marine Limited in Eastmere Crown Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Ashcombe Trading Limited is a party to the Settlement Agreement LEG-029.

Nolan Quade signed the Settlement Agreement LEG-029.Xander Garrow is the chair of Ashcombe Trading Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Eastmere Bank Limited lent Ashcombe Trading Limited GBP 28 million on 14 December 2022.

Ashcombe Capital Limited guaranteed the loan to Ashcombe Trading Limited.

Ashcombe Trading Limited owes Eastmere Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 19 December 2022.

Gideon Nyle is a shareholder of Ashcombe Trading Limited.

Lorne Sable is a family member of Nolan Quade.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Ashcombe Trading Limited owns Ashcombe Partners Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Ashcombe Trading Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ashcombe Marine Limited on 17 September 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.
