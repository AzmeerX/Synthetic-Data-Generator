Board meeting minutes of Harrowden Marine Limited

Date: 15 August 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00028.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Yarrow LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Everard, Bastian Grenville and Alden & Yarrow LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Harrowden Marine Limited owns Eastmere Terminal.Bastian Everard is the chair of Harrowden Marine Limited.

Bastian Jeffreys is a shareholder of Harrowden Marine Limited.

Bastian Balfour is a family member of Bastian Cavendish.

Bexley International Bank Limited lent Harrowden Marine Limited GBP 42 million on 28 July 2025.

Harrowden Property Limited guaranteed the loan to Harrowden Marine Limited.

Harrowden Marine Limited owes Bexley International Bank Limited GBP 9 million as at 2 August 2025.

Alden & Ulric LLP represents Harrowden Marine Limited in the matter.

Harrowden Marine Limited owns Ironside Foundry.

Ironside Foundry is located in Eastmere.

Harrowden Marine Limited is a party to the Board Resolution LEG-116.Bastian Cavendish signed the Board Resolution LEG-116.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Xander Garrow, chair of Harrowden Marine Limited, proposed to acquire Harrowden Systems Limited on 15 August 2024.

Xander Garrow confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Harrowden Systems Limited by Harrowden Marine Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Harrowden Systems Limited by Harrowden Marine Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
