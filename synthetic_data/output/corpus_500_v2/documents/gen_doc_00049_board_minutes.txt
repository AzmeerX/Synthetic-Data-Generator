Board meeting minutes of Jarrow Group Limited

Date: 9 January 2025.

Document reference: GEN-00049.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Orrick LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Yardley, Calder Alden and Alden & Orrick LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Jarrow Group Limited owns Jarrow Logistics Limited.Bastian Yardley is the chair of Jarrow Group Limited.

Calder Dunmore is a shareholder of Jarrow Group Limited.

Bastian Villiers is a family member of Bastian Whitlock.

Greyhaven Bank Limited lent Jarrow Group Limited GBP 40 million on 22 February 2026.

Jarrow Trading Limited guaranteed the loan to Jarrow Group Limited.

Jarrow Group Limited owes Greyhaven Bank Limited GBP 8 million as at 27 February 2026.

Alden & Jarvis LLP represents Jarrow Group Limited in the matter.

Jarrow Group Limited owns Calderport Warehouse.

Calderport Warehouse is located in Jarrow Cross.

Jarrow Group Limited is a party to the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.Bastian Whitlock signed the Guarantee Deed LEG-009.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Nyle, chair of Jarrow Group Limited, proposed to acquire Ravenmoor Group Limited on 9 January 2025.

Amina Nyle confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Ravenmoor Group Limited by Jarrow Group Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Ravenmoor Group Limited by Jarrow Group Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
