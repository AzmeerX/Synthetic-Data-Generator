Case notes for Larkspur Industries Limited against Ashcombe Holdings Limited

Date: 30 July 2026.

Document reference: GEN-00130.

1. Parties

The claim is brought by Larkspur Industries Limited against Ashcombe Holdings Limited in Bexhaven High Court.

2. Procedural history

The claim was managed in the ordinary commercial list.

Larkspur Industries Limited is a party to the Lease LEG-022.

Quin Pell signed the Lease LEG-022.Anika Dunmore is the chair of Larkspur Industries Limited.

3. Submissions

The parties made written and oral submissions to the court.

Fenwick Bank Limited lent Larkspur Industries Limited GBP 54 million on 28 September 2022.

Westmarch Logistics Limited guaranteed the loan to Larkspur Industries Limited.

Larkspur Industries Limited owes Fenwick Bank Limited GBP 25 million as at 3 October 2022.

Nolan Quade is a shareholder of Larkspur Industries Limited.

Anika Dunmore is a family member of Quin Pell.4. Findings and allegations

The court considered the evidence and the pleaded case.

Larkspur Industries Limited owns Northcliff Group Limited.

5. Orders and next steps

The court gave directions for the next hearing.

Larkspur Industries Limited did not guarantee the facility for Ashcombe Holdings Limited on 30 July 2026.

The facility will be reviewed six months after the date of this note.

Bastian Underwood noted that a former employee alleged that Larkspur Industries Limited misstated its revenue.
