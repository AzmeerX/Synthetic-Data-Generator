Board meeting minutes of Inglemere Energy Limited

Date: 21 November 2024.

Document reference: GEN-00042.

1. Attendance

The meeting was held at the offices of Alden & Hale LLP and was quorate throughout.

The following directors were present: Bastian Sinclair, Bastian Underwood and Alden & Hale LLP.

2. Agenda

The agenda covered group structure, financing arrangements, legal review and the acquisition proposal.

3. Matters discussed

The board reviewed the documents circulated before the meeting.

Inglemere Energy Limited owns Inglemere Property Limited.Bastian Sinclair is the chair of Inglemere Energy Limited.

Bastian Wyndham is a shareholder of Inglemere Energy Limited.

Bastian Osborne is a family member of Bastian Pemberton.

Northbank Bank Limited lent Inglemere Energy Limited GBP 28 million on 18 December 2025.

Inglemere Ventures Limited guaranteed the loan to Inglemere Energy Limited.

Inglemere Energy Limited owes Northbank Bank Limited GBP 12 million as at 23 December 2025.

Alden & Dunmore LLP represents Inglemere Energy Limited in the matter.

Inglemere Energy Limited owns Westmarch Office Park.

Westmarch Office Park is located in Denmoor.

Inglemere Energy Limited is a party to the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-003.Bastian Pemberton signed the Share Purchase Agreement LEG-003.

Nadia Zell of the Treasury Department and Nadia Zell of the Compliance Department are different individuals.

Amina Fenwick, chair of Inglemere Energy Limited, proposed to acquire Jarrow Holdings Limited on 21 November 2024.

Amina Fenwick confirmed that regulatory approval for the acquisition of Jarrow Holdings Limited by Inglemere Energy Limited has not been granted.

The acquisition of Jarrow Holdings Limited by Inglemere Energy Limited is conditional on regulatory approval.

The treasury plan covers FY2025, which runs from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.

4. Resolutions

The board resolved to note the facility arrangements and the legal review.

5. Actions

The company secretary will circulate the signed minutes within five business days.
